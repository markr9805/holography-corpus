BobH Thu Mar 17, 2005 4:05 am #3
> What would this holographer have done differently than a hobbyist shooring Denisyuks?

Paid a professional to create their subject matter, spent the time to light the scene adequately, used spatial filters on all the beams to keep the lighting clean, used a split beam geometry to have control of the uniformity and efficiency of the transmission master, and used (most likely) properly collimated beams to prevent distortions of the image. Also, the Denisyuk technique doesn't allow for 2-step animation of the scene.
