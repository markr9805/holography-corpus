JohnFP Wed Jun 23, 2004 10:18 am #3
This probably is not the information you are looking for, but I used to make 2 channel rainbow masters for a company. We has one channel as the background. It was a litho adhered to a ground glass plate such that we could back light it and at the same time we dropped the lighting to the object thus creating a silloette of the object with the illumintated background. For the second channel we dropped the backgound lighting and illuminated the object. The effect in the final hologram was a displacement of the rainbow colors of the object and background as each slit was about two inches wide or so, side by side.
