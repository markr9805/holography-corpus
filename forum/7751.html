I got the idea some years ago from my French girl-friend.

I was on a beach with her in France feeling a bit embarrassed and overdressed

among the sun- bathers around . As I started to strip off she looked at me sternly

and said:

"Jeffrey, tu es blanc comme un aspirin! "

So now I find the those universally available aspirin tablets the ideal “white body” testing agent when placed on top of the obligatory polished coins we all use.

Sure you do need the good old reliable coins to tell you if you have any hologram there at all, but you need those aspirins to tell you if you are actually getting good diffraction efficiency or not . I am currently working on photopolymer formulations and I can see at once that if my aspirins are just dark disks on my shiny coins then I have got a lot more work to do. They have GOT to look like proper aspirins complete with their indented dividing lines across their middles and not just shadows.

(Yes, they do have a slightly convex shape which can prevent them sitting flat down

on the coins stably but that is easily righted by rubbing their backs on say a nail file or fine sandpaper).

Jeff
