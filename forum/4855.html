Colin Kaminski Sat May 21, 2005 2:21 pm #3
If you try to dent a can with liquid in them you will see how much stronger the can is when it holds pressure. Air cans are much more durable. 319 is not very many. When I was looking I wanted 4x8' and 3 cans high! So buying air cans is also easier than collecting 1700 cans or whatever it worked out to.
