maskedkoala Tue Aug 18, 2020 10:06 am #1
Is there an easy way to remove emulsion from the plates? I'd like to resuse the glass from old or failed holograms and reuse them for other purposes.
jrburns47 Tue Aug 18, 2020 10:28 am #2
Straight Clorox bleach in a Pyrex glass dish. Wear nitrile gloves, mask, goggles & do it outside or use a fume hood.
maskedkoala Tue Aug 18, 2020 7:17 pm #3
Awesome. Thank you!
