JohnFP Thu Nov 16, 2006 12:43 pm #6
Cristiano, when making a Denisyuk, try to lay the plate horizontally right on the object. Choose an object that has three points the plate can lay on. Most vibrations will not ruin this type of set up. You can then remove the interferometer and use the additional power. Use the least amount of optics also. You can then expand your beam more to remove the hot spot without increasing the exposure time too much. In fact I would not be surprised if you can go to 10 minutes with this set up and still have no movement.

When I shoot Denisyuks with the above geometry, even though I shoot for about 1 minute, I keep the music playing, I walk around, the water is running there are no worries about vibrations. I think I read on the .it forum that you use tire tubes for vibration dampening. If so, I believe not even the train will cause movement if you lay the plate right on the object.

Keep up the great work. Your holograms looks fantastic.
