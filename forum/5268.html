Hi Don,

Good to have you on the forum. I agree with Jeff that it would not be beyond you to coat your own plates and save a whole lot of money. Dave’s coating method works very well is straight forward and incredibly inexpensive. Anyway incase you are interested here is the recipe and processing I am using. Probably not the best way, but it has been working very well for me.

100 ml distilled water.

2 packages of Knox unflavored gelatin, about 14 grams.

A small amount of AmDi ( I don’t know how much in grams I use, but it is a little over ¼ a teaspoon, probably less for your wavelength.) here is a good inexpensive source of AmDi

[http://cgi.ebay.com/Ammonium-Dichromate ... dZViewItem](http://cgi.ebay.com/Ammonium-Dichromate-99-7-Lab-Chemical-1-lb_W0QQitemZ6235451997QQcategoryZ413QQssPageNameZWDVWQQrdZ1QQcmdZViewItem)

Mix the gel and water until it is a thick apple sauce like consistency and then heat the mixture in a warm water bath to about 130F – 140F while stirring slowly until cooking temp is reached and the gel is completely melted and consistent.

Under safe light, mix in the proper amount of AmDi and stir at temperature until the AmDi is in solution.

Filter the whole mess through a coffee filter.

Follow Dave’s coating method (You can easily coat 10 4x5 plates with this amount) and let the plates cure for a few hours before exposing.

Processing:

Fix in Kodak rapid fixer with hardener for 1:45 seconds to 2:00 minutes.

Soak plate in warm water for 7 minutes.

Soak plate in warm 70% IPA with occasional agitation for 1 minute.

Agitate plate in hot 100% IPA for one or two minutes.

Rapidly dry the processed plate with a hot blow dryer for 2 + minutes.

My only experience with a HeCd was in Photo Resist and I remember the coherence length to be very short. Just keep that in mind.

But as for DCG, I believe it will like the HeCd line very much and will be very sensitive to that line.

I use 457 in DCG (about 20mw output total) and my exposures are about 45 seconds for a 5" x 7" plate (beam expanded to about 8 or 9 inches in diamter. The greens in my "The Model" and 'No-Feed No-Fuss Fish Tank" in the gallery are from 457 in DCG. I found that DCG is about 10 times more sensitive to 457 then 514.

Have fun!
