edelbrp Thu Dec 30, 2004 5:35 am #2
Hellohello,

I have one of those 5mW green laser pointers. Very cool! They are very useful for holography: they work great as a sort of instant-on safe light, you can illuminate holograms with them (for short durations), and they work great for presentations.

To make holograms? Probably not.

You can only keep it on for less than a minute at a time (or it overheats), and in a dark room it is quite visible from the illumination that the beam is barely stable (lots of erratic fuzziness.. mode hop?). Lastly, it's reported that these at best have a coherence length of a few millimeters, meaning that's the best depth you'll get from any hologram you make with it.

For these reasons it might make sense why similar technology green 100mW lasers with long coherence, like [this](http://cgi.ebay.com/ws/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&category=53141&item=3863589912&rd=1&ssPageName=WDVW), sell for more.

If you do buy one, I would recommend Altasnova. He's very honest about what he is selling, and it's a good price (well, quite a bit less than thinkgeek anyway). [(one of his ebay offerings)](http://cgi.ebay.com/ws/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&category=14954&item=5739989316&rd=1) Not to mention he has a 100% rating with 7000+ transactions.

-Phil
