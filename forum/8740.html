Holographic Salon, Lecture, and Book Signing at the Home + Studio of E. Wesly & Sons

Hot off the international lecture circuit, Hans Bjelkhagen will present the talks he has given in Warsaw, Poland; St. Petersburg, Russia; and San Francisco, USA!

See newly acquired items from his collection such as Zebra Imaging Star Wars Holograms plus the holograms on the Star Wars vinyl albums! A Michael Jackson perfume bottle! And a veritable cornucopia of holographic incunabula from the Home + Studios collection!

And he will autograph copies of his books if you bring them!

Sunday Afternoon, February 5th, 2:00 PM

Details at: [http://edweslystudio.com/HnEPortfolio/Hansfest2017.html](http://edweslystudio.com/HnEPortfolio/Hansfest2017.html)

Fill up your Orange Boxes and bring them over!

Happening this coming weekend! Out of town guest stars at this gala event include Mark Holzbach, co-founder of Zebra Imaging; John Klayer, speleo-holographer; Rob Taylor, Forth Dimension Holographics; Fred Needham, OSU and who knows who might surprise us!

On view will also be a Paula Dawson laser lit piece, and maybe even some Rudie Berkhouts! Plus a bunch of grab bag stuff!

See you then!
