Hi Folks,

I will be holding a Spatial Imaging Workshop in San Francisco, on Sunday, May 17th, 8:00 PM. Present will be a true 3D display, allowing 90 degrees of full horizontal parallax, with INFINITE stereoscopic views possible. Of course it is viewable without 3D glasses. All images move, interact with sound and can be controlled in real-time.

The visual quality is similar to a rainbow hologram, but not actual holography. It's a dynamic parallax barrier display, based on a high speed Cathode Ray Tube.

The workshop will be focused on image generation and processing techniques for the creation of true 3D moving images, plus spatial sound integration. Other types of 3D displays will be covered, and we'll work with the display at the workshop. This workshop will be introductory in nature, as part of an ongoing series with the intent to raise awareness of true 3D moving image techniques for the creation of art.

This first event is not 100% public, but if any members of this list wish to attend, please send me a message through this board, or respond to this message with some contact info. It's free.

Thank You,

Walter Funk

[http://www.hologlyphics.com/](http://www.hologlyphics.com/)

PS Later this month, on May 30th & 31st, I'll be showing the actual content, aka Hologlyphic Movies, at The Maker Faire in San Mateo, more on this soon.
