Colin Kaminski Mon Jan 24, 2005 4:15 pm #2
What is you intention for it and what continent are you on? There are very few turnkey "hologram machines". There are some small machines designed for teachers to show the concepts available for about $400 and then there are large machines designed for very large production runs for $100,000 and up. Most holographers simply build their own as it allows you to meet your needs better. If you are looking for a large machine that is beyond the skill level of your corporation there are consultants who can do the design, implementation and train your staff.
