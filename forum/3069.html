Colin Kaminski Wed Jan 26, 2005 3:52 am #2
I have destroyed diodes by using too stiff wire. Bumping into them in the dark tends to break the very fine wires in the inside. My solutiotion was to get some 23 guage stranded 3 conductor wire. The type I got was teflon coated and sold by Mc Master Carr. We use it at B3 for thermostat wires that get close to the flame.
