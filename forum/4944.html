jeff-blyth Sat Jul 09, 2005 6:47 pm #1
It is not permitted to pour dichromate down the sink, it is really a quite toxic material as it is a real flora and fauna killer.

But you can kill it's toxicity by chucking it into a container with your old developer solutions. That instantly converts it to an insoluble sludge of basic CrIII compounds. Don't put a lid back on the container tightly though as CO2 gas needs to come off from your carbonate and acid reaction.

[/b]

You do need to make sure your developer is in great excess over the dichromate Colin , so don't pour dev into old dichromate but dichromate into plenty of old dev.

I say this because although I never myself on principle use any developers that contain sulfite (eg D19 ) if such developers get turned to acid then you can get SO2 coming off. That will not happen as long as everything stays very alkaline.
