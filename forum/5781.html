I have found that if I use Glassex to clean my plates as the final step before coating, that I get no spots where the gelatin wants to run to or run away from on the glass. Nice coatings each time. Glassex is the blue fluid that can be bought in those plant spray bottles to quickly clean window glass.

Welcome everybody !

I'm completely new to holography - for now I've been making stereophotos, some CGH's and one hand-drawn hologram.

But I'm going to try making little holograms with PFG-1 plates and 7 mW diode laser.

About cleaning plates - maybe our technique for cleaning quartz wafers (before photolitography) could be useful. Cleaning solution is "pirahna" - 1:1 vol. mix of 95-98% sulphuric acid and 30% hydrogen peroxide.

Acid added to peroxide (!). Good wentilation required.

The mixture heats up itself to about 110 deg. C and is ideal for removing anything organic (i.e. photoresist) from our wafers. Removal of 4 um photoresist layer takes about 10 s (when mixture is not heated and not stirred). When heated to 180 deg. C it eats pieces of paper instantly.

After cleaning I rinse wafer in DI water (about 1l/wafer) and blowing with nitrogen (5N), quartz surface becomes completely hydrophilic.

Hope it helps.
