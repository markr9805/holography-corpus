BobH Sun Aug 10, 2008 2:59 pm #2
You want to find a "transmission" type hologram, also called a "laser transmission" hologram. You don't have to cut it in half, just cut a hole in a piece of black paper and hold it over the hologram to show the image from random different positions on the hologram.

What is generally said about all parts of a hologram containing the whole image is false. A myth. In fact, each part of a hologram contains information about the image from that particular range of perspective views. A small piece has less information than a large piece. A single point on the hologram contains a single perspective view, and at much reduced resolution than is visible to the human eye looking at the original object from the same perspective.
