MichaelH Thu Dec 01, 2005 3:20 am #2
If the lens you got is already bad, you might try cleaning it. You'll probably have to take it apart but I've had good success with two out of four attempts (since they were already bad, I could only make them better).

Yes, if you use the cleanest lenses you can, you'll end up with more light passing through the pinhole.

Also be certain you aren't using too small a pinhole. That can cause you to lose light as well.

BobH Thu Dec 01, 2005 3:51 pm #4
50% light loss through the spatial filter is waaay too much! Are you certtain you're measuring all the light that makes it through?

Think about how much you paid for each milliwatt of light. A "cheap" lens that wastes that much light is not "cheap". Could be the reflectance of the coatings. Could be the beam diameter is too big and there is some clipping. Could be dirt of the surfaces. Could be scratches all over the output surface. Could be mis-alignment in use.

I treasure my best set of microscope objectives, and always keep them in clean containers when not in use.

"The sadness of poor quality remains long after the joy of low price is forgotten."

Tony Wed Apr 26, 2006 3:12 pm #20
The use of a spatial filter is optional but usually desired, to clean up the beam for holography. The size of the objective lens depends on the size of the film/plate you are using as well as your beam diameter. Once you determine the size of lens, you can then determine the size of the pinhole. A useful chart of pinhole sizes can be found at:

[http://www.edmundoptics.com/onlinecatal ... uctid=1291](http://www.edmundoptics.com/onlinecatalog/DisplayProduct.cfm?productid=1291)

...and you can determine the focal length of the objective lens from the magnification using the web tool at:

[http://www.olympusmicro.com/primer/java ... callength/](http://www.olympusmicro.com/primer/java/microscopy/focallength/)
JohnFP Wed Apr 26, 2006 3:48 pm #21
The 40X will work to expand you laser beam. But at 3 - 5 watts, make sure it does not have any coatings that will burn. You will see these burns when you hold the objective up to the light see little black dot(s) within the objective. We used to burn out some objectives with only a watt or so.

The pinhole will clean that expanded beam and if you already have a 25um, that will work fine.

It is not necessary to use a pinhole to make a hologram but it makes a cleaner hologram depending on the amount of unwanted diffraction you get from dirt on the optics leading to the expanding lens (objective) including that lens. Also be aware again that at 3 - 5 watts, some pinholes may not hold up to the heat. A thicker (2 mil/50 micron thick) instead of a 1/2 mill SS may be needed or gold plated high power pinhole may be needed.

If you do not have the objective and pinhole yet and have to purchase them, review Tonys post and remember to get an objective and pinhole that can handle the CW (continuous wave) power you are squeezing through it (you can ask the manufacturer for this).
