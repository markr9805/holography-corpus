A while ago I was doing some research on Solar Cells for another project I was doing and found some interesting data on solar cells. Most solar cells are wavelength selective, as in the Amorphous Solar Cells, and their spectral response reaches maximum at the wavelengths of blue light. Longer wavelengths only heat the cell causing faster degradation. So why not cover with a narrow band blue wavelength transmission HOE?
