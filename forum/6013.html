BobH Fri Jan 26, 2007 4:16 pm #6
I think it was Lloyd Huff that first described the "slice & dice" technique for making one-step stereograms. His paper is in the proceedings from the first International Symposium on Display Holography.

Ken Haines patented a technique for using a curved master to make flat stereograms in the late '70s I think. His system may still be in use at ABNH.
