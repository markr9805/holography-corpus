wler Sat Apr 09, 2005 7:58 am #2
Hi,

the question about the suitability of air-cooled argon lasers comes

up all the time... shouldn't this be put into a FAQ at some point ?

At any rate, the answer is that this kind of lasers is not well

suited for holography applications, one reason being the short

coherence length. Only ones equipped with an etalon (and preferably

are water coolied) are good for this.

A laser like this is worth quite a bit, I am not sure precisely how much,

but I would say at least 1-1.5 K$ and more, depending on whether

it includes a power supply, the age and well-being of the tube etc.

This kind of lasers is in high demand by the light show people, but

since more and more cheap solid state lasers become available, the

value of argon lasers goes down all the time, so if you want to

sell it, do it soon. If you just want to do holography, why don't

you swap it against a suitable solid state (DPSS) laser.

Well, there are many answers for this. Think not of what is the best laser but what is the image you are looking to create and what laser will help you do that.

A good laser would be an Argon laser with an etelon for blue or green. A C315M green laser or perhaps a High Power HeNe for red.
