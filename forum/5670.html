Colin Kaminski Sun Jul 02, 2006 5:05 am #5
I have not produced lenticulars but the simplest ones have a prism grating on the front. It serves to send every other line to a different eye. Then you take two images and stripe them together at the same spacing of the lenticular lens. The higher number of stripes the better the image looks but the more difficult it is to align the stripes to the grating.

I am not sure if you know what beat frequencies but thay are particularly troubling in high LPI lenticulars.
