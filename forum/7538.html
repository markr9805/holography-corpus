This I hope will be a bit of a "stirrer" posting.

The heart of the problem is the way our human brains have evolved.

We are where we are as a result of inherent neophilia by which I choose to mean

excitement by any newness that we have not seen before. Once we have seen it , then

we quickly get bored and want to move on and find the next new thing.

Todays miracle or rather WOW- LOOK- AT –THAT- CAN –YOU- BELIEVE- IT! item , is tomorrow’s big yawn.

In evolutionists’ jargon this means that this behaviour pattern for quickly getting bored with what’s new has been selected for, because the alternative strategy coming from alternative gene arrangements or alleles which govern brain structure and our behaviour which would make us satisfied with our lot would have left us still living up trees or in caves with our ape cousins.

So most of us on this website still have a memory of the huge excitement we originally got from either seeing a good art hologram and even more from making one. Even the monochromaticity or eerie all- red or all- green colour of Denisyuk holograms was a cause for excitement whereas (from recent postings here) that all -green color is actively disliked by many old hands today. And yet I am firmly of the opinion that going back to the time before the general public had ever seen any hologram (around say 1978 say), had a Denisyuk hologram in full colour been on sale at a reasonable price next to another one at the same price in say 514nm color , then the general public, totally technically ignorant, would have been more inclined to buy the weird monochromatic one rather than the much more natural looking true color one!

The public mood was for novel weirdness not familiar naturalness. When it comes down to it , only we holographers really appreciate full color Denisyuk holograms.

As far as the general public goes is the full color Denisyuk worth that much more than the full color lenticular postcard?

What is left for excitement for me now is just the science behind it all and that is due to its boundary-less nature and the development of new PRACTICAL uses, that is enough to satisfy my personal neophilic cravings.

Let us just take a hard look at the idea suggested in this thread of hoping for the arrival of a holographic Leonardo Da Vinci .

We should have the greatest contempt for the world art market, (unless we are in the big Auction Houses business of course). If Leonardo’s Mona Lisa came up for auction it would fetch more than the cost of building and equipping a new large hospital capable of saving the lives of many and improving the lives of many more. Leonardo himself would have had the greatest contempt for this situation. If people REALLY revered this painting so much they could all have a perfect reproduction of it for peanuts in their own houses, but do they? well obviously not . This has nothing to do with an art and inspiration, it has everything to do with contemptible hype, fashion and all the rest of it.

Jeff
