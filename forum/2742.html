What do you mean by "animated" and "standing in air"? You cannot have a free floating hologram of an object standing in air with no plate. You need to illuminate a plate somehow. This is one of the most famous: [http://www.physics.umd.edu/lecdem/servi ... /m6-12.htm](http://www.physics.umd.edu/lecdem/services/demos/demosm6/m6-12.htm)

Basically you project imndividual frams onto an LCD or with a movie projector. You then use optics to convert the image into a single thin slice and image the slice onto the holographic film. You move the holo film, slice the next frame and image that etc for all the frames.

where k = Boltzmann's constant

and T = temperature.
