I personally cannot stand magnetic stands. I'm very clumsy and tend to knock things, and hate when I knock a stand by a millimeter. Also I find it hard to move them accurately - they take a fair amount of force to move.

I used to like the bases you can screw down, but found it hard to move them by a small amount in the direction I want - the nearest hole always been too far away or too close.

My favorite by a long stretch is the design that looks a bit like this:

r--o--C

one end pushes into to table anywhere (illustrated with the 'r') and one end is a C shape that holds on to the base. Then a bolt goes through the "o". Allows me to hold down posts securely, and yet i can move them with ease.

MichaelH Mon Apr 04, 2005 11:34 am #8
> JohnFlux: I personally cannot stand magnetic stands. I'm very clumsy and tend to knock things, and hate when I knock a stand by a millimeter. Also I find it hard to move them accurately - they take a fair amount of force to move.

I've found the Harbor Freight magnetic mounts to be reliable in both areas. I'm often running into a mount in the dark and have only had them move when they aren't turned on. Turn it off and it's as easy to move as any gravity mount. Turn it on and it's locked in place.

BTW, never shift a mount around on the table when it's on. Any burr or piece of grit between the mount and the table will cause a deep scratch that can negatively affect the flatness of the table in that area.
