Martin Tue Oct 10, 2006 5:18 am #4
> Hans: Ah, and I forgot to mention that this developer bleaches the film at the same time as during development.

The stock solutions of those colloidal developers keep pretty well.

Colloidal development forms an amplitude and phase hologram at the same time. So such developers don't bleach the emulsion actually. Upon development tiny disc shaped silver particles are generated, resulting in (yellow-red) colloidal layers.

al_gont Tue Oct 10, 2006 6:01 pm #7
The matter is not in the type of hologram but in the size of grains of recording material.

GP-3 is to be used for PFG-03m. It won't work with VPR or PFG-01 that are usually used for recording transmission holograms.

In fact, transmission hologram can be recorded on PFG-03m. It is about ten times less sensitive and exposure will take much more time. But it can be proccessed with GP-3.
