Tom B. Tue Jan 01, 2008 7:28 am #9
1) TEA generally increases film sensitivity by a factor of 2 or more. You should re-check your exposure to avoid overexposure which can lead to milkiness.

2) A more subtle problem which I rarely see mentioned is that even if both sides are squeegeed, TEA leaves a hazy film on the glass (or non-emusion side of the film) which shows up as diffuse milkiness in the hologram. The cure is to wipe the non-emulsion side with a clean wet paper towel and then gently wipe dry with a dry cloth. You can see the hazy scum by reflection in a safelight and it is easy to see when the plate has been cleared.
