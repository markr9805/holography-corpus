MarkCavin Thu Oct 21, 2004 2:56 am #4
Hi guys,

Thanks. Colin, I think 03M and 03C just make very good Denisyuk; better than VRP. I like VRP and PFG-01 for transmissions. Working with 03C is pretty much identical to working with 03M, except a lower intensity safe light is needed. 03C is least sensitive in red so I use a red party bulb enclosed in a garage door opener light housing. I get a nice diffused dim red glow in the lab and notice no fogging in plates exposed for short periods of time. I try to limit light hitting the plate by carding or boxing between the plate and party bulb that is some 15 feet away from the table and 8 feet above the floor. If I had a red laser I would use 03M for Denisyuk and PFG-01 for transmissions. I just applied the same logic for green lasers; 03C for Denisyuk and VRP for transmissions.

Mark
