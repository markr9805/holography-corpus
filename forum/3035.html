Folks -

Reminder: from Jan. 17-19 2005, the annual 3-D display conference will be held in San Jose, CA: "Stereoscopic Displays and Virtual Reality Applications." It is part of the Electronic Imaging 2005 symposium. Also, there will be a demo session of many 3-D displays.

For details, visit: [http://www.stereoscopic.org](http://www.stereoscopic.org)

See you there. I'll be part of the 3-D display conference committee & will present a paper which covers quite a bit of ground, discussing a software architecture that will be able to extend the reach & applicability of 3-D displays by offering a unified display-independent architecture.

Gregg Favalora
