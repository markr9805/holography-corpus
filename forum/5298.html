jean Thu Dec 29, 2005 3:30 pm #7
You can begining with a glove box.

[http://www.omnisterra.com/botany/cp/slides/tc/hood.htm](http://www.omnisterra.com/botany/cp/slides/tc/hood.htm)

But, before begin thinking about free dust cabinet, try first making your plates. There is a lot of tips and tricks you need to learn, specialy with silver halide emulsion. Don't be to paranoïd about dust, at least for your 2nd or 3rd batches. You must try several methods of coating, drying, hardening, etc.

If you work in a clean room and wear clothing out of cotton or synthetic (not wool pullover) and a shower cap (if you have tendency to lose your hairs) and glove, of course, there is minimal risk of dust.

Other point is (for diffusion method), if you are not satisfied of the coating, you can easily remove it (hardening not yet occured).

Don't forget, making our own plates is about 1/10th of the commercial cost and it's really fun to deal with this process.
