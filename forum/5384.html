MichaelH Sat Feb 18, 2006 3:57 pm #4
All good info and you're in luck. I use the same chemicals and very similar laser.

To start, make up a 4% solution of TEA (4ml of TEA and add water to 100ml if you're using small film/plates) and soak your film in it for two minutes. [Squeegee the film](http://www.dragonseye.com/blog/archives/11-Squeegeeing.html) and let it dry.

Now make the first exposure but cut the exposure time by 1/3.

Take the film and rinse out the TEA, squeegee and dry again.

Now make a second exposure with a different subject (or move your original subject over slightly so the two final images don't occupy the same space)

![Image](http://www.dragonseye.com/gallery/d/2872-1/TEAMultiExposure.jpg)

and and expose again with 2/3 of the time you normally would.

Process the hologram normally and after drying you should find a hologram with one red object and one greenish object in the same hologram.

Playing with the TEA % will allow you to get some pretty precise control over your colors.
