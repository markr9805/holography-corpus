I wanted to continue your conversation from introductions.

Those MBDCG holograms you made and faded or more correctly, shifted to blue or violet can be reprocessed and brought back to life. Soak them in lukewarm water for 5 minutes. Then dehydrate as usual in alcohol, then blow dry.

JohnFP Tue Nov 07, 2006 3:58 pm #3
I have not tried it but let's talk about it.

There is no more sensitiser or dye left in either.

Both have been crosslinked at the light sites more then crosslinking at the dark sites. After processing we have a differential of index of refraction between the two sites.

As the hologram sits is absorbs moisture and causes the fringes to swell probably more so in the less crosslinked sites.

Reprocessing only takes this water out (simply put).

So to me after they are processed the two holograms MBDCG and DCG are the same and thus I still believe reprocessing would work.

Any adverse conjectures?

Hans Wed Nov 08, 2006 8:13 pm #4
Yes, you can re-process them just like normal DCG. I do feel though that after many re-processing a little noise comes into the hologram. For optimum processing I cut a plate in half. Expose the first half the second half identically. I then find the optimum water temperature through several re-processing steps for the first plate. Then I process the second plate at that optimum temperature in one go, for the best result.

If a plate fades to blue after some time (because it was not covered with something), you can then re-process it again at that same water temperature.
