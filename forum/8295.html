BobH Sun May 23, 2010 2:07 pm #2
Embossed hologram materials can certainly be put onto Lexan. Bending it hologram will mess up the image. Even the dot matrix images. Holographic films are not very durable. I'd think they wouldn't last very long in sunlight, or being cleaned of outdoors contamination.
