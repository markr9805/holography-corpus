It does not matter what color you start with you can make the final hologram any color you wish. I prefer gold for most holograms.
Tony Thu Mar 23, 2006 11:59 pm #3
35 mW HeNe's can be had for around $300 (bare head). You can build a supply for under $100.

I think there's more emulsions available for red...

With green, if you go to the 100mW level, the common laser is the Compass 315M which will cost you around $1500. However, with all that power, exposure times get very short and vibration is less of a problem.

If you're resigned to dropping a couple of g's, keep an eye out for a Compass as Tony mentions. You can get loads of power (100mW) for $1500 and end up with very short exposure times (unless you go DCG) and extremely long coherence length.

If you only care about the final color of the hologram it won't matter what color your laser is. You can pre or post swell the emulsion to get almost any color in the rainbow.

It all depends on how complex your setups are. I just finished a custom HOE with a 25mW laser and my exposures were 8 seconds. This was basically one 8" diameter collimated beam and one 2" collimated beam hitting the plate.

My average three beam transmission master (generally 5x7") exposures run anywhere from 20 to 40 seconds depending on the subject.

having 4x the power would greatly reduce that time.
