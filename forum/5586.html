Ed Wesly Tue May 30, 2006 2:13 pm #4
Correct! The glow around the object is the evil intermodulation noise caused by self-interference of the object beam. Increasing the reference and lowering the object intensitites will cure that.

It varies from object to object, that'w why there are no really hard and fast numbers for beam balance ratio. Not bright enough, jack up the object light. Shadows not black and haze around the object, you've overshot and need to bias toward the reference.

Haze on the surface of the hologram is another story.
