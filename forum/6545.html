Acrylamide Polymer

-----So, does somehow due to the action of UV light, the C=C bond at the bottom of the Acrylamide molecule re-arrange into a sp3 bond together with the next Acrylamide bond?

Often, Acrylic Acid is used in the polymer mix.

Acrylic Acid:

![Image](http://hologramhans.com/pictures/Acrylic%20Acid.jpg)

And, also N,N'-methylenebisacrylamide, which has a structure as follows:

N,N'-methylenebisacrylamide:

![Image](http://hologramhans.com/pictures/Methylene%20Bisacrylamide.jpg)

-----Why are these two compounds often mixed with Acrylamide in a photopolymer? I can imagine how they can polymerize together with Acrylamide because they also contain the magical C=C pi bond. Is it perhaps advantageous because non-linear polymers can be formed with branches in different directions (and thus making the larger polymer molecule more localized)?

And, in all the articles I have seen, PVA is used.

PVA

===================================================================================================

So, I found a recipe of which the authors claim a resolution of more than 3000 lines per mm. Furthermore, they claim a Diffarcttion Efficiency of more than 80 with this dry mix. I have tried it, but it did not work for me. Here is the recipe:

5% (wv) PVA (Wolecular Weight that is 9000 or less)

2.4% (wv) Acrylamide

0.45% (wv) Methylene Bisacrylamide

2% (vv) Acrylic Acid

0.2% (vv) TEA

.002% (wv) Methylene Blue

Coat and dry for two days.

I am not sure why it did not work for me. The only variable with my mix was that I had a PVA with a higher molecular weight. I used 4-88 PVA. One problem with this PVA is perhaps that it is not fully hydrolized. But the molecular weight of this PVA should still allow transmission holograms. So, is it perhaps that the PVA that needs to be used must be fully hydrolized?

If I shone an undiverged laser beam at a drop of this mix, it did not solidify. Should it?

I'm not much versed in hydrophilic systems (because the use of water soluble MB) but the system compared to Polaroid DMP-128 one, it need some humidity ~50%(?) to promote activation with oxygen (!) so O2 takes part of system.

The PVA seems used only for a viscosity increase (binder element) and does not react with acrylamide, the in situ radical polymerization need to be real time and you could verify it even when recording the hologram.

> If I shone an undiverged laser beam at a drop of this mix, it did not solidify. Should it?

Yes, it should solidify and bleach. Polygrama has an experimental photopolymer film - Croma Red that is a fully real time photopolymer material (Hydrophobic however).
