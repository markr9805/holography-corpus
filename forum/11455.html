You're probably getting this distortion because of a mismatch between the recording beam (assuming this is a Denisyuk) and the reconstruction beam. The divergence of the recording and recontruction must be as equal as possible, so if you're using a divergent reference beam at, say, 30 degrees, the reconstruction must also be at 30 deg, or as close as possible, other wise distortions - usually magnifications - occur.. The sun is a collimated source (zero angle of divergence) and the LED is typically a 20 deg divergent source.

If you say how you're expanding the reference, and the distance from laser to film, it's possible to calculate the reference beam divergence.

The rings are probably film movement. The film moved at the centre of the set of circles - possibly because you screwed that corner too hard.
