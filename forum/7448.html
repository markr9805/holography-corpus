Here's another formula for a permanganate reversal bleach, avoiding sulfuric acid:

potassium permanganate...............................1g

acetic acid................................................100ml

distilled water..................................................1L

Right after the bleach put your plate/film into a clearing solution, formed by:

ascorbic acid.................................................20g

water................................................................1L

This will remove the mangane stain almost instantly. A thorough water wash will then get rid of any trace of ascorbic acid – in order to prevent darkening of the layer.

> Sergio: Great, would work with Ultimate? WDYT?

I haven't seen any Ultimate films/plates for a while now, so I am not sure.

Based on the assumption that Ultimate has a high silver concentration, my guess would be that revesal bleaching may be applied in this case.

PFG-01 and alike seem to be best qualified for this treatment.

The only emulsion I'd definitely rule out is PFG-03.

Generally speaking, reversal bleaching may be interesting:

- where exposure levels are low;

- where a wavelength shift towards blue is intended (though with alcohol dehydration/“drying“ no wavelength shift will take place);

for transmission master holograms.
