Hi.

Some time ago I've made an MBDCG hologram. Its size is something like 3,4cmx2cm, Jeff's classical MBDCG formula (with acetic acid), 20' exposure with 0,9mW HeNe, beam diameter 3,5cm. Swollen in 25degC. water, IPA 90%, IPA 100%. I used mold coating with 60um tape, so gelatine thickness should be something like 7-8um. I used less methylene blue (5ml instead of 6ml) since I don't have thinner tape.

Parts of the object that were contacting the plate are very bright, golden in appearance and visible over wide angle.

Everything that was further than 1mm from the emulsion is also visible, but is dim and can be seen over very narrow angle. Looks more like in embossed holograms.

What's the problem ? I would suspect loss of light caused by thick layer of gelatine containing methylene blue. Maybe diluting the mixture would help ? What do you think guys ?

greetings
