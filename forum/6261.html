Dave, if your getting very nice narrow band reflection holograms, maybe you would like to try the following. I am sure it would be an impressive hologram.

Taking into consideration the way embossed transmission holograms are displayed how about the following.

Make a very narrow band DCG reflection hologram, wavelength not important at this time. The Reflection hologram will simply be a mirror, or a grating. Then make an image planed tranmission hologram.

When viewing place the narrow band reflection "mirror" behind the transmisson hologram such that in replay the illuminating white light passes though the transmisison hologram, is narrow bandly reflected back through the transmission hologram illuminating it with a narrow band and thus a very deep transmission hologram can be viewed in a reflection illuminaton configuration with white light.

Simple, but I bet impressive.

Uli Tue May 22, 2007 12:36 pm #11
Answering to Adam about silver, the answer is yes. Before joining Colourholographic I use to work with Fimia's group at Elche University, and we started some works on recording 3D structures inside holographic materials (photonic crystals). Using a He-Ne laser and BB640 plates we recorded two crossed unslanted transmission gratings and one unslanted reflection grating in the same plate. The amazing result was that measuring the DE of the individual gratings we got between 50% and 60% for each of them. The result was published in Optics Express: [http://oe.osa.org/abstract.cfm?id=80310](http://oe.osa.org/abstract.cfm?id=80310)

I was always thinking what would happen if you record one transmission grating together with an image reflection hologram (say a Denisyuk). If you tune them properly (and there is where the main problems will be), would that have any advantage compared to the single Denisyuk in terms of reconstruction? Will that improve or add flexibility to the illumination conditions of the hologram?

I did it with the experimental P635C Polygrama Photopolymer film, a Denisyuk reflection with the aid of a linear mirror at the center of image produced an efficient transmission grating while the reflection image was illuminated with the aid of same mirror produced the improved part.

Onto image the central part (Shadow) is were the mirror fits, light exposure comes from up in a single beam setup, "ADR M5U2" is the part where transmission grating is, (note the dimmer effect on the reflection image) the Japanese letters are on the assisted beam illumination enhances the reflection image.

90 sec exposure on dry photopoymer film lamimated onto glass film thickness 50um photopolymer: 20um, 5mW diode laser. Only tungsten light post fixing.
