I've been looking at old Multiplex holograms recently and thought I'd post a pic of a couple. The first is of a model of the starship "Enterprise" used for "Star Trek, The Motion Picture", made in 1979 by the Multiplex Company. I was told by Joe Belk (who worked there at the time) that the hologram was commissioned as decoration for the cast party at the opening of the movie. It has a nasty scratch on the base side of the film that can't be seen in this pic. I hope it can be indexed away by laminating the hologram to a cylinder in the future.
