Guillermito Thu Aug 23, 2007 12:37 pm #2
I don't live in France anymore, but I tried several times

in the last few years to have a look in The Halles when I

was in Paris, and there is no museum. That pissed me

off quite a bit, because they don't even say in their own

website ( [http://www.museeholographie.com](http://www.museeholographie.com) ) that the museum

does not exist. And it is listed on every damn tourist site

(example : [http://www.museums-of-paris.com/musee_en.php?code=395](http://www.museums-of-paris.com/musee_en.php?code=395)

), with opened hours and everything, as if it was real.

From what I understand, they still have their big collection,

use it for temporary exhibitions around Europe, but they

do not have a permanent place to show it in Paris, although

they would certainly like to have one.

After some research, I found a post on their blog (in french)

which starts with "Le Musée demeurera virtuel en 2007,

sans exposition permanente." which means "The Museum

will still be virtual in 2007, without permanent exhibition".

[http://museeholographie.over-blog.com/a ... 60721.html](http://museeholographie.over-blog.com/article-5260721.html)

I saw it once there by the way, but that was probably like

fifteen or twenty years ago.

walschuler Mon Aug 27, 2007 6:16 am #4
I visited the museum of holography in Paris at its les Halles location. It closed as a visitable museum back in the 1990s or early 2000s, I think. I was surprised to hear of its web presence.

I think the 2 large, still visitable holography exhibits are at the Museum at MIT, on Massachusetts Ave., in Cambridge, which bought the collection of the Museum of Holography in NYC, when it failed, and the Chicago Museum of Holography, just west of the Loop there.

The Science Museum in London has a smaller exhibit. Jonathan Ross has a private collection at his London home (see his website) and he often has small exhibitions there. It can be visited, by pre-arrangement.

There may be an exhibit in Germany, and a company in Moscow, that has a web presence, has some sort of large in-store exhibit, I believe.

Anyone have any details on those, or any other major exhibits?
