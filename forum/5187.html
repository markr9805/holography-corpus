Here is a new idea I have for a holographic camera to take pictures outside during the day or night. I know it sounds silly, but I am full of wild ideas that are impossible.

Starting from right to left we have

The Pulsed laser with KTP green with control electronics

A beam widening lens

The film held in an open frame

A slowly removable shutter that blocks light in transit till you are ready to shoot

An LCD shutter comprised by 2 cross polarizers with the liquid crystals between them

All these items comprise a light tight enclosure. When the LCD is getting no DC power, it will be nearly dark (4000:1 contrast ratio). The added shutter is for extra protection.

To take a picture, select an object that is in the shade. Remove the internal shutter. The LCD will be off and the film is still in the dark.

On pressing the electronic snapshot button, it applies a 50 volt DC to the capacitor like LCD element. It "clears" in about 5 milliseconds. At that time the Laser is fired for a 4 nanosecond pulse of green light that passes through the film once, passes through the LCD open shutter losing 42%, reflects off the close object, and passes back through the LCD losing another 42% to hit the film again to make a reflection hologram. Object and reference beam ratios may be off, but it might work.

Then the shutter is closed over 0.005 sec. At your leisure replace the internal shutter and go home to develop, or forward to next film shot in the "roll" of hologram film.

Key to this device is that the shutter must be fast enough to avoid the normal ambient light from fogging the hologram film. Direct intense sunlight is 1 Kilowatt per meter squared. Or 1/10 Watt per cm squared. For an open shutter of 0.005 sec we let in 500 microjoules per cm squared. In the shade we can expect maybe 1/100 th of that intensity. Which gives is 5 microjoules per square cm. Green hologram film is sensitive to 20-40 microjoules per square cm so we are OK.

This is just a proposal of an experiment. The unit can be made portable by the use of a 6V lantern battery, and power inverters which are available. I have ordered a free sample of the LCD unit. I don't have the time to begin testing it yet. Think about Hologram photo booths that spit a picture of you out in 5 minutes!

What do you think, is it too weird to be done? Am I missing something? Anyone want to try it till I have more free time? Comments are welcomed.
