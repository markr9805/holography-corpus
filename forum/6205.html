The Borg have had the hologram eyepatches since they first appeared in one of the movies. That design was an off-the shelf image at the time. Maybe still is!

Reminds me of the Star Trek holograms I have in my collection. One is probably the first transmission hologram I made in school. It's a pewter model of the original Enterprise on a 4x5" plate. The other is a 120 degree Multiplex hologram of the Enterprise model used for the first movie. I was told the image was made for a "cast party" when it was given to me a long time ago. I've held it up by hand to see it, but it's never been properly mounted or displayed. Another project!
