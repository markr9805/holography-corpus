Presumably most of you are read-up on this, but the recent advances in the computer-graphic field of "light field rendering" have intellectual implications (or, not to sound trendy, synergies) with the field of holography.

Marc Levoy and others wrote a series of articles for IEEE Computer. (At least) the primary survey article is available online:

[http://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/lfphoto/](http://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/lfphoto/)

E.g., some of these concepts relate, perhaps, to the "3-D" lens that Kaveh brought up on the other-topics forum, and certainly to many emerging quasi-holographic 3-D displays.

Thoughts?

Some of this may be tough theoretical sledding. This is the entry point to a gold mine of articles and videos to get you thinking: [http://www-graphics.stanford.edu/projects/lightfield/](http://www-graphics.stanford.edu/projects/lightfield/) . You might want to spend a few minutes looking at the papers and downloadable videos linked at the bottom, Recent Papers in this Area.
