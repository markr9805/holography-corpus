Maybe OMSI? There are some travelling hologram exhibits that have visited OMSI in the past. I couldn't tell if they were going to visit OMSI soon, but you might email them and ask.

[http://www.holophile.com/html/exhibit.htm](http://www.holophile.com/html/exhibit.htm)

That said, OMSI does have a holography lab where they do demonstrations and make a hologram. I haven't seen it, but it sounds neat.

[http://www.omsi.org/visit/physics/holography/](http://www.omsi.org/visit/physics/holography/)

I doubt they have much like Gentet's work, but it's a place to start.

I also just ran across this:

[http://www.3dcenter.us/](http://www.3dcenter.us/)

Post a follow up if you find out more. PDX isn't too far from where I'm at, and it might be worth the trip if there's a neat event happening. ;')

I am not aware of museums with Gentet's work. If you are willing to hop in a plane, I recommend:

MIT Museum (Cambridge, MA, which is across a river from Boston)

They have breathtaking holograms, as well as other techie-friendly exhibits. I must've gone five times.

[http://web.mit.edu/museum/](http://web.mit.edu/museum/)

[http://web.mit.edu/museum/exhibitions/holography.html](http://web.mit.edu/museum/exhibitions/holography.html)

Museum of Holography, Chicago

[http://chicago.citysearch.com/profile/3658183](http://chicago.citysearch.com/profile/3658183)
