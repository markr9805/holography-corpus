Andy Dean Tue Oct 12, 2004 6:09 am #1
After reading page after page of “criticism” thought I would start a thread on a positive note.

For me the one hologram that stands out is “The Painter,” by John Perry Please see links below.

I first sew this hologram in the Chicago Museum of holography, and was blown away by it.

It was not the biggest or technically advanced hologram I had ever seen but it was the most “complete” hologram I have ever seen. It’s a hologram about holography.

I always liked the Quote “Holography is painting with light” and as I turned and stud at the far end of the dark gallery space, there at the far end was the aprox 4 feet by 4feet Transmission hologram. I saw the bearded figure holding a paint brush through a “goldish” picture frame and the quote came flooding back. For some reason I began to walk across and towards the hologram and was amazed to see the figure swish the paint brush through the air leaving a trail of paint which changed colour as I moved.

It is the holograms content and its interacted with me as I moved that still to this day makes it the best hologram I have ever seen.

Andy.

You will find an amimation and more info here.

Dinesh Tue Oct 12, 2004 12:02 pm #5
[quote:0d1ff73ab7]It's description says "Animated, Full-color Transmission". What does that mean? What would be the lighting requirements?[/quote:0d1ff73ab7]

It's probably a stereogram or a multiple channel, depending whether the movement is smooth or jerky. I suppose a stereogram is just a multiple channel with lotrs of channels. The "Full-color" probably means that it's a so-called "pseudo-color" rainbow., where you superimpose slits so that at the right viewpoint you see full color. I was going to talk about this at PCGII if it happens.

Best hologram I ever saw would be the Dizzy Gillespie. I felt I had to actually duck under the extended trumpet, or whatever the musical instrument was.
