Dinesh Sun Aug 28, 2005 12:42 am #3
Well, it says it's a 50mJ laser, but doesn't give the pulse time. What you need to do is to find out the pulse time, divide out the power by the time and work out the energy delivered/pulse. Then you need to figure out what the rate of change of phase for your moving object is (assuming you're trying for a mving object). Basically, the phase must not change by pi/2 over the time of the exposure. The above figures will give you the amount of energy released in the calculated time of exposure. Knowing the sensitivity of the film, you now have the maximum velocity an object can move to be holographically captured by this laser.
