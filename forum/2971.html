Hi,

I am planning to do some experiments regarding exposure time with PFG01. To save developer and make sure I am developing for exactly the same amount of time, I plan to use 1 strip of film and expose different areas for differnet amounts of time. Then develop the whole strip for 30 seconds and bleach for 30 seconds. (in JD-2).

I plan to use the setup below (film between two bits of glass, viewed from above)

![Image](http://www.geocities.com/rja4096/multiexposure.gif)

I will expose the first area, then cover the laser, move the black cover (in front of film) to expose a new area of film, then lift the laser shutter, to expose again. I might actually use 4 different areas of film exposing for 3,6,9 and 12 seconds.

Can anyone see any problems with this? will I get some reflections onto areas of film that are covered up?

How do the exposure times listed above sound for a 3mw laser diode at 18 inches away?

Many thanks, Rick.

The back reflection could be a problem The back of the film won't get a holographic recording (no ref) but it will fog. If you have a small object (roughly equal to the mask size) pianted flat white and kept close to the film, you can minise back scatter. If you choose the object so that it refelcts straight back and not in a spread out manner, it'll be even bettrer. Coins (painted flat white) are better than ping pong balls.

The set up seems fine.
