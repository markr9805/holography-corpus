Jeffrey Weil Thu Jan 31, 2008 3:34 pm #4
Hello Steve_rb,

The rollers on that machine are "hard" Just d5 hardened steel highly polished. The more advanced rollers are "soft" they have a layer of a fancy polymer that allows the rollers to conform to each other and the shim.

This allows you to make "flat" shims. For hard rollers you need to make "crowned" ones. Much harder to do and to tweak out to each machine.

A crowned shim has been made through a series of apertures that leave the shim with a complex cross section that has different areas of the shim being different thicknesses. After its been run a few feet it flattens out to the right shape for a nice emboss across the rollers.

A flat shim has no apertures in front of it during the electroforming process so it just grows in a regular slight parabolic shape. During embossing the rollers conform to the needed shape for a nice emboss across the machines width.
