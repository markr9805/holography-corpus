dave battin Sat Apr 29, 2006 12:20 pm #2
> Thesrot: 'Off axis Fourier transform lens matrix holographic optical element'. Can someone take a shot at explaining to me how these were made? Dave

Off axis Fourier transform-a hologram using a reference beam probably coming from above (and not straight on at 90 degrees to the plate).

lens matrix-the object ,a lens used a number of times,for multi objects ,hence (matrix)

holographic optical element-optical component to bend or control light.

that would be my take on the title and not done using a computer....

anyone else to give it a try ?
