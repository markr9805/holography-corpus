I downloaded MSDS last night on Methylene Blue and found both Methylene and Methyl Blue.

I think it is important to point out that Methyl Blue and Methylene Blue are completely different compounds with different properties. I believe it is Methylene Blue that is used to sensitize into the red. I know I have used the term Methyl Blue in the past and it is incorrect. Just thought I would point it out.

Anyone have first hand experience with either of these compounds that can ellaborate? I know Jeff Blyths paper mentions Methylene Blue not Methyl Blue.

JohnFP Wed Mar 09, 2005 4:02 pm #3
To me it was not obvious not having a strong chemistry background. I thought one was just a short way of sayin the other.

Kinda like, if someone's name is Jolene then they may be refered to as Jo or Jol but they are the same person.

Of course those versed in chemistry and suffixes would automatically know they are different , and to them this would not apply.

I wanted to buy some dye and almost bought Methyl Blue, which was my mistake, so I just wanted to try to help other from making the same mistake. Funny thing is, without having any guidance the MSDS tipped me off the most because one is colored dark green and one brown. Just goes to show, never truncate chemical names, it magically transforms them into another substance.
