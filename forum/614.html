Just got a nice little greenie from Dave. It got very hot very quickly and shut down when we first set it up. However, with a little bit of customer support from Dave and some shopping around, we were able to set it up and mount it without it getting hot. Since I have a few ideas concerning colour holography and need some experimental verifiction for some theoretical ideas, I think I need to hit this colour holography thing on the head. To that end, I have my three colours (633, 532 and 457) and I just bought a box of PFG-03C plates. So, I'm going to be busy in the Batcave for a while and I hope to come out with good, bright colour holograms. Dave's laser is particularly nice because firing up the giant Argon is expensive, consumes 3 gallons of water every minute and it gives off too much power for silver halide. This greenie is compact, powered off a wall powerpoint and has just about the right amount of power for silver.
