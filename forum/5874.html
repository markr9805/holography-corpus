Well, I have been working on a copy from some masters I got from Tony. Bless him, I have had them since the begining of the summer. First my laser died, then I had a few other problems. The masters are split beam transmission H1's, using a (semi-) collimated overhead reference beam. 35mW Hene laser, 4x5" BB-640, JD-4 developer, Fe EDTA bleach.

Thanks for the kind words!
JohnFP Fri Nov 10, 2006 4:00 pm #6
The recoding geometry is pretty much the same as the way I did Michael's elephant. I don't have a decent BS so I don't use one.

5x7 DCG. Plates were 4 1/2 (seahorses) and 5 hours (dragon) old. Emulsion facing object.

Exiting the spatial filter, the blue lines are the reference beam and the red lines are the object beam. The flat and concave mirrors are mounted over one another.

![Image](http://www.holograms3d.com/PhotoGal/MichHolo/IMG_2764.JPG)
JohnFP Fri Nov 10, 2006 4:23 pm #7
As per a conversation with Dave.

Here is the same set up but with the configuration of having the H1 masteing set up built in. This configuration allows an H1 mastering and an H1 to H2 without changing anything, just blockdifferent parts of the beam exiting the spatial filter. For mastering cover the upper left mirror and for H1 to H2 cover the two mirrors on the plateholder and object.
