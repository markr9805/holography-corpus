MichaelH Thu Jan 04, 2007 1:59 pm #5
> Pianomaxx: ...but I read that many holographers had problems in getting bright Denisyuks on PFG-01 in the beginning.

Piano, (may I call you Piano? ) I hadn't realized you were using PFG-01. That medium is a hard one to pull bright single-beam (which is what I presume you mean by Denisyuk) holograms out of. The dye in the emulsion absorbs a lot of light.

You can brighten them up quite a lot by adding mirrors and redirecting the spillover light back to the subject.
