ron olson Thu Nov 01, 2007 2:52 pm #5
As an update on my search:

There is currently (1) company in the U.S. that will quote on producing photo-resist H2's for small-medium embossed runs (non-security). I would suggest to someone with a stable table and a yen to start a small part-time business that this might a nice niche - albeit one requiring an investment in a fairly high-power laser. Prices quoted to me for photo-resist transfers are running in the neighborhood of $3,000 per H2 (the aforementioned domestic source and one European source). At $2,000 per successful H2, I would be willing to commit to 6 next year; at $1,500 I would commit to 12 in 2008. I know I'm not the only guy in the world that believes that $5 embossed cards can sell (assuming the image looks like something other than befits a toothpaste box).
