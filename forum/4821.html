Using fixed commercial SH emulsions for the diffusion method might turn out to be a tricky thing. In my opinion the main issue usually is one does not know its previous pretreatment or certain key specifications like grain size. This may differ from one type of commercial emulsion to another.

Secondly, I have serious doubts that by fixing out the silver halides from a commercial emulsion (and this might particularly apply to larger grains), one will be left with a “virgin” gelatin layer. I don't know what actually happens but it may well be that insoluble by-products remain in the layer. When I tried to reload 8E75 and Ilford SP-673 by means of the diffusion method, I ended up getting very noisy layers (and those grains were in the 30nm range).

Instead of coating the plates/films oneself one might purchase plates/films from places like: [http://www.filmotec.de/English_Site/Pro ... _40_e.html](http://www.filmotec.de/English_Site/Products/GF_40_e/gf_40_e.html)

By the way DuPont used to sell a gelatin coated Cronar polyester film. Unfortunately, we were never able to find any resellers to deal with small orders...
