I know this is off topic, but I am so happy with my visit to the [http://www.holography.ru/maineng.htm](http://www.holography.ru/maineng.htm) laboratory that I wanted to share the experience with you all. I contacted Sergey through this forum and we made an appointment to have two portraits made during my short visit in Moscow. It was a real memorable experience. The lab is at the All-Russian Exhibition Center, close to the VDNKH metro station. Sergey met us there and we (me and my girlfriend) had our portraits made. There is also a shop there where there are the most pretty holograms I have ever seen.

The next day we could collect our H2 reflection copies (30x40cm). Sergey had made a display setup for us in a romantic spot in the laboratory with some great champagne like drink and some small bites to eat and we had some time to talk about holography.

I would like to take the chance to heartily recommend anyone to visit Moscow and have their portraits made. And I would like to thank you Sergey for your great Russian hospitality and making my visit to Moscow so interesting and so much fun.

(Unfortunately it my digital camera broke during our visit because it was -27 Celsius. When I have a new one I will post a picture of the portraits)
