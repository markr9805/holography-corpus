Tom B. Sat Aug 30, 2008 7:49 am #2
Since this is not a high speed digital connection and unlikely to be high current, I think 30 cm should be fine. You might want to confirm (by probing the existing cable with a VOM) that the connections are truly 1 to 1 and that they are not swapping pins inside the connectors. But if you are adding this to the existing cable as an extension rather than replacing it entirely it would not matter.
