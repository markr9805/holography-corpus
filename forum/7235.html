Dear All,

I am trying to catch up with what has changed in the last 20 years. When I was making holograms in 1979 to 1984, I was always frustrated that I could not record live subjects like apples, peoples faces etc. Therefore, I would make life casts of peoples faces which included glass eyes, hair, eyelashes etc, airbrush the plaster casts with grey satin finish paint. This took forever to make the models. Is there now an affordable pulse laser on the market, and by affordable I mean a few thousand dollars unlike the $250,000 that pulse lasers cost in the late seventies/eighties.

Prices for this year (2008):

2 Joules - 67,000 Euro;

5 Joules - 72,000 Euro;

8 Joules - 96,000 Euro.

Prices above are without delivery. Those Joules are in green light pulse,pulse duration ~40 nanoseconds.

Do you make a 3 color pulsed portrait system yet?
Stas Mon Apr 28, 2008 5:33 am #5
> Do you make a 3 color pulsed portrait system yet?

Yes and no. We are manufacturing pulsed colour lasers, but with energy ~2-3milliJoule per pulse. It is not enough to make pulsed portrait directly. So we are incorporating those colour lasers into our digital holographic printers and feeding those printers with digital data obtained from our digital image capture devices - HoloCams. So we can do colour portrait, even with animation, but not directly.
