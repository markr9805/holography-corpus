Hello Everyone;

I just wanted to introduce myself. I'm new to holography and just getting started, and I have a few questions for the experts out there.

I am making holographic emulsions using pinacyanol chloride(also known as 1,1-diethyl-2,2-cyanine chloride) to sensitize to emulsion to red light as described in Saxby's book on page 475. Do you guys think it will work to use a safelight built by putting an incandescent lightbulb in a cardboard box with a window cut in the top, and then putting a dish containing the same dye, pinacyanol chloride, over the window so that all of the light leaving the box is flitered by the dye,which absorbs red light.

Thanks in advance for you expertise.

Mike

Sergio Wed Sep 02, 2009 6:43 pm #3
This dye is green absorbing according to my notes, let me know if I'm wrong, unless this is very cheap I would not recommend it as the dye may fade with time exposed with such light.

A good example is the cryptocyanine cell used in ruby lasers because this dye is a saturable absorber... anyway the dye cell can be well absorption controlled!.
Doescher Wed Sep 02, 2009 7:47 pm #4
Sergio is right, I had a typo in my formula 1,1-diethyl-2,2-cyanine chloride is green sensitive, I should have typed 1,1-diethyl-2,2-carbocyanine chloride which is Pinacyanol chloride. It's also known as Quinaldine blue. The carbocyanine dye has a conjugated carbon chain with two more carbons than the cyanine version. Aldrich lists this dye as having a λmax = 614. It is a little expensive 250 mg for $33.50, or 1 g for $101.50, but I have a bunch of the dye. I do not yet have a safelight.
