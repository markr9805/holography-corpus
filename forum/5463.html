Well, I dare say you're not alone with that impression. I guess SHSG and DCG cover aspects which “are not fully understood yet”. Actually, that's been the exactly same phrase I used to read in the early DCG and SHSG papers and that's what seems to have persisted over the last fourty years almost. Probably, it will remain a mystery for a next life. I guess the main difficulty is observing “in vivo” the micro-/nano mechanism involved during exposure/processing.

In theory SHSG was assumed to mimic DCG. Whereas in DCG the creation of hardened/unhardened areas (or “voids/non-voids) is taking place place upon actinic radiation, the same was supposed to be initiated by “chemical means” - upon carefully selecting and balancing the processing baths subsequent to development. The result of this would be a silver free gelatin layer. The holographic image is modulated by refractive index differences caused by hardness differences in a pure gelatin layer.
