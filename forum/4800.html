I mentioned I used HydroChloric acid to strip, clean and presub my plates. A very weak solution of about 5%. It works great and I have never had emulsion bubble or lift off the glass. It can be bought as Masonry Cleaner under the name muriatic acid. I have been using the same 1/2 gallon of the 5% for over two years now. I usually put the plates in the day before I am coating. Then scrub them with a plastic wool for teflon pans (non abrasive) the day I coat. And rinse thouroughly in a pan with running water. I then rinse under tap water and lean against wall. Before they are dry all the way, I use a paper towel to wipe both sides using the few drops of water still on the plate as a slight dampener for the paper towel.
