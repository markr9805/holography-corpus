BobH Thu Jul 13, 2006 3:44 am #7
There are many coaters for astronomical optics. Overcoating makes the coating strong enough to be cleanable. I'd go for enhanced aluminum, and get maybe 8% more light off the mirror. How much did you pay for 8% of your laser light? The big cost of recoating will probably be shipping and insurance. The extra cost of enhanced aluminum is probably a good deal.

BobH Thu Jul 13, 2006 2:00 pm #9
There will not be a double reflection from a mirror coated with "protected aluminum". Your question about local coaters is probably best asked on forums for amateur astronomy, such as on Astromart and Cloudy Nights. Go to the "ATM" or "amateur telescope makers" section. Also, try searching for Stellafane. It's the biggest ATM conference in the country, probably in your neck of the woods. I forget which club hosts it, but they can certainly advise you best.
