Here's the results of my latest round of holograms. These are all done using the same setup and processing. They are H2 reflection copies of transmission H1 masters. All copies and masters are made on BB-640, processed in JD-4 with no post-treatment. No preswell or presensitization.

11mW Hene, 31 second exposure for H1s, 48 seconds for H2s. All are 2.5 x 2.5 inch size. Back side painted with flat black paint.
