Someone said (in a paper) that dichromate should be dissolved in water before being added to the heated gelatin/water mixture, but without any further explanation. Has anyone heard of this? Perhaps it helps reduce noise by making sure the dichromate is completely dissolved first?
dave battin Mon Aug 20, 2007 8:00 pm #2
hi Joe glad to read you are starting to try some DCG holography. i can only say what has worked for me . i usually microwave some DI h20 to 80C and then throw in the gelatin and keep warmed to 60C on the stirrer for ten min (set at slowest spin) when i see the water gelatin mix is clear i pour my Dichromate(under yellow lights) and spin for only a few min (until crystals are dissolved).

your concentration should be somthing like 100ml h2o,7grams gelatin and 3 grams of Am Dichromate... too much AmDi and your blue light wont pass thru your film (keeping light from reaching object!) .......
