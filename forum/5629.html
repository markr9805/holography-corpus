3-D Holographic Spirographs (SpiroHoloGraphs)

Ok, this may be far out and I would love to try it but I am afraid I don’t have the necessary equipment. I may give the first part a try by hand though.

First take a film plate and lay it on the table with a front surface mirror under it.. Then take a Spirograph outer ring and fasten it to a piece of glass. Mount this just over the plate and mirror. Use a piece of opaque material to block all but inside the ring. Also blacken the ring itself so that it is also opaque. Now, take the inner disk and cover it with a large piece of rigid black material such that no matter what position it is in within the outer ring all of the rest of the inner hole of the outer ring is covered. Now make a hole in the opaque material where you want to insert the laser “pen”. The disk’s original hole may need to be enlarged. Glue a small bearing so that is it centered over the hole. Now attach a handle to the outside of the race bearing. This allows you to move the inner disk around the Spirograph as normal. Enlarge a beam to cover then entire area of the inside of the inner ring. As the beam is stable and remains in position and only the hole is being moved the system should produce interference patterns where the hole is. Speed would have to be controlled.

Now for truly 3D, mount the film plate about 3 inches above the table. Take the front surface mirror and replace it with a white card. Have this white card on a translation table that moves in the Y axis (up and down) direction. While the wheel is being turned the Y axis can be moved either up or down to produce truly 3D effects. If it is possible to automate the wheel movement and the up down movement, a very volumized Spirographic sphere could be made.
