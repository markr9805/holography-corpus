I use a Buchner funnel and filtration paper or coffee filter. I believe a funnel is the best way to go and this product will not work and here is why.

This system will indeed filter out gel globs, dirt etc... But one of the functions of filtering is also to remove any foam or bubbles. This system will surely introduce bubbles as the emulsion drips flatly across the area. The funnel, captures the emulsion and allows it to gently slide down the funnel taper, down the shank and drip gently into the beaker. I use a beaker in which my funnel end is very nearly touching the bottom. I then tilt my funnel, which has a taper to the exit shank, so that the first ml of emulsion makes a puddle at the bottom of the beaker and the rest of the emulsion does not actually drip, but slided into the emulsion in the beaker. I rarely have to worry about any bubbles or anything with this method and the emulsion is ready to coat as soon as it is done.

I also tried a vacuum flask with the funnel and that does not work as it created bubbles also.

So if anyone tried this, let us know. May I suggest trying it with plain gelatin and water first, so if it does not work you can always use it for that single cup of Java you want at midnight while in the lab, without having to make a whole pot.....

EDIT: I forgot. The funnel I have is ceramic and it works real well in holding heat. I heat it up to just warm with a blow dryer just before I use it to filter the gelatin. This way the gelatin does not cool and gel in the 5 minutes it takes for it to filter.
