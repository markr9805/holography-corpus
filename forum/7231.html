About a year ago, I got a Quantel pulsed frequency doubled and tripled YAG laser off Ebay. I'm not exactly sure what its model number is, it just says Quantel, though the various things in the power supply are labeled. This weekend, after many adventures, I finaly got fringes out of it. No holograms yet, it was an interferometer, but I finaly have real evidence that it might actualy work.

So I've been writing up my notes on this specific laser, they can be found at [http://www.bobdbob.com/~tjohnson/quantel/](http://www.bobdbob.com/~tjohnson/quantel/), in the hopes that they would be useful to someone.

How calibrated is zapit paper? There is the black layer, then they gray layer, then the white base. Does removing the black layer but leaving the grey layer take a consistant number of joules per unit area? And if so, does anyone know the number?
Ron Michael Sat Mar 22, 2008 8:44 pm #2
If my memory serves me right with regard to tem00 mode on zapit paper and gray only marks for q switch pulses. You are looking at 3-5 milli-joules over a 1mm square area with the value closer to 3mj. It's not an accurate way and only an energy meter will give you the correct energy level. However, I seen quite of few of these pulses and when you get to 8-12mj/mm you will have white also in the center.
