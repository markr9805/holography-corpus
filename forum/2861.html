eric343 Tue Nov 02, 2004 3:41 am #2
It has a coherence length of ~100 meters thanks to a ring-cavity design. No kidding... it's one of the diode lasers everyone recommends for holography.
Colin Kaminski Tue Nov 02, 2004 4:04 am #3
They work well for holography. The coherence length is closer to 50M and actually it is not a ring cavity even though that was what was assumed for a long time.

Yeah, It has a tunning program that makes it flicker and change brightness until both LEDs on the test connector are lit. After running mine overnight the laser heatsink is quite cool, at least with a 70F ambient. Coherent recommends 150 watts and Laser Sam uses a 80 watt supply.
