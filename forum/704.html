Johnfp Fri Apr 19, 2013 7:37 am #4
In my opinion, with the wiki and all the information that has been accumulated on this forum, there really isn't anything new to really talk about unless you get into the playful and mind stimulating hypotheticals. And of course, the new entry to the world of holography that would like some guidance.

I also found, that not many holographers out there did holography like I did. Everyone seem to have an end goal, a final hologram. Well, with that, there is not much to discuss and you alreay have the knowledge and are just tweeking the process. No reall stumbling blocks that warrents a post on the forum. Now me, when I was doing holography, especially DCG, it was solely for experimentation. I never really tried to make a final hologram that I would give away or keep and hang on the wall, although some came out quite well and I did just that. For me, holography was learning inside and out the relationship between the light, the medium and the processing. I streatched every parameter to it's limit just to see what would happen. I threw a lot of ideas on the forum to see if there were any suggestions on how I should proceed. Then I tried to do it whether it seemed possible or not. Just look at my illustrations page and my Testing and Research page. I always had something to talk about thinking outside the box and stretching the limits.

Anyway, just my humble opinion.

When a new breakthrough happens with holography, then you will see the chatter increase again.

Peace all

John
