Jeffrey Weil Sat Jun 30, 2012 8:36 am #1
Wow.

This was a great symposium. I'm sure all the people that were there will fill in lots of details. I just wanted to give my perspective on the event.

I'm still here, just getting my stuff ready to check out.

I was pretty surprised how great this one was. For those that came to their first ISDH I can tell you from experience this was very close to the feeling of the original ones held at Lake Forest back in the day.

Being in a big place like Cambridge added it's own flavor to the event but for the most part this was just like Lake Forest.

Finally meeting up with the forum members was also a great thing. And now, just like back then, the people from the forum that went have a bond that didn't exist before. Friends now, not just "forum" friends.

One word of warning for those that this was their first symposium. Don't expect any other event to be like this one. Photonics West, Holopack, etc....those are "normal" symposiums. Don't expect the same feeling of community. That only happens here.

See you all in Greece in 3 years.

Jeff W

Jeffrey Weil Sat Jun 30, 2012 1:45 pm #6
No Danny, I've been to many other symposiums for over 25 years. They WILL NEVER be like Lake Forest.

The other symposiums are too large, all about biz, etc...Trust me, if you come to the others expecting or trying to make it like ISDH you will be looked upon as a kook.

Holopack is about business, if you have nothing to add to their company or are not already a friend you will be excluded from all the fun. No one is going to take someone they don't know out for a $1000 group dinner where they pick up the bill. Nor will they go out partying with someone they don't know and risk their reputation. Clients and competitors are also there.

The papers are also different, no art, mostly business. Even the tech papers are very different than what you'll find at Lake Forest. The people at other symposiums have been brought together in a very different way and for very different reasons than ISDH. The other meetings are great, but totally not the same thing.

You don't even dress the same at any other meeting. I love the others, especially Holopack. Ian has done an amazing job of putting that together, but it is NOT the same. Very few of the attendees are even holographers. They are security people, government officials and owners of large holography companies.

You'll soon hear about the dorm party. That's a good example of what I'm talking about. There is never a time like that at other symposiums where everyone attending is together and getting loose. NEVER, in other symposiums people break up into different groups to have fun.

The history between the people at all the other symposiums are also very different from ISDH.

“Standard” symposiums like Photonics West are very large, ISDH is small. Most people at larger conferences don't know each other, or have a common friend to introduce them in that style. The ONLY reason ISDH is a community is because it has always been that way. It was designed that way from the start and had it's beginning when things were very different in holography. ISDH is from a different world and time. TJ created something that cannot be replicated. Don't minimize his achievements.

Lake Forest was born out of the counter cultural ideas from the 70's. In many ways, it's the "hippy" conference. The venue also radically changes the flavor of the event. At ISDH you're usually somewhat isolated from the rest of the world. That is not the case with other conferences. The whole flavor of the experience feels very different. Every other symposium is about either business or technology, ISDH is about passion.

You know I usually don't respond to your posts Danny but I don't want you giving people the wrong idea, based on nothing but your inexperience with symposiums, and sending them off with a mistaken idea of what they are going to find. It costs a lot of cash to go to these things. Naive statements like yours are going to waste people's money.

Ask anyone that's been to ISDH and any other symposium if they think the two could be the same....they will set you straight.

One last thing before I go back to not responding to your comments....please stop quoting peoples entire post for just one sentence or paragraph. It wastes bandwidth and makes threads harder to read for everyone.

Jeff W

ISDH2012 was a wonderful experience! It was my first holography-focused (no pun intended) meeting and my hopes were high going in. Reality was much better than my fondest hopes! I have been to many other scientific conferences (though no others devoted to holography) and I can echo Jeffrey's comments on the unusually warm and welcoming atmosphere. And, btw, the continuous running of photos from previous ISDH meetings as well as photos of early display holography pioneers from the 1970's added a lot to that sense of community. It was great fun seeing what some of the people sitting next to me looked like 30, or more, years ago!! So, thanks for setting that up Ed Wesley!

I came away energized and ready keep moving forward in holography.

Ron
