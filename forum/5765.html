Colourholographic Ltd. is happy to announce that we have negotiated a new agreement with VinTeq Ltd as our Official Distributor for the BB range of holographic plates in the US.

The result is a substantial price cut in the US for BB640 plates of the sizes 2.5"x2.5" and 4"x5". Prices are down to $ 76.00 from $ 95.00 for 2.5"x2.5" and down to $ 122.00 from $ 160.00 for 4"x5".

That will put prices of BB640 plates in the US at the same level as if ordering directly from Colourholographic, but without the expensive international shipping costs and customs.

This agreement will extend to other sizes and to BB520 in the future.

Visit Vinteq webpage for more information:
