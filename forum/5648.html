I and the couple other people that I've been working with on true color holography this summer thave been looking into what would be a suitable blue laser. We first experimented with a 405 nm Crystalaser diode laser that was recently purhased by a physics department faculty member for an entangled photon experiment. We never really intended on using it because it was too violet and the beam looked horrible (kind of like an oval with a slit in it, I'm guessing it was TEM 01 mode), but that was sent back and we are looking into purchasing a new blue laser for holography purposes.

We were originally looking at this one

[http://shop.mellesgriot.com/products/la ... 4&mscssid=](http://shop.mellesgriot.com/products/lasers/detail.asp?pf_id=35%20LAP%20321-120&plga=063504&mscssid=)

but the overall consensus on this forum seems to be that the air cooled argon variety are not well suited for holography. The company told us that the coherence length for that model was around 10 cm, which doesn't seems to be that much.

I'm a little weary of diode lasers, I think because of my bad experience with the 405 nm previously mentioned laser, but I think I may be giving all diode lasers a bad rap. So does anyone have experience with blue diode lasers and how well they work for holography, coherence length, how circular the beams are, etc. Or do you guys think that a gas type is still the better option.
