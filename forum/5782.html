I made a good coating rod for less than 5 dollar. I used a metal tube with a diameter of 1 cm. At both ends I drilled a small hole. For winding the rod I used nylon wire. Nylon is much easier to wind on a rod because it is much more flexible than metal. You can get a smooth and regular rod each time. It can be bought in all sorts of diameters. Just sick the wire through the first hole in the rod, tie a knot. Then wind it tightly around the rod until the other hole is reached and do the same.

For my first rod I used 0.5mm diameter nylon wire. That should be the theoretically ideal thickness. I found that makes my coatings very smooth and regular, but a little too thick. Next time I will try one with 0.25 diameter wire.

Hans Wed Sep 13, 2006 5:20 am #4
Yes, I am sure that fishing line works also. My wire looks similar. I bought it in one of those stores where they sell stuff for home hobbies, such as water paint, clay, fabrics, etc...

(I first tried to make one with 0.5mm metal wire. But the problem there was that even the smallest bend in the wire shows up on the coating rod, making the edge of the rod not straight. The nylon is so flexible that it does not have this problem. Just wind it as tightly as you can.)
