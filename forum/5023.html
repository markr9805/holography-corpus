MichaelH Mon Sep 05, 2005 1:54 pm #5
This does raise some good questions...

If the volume is low, is it better to ask for forgiveness rather than risk being told "no" when asking for permission? The word "better" is certainly open to interpretation and I welcome anyones constructive input. There are certainly moral implications.

Also, how low is "low"? Using me as an example I've made several master holograms using licensable imagery and can make copies for sale or gifts without much trouble. I've given away some to friends and family and have sold a total of three of them. It can be argued that three sales haven't hurt the license holders one bit and I can (I think rightfully) argue that I'm not likely to do any volume that would ever show up on their balance sheets either as a negative (non-licensed) or as a positive (licensed).

On the other hand, I also haven't invited them to participate in the potential profit-making by seeking out a license. I certainly can't argue that's the "right" way to go about it.
