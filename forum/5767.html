ron olson Fri Aug 25, 2006 6:56 pm #4
I think your best bet is to buy a junked laser power supply. [http://www.laserresale](http://www.laserresale) currently has a Korad ruby power supply (ID#1691) for $1,000 that probably contains what you need (most ruby and YLF systems have pretty similar pumping schemes).

You need to ascertain the characteristics of your flashlamps before you build your power supply - they need to be a matched set to avoid problems like high-voltage ringing.

I think you've set an unrealistically low budget for the output you're targeting. If you were to budget around $15k you could have a good chance for your 1J of green light.

Ron Michael Fri Sep 01, 2006 1:30 am #7
You requested new capacitors.

But used non-pcb oil caps rated for pulse energy storage use and if within 5% of stamped value can be a good buy. For example the cap may be designed as 185ufd but is stamped at 177ufd. If the measured value is 168ufd then this would be within 5 percent. Generally stay away from those that are greater than 10 percent out of spec. My personal preference is Maxwell. But NWL and Aerovox are good caps also.

Here is an example of a guy who has plenty of these caps:

[http://cgi.ebay.com/ws/eBayISAPI.dll?Vi ... mot_widget](http://cgi.ebay.com/ws/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&item=7630022801&ssPageName=MERC_VI_RSCC_Pr4_PcY_BIN_Stores_IT&refitem=3846894581&itemcount=4&refwidgetloc=active_view_item&usedrule1=StoreCatToStoreCat&usedrule2=CategoryProximity&refwidgettype=cross_promot_widget)

These caps are heavy and best used with long flashlamps like helicals or several linears in series.

As of last resort you might try:

[http://www.surplussales.com/Capacitors/MaxwellList.html](http://www.surplussales.com/Capacitors/MaxwellList.html)

He is too pricey but I've bought a few when I really needed a specific size.

I've done better looking for the right caps on ebay. I updated the links page in this section earlier regarding new maxwell caps from ATR Electronics in Israel. These 185ufd 3kv caps were going for 50 to 75 each and were an excellent buy.

FYI, Korad lasers generally are helical type with similar specs and weight as cap in the ebay listing.
