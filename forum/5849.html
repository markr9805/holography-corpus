I have mixed some glass glue (the type that needs UV to cure) with MB and TEA. Sandwiched it between two glass plates and exposed an image on it. As expected the MB bleached in the exposed area's. Afterwards I exposed the whole plate under a light bulb. The rest of the plate bleached, but in a slightly different colour than the area's that had the initial exposure. I wonder if this can be made to use for holography.

Tom B. Wed Oct 25, 2006 3:16 am #3
You were exposing with a red laser I presume? Why not try putting the sandwich up against a mirror and making a tiny diffraction grating? With a beam size of say 1 cm diameter it would only take a few minutes to get exposure energies > 1 Joule/cm^2 even with a small laser. If you are getting any refractive index modulation at all you should see at least some hint of a grating.
