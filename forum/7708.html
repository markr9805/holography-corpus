I am using an ascorbic acid/sodium hydroxide/metol developer and my bleaching recipe is EDTA, furric sulphate, potassium bromide, and sodium bisulphate. There is an odor of sulphur when the plate is placed in the bleach. Is this normal, or could be it be due to an error in my prepartion of the bleach, or some other problem?
Tom B. Sun Feb 01, 2009 8:35 am #2
This is normal. The sulphur smell gets stronger as the bleach gets older but the results are still just as good. I usually just throw it out

when it gets really stinky but I have no good reason for this.
WorryFree Sun Feb 01, 2009 3:48 pm #3
> Tom B.: ...I usually just throw it outwhen it gets really stinky...

How much time does it usually take to get to that point? And has it caused a noticeable permanent odor in your developement area? (I live in a rather small space, and I have limited experience with chemistry.)
Tom B. Mon Feb 02, 2009 6:29 am #4
Maybe a few months of usage? I keep most of the mixed bleach in a glass bottle, pouring out enough for plate development into one of those plastic rectangular storage containers with locking gasketed lid (Lock&Lock brand). The smell is only apparent when opening the container to drop in or remove a plate, and is never really strong enough to be a problem. It should not cause a permanent odor in the work area if there is any ventilation at all.
