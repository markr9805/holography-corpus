Colin Kaminski Fri May 23, 2008 2:19 am #7
I have a cleaving method from "Procedures in Experimental Physics" as well as some more recent revisions. I have a copy of the patent of a mica holder that could be improvised. I have also worked out an experimental method to see how close you are to a true waveplate. However getting the waveplate the right thickness is done with trial and error, you cleave a plate and measure it and try again.

It turns out that scanning electron microsopy uses V-1 grade mica to make slides. I found them for a few dollars each and they are good enough for object beam rotation. I have not made one I can use for a beamsplitter aplication yet. But this is something that practice is everything. I'll write up everything when I am better at it but that is the outline.
