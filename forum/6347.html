Hi everyone

I have finally shot some good holograms

![Image](http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1163/847290335_ba7a338f10_m.jpg)

this is a home made silver halide plate

20 sec exposure

6 min development with gp-2 diluted 15ml to 200ml,plus 10 drops 6% ammonium thiocyanate

Then 3min each 70 and 99% IPA(it dosen't photo too well)

![Image](http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1174/847290287_272b5d9cdf_m.jpg)

This is a MBDCG plate,made to the HoloWiki article formula

10 min exposure

soak water @ 26deg cent

3min each in 70 and 99% IPA

Both were shot with a polarized 20mw hene

I also used windshield repair acrylic to seal both holos--works really well

rob
