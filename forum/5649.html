Hans Wed Jun 28, 2006 1:03 am #9
Actually, the C315 is not a ring laser. The way it gets it's great coherence length is through strict control of the temperature of the KTP crystal. In the C315, the KTP has a dual function. It acts as a frequency doubler, and also as a etalon. Because the temperature range at which the KTP doubles the frequency efficiently is rather broad, that left the engineers room to use the same crystal as an etalon by fine tuning it's temperature (and thus it's thickness) to ensure single line operation. The following link describes this: [http://members.misty.com/don/laserssl.htm#sslc315f](http://members.misty.com/don/laserssl.htm#sslc315f).

Coherent does sell a ring YAG laser. It is called the Coherent Compass 532 laser.
