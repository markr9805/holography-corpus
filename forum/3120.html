I wanted to finally get organized with my lab notes. I have been jotting down the individual variables but still did not feel organized and sometimes I felt I may have missed something. So I made a template that I will be using. Click on the hyperlink "Lab Notes Template" at the addres below. You can right click and click save as if you want to use it.

[http://www.sketchys.com/Holograms/myholograms.htm](http://www.sketchys.com/Holograms/myholograms.htm)

It is designed for DCG.

The "DateTime#" I use is F or H (for film or Hologram), date then time

Example for film I made today F0211051300 For Film made 02/11/05 at 1:00 pm. I use the same scheme with an H for hologram in the begining.

I also cross reference the Film DateTime number for each hologram, thus knowing how old the film is and how it was made.

The "Energy at plate" is only for the reference beam.

The rest of the template is pretty self explainatory. Feel free to use or ask any questions.
