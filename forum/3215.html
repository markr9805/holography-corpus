Lock&Lock is a brand name for a gasketed snap-lock polypropylene food storage container that I find VERY useful for holographic processing.

The size I use for 4x5 work is here:

[http://us.locknlock.com/productDetail.a ... tNumber=62](http://us.locknlock.com/productDetail.asp?productNumber=62)

I store my working bleach mix and various TEA baths in these and also have reserved a couple for developing. One small modification - there is a small molding bump in the inside center bottom which I carefully shave off with a single-edge razor blade to make the bottom absolutely flat. They're not cheap, but they are well made and to me easily worth the price.

These are also good for storing film in the refrigerator (or even out). Wrap your film in lightproof acid free paper and put some dessicant into the containter and you've got an air-tight way to keep your film and plates fresh and dry.
