I had a dream last night. Here it goes...

We have the biggest collection of communicating holographers the world has ever known so far, thanks to this forum. So why not organize a group hologram that is made in pieces, one piece by each holographer from all over the world, then assembled into a single piece of art to be given, or consigned to an art museum.

A small blurb with or below or on the hologram could read something like this.

"This hologram is the joint effort of holographers from Germany, United States, England, Netherlands, Russia, Cuba, Canada".

We could even make the theme a statement that promotes world countries from around the world working toward a single goal.

What do you all think? Maybe we can supliment this with the next contest. In other words have the contest, but the theme of the contest will allow us to connect all the holograms together to make a single large hologram. The differnt mediums and techniqes by everyone would make the hologram truely an original and one of a kind. We could then donate (or sell) it to some gallery.

Some possible ideas for a group hologram could be:

A mobile (everyone do a planet).

A window pane (each hologram can be framed by the molding and need to relate to one another).

A cube (or two or three) each of which has 6 holograms and is hung by one corner on a string thus allowing muliple images occupy the same space within the cube when is moves in the air (images need to realate on one another only slightly).

ErichRose Wed Feb 20, 2008 2:57 pm #19
No John I think it is an excellent idea! The design process has a way of balloning out sometimes and that is usually really good if a bit daunting. Opening these things up to a bit of blue sky thinking is fun and can lead to great ideas. But the next step is to take all those ideas and compare them to the goals or objectives. The basic goal being to create one large collage of holograms that can be assembled and viewed as one piece made up of individual holograms submitted by members. Then it is possible to actually sort the various ideas out. I also think that by keeping the first one (should this become an annnual event) simple would be very important.

So here is my suggestion:

All images are recorded at a set size. The group could chime in on the prefered size, but make it a nice horizontal rectangle with proportions that maybe fall in the golden mean (1:1.6?) so that when all are assembled it will look good. Or it can be made from squares that equal the golden mean when assembled such as 5x8 = 40 plates. Cheap trick, but works.

Then make them all reflection holograms that reconstruct with a given reconstruction angle and distance. They could be single beams or H1-H2's. but all are front lit as a specific angle. If this gets large then maybe someone maps out the individual units and calculates the angles and we assign the locations. Example being that the top left corner is a bit different than the lower right. We are all smart enough to figure that one out I hope.

Images are all mounted on a similar substrate to simplify assembly. Film is mounted to glass or plastic to equal thickness of typical glass plates. They are all to be glued in place.

All images are simply mounted to a large sheet of material such as 1/4" thick polycarbonate or such. No fancy windows or frames. And the holograms are just butted right up next to each other so that they just flow, snap, crackle all together as one.

The subject is more general just as the earlier contest has been. Love, peace, growth, home, etc.

And here is the hard part. We all make enough copies so that everyone that participates can build and display one where they live. Obviously this would be optional and someone would have to be the designated "Home" for the original. And for that I suggest someone who might be close to a museum, gallery or university that would be willing to display the final piece.

If we set the due date for later this year I will make a major effort to contribute. I think it was an excellent idea and maybe the next year we try the 360 multiplexed idea or the window panes.
