Hi,

Since the TEA hasn't arrived yet, I thought I'd try a shot on the BB-640 anyway. I gave it a good shot (1.5mj cm-2) and got a reasonable reflection hologram out. When I developed it, it wasn't very dense, but still the results were OK, but not great. Some things I noticed were that the emulsion seems harder than PFG-03M, and it seems like the coating job on the plate was much better. I had noticed that some of the PFG-03M plates that I have didn't seem to have very even coating - you could see this even when they were wet. Has any one else seen this in their batches? Also, the emulsion seemed to be much harder than that on the Slavich plates. I may try making another hologram tonight, but with a bit more oomph from the laser.

I suspect DCG could produce better results than the silver emulsions, but I don't have deep enough pockets to purchase the laser required for such an effort.

Hi,

I just want to add a helpful note that you can go out to a drug store and get vitamin C powder and glycerol and if you have something a bit alkaline like sodium carbonate you can replace TEA. A rinse of a BB 640 plate in say 1% vitamin C at around pH 5-6 (on its own Vit C is around pH3-4 ) . This will activate the plate even if you rinse the stuff out before exposing which you should do with photoflow etc to keep it smear free. (You can even activate plate in diluted developer but I dont want to recommend it.)

This will however of course leave the plate unswollen once it is dry.

This is where the glycerol rinse comes in. You can treat it like TEA although it is a bit more viscous. I often use ethylene glycol too but have not actually tried using a can of antifreeze. My guess is that antifreeze would work but please don't bill me for the plate if it does'nt.

Hi,

the most comprehensive article for PFG-03M and BB-640 can be found at:

[http://www.ph.utexas.edu/~jfelkl/Holography.pdf](http://www.ph.utexas.edu/~jfelkl/Holography.pdf)

One part of the conclusion from the article is:

"We demonstrated several easily reproducible methods to make nice reflection holograms on two media PFG-03M and BB-640. We can indeed view these holograms in white light, yet they show greater detail and depth if looked at in laser light."

For processing the PFG-03M plates they used JD-4 developing process
