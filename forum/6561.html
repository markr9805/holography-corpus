I recently took possession of a new product from StockerYale: what they are calling the “high power LED spot projector”. The one I tested is amber in color (590nm) but the units are available in blue, green, red and white as well. First the bad news: they cost $510 with power supply. Now the good news: they are easily the best (non-laser) illumination source I have ever seen for display of large format reflection holography. They consist of an array of (3) LED's with lensing to deliver a 12 degree spot light of very uniform - very intense light. At a distance of 1 meter I can illuminate a 60cm x 35cm image, exhbiting almost 30cm of scene depth with minimal distortion.

We will be using them in our high-end window displays and even if their expected lifetime of 100,000 hours is optimistic by a factor two – they will be well worth the investment. They are so new that they don’t appear on the company web site but they are available by contacting Brian Merz at StockerYale 800-843-8011.
