Anyway, assuming I am allowed to answer a specific mathematical query without being penalised for using mathematiucs, and assuming I can answer here where the question was posed.

The reason for the "2" "not" being used is that due to the vector nature of electronmagnetic radiation, the expansion is done in complex numbers. The Intensity profile of a beam is given as the cmplex square of the complex amplitude modulation. Thus, if the two plane beams are given as:

A_1 = A_01exp(i((k1_xsin(theta) + k1_ycos(theta)) - wt))

and

A_2 = A_02exp(i((k2_xsin(theta) + k2_y*cos(theta)) - wt))

Then, clearly from De Moivres Theorem, this forms the solution set of a second order partial differential equation. Without too much detail (which can be provided if more detail is required or further questions are posed), the full set of the four partial differential equations from Faraday's experimental observatiuons can be reduced to a second order partial differential equation in two variables. However, if we now add in the Lorentz foce equation and Poynting's Theorem, the power is seen as the amplitude multiplied by it's own complex conjugate. The complex conjugate brings in the phase relationships that are coded into the complex amplitude that make interference possible. The same for the triple binomial expansion.
