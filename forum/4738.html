Here two holograms I was doing the other day, viewed in plain sun light Both are H2 copies

on Ultimate15 5x8 plates, made from Ultimate08 masters of same size. Recorded

at 514nm (ca 100mW 8 sec) and post-treated with KJ to shift the

color to ca 530nm. They are very bright and noise-free.

![Image](http://perso.wanadoo.fr/redlum.xohp/holography/pics/146-4611_IMG.jpg)

The following one was a study concerning image depth. The chandelier

sticks out more than 4'' from the plate, and its size is like 18''.

It is reasonably sharp even in sunlight. There is also a brief [movie](http://argonholo.webhop.net/holography/video/146-4605_MVI.AVI) (3.7MB) which better shows the depth.

![Image](http://perso.wanadoo.fr/redlum.xohp/holography/pics/146-4608_IMG.jpg)
