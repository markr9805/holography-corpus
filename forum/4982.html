JohnFP Wed Aug 17, 2005 2:30 pm #5
For the part of the object you would like the final color to be;

Green, paint that part of the object with Windsor Newton Galeria – Flow Formula Acrylic – Cobalt Blue Hue

Yellow, pain that part of the object with Windsor Newton Galeria – Flow Formula Acrylic – Titanium White

Orange, paint that part of the object with Windsor Newton Galeria – Flow Formula Acrylic – Cadmium Yellow (although my test were for 514, this was the best paint that reflected 514 but absorbed 457 and should be good for 532).

You should be able to see the Cobalt Blue Hue look black with the Green laser and the Cadmium Yellow look black with the blue laser and the Titanium White to look the color of each laser when illuminated.

After the object is painted expose with 532 and then 457 individually and independently. I found that the total energy needed to the plate was about 1/2 for 457 then my 514 and overall exposure energy to the plate (both added) was about 25% more, yes more then a single line alone. This may be a good starting point. So 100mw at 532 for 2 minutes then 200mw at 457 for 30 seconds (100mw at 457 for 1 minute).

I also found, but only performed limited testing, that it did not make a difference which line I exposed with first.

You may even be able to expose with both lines at the same time since you are using two lasers. I was using only one laser and had to close the shutter and tune in the second line, then expose a second time with the second line.

Then process to get a replay shift toward the red (about 80nm). Your "orange" may even be a little redder then mine were as you are using 532 and I was using 514. (You may want to consider a red shift when preparing your emulsion and coating).

I have not tried mixing the Cobalt Blue and Cadmium Yellow yet. I presume a 80% Cobalt Blue and a 20% Cadmium Yellow may yield a final replay of greenish yellow. Likewise, a 80% Cadmium Yellow and 20% Cobalt Blue may yield a final replay of light orange. And further ratios should give the full spectrum between light-green and red-orange.

Gosh, I really miss dabling with this DCG color stuff.

Good luck and hope some of this may help.
