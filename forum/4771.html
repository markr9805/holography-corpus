This hologram is a 5x7 Color DCG using 514 and 457. The hair is yellow, the dress orange and the shoulder piece green. The skin is very skin tone and the top of the garter black. I am disapoined I took this movie clip with the camera on its side and have not been able to rotate the movie 90 degrees with software. Also, the movie does not do it justice. It is very bright and clear in real life. Yes, she is fully clothed. Lighting is Quartz Halogen about 4 feet away.

[http://www.sketchys.com/HoloPage/HoloFi ... kModel.avi](http://www.sketchys.com/HoloPage/HoloFiles/ColorDCG/Model/BlackBackModel.avi)

This is the DCG hologram done with on-axis refereence beam. It is about 3x3 inches square. As you can see the image is very clear from all angles. Lighting is Quartz Halogen about 10 feet away. This hologram is even nicely visible with a regular frosted light bulb on the kitchen table at all angles.

[http://www.holograms3d.com/PhotoGal/OnAxis/OnAxis2.wmv](http://www.holograms3d.com/PhotoGal/OnAxis/OnAxis2.wmv)
