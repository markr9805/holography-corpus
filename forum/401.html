Jeffrey Weil Sat Nov 19, 2011 1:21 pm #9
Hello John,

A "solid" steel door is not really solid. A true solid steel door would weight hundreds of pounds and would cost way too much. Those are probably filled with some fire resistant stuff, not intended or designed for what we do. Making something fire resistant has nothing to do with making it stiff.

They are also not a good aspect ratio unless you use 2 side by side, then you have motion at the joint.

Most of the hallow doors that have corrigation inside to keep it stiff are actually cardboard corrigation. Not stiff.

BobH is correct in everything he says above.

I've used doors in the past, they did not perform very well once the exposures got long.

Jeff W
