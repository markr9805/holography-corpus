From reading Colin's posts, I'd like to build a constant current driver for maximum stability of my diode. Obviously I'm trying to minimize mode hopping. I've been through Sam's laser FAQ and have seen the LM317 solutions. Any other suggestions for clean, stable constant current drivers for 50-150ma diodes?

Colin Kaminski Wed Feb 09, 2005 3:00 am #2
The easiest, but not the best, is simply a battery and a resistor! It is constant current as long as the battery holds out. A well regulated lab power supply works well also. My library is still in boxes but there is a section in "The Art of Electronics" about constant current drivers I found quite helpful.
