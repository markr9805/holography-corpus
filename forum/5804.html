Laminating PFG-01 to glass. Super SUPER simply. :D

Hans Wed Sep 27, 2006 11:20 am #1
I took a cheap 20 Euro laminator and disconnected the heating element. Then I took a thin glass plate (10x15cm) and taped an almost equal size PFG-01 sheet to the glass on only one side, so that the film could hinge on it.

Then I placed a stripe of children suitable glue in the hinge point and gently tilted the film back onto the glass plate again. I put the plate+film between two sheets of paper and ran it through the laminator. The film stuck perfectly to the plate. No streaks, no micro-bubbles.

I waited 20 minutes for the glue to harden.

But the really nice and big surprise came after processing the film.

When I dried the developed film with a warm hair drier, the film came loose from the glass again and the remainder of the glue on the back side was easily washed away with warm water. This is perfect for me because I like to catalog my PFG test shots in a small booklet. I tried to laminate 4 more sheets to make sure it really was that easy. All came out perfect. YAHOO!!!

I used a "Pixmaster A4 Laminator"made by "Pavo" company. It can laminate 250um thick. I took the laminator apart and cut the white heating wire that runs along the top and bottom of the aluminum block that contains the rollers.
