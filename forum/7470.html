My idea here it to provide a kit that anyone beginning in DCG can take and come up with fairly good result in the first try, and to use this kit for testing as they get a good feel of dcg.

When I was first starting to understand DCG I found a few things that helped me in developing my processing and exposer skills.

One was using a very bright DCG as a master in a copying format to test my coating processing and exposers.

Another great leap for me in my testing was to put a black mask in one of the corners it allowed me to test the time in the fixer or a just my exposer.

With this method of testing I found I only had 2 variables to narrow down between different batches of formulas, overall hardness (fixing times) and exposer, as long as my process remained the same.

So my idea for a "hero kit”... I forgot who on this form gave it that title.

Is

1: 3 working formulas and process

2: 1 roll of scotch tape

3: 1 6x5" double thick glass (the cast plate)

4: 1 4x5" single thick glass

5: 1 8oz rain-X

6: 4 packages of knox

7: 1 digital thermometer

8: 2 coffee filters

9: 1 polymer hologram (Green)

10: 4 paper clips

11: 1 black mask for corner

And one candy bar and a sticker that says “Well Done” or something like that

This is the start of the idea the called the “hero Kit”
