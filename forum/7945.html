Good Day all,

When I mix gelatin, I try very hard to mix only what I need to coat the plates that I have. I then pour the excess into a jug that will one day be safely disposed of. In the pass I tryed storing the excess in the frig then reheated it. The time it took to warm up was like an hour or more and some times would get goopy gel. Any of you DCGers out there make large batches of gels and store them?

Enjoy your Sunday,

Tony
Joe Farina Sun Aug 23, 2009 4:43 pm #2
Checking the notes from an old Lake Forest tutorial by Richard Rallison, he mentioned:

"Film mixtures may be stored in a refrigerator for a week or two and reheated in water or a microwave oven as needed. When stored longer they become less and less likely to flow when warmed."

That sounds about right. I remember I stored a mixture in the fridge for a while (I forget exactly how long, but not very long), and popped the gelatin out from the bottom of the Ball jar, and it was more like a hockey puck than a gel (it was Knox gelatin/ammonium dichromate).
dave battin Sun Aug 23, 2009 5:00 pm #3
The AMDI/gelatin mix will last two weeks or so ,and then begins to solidify, almost as if it starts to crosslink and become one blob.......

The mixture when new acts somewhat unpredictable, as it ages a few days seems to act more uniform.
