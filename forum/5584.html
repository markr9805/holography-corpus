More blue lasers have entered the market. The newest is a 460nm Coherent saphire laser.

The new semiconductor lasers from Coherent are single mode and TEM00 making them ideal for holography. The most attractive laser for holography so far is the Saphire 480nm at 50mw.

Tom B. Sat Jun 03, 2006 2:58 am #12
I think it likely that unless stated otherwise, the too-good-to-be-true cheap blue DPSS lasers are probably not single longitudinal mode (SLM). Multiple modes would cause the notorious contouring (sliced bread) effect in holograms.

Another blue laser purveyor::

[http://www.crystalaser.com/new/blue.html](http://www.crystalaser.com/new/blue.html)

These guys have a variety of blue DPSS lasers available (and some Q-switched too) , and are much more informative about longitudinal modes. No prices, though. I wonder, since SLM is usually optional, if the S versions are different laser designs or they are just batch selected.
