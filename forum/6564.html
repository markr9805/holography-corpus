JohnFP Fri Nov 30, 2007 1:17 pm #3
Dave, when you use the term "meter" it sounds as if you know of a specific quantity. And not being familliar with the process, I didn't want to respond.

Tony,

Take the area of you plate and mulitply it by the thickness of the tape;

Example

5x15" plate is 12.7cm x 38.1cm = 483.87cm^2

Tape thickness is approx 50um or .005cm

So 483.87cm^2 x .005cm = 2.419cm^3

1cm^3 = 1ml

So the exact quantity needed is about 2 1/2 ml. But I doubt that would be practicle.

I would imagine you could use about 5 - 7 ml and have minimal mess as a base line starting point.

I know how precise you like to be so if you don't know the thickness of your tape and you would like to know, let me know the exact brand etc. and I will mic it for you. I guess the best way would be to place the tape on glass and measure the glass then the tape/glass with micrometers side by side close to each other. This would be handy in using with your Gel concentration to know your final thickness of your emulsion. I have found that clear celephane tape can vary as much as 25um in thickness. So a foruma someone else is doing may not work exacly unless the tape is the same.

and as far as cutting glass i have a jig to lay the film plates on (face down) as long as the film is dry this is ok ,you could give a little(blow dry)heat to insure is hardend enough. i then place a guide and cut using a GOOD glass cutter ($25) snap" and bend backward,others score the film side,but i havent had any problem (so far)

ill probably do a quick video on the glass cutting soon. if you not able to use a laminar flow ,i used to lighty polish the film side to rub off the micro-particals before placing it on the table, again as long as your gel is hard enough

and you use a soft wipe (i use KIm wipes,lint free)it wont scratch the film.

The tape I used is plain scotch tape and is .0025” or @ 63 microns thick.

the film thickness came in @ .0004” or @ 10 Microns
