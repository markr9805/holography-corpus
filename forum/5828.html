holokid Tue Oct 10, 2006 1:37 pm #9
It's a small holographic world isn't it… XYZ Imaging, Geola, Zebra, Liti etc. etc

Just to clarify a couple issues though...

Geola uses the XYZ Imaging printer to produce the color holo's seen on various Geola related sites. They were once shareholder of Imaging but not any more. The recent systems developed by Imaging use proprietary lasers developed by Imaging, which is especially true for very large holo’s where laser stabilization is a key factor.

Zebra's holo's are typically created with CW lasers on glass (4 sq. ft max) using a dry film technology, very long process and typically more expensive. Imaging's are single write on film which is a wide format 1.2 meters by the length of the role, compareatively fast and cheaper. Zebra prints full parallax where the image is rendered on the fly (lower quality CG models required), where-as Imaging uses pre-rendered Hollywood imagery to achieve superior quality in single parallax fashion. While Imaging’s printers can print full parallax the memory requirements would be insane i.e. 1024x2048 high-definition fully CG scenes> if one frame only took a minute to render then a full parallax scene would take 4 years to render completely.

[quote="holokid"]It's a small holographic world isn't it… XYZ Imaging, Geola, Zebra, Liti etc. etc

Just to clarify things more:

The digital holography with pulsed laser was developed by Geola, patent priority from december 1999. The RGB pulsed laser for holography also was developed by Geola in 2000. Then various laser energy stabilisation schemes were developed by Geola and by XYZ. At the moment here in Vilnius (Geola EU office) we have one printer which working principles are based on Geola first patent. The printer was manufactured by XYZ.

The diffference between our holograms and those of XYZ and Zebra is that we mostly are printing the life scenes, like museum artefacts, portraits etc. For that we have built the "camera-on-a-rail" type device that shoots and prepares the files suitable for the printer.

To those who like to read scientific articles I've put our three latest ones for download on [http://www.geola.lt/download/Geola_arti ... 06_pdf.zip](http://www.geola.lt/download/Geola_articles_2006_pdf.zip)
