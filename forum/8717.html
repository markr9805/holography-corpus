Jonathan Ross asked this be shared:

I am sorry to be the bearer of sad tidings but I thought you would want to know that Margaret Benyon, a pioneering holographer and much loved friend and colleague of many of us, died on October 21st, aged 76. Following her move to Australia in 2005 she had been in declining health for a number of years.

Her funeral will be held at White Lady Funerals, 402 Pennant Hills Road, Pennant Hills, NSW on Saturday, 5th November 2016 at 11:00am. The family plan to gather afterwards to remember her in the presence of some of her work at her home. For further details or to send messages of condolence, please contact her husband William Rodwell, wrrodwell AT optusnet DOT com DOT au
