Junior Thu Jun 16, 2005 5:38 am #5
I use a CNI MSL-20 DPSS rated at 30mW for two beam setups.

H1:

-VRP-M 5x4"

-exposure 10 sec

-development with Dokumol (commercial developer, Tetenal)

-fixing with Superfix (commercial fxer, Tetenal)

-bleach K3[Fe(CN)6]/KBr

H2:

-Ultimate15 5x4"

-exposure 20 sec

-development with Gentet's standard chemistry
