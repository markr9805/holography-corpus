142laser Tue Jul 07, 2009 3:02 pm #12
Sounds excellent. Do you have the manual? If so you can check on the rear connector the diode current and the diode current limit. If the laser is running at full current to make the 200 mW it will drop into current regulation and out of light loop; the power will drift like a heNe would. By carefully adjusting the diode TEC and crystal TEC you may get the 200 mW at a far lower diode current and have far more life but it sounds like you have a good laser. Every C532 Sam and I have sold has been opimized like that before we ship it out and all had near 1/2A diode headroom when we sold them. Check the manual. Best regards, Phil

PS If you don't need 200 mW you can probably add a pot and turn it down to say 150 mW to get far more life also!
