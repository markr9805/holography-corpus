You can check modes with a very small, highly curved (very short focal length) mirror, some people call this a "mode mirror" , and reflect your raw, unexpanded beam onto a wall. The modes will show up in the reflection. It may be possible to check multi-linen with an interferometer, eg Michelson, which will give two sets of lines if you have distinct lines. The problem will be distinguishing lines from a multi-line output to fringes from intrnal reflections etc. If you have multiple modes or a large bandwidth, the path difference will be small. The surefire, but possibly expensive way, is a spectral analyser. This is basically a crt attached to a light meter. You may be able to make your own, but I understand buying one runs into the 1000's.
