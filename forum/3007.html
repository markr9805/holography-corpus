This past holiday I was looking at some family projects we could do. Specifically, Christmas Tree ornaments. I remember as a kid doing them with my mom and we still hang a few of them on our tree today that I made when I was about 7 years old. Anyway I did some searches on the internet and found a salt dough recipe. You roll out the dough, cut out the shapes, microwave it then paint it. It is very rigid. I believe this would be a good modeling substrate which is made with everyday home ingredients: Water, Salt and Flour.
