TIME, SPACE & MOVEMENT

17th – 23rd December 2006

A contemporary art exhibition that simply needs to be seen to be believed.

Opening reception:

Tuesday 19th Dec,

6.00pm-9.30pm

R.S.V.P

Martin Richardson: Time, Space & Movement

The AIR Gallery, 32 Dover Street London W1S 4NE

open: Mon-Sat, 10.00am-7.00pm

A major exhibition of Digital Holograms and Prints from the medium’s foremost practitioner.

The culmination of over 20 years’ work in 3D holography and digital imaging, this exhibition demonstrates how such techniques can be used to create vibrant art, and includes new work not seen elsewhere. Mesmerising digital scans of faces; heads and reclining forms link the work to traditions of portraiture as well as cutting-edge practice in new media.

"I don't think I've ever quite experienced holograms like these – they really are magnificent. They almost become part of one's family, and a very disturbing and 'otherly' family at that."

David Bowie on Martin Richardson

As a leading exponent in the movement to win artistic acceptance for digital imagery and holography, his work challenges our understanding or ‘visual reality’ with an unsettling ambiguity. Many of the works exhibit an eerie, strangely beautiful, hypnotic and otherworldly quality… particularly those referenced as ‘portraits’ being both familiar and thought provoking.

‘Time, Space & Movement’ will also be the launch for Martin Richardson’s new book ’The Prime Illusion: Modern Holography In The New Age of Digital Media’.
