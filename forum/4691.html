MichaelH Mon Apr 04, 2005 8:27 am #4
It definitely belongs here.

I'll stress this point some more. If you have even a remote inkling that you'll go beyond your first package of plates or film, take the time to make or buy what you need to test your table with an interferometer.

The environment you use when making holograms is talking to you constantly and the interferometer is the translator. It will give you a huuuge amount of information about the vibrations coming into the area as well as the sorts of things that can happen (can you walk around or open and close doors while an exposure is going on?) and things the shouldn't happen during an exposure (don't open that garage door!).

Listen to your table. It can turn what would have been failed holograms into successes.
