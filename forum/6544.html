favalora Fri Nov 16, 2007 12:45 am #2
The movie of a display you referred to was built by researchers out of USC; their SIGGRAPH paper explains how it was made and how it works. My company, [Actuality,](http://www.actuality-medical.com) built a similar system as reported in Applied Optics, as well as a commercially-available volumetric display, Perspecta. The nomenclature in the field is still up in the air, but you can call these volumetric displays or multiview volumetric displays, or perhaps even light field displays. There are other ways to make volumetric displays too. But be aware that there are many years' of patents covering these technologies.

-gregg
