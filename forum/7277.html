Hi all

I just wanted to share this photo of a hologram I made today.

I'm still very much a newbie when it comes to making holograms and am limited to making SBR Denisyuk type holograms only due to lack of space and stability.

I got a C315m a few months ago, but due to illness and too much work I haven't had much time to play with it. Anyway, today I fixed up the laser and fired the beam through a spatial filter fitted with a 50x objective. I couldn't manage to get the laser far enough away from the plate so that the expanded beam covered the entire plate hence the 'circle' where the beam hit.

The plate is a BB520 from colorholographic, it was presoaked in 3% tea with a little Photoflo for 30 seconds, exposed for just under 2 seconds and developed in pyro/sodium carbonate, bleached in EDTA and dried. I then spray painted the back of the plate with black enamel.

It's the brightest and cleanest hologram i've ever made. It's clear under just about any light from any angle. I'm amazed!!

Very very nice Jen.

I've been struggling with space as well. I took the suggestion of I think Dave and added a negative lens on the other side of my spatial filter. I used a -17mm FL and about 1 cm diameter. You can then set (z axis) it to pretty much whatever spot size you want at the target (up to the clear aperture of the lens). The nice thing is that the beam retains a clean spot from the spatial filter.

If you are ever interested in trading a BB520 plate hologram for a AmDi one let me know. I just make some coins today (will post later) and they look pretty good. I would like to compare the brightness between the two if possible. Just PM me if you are interested.

I noticed these are green in color, is the due to lighting, the laser, the film? I know with AmDi it is due to slight over exposure.
