BobH Sun Apr 09, 2006 2:25 pm #2
A "tanning" developer hardens the emulsion, like tanning animal skin makes it leather. Drying a hologram in graded alcohol baths is a good idea, but the benefits (a bit higher DE and a little less noise) generally do not outweigh the hassle of doing the procedure in a home studio.
