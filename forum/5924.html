It is impossible to obtain chromates as a private person here in Sweden. One need to have a company to be able to buy those chemistries. I have talked to different authorities and for several dangerous chemicals it is possible to get sanctions for artistic or hobby work. Except for chromates. You are in no way able to obtain those for private work. So, to be able to experiment with DCG I have to register a company.
