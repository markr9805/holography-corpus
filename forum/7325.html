Joe Farina Fri May 09, 2008 11:33 am #3
> Danny Bee: i will be trying this again.. maybe more narrow band

Fantastic job Danny, congratulations. I think that's a good idea to try narrowband. If you're doing an object with rather saturated colors (say for example blue and green), it would definitely produce better results going narrowband (assuming you could control the shrinkage of the final DCG layer). But since it's a metallic badge, it's hard to say what would look better, broadband or narrowband (assuming a two-color exposure at 457 and 532) or something in between. I'm looking forward to seeing more of your two-color work. By the way, this is the only kind of stuff you need to do to keep holography going strong
