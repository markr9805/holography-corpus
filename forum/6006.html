Jonathan Ross presents

LUST FOR LIGHT

Hologram Nudes & Erotic Photography

Gallery 286 February 8 – March 6

To add some brightness to the dark February days (and nights) and

to banish SAD I have put together an exhibition focussing on nudes

and light.

The inspiration was my latest acquisition, a beautiful double exposure

reflection hologram by Ana Maria Nicholson, Cocoon II (pictured above

left) and the realisation that I had enough images of the nude in one

form or another to make a small survey of how the subject has been

handled by holographers over the years.

The exhibition includes one of Peter Claudius’s risqué integral holograms from

the Multiplex Company during the 70s, some still life images of nude

figures by various artists, pulsed laser images from Paul Newman,

Martin Richardson and the Olsons of San Francisco, Russian stereograms,

animations from David Pizzanelli’s Jolly Series and a couple of pieces from

Jon Mitton’s notorious Hardcore Holography show, amongst other things.

You will find examples of Fine Art and borderline kitsch all employing the

medium of holography. I leave you to decide which is which.

To complement the holograms I am delighted to be showing The Erotic Art

of Matt Slade.

Matt photographs gorgeous nude models bathed in psychedelic fractal light projections

(see example above right) and a selection of his award-winning prints

will be on sale for your continued mind-expansion.

6.30 – 8.30 pm at Gallery 286, 286 Earl’s Court Road, London SW5 9AS

Viewing at other times is strictly by appointment only.
