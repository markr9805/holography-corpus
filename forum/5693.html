MichaelH Fri Jul 14, 2006 5:11 pm #3
> JohnFP: Michael, that is superb!! Really!!! I like it!!!

Thanks. I'm really happy with it too.

> JohnFP: How did you create the object of the fireworks?

Paint and glass. There's more information in my original post [over here](http://www.holographyforum.org/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=36782#36782)

> JohnFP: Why do you pretreat first, dry, then wash out and have to dry again to do the second exposure. Wouldn't it be easier to do the first exposure (red fireworks) then treat with TEA and dry, then shoot flay and just process?

I did it that way partly because my film was already treated for green (I tend to do it in small batches so I don't have to wait every time I want to shoot).

Even if I'd been starting fresh I would have at least treated the fireworks just to shorten the exposure time. So either way, there'd be two trips through liquid before I developed the film. So the overall process is: TEA treat, dry, shoot, wash out TEA, dry, shoot, soak in water, develop etc.
