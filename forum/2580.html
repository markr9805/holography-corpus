[http://www.diybeamer.ch/portal/tftdb.php](http://www.diybeamer.ch/portal/tftdb.php)

A list of TFT monitors, that can be reassempled.

The separated TFT panel can be used to make holographic stereograms.

There are so many monitors listed there, that one has the impression that every LCD-TFT monitor could be reassempled.

The site is in german, but anyone can read the list.

For some models, e.g. for the panel BenQ FP 581s, there are detailed instructions (Anleitungen) on how to do this.

(click Anleitung: hier).

The cost of a new TFT monitor, XGA resolution, is about 300 Euros (or $) nowadays.

Normaly (if one could tell that such prices are normal) the cost of a bare TFT module

(e.g. from [http://www.crlopto.com](http://www.crlopto.com)), XGA resolution is about $5000.

>>What I want to do with it requires very, very high resolution. Not even sure if the resolution I want is available yet, 6400x4800 minimal<<

I have already made some tests with a 10" TFT overheadprojector panel (resolution XGA, 1024x768) and the resulted holographic stereograms were good.

I think that the resolution you mention (6400x4800) is overdimensionated.

If you use such a panel (6400x4800) for an hologramm of say 8"x10" (250x200mm),

then the pixel of the image in the hologram will be 250/6400=0.039mm.

This resolution would also be good for a very larger hologram, 2.5x2.0 meter. The pixel size would then be 0.39mm.

"Is there such thing as a transmission plazma"

Plasma panels are emissive, so they emit their own non-coherent light. They are not proper for our purpose.
