I just spoke with Dinesh and he explained that no one knows what this is. There was some text explaining it below the video but I guess no one has noticed it. Here's the text again.

In short though, this will allow you to run a stepper motor without a controller. Simple manual control. The connector you see attaches to the motor, the wires leading to the motor are just out of the frame.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To drive a stepper motor with manual control send a low voltage ac signal through the motors coils with a capacitor delaying the wave going to one of the coils.

The stepper motor is now being run as an induction motor.

The switch is just a convenient way to change directions. The same can be done without the switch by moving the capacitor from one coil to the other. Do not use the 1/2 taps on a 6 wire motor. Only use the full length of the coils.

This is an 11 volt 0.44 amp stepper motor and the capacitor is 10uf. Experiment with the value of the capacitor to match the inductance of the motors coils. When it runs smooth you have the correct value.

The motor will run at 60 steps per second. The line frequency of the 12 volt ac transformer used as the power supply. The table is geared down. The motor itself is turning much faster.
