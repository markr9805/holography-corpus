This is a Hologram of the Light House I did using Pot DiCr and exposing to 488 and 514 wavelength one at a time with same reference angle. In real life the color is more pastel then this photo.

![Image](http://www.sketchys.com/Holograms/011005/LightHousePseudo.JPG)

This is the same hologram viewed pseudoscopically.

![Image](http://www.sketchys.com/Holograms/011005/GlassChips.JPG)

This is the glass chips hologram. I glued the glass shards to a piece of glass. Then set it up with glass shards plate, holographic plate, then mirror in a single beam exposure with just 514 wavelength. Nice effect but not what I wanted.

![Image](http://www.sketchys.com/Holograms/011005/MultiPaints.JPG)

This is a hologram of the Multi Colored paints using all lines of the Argon Laser. The original paints varied from deep blue to red. This was using Am DiCr and processed with cool baths. Got greens, yellows and oranges but the blues came out blackish. Maybe not enough energy in the blue line. It's a start though.

![Image](http://www.sketchys.com/Holograms/011005/MultiPaints2.JPG)

This is the same object but I processed with hot baths for broadband and used all lines of the Argon laser.
