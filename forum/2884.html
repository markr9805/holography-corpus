> Technodreamer: But how do I actually make a hologram? Do I have to buy special chemicals, or can I produce them myself? I have soem basic elements and compounds. I see I need a film of some kind.

Use just one laser. Make a few Denisyuks for starters. You need some holographic film (PFG-03M is a good place to start) and some chemicals (JD-4 is good for PFG-03M).

You can get the chemicals and film at Integraf (click the banner at the top of this page) as wel as some other places. They also have some good practical info:

[http://www.holokits.com/a-teaching_holography.htm](http://www.holokits.com/a-teaching_holography.htm)

Photographer's Formulary also has the chemicals (actually, if you buy from Integraph, they come with PF labelling):

[http://www.photoformulary.com/](http://www.photoformulary.com/)

When you get the chemicals, they are premeasured compounds you mix together. If you have the chemicals already on hand (or just need to order 1 or 2 you don't have), you can look up the formula for JD-4 and measure and mix yourself.

Good luck!

-Phil
