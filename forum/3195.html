I put together a collection of everyday items that can be used in holography. They are by no means soley my ideas, just a collection of things I could think of. Here are a couple.

Polarizers can be found in polarizing sun glasses. These can be used

to slightly modify the intensity of throughput laser light by inserting

it into the beam path and rotating. They can also be used to compare

the polarization of light at different locations in an optical set-up.

A thick piece of glass, 1/4" or thicker, can be used as a beam splitter.

Using the thick piece of glass allows a small piece of electric tape to be

placed over the glass to block the secondary reflection off the back.
