Not quite. Brehmstallung radiation is caused by a charged particle whizzing around a particle of opposite charge. An electron whizzing around a proton, for instance. Any accelerated charged object radiates, hence the acceleration caused by the second charged body on the first causes radiation. For your cat to give off Brehmstallung, it'd have to be charged and you'd have to have another charged body nearby.

For those intereted, this is an FEL:

[http://www.jlab.org/FEL/feldescrip.html](http://www.jlab.org/FEL/feldescrip.html)
