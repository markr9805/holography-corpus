I have an interest in computer generated holograms and digital holography. I've been developing code to create off axis Fresnel type holograms, currently limited to between 4 and 16 megapixels. The holograms of course are small, and the plane wave reference angles are no more than 1 to 3 degrees from normal incidence. The object is to test the functionality of the code and then proceed from there.

Assuming a good hologram image has been created, what would be the best way to have it produced? So far the only thing I have come up with is having them output using a digital to slide film recorder with the best b&w film it can handle. However does anyone know of a lithographic or similar (e beam ?) service which could handle better than 100 lines per mm in small quantities?

Perhaps someone here has tried this and are able to answer or provide sources for additional exploration of the production question I have.

Glenn
lobaz Tue Aug 06, 2013 9:05 am #2
Hi,

we have a laser litography system at the University of West Bohemia, Czech Republic. We can make pixel about 1 um big, the photoresist size is about 5x5 cm. You have to ask for the price but last time it was about 350 EUR (about 450 USD) for a hologram 3x3 cm. PM me for details.

You can also ask professor Kyoji Matsushima from the Kansai University, Japan, how does he print his holograms. I think the laser litography system he uses was developed by professor Sumio Nakahara, the same university, but I am not sure.
