Ok, I broke down and bought some, the type used for drying flowers. I figure if it saves me money on my 100% alcohol it would be worth it.

I added some to alcohol that was 97% and want to see if bring the alcohol concentration up. The crystals are pretty fine and will have to be filtered out with a coffee filter. If it does work then I would like to make a cloth bag to contain the crystals so that they can stay in the alcohol all the time.

I also did an experiment this morning (Seems I can't sleep much lately). I took 30 grams of crystals and put them in 200 ml of water. when the crystals turn pink, which means they will have reached their capacity to absorb water, I will take the crystal out and dry them (Just slightly with a blow dryer to rid the attached water to the outside - to dry the crystals them selves takes 4 hours in an oven at 250 degrees F.). Then I will weight the crystals and find out how much water 30 grams of crystal will hold before they become saturated. Using these numbers, I should be able to formulate a chart which predicts the amount of crystals to add to a know volume of alcohol/water of known concentration to bring it to a desired concentration once the crystals absorb their maximum of water.

I was also day dreaming about drying out alcohol with gelatin. Here is what I thought. Take heated dissolved gelatin and coat an 8 x 10 plate pretty thick, no AmDi. Let it cure for 24 hours. Then run it through the alcohol baths up to the 90% but not the 100%. Then dry it thoroughly with a blow dryer. This plate can then be placed in the ~100% alcohol and left in there during processing of holograms. It should absorb the water in that bath that comes out of the holograms. After a few processing’s just take the plate out and dry it thoroughly again with the blow dryer and reuse. Just a thought and maybe I will give it try.
