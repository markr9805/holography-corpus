Flatting spray is simply clear laquer with a flatting agent. You can make your own if you have a air brush. It does not wash off. Hairspray is very similiar. Test your brand as it can be hard to wash off.
BobH Thu Oct 21, 2004 5:52 pm #6
There is certainly water soluable dulling spray. Try art stores. After dulling, I'd use big diffused light as suggested by Keveh and cut the specular reflection down with polarization manipulation.

JohnFP Thu Oct 21, 2004 10:01 pm #12
How about using a water based, washable paint to paint the reflective spots. I am assuming the continents are not reflective. This would take time but the object can be soaked and cleaned easily. If they contents are also reflective you could paint them also. I did a similar hologram years ago and want to recreate it in DCG. What I did and it worked pretty good, was to get a light fixture globe, which was glass and paint the continents on it. Then I could made letters out of balsa wood which I placed inside that read, "Save the Earth". Anywhere there was not a continent you could read the lettering inside. Good luck.
