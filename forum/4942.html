Tom B. Sat Jul 09, 2005 4:29 am #1
Film test strips need to be marked for later identification, but alkali developer quickly washes off permanent markers and grease pencils. The markings can be protected by a small strip of tape pressed firmly down to cover them. I tried Scotch Magic Transparent tape tonight and it worked well with no peeling after over 3 minutes in JD-4 developer. I think, though, that there was some slight degradation of the tape surface, so more corrosive developers might be a problem. Other tapes such as polyester or Kapton for example should be much better at resisting developers.

walschuler Sun Sep 25, 2005 2:24 pm #3
After testing a number of pens, I settled on the fine tip "Stylist" model NSP-100 by Yasutomo & Co. It is available at many stationery stores. Comes in colors. Not absolutely perfect, but better than Sharpies, to my surprise. If allowed to dry, it often lasts unprotected from post-devo thru bleach, re-devo and final photoflo. If it fades, it can be reinforced at the end. Whoops! More than 1 line!
