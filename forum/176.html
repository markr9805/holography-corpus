Hi -

An approximately 10-piece traveling collection of extraordinarily high-quality or historically-notable holograms on view OUTSIDE the MIT Museum. What I mean is, the holograms are mounted in the window, visible to passers-by at night. There are works from Yves Gentet, Zebra Imaging, the Media Lab, RabbitHoles and others. They are real treasures. My colleagues and I spent a very long time enjoying them. Through Apr 1:

[http://web.mit.edu/museum/exhibitions/l ... ndows2011/](http://web.mit.edu/museum/exhibitions/luminouswindows2011/)

Enjoy!

gregg
