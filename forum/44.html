Jem has reported the same problem in the now closed holography forum, but you can read the discussion here:

[http://www.natscience.com/Uwe/Forum.asp ... ower-level](http://www.natscience.com/Uwe/Forum.aspx/laser/5409/Adjusting-C315m-Power-level)

I would suggest not to play with P6 pot though, if you are missing only 10 mW.

I have also had the same problem and could easily raise the output power from ca 80 to 100 mW by turning the pot P6.

The laser run fine for ca 2 years producing stable 100 mW, but since about a month ago I have serious problems, probably due to this intervention.

The laser doesn't obey any autostart adapter's commands and always runs unstable emitting around 150 mW at 2400 mA.

The digital controller says that the power meter inside the laser head measures zero (0) mW, so the analog controller thinks that there isn't any laser beam and raises the current until the upper limit is reached.

142laser Wed Nov 17, 2010 12:48 pm #6
The 315M is a great laser for holography. Unfortunatly as these lasers age many of them willl loose power when the setting remains the same. Sam calls this C315M loss of power syndrome. It seems to be due ti increased scatter inside the laser head from the beamsplitter that samples the beam becoming contmineted. The scatter is mistaken for laser power and the controller turns the laser down. On a newer 150 mW head we have observed about 1 mW per month power loss with NO ON TIME!! Even just sitting the lasers will loose power like this. Adjusting the pot per the instructions on the FAQ solves this except in extreme cases and is much easier than oening the sealed head to clean the under side of the beam splitter. Coherent should solve this...if it wern't for this the C315M's would be about perfect. Phil Bergeron, 142laser.
