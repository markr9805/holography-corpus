Hans Sun Jul 23, 2006 7:31 pm #8
Yes, they are H2's. The H1's are transmission holograms with the object (persons ) lighted from both front sides through diffusors, and the reference beam from behind the person above. Sergey showed us the masters. It was an incredible experience to hold those super sharp masters in our hands and see the bright images through a widened beam. Sergey uses a self built H1-H2 copying setup. A large metal frame where the laser sits on top and illuminates the H1 and H2 from opposite directions, with the beams reflected off truely huge collimating mirrors. The plate holders can be shifted horizontally, in order to adjust the object distance to the H2 plate. The H2's have a great depth of at least half a meter. The decorative curtain that hangs behind the protraits is clearly visible.

I was most impressed by Sergey's eye for detail and his passion for making the holograms perfect. He showed us that he had made several H2's before the ones he sold to me. To me they all looked perfect. But he sowed some small imperfections that "made them insufficient to sell to us".

The shooting of the portraits were a great experience also. When Sergey was doing some exposure tests with small laser output, I thought that was the 1J pulse. But then he said to hold that smile and charged the laser up to it's rated power. When he pressed the trigger, there was a loud BANG and an immense flash of light. Quite an awesome experience. I have been thinking about making portriats ever since.

The laser used is an nd:YLF laser with one resonator and one amplifier. The light passes twice through the amplifier and a SBS mirror is used to compensate for imperfections of the amplifier crystal and thermal lensing before it is reflected back for the second pass.
