[http://www.holography.ru](http://www.holography.ru)

This is one of my favorite sites on the web. It has a great mix of holograms for sale and instruction for making holograms. They sent me this hologram this week:

![Image](http://www.holography.ru/images/forums/rumki1.jpg)

It has flawless processing and is quite bright. Their ability to take highly reflective objects and place them close to the film plane is quite accomplished. The composition of this hologram is very nice as well. It is a beautiful addition to my collection. The mirror for a floor adds great effects and the rainbows created have to be seen in person to be appreciated.
