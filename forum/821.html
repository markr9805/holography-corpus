Hi everyone. I just received notice that for the third consecutive year I have won the "People's Choice Award" for best photographer at the San Francisco erotic art show "On the Edge IV" - in spite of ballot-stuffing by my competitors!

I'm a 3-D photographer to pretty much everyone these days - about the twentieth time Microsoft Word corrected "holographer" I swore off the term at least for the time being.

To any out there who still feel that analog, monochromatic holograms aren't cool, try attending one of my shows and gauging public reaction. I hate to harp but if ever Holotown would wise up to the fact that Q-switched Neodymium based lasers are indeed the way to go - and indeed very expensive - then maybe we could put together a few we'll-equipped communal studios that would offer the viewing public something to get excited about. By the way, my winning image this year was a PG rated glamour/pin-up piece featuring a lovely young woman's face and torso. It is the eye-to-eye engagement absolutely unique to holography that most viewers (to include artists of many different disciplines) point to as most compelling regarding my work.

Keep your eyes and ears open regarding possible display venues for my erotica - I'm on something of a roll.

Hi Gang, I certainly don’t think this post got the attention it deserved.

Hey Ron, great to see you are still getting the recognition, keep the (green) flame burning! Nashville Indiana- has presently been in storage for the last five years, 2. St. Charles Missouri- Allen Fox Studio) I think that makes up the commune for now.

For God’s sake let’s get some use out of these wonderful lasers! Ron, Allen, and I aren’t exactly spring chickens anymore and we are definitely due for some new blood in the field! For serious, artistic, pictorial holography, Ron is indeed correct, these things can’t be beat! No, they are not the be-all, end- all out there for holography artists, but essential for specific content generation. It amazes me to this day that display holography in general, and specifically “pulsed green” cannot garner the sustained acclaim for a few small “well-equipped communal studios” to collectively operate out there!!?? We now have a whole new generation of folks that have never even seen these things, they’re going to go ape!!! It was never supposed to be some “secret exclusive club”. I think Ron is being pretty stand-up (and cheap) with his generous offer. HOLO Artist’s out there should take him up on this... hell, you can’t even rent a photography studio on the west coast for that price! Let’s start seeing some arising, inspiring, creative new work! I’m in till the end, so however we want to work this thing is fine with me. I just want to see people working together, making holograms that are getting shown (and sold), and perpetuating the craft in a much larger arena than it is presently. Kumbaya, anyone?

With all that said, I have been still kicking some stuff out lately, getting to work with some cool people on some cool projects but there could be so much more!

My two cents worth (and a couple of big-a* lasers),
