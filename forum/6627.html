Colin Kaminski Wed Jan 02, 2008 4:12 am #2
You willl want a heated stir plate to mix the chemicals. Ebay is a good source but bidding is competitive.

You must heat the final bath with much care as it is dangerous. Use a ventilation hood! A bathroom fan is not enough. I have used a heating pad but that doesn't give me much temperature control.

The problem is not diluting the 100% but trying to maintain it. Most people buy it at the drug store as they can order it. I get it from a chemical supply house that I use for work anyways. It saves me money to buy two concentrations as the pure cost me more. It is helpful to have a distillers hydrometer to measure the concentrations of used baths.

Hans Sat Jan 05, 2008 8:59 am #5
You can do without a heated stirrer, really. I have one, but never use the stirrer part any more. I use a pan of water and put it on the heating pad. Inside the pan of water I put the glass beaker with gelatin. Occasional slow stirring with a spoon dissolves the gelatin bubble free in about one hour.
MichaelH Sat Jan 05, 2008 12:15 pm #6
I can't imagine not using a heated stirrer. I got really lucky and found one that was "broken" at a local junk sale. Turns out the magnet had come off its mount and jammed the stirrer. A little work with a screwdriver and epoxy and I had a fully working stirrer for practically nothing.

I now use it for mixing all my chemicals and it's a great time saver. Put your solutions on the plate and let it do all the work.
