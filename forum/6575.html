Joe Farina Wed Dec 05, 2007 6:00 pm #4
My thought was to get together in a fairly "serious" manner with the intent of producing and selling DCG holograms. Personally, I don't feel comfortable telling the whole world everyting I know on the internet. Maybe others feel the same way. If there is some type of commitment among members of an association, then it would be different. Perhaps there could be some sort of "division of labor" among members to acheive this, along with the free sharing of information. This of course assumes that the members have common goals.

This is not supposed to be some kind of "secret society" as membership wouldn't be exclusive. Maybe you could compare it to a health club: you pay a yearly fee, and get the benefit of using all the equipment. I'm not necessarily talking about a fee to join, but some kind of "effort" or commitment. Exactly what this may be, I don't know yet. It stands to reason that DCG holographers have different kinds of skills and expertise, and that in isolation, it's difficult to make progress. Look at the situation before the internet, for example. But the internet is only good up to a certain point.

My goal is to present high-quality DCG holograms to the public, for sale. I'm not interested in holography for the purpose of making money (I hope that's obvious by now), my objective is really to spend more time making good-quality DCG holograms. We all probably reach a certain point when time won't allow us to practice our DCG (or whatever it is we practice) continuously, which I think is essential for obtaining the kind of results we desire. The effort needs to be compensated (monetarily) for the effort to flourish. In other words, the public needs to be willing to place a value upon a good DCG hologram, and the quality needs to be there for this to happen.

Instead of "DCG Association" perhaps it could be called "DCG Guild."
