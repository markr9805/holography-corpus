Hey, this is really interesting. Can one make a suitable ECDL grating with a hologram? Will this setup prevent mode hopping. Can I do the same with a YAG crystal?
Colin Kaminski Wed Mar 29, 2006 2:09 pm #6
Temperature stability is the first chalange. If the grating to diode distance changes then you will have mode hopping problems. I can't remember who it was, but one of our members succeded in making one 100mm long. I would get a grating from Edmund Industrial optics, a TEC from Melcor and a TEC controller and diode driver from Wavelength Electronics.
