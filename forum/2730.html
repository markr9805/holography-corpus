I've been asked to move the teaser video here and thought I'd do one better by posting a note so that people who haven't heard of the PCG will have a chance to be exposed to it (as it were).

What's the PCG? You are! Even if you don't know it.

The PCG is a group of holography enthusiasts of all kinds from rank amateur to pro who have decided that there just aren't enough open groups devoted to holography and are working to do something about that.

We had our first meeting back in April in Plano, TX and the second is coming up in November in San Diego, CA.

There's a teaser video available [through this link](http://holographyforum.org/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1634) which has clips from various talks and discussions during PCGG-I.

The planning and post-meeting pages for PCGG-I are available [through this link](http://www.dragonseye.com/holography/PCGGI.html).

And not least, the discussion for PCGG-II [can be found here](http://holographyforum.org/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=30).

If you can make it to San Diego in November get involved in the discussions now and save your place. We'd all love to see you there.
