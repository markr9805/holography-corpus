This is a birthday present for a particularly geeky friend of mine. I gave him the transmission master too. The closest T's are 3 or 4 inches in front of the plate. The only problem is that the happy birthday sign in the corner moved, obscuring the "Happy".

![Image](http://www.bobdbob.com/~tjohnson/misc/hologram52.1.jpg)

H1H2 onto PFG-3c with a 315m

Exposure time was 17 seconds, 40 seconds in JD-4 developer, about a minute in JD-4 bleach, 10 seconds in 2% ammonium dichromate solution (to kill the sensitizing dyes), wash 10 minutes, blot any droplets dry, air-dry from there, and spray paint the back matte black the next day.
