Hi again,

On my latest batch of holograms, I'm getting some small, colored spots on the hologram, and I'm at a loss to know what is causing this. When I was developing some of the holograms, when I was rinsing, I noticed that there appeared to be small irregularities on the emulsion. As a test, on some of the plates I tried washing them off with the faucet, but they didn't come off. I've made sure that the solutions I used for developing were clean and free of particulate matter. Has anyone else seen this? I'm air drying the plates, and not doing any sort of squeegee on these holograms.

I'm using JD-4 developer on PFG-03M plates.

This shot was taken with flurorescent light so the green areas would show up better. Ignore the reflection of the webcam

Thanks for any help you can provide,

Tom B. Sat Nov 20, 2004 7:30 pm #2
Is the problem associated with a particular batch of plates? I recall noticing (under safelight) dust on some batches of PFG-03M plates which would introduce contamination. I wiped them clean with a soft cloth before using them.

Also, the emulsion edge on some plates seemed very ragged and delicate - during processing, tiny flakes of emulsion would sometimes float off, redeposit on the plate surface, and stick there tenaciously. Maybe more vigorous rinsing or brushing with a soft brush under running water would remove them?
tpark Mon Nov 22, 2004 6:09 pm #3
Thanks - the emulsion could have dust on it in advance of exposure. I had assumed that the plates would have been clean when packed, but I guess no such guarantee. I'll check my batch of plates and see if that's the problem.

I guess the big hint should have been that I'd not seen this before with some of the many boxes of plates I've used, then suddenly the problem appears.
