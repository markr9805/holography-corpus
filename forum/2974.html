No, It was just an idea I thought of sparked by the link [http://holography.co.uk/newsletter/holnews-Nov04.htm](http://holography.co.uk/newsletter/holnews-Nov04.htm) that Martin posted. In that newletter is an article concering "An unusual method for lighting small holographic subjects". What I originally started to try to think of is how to incorporate two HOE into a hologram such that the two HOE's diffrated additional light to the object. But I found one fatal flaw. The hologram's HOEs on the sides would have to be developed in order to refract the light toward the object durring the object recording. As I stated it was a major flaw but it brought to mind this configuration. It may be something someone else wants to incorporate into their hologram, thus the reason for posting it. Do you see a flaw other then cutting the two touching edges of the mirrors to 45 degree angle such that there is no reflection from the point where the meet?
