Thanks

I used the hair dryer with glass funnels too and it works , but requiring you to hold the hair dryer until things warm up and turning the funnel or the hair dryer around and around. The funnel seems to get colder after a while and needs the hair dryer again.

It depends on the mass of the funnel, how much warmth it can absorb and buffer. Usually i take the smallest possible funnel and filter (to keep the losses lîmited), but then the hair dryer trick does not work that well or needs constant blowing. And with plastic funnels it gets difficult too.

My hair dryer is quite heavy, that's why i looked for alternatives.

But each method has its merits i guess.
