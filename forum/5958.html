Similar to Tom [over here](http://www.holographyforum.org/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=2132), I've been using tupperware and locking containers for a while and just started using a combination of that and Wine Savers to make my working developer last longer.

In a nutshell, cut a hole in the top of your container and glue a wine saver stopper into the hole. Now you can use the pump to remove a lot of the air inside the container and your working developer will last longer before you have to throw it out.

More details on:

[http://www.dragonseye.com/blog/archives ... er-tm.html](http://www.dragonseye.com/blog/archives/238-Developer-Saver-tm.html)
