Jim McPherson Sat Mar 26, 2005 2:17 pm #1
Last night I met JohnFP for a night of making holograms. I'm a rank amature who knows only enough to get by, however John graciously invited me into his lab to show me the way. I learned the wonders of DCG and will have a hard time going back to my silver halide setup. DCG has got to be the coolest material I've ever seen! The bright bright holograms, interesting colors, and ease of use are amazing.

In addition I learned some simple tricks of the trade that make life a lot simpler. John also helped me diagnose the characteristics of my new diode laser and we worked to get it up to par for hologram making. I think we got it going well and I hope to start using it tonight.

I really appreciate more experienced holographers sharing their wealth of knowledge and helping little guys like me have more fun in the hobby. John certainly did that for me last night!

With that spirit I think holography will continue to grow and grow. Thanks!

-Jim

Colin Kaminski Sun Mar 27, 2005 3:32 pm #7
Moisture from my compressor was a big problem is spraying laquer onto my guitars. Here was my solution, a bit extreme but it worked very well.

Empty all of the water from the tank with the bleed valve before each use. Run the compressed air through a 25' 3/8" copper coil made from refigeration copper. The coil was imersed into a bucket of ice water. Then I put the air through a filter and a standard air line dryer (available from any automotive paint supplier).

When we were done it was quite dry.
