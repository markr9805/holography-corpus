> now there has been test in holography plate making where the silver nitrate was mixed with the gel and then coated to the plate, and then after they have dried put them in a bromide bath... but what if it was done in the same order as they did in the collodion process? this would be very intresting to try for making hologram plates

Yes, that might work Danny. But the problem you would get there is that the bath of AgNO3 would likely be ruined after one dip of a plate into it. And since the AgNO3 spoils quickly and the LiBr is cheaper, it's more cost efficient to first load the plate with AgNO3 and then dip into a LiBr solution.
