BobH Fri Dec 19, 2008 1:06 am #3
I have no experience with VRP or VRP-M, but the anti-halation layer on Kodak HRP plates was on the opposite side of the plate from the emulsion and was easily removed in a methanol bath. It was a red dye for the green sensitive plates.

You could use a piece of smoked glass index-matched to the plate and get the same result. A piece of glass with AR coating on one side only will also work but might be hard to find, especially on a budget. That would be a good group buy ... single sided AR coated glass. Anyone interested?
dave battin Fri Dec 19, 2008 1:45 am #4
some of the early kodak films had an AH backing, that was easly remove by a soak in H2O, currently ive had VERY good results using fuji film ,index matched to glass with the back painted black. i simply put a measured ammount of filtered mineral sprits (oderless type) on the glass and then place film on top with emulsion contacting the glass/index fluid, cover it with a paper towle roll out the exsess fluid, then tape ALL the edges. and give a quick buff to clean it up. the after exposuer i give the film a quick wash in water and process a normal .............
