tpark Mon Dec 06, 2004 12:52 am #6
Closest object is about 1cm behind the plate. It's PFG-03m, processed with JD4. I used a diode laser, and the side beam spillover was reflected back to the object so it would be more brightly illuminated. It appears much as you see in the picture, but nothing was done to make it change color, so I think it's an artifact of developing/illumination angle - it can be positioned so that the whole hologram appears pretty much reddish/yellow. Exposed about 12 seconds with 100 micrwatts/cm**2 at 660nm. I'll put up some images of the bb-640 plates later.
