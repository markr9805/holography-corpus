I enjoy testing when it leads to a better understanding of what I am doing. Being in the elementry school of DCG it should be required of all students

Part of this was understanding exposure parameters. Although I understood the basic effects of exposure time and color by reading about it, I did know by how much. I appears with this set of objects it is +/- 20% exposure time in seeing noticeable effects, with the optimal exposure time most likely +/- 10%.

Last week I also did gel thickness testing where I used Danny B's suggestion of 3 plys of tape on one side of my mold and 1 ply on the other (5"X12"). I will post those results later.
