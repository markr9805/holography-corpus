wler Sun Jan 29, 2006 10:00 am #1
That's not a tip but a question: what is a good, cheap and (in Europe) easily availabe glue for putting together optical components (the glue being in the light path)?

The usual glass glues from the supermarked are of this cyanoacrylate kind that tends to outgas and ruin optical surfaces...

What I like to try is to get rid of a frosted backside of a mirror, by gluing a flat polished piece of glass into it, hopefully eliminating the "frost" by index matching it away.

Loctite has a range of UV curing adhesives:

Loctite 302

Loctite 322

Loctite 350

Loctite 352

Pascofix (/[www.pascofix.nwy.at/](http://www.pascofix.nwy.at/))

has interesting non-outgasing cyanacrylate adhesives.

Otherwise a couple of transparent epoxies can be found easily. And I think there are some styrene based resins (“Giessharz”).
