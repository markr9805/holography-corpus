Tom B. Thu Apr 14, 2005 6:04 am #3
Old laser printers and faxes will be using an infrared diode, probably 780 nm. If you could find a REALLY REALLY old laser printer, you might get a HeNe laser out of it but these printers are very rare. Lots of other neat parts though. See

[http://www.sas.org/E-Bulletin/2002-12-2 ... /body.html](http://www.sas.org/E-Bulletin/2002-12-20/labNotes/body.html)

Modern laser printers with resolution >= 1200 DPI might possibly use shorter wavelength diodes - this would make the optics needed to get small spot size with good depth of field cheaper.
