> JohnFP: Michael, very excellent hologram! What did you make the spaghetti out of? They look to be thin and suseptable to movement but really show off nice paralax.

Sorry, forgot to mention what the model was made of.

The main body is non-hardening clay. I used that because all of our self-hardening clay had self-hardened while in storage (grrr). The spaghetti is made from a mixture of flour, eggs and salt. Yeah, it's spaghetti noodles.

I just soaked the noodles in salty water until they were nice and soft and then draped them around the main body until I was happy with the shape. For some of the noodles I found things to rest them on so they'd be curved once they dried. After that, the noodles are nice and hard and don't move at all. Unfortunately you have to be very careful as the noodles are very brittle and break easily.

The background is gold fabric draped over foamboard which has a floral wire armature stuck through it and the FSM is held up on the end of that.

The whole mess is sitting on a Lego stand, which I use because it's easy to raise and lower it while keeping the base stable.
