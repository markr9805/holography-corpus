when i set up for transmission shooting i use a black painted metal plate

set in my film holder with spray photo mount glue applied to the surface,(if its to sticky at first i use my palm as a blotter ,to lessen the stickyness)

then apply my test film with a small ammount of pressure it easly sticks and i can shoot within a few min. of settle time ,and when done qive a quick pull and off it comes with no residue. in the old days we had anti-hilation (sorry spelling) film ,which had a darkend back and would block any contact or reflections on the back.... i still have a large roll 35mm 10 e 75AH i use for rainbow work.
