The dates for the 2026 International Symposium on Display Holography at Lake Forest College have been confirmed to be Monday, June 22nd through Friday June 27th. Everyone who registers gets a 15 minute time slot to make their presentation in a classroom or to describe their works in a Salon Talk in the Pop-Up Hologram Gallery. If you don’t have a prepared talk to give in your slot, you can ask questions of all the other enthusiasts in the audience who should be able to solve your quest for knowledge, as the goal of Symposia like this is the open flow of knowledge. Which can happen not only during the scheduled sessions, but at mealtimes, as three meals a day plus a Happy Hour are included in the conference fee!
