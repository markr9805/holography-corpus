I'm pretty sure it can be used for holography, but the parameters will be very different. If it can be prehardened by some sort of polymerisation process, the same process should be capable of holographic cross-linking. Whether the resolution will be the same is another question.

I think they may have taken it off their site because a lot of holographers must have asked for details on making holography with this stuff. However, "a lot" was probably too small a market for them to devote any resources to explore the holographic aspect. This was the problem with Agfa, from what I've heard - the market simply wasn't worth it anymore for the 8E series. Besides which, I suspect Norland may more chemically inclined than being Optical Physicists. Clearly they intend their market for standard film bases.

As someone (John?) suggested in the old forum, get hold of some and try it!
