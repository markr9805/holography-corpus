wler Sun Nov 28, 2004 9:59 am #2
> eric343: ...Therefore, can I let it warm up on 'idle,' and then increase it to full power when it comes time to do the exposure? If I just run it on idle for the exposure, then the exposure time gets into the half-hour to full-hour range (!!!).

The thing needs to be in thermal equilibrium, otherwise you'll have

lots of mode jumps. Typically this takes at least 1/2h or so,

from cold start. If it is already warm from idling, it may take

much less time. So try to tune down the power as little as possible,

so that the temperature difference to full power is as small as

possible.

Best thing would be to build an interferometer with large path

difference, and just find out how long it takes until the fringe

pattern does not jump any more.

What laser is it BTW ? 40mW sounds like an air-cooled one.
