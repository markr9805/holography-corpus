Ed Wesly Wed May 11, 2011 11:31 am #4
I have seen and used Kodak SO-173 and Kodak SO-253 holographic film in 35 mm perforated stock. Agfa did make Holotest 10E75 in 35 mm unperforated stock for Laser TEchnology Incorporated's liquid gate cameras for real time interferometry.

Don't ever recall seeing holo film from anybody in the 120 or 2 1/4" format. I have been shooting holograms on mid-80's vintage 10E75 AH 70 mm perforated stock and 70's vintage Scientia 8E56 AHI 70 mm non-perf stock. You might be able to scrounge up some from the surplus market. PM me c/o this forum for more info.
