lobaz Thu May 09, 2013 1:34 am #3
Hi,

I am in Pilsen, Czech Republic. I will have free public classes in September, but I can arrange a special one for you. Holographic materials: for beginners I recommend Harman HoloFX, [http://www.ilfordphoto.com/holofx/holofx.asp](http://www.ilfordphoto.com/holofx/holofx.asp). They can be processed with JD-4 developer and bleach. They do not expire as fast as Slavich PFG-03M (that are included in e.g. Integraf holographic kit) and they dry much faster. Other silver halide materials I know are by Colour Holographic and Ultimate Holography. Colour Holographic plates require some treatment before use (e.g. triethanolamine bath), co they are not so convenient. I do not have experience with Ultimate ones (I am going to test them soon); they are more expensive than Slavich and Harman and I think it is better to start with something cheaper.

Holographic studios: what type of holographic studio? Artistic, security, research, ...?

Petr
