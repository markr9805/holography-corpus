From Jonothan Ross:

The Royal Photographic Society Holography Group regrets to announce that,

following the closure of The Shearwater Foundation who were providing the

finance, the Conference scheduled to take place at the Royal College of Art,

London, on April 30 2005 has been cancelled.

We are planning a programme of individual talks which will take place at the

Camera Club later this year and will inform you of the speakers and dates

when these are confirmed.
