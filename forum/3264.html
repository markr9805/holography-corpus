I don't believe the Kiss I was ever produced but the Kiss II was in production. It is a wonderful hologram that got me interested in holography back in the 80s. Loyd Cross is no longer making holograms and his company the Multiplex Corporation is no longer producing holograms as well. The master is owned by one of the hologram distributors in Canada and they can make new versions of the Kiss II hologram with a new display. If I remember corectly they quoted me about $2000 for one. I bid on one on ebay and had to stop at $500. If you ever sell it please contact me as I would be interested (if I had the money at the time).

Ed Wesly Thu Nov 17, 2005 3:49 pm #4
The Rita holograms were produced by Craig Newswanger in the late '80's/early 90's. He could probably be reached via Zebra Imaging.

They are image planed reflection stereograms, with a unique twist to the masters. (I have seen a few of them in the old CFC warehouse.) The slits are arranged radially on the H1, which is held parallel to the H2 when transferring. Both the master's replay beam and the copy's reference beam are normal to the holographic plates.

There were several other images produced other than the Rita, I think Spiderman or Superman, and I remember seeing an homage to Buckminister Fuller, "Bucky's Pyramid" at the 1991 ISDH @ LFC, which was computer generated imagery of a geodesic pyramid, I think.

I also seem to think that this was discussed in a previous thread here, like Princess Leia, which was what Craig was trying to emulate, plus find a use for a big mirror that came out of the Applied Holographics Holocopier.
