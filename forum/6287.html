I saw this in your project list. What does this really mean? Can you please exlain this to me.

![Image](http://img87.imageshack.us/img87/9521/edbd4.jpg)
Ed Wesly Fri Jun 01, 2007 7:24 pm #2
This is a technique for photographing the image of a laser transmission hologram without using any lenses. You may have tried the trick of sending an undiverged beam through a classical laser transmission hologram to project the real image on to a wall. This time the image is being projected onto a Polaroid film pack, which is fixtured outside of its camera body.

When the film pack is inserted into these types of cameras (SX-70, 600, Spectra System, but not the pack type) it spits out the cardboard dark slide, or in this case, the top piece of film, which you have just exposed to the real image of your hologram. So the film develops in front of your eyes to a flat image of your previously holographed object.

Hope this explains it.
