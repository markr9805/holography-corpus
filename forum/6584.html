Ok, not sure how "secure" you want these but a simple and cheap way of making some cards would be as follows. With a small family owned company, I assume you have a low number of employees. So unless you want to spend hundreds per person, here may be an alternative.

Get some holographic foil, the kind you see as wrapping paper with some adhesive backing. Cut a large size that can fit through a laser printer. Print you logo or name or whatever you wish right on the holographic foil. Use the adhesive to stick the foil to some plastic card stock on both sides. Then laminate the whole think in some clear laminating material, heat sealed looks most professional.

Or get that same holo stock and print your name on a small square of it. Then cut out that square and stick it on the security card. Then laminate.

If that will not do it, it would be possible but more ellaborate to use a laser to cut the name of you company out of that same holographic foil. Remove adhesive backing and stick the "letters" to the white plastic card stock. Laminate in clear stock as above.

Just some economical suggestions. Good luck.
