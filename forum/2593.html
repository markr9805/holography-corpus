Dinesh Fri Jul 02, 2004 9:17 pm #3
We do mostly H1 on Silver (Slavich VRP-M) and H2 on DCG. We've done H2 on Silver as well, but I like DCG because you don't need a light to reconstruct. Well, you do, but they show up fine in standard room lights.

We've done achromats and rainbows on Silver. Colin and I did a rainbow on DCG. But mostly it's H1 on Silver and H2 on DCG.
tpark Sat Jul 03, 2004 2:01 am #4
I make mostly transmission holograms on PFG-03M, although I've made a fairly large number of other types. I've done a fair number of reflection holograms, and some copying (focused image). I'm really pretty much in the learning phase of holography, so I haven't tried anything too elaborate yet.
MichaelH Sat Jul 03, 2004 2:03 am #5
I see a difference between "most popular" and the ones I make most often so I'll answer both questions.

The most popular (IE, ones I like the most) are transmission. Can't beat the depth and speckly goodness.

The ones I make most frequently are H2 reflections on silver from silver transmission masters.
