Dinesh Wed Jun 30, 2004 12:44 pm #3
You could try the MSDS for the particular chemical. Most of them are available on-line. Here, for instance, is the MSDS for Silver Nitrate: [http://www.jtbaker.com/msds/englishhtml/s2282.htm](http://www.jtbaker.com/msds/englishhtml/s2282.htm)

You'll notice that section 10, Stability and Reactivity, tells you that Silver Nitrate is stable. Of course, that means under normal room conditions (STP). You may need to read most MSDS with a large grain of salt. They scare the blazes out of you if you take them too seriously.
