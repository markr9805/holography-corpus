Martin Thu Feb 15, 2007 9:01 am #5
> glajciorz: So why don't we use higher gelatin concentration (for example 10-15%) and higher silver concentration (for example 6-7%). Where is the catch ?

Grain growth. It's difficult to form ultra-fine grains at high gelatin/silver concentration.

> Did it work (for holography) ? What was the problem ?What about spectral sensitization - won't pinacyanol do it for us ?

The problem is, spectral sensitization without reduction/sulfur/gold sensitization provides speed levels in the DCG range only. Dye concentration is very crucial - too little or too much dye may actually desensitize the emulsion.

> Martin, do you have detailed informations about this method ?Or about double-jet method ?

Regarding the freezing/thawing method, if you read German there's is a German patent, DE 1103760, (Schlossner, Adox Fotowerke, Verfahren zur Herstellung wasserarmer fotografischer Halogensilberemulsionen), which seems to have greatly inspired Kirilov/Vasilyeva/Zelikman (that is, the “Russian method”).
