Here’s my latest:

![Image](http://home.comcast.net/~stampydoo/seahorses_sm.jpg)

I’ve decided to try to wean myself away from just making holograms of commercially available sculptures. Perhaps this marks the end of my ‘Disney’ period!? Anyway, it occured to me that my holograms aren’t really originals anymore then photographs of the Mona Lisa. Therefore I have tried to come up with some originality in this shot.

The sand sea floor is made of oven-bake polymer clay over a 5 inch bathroom tile, sculpted to have the wavy texture. Craft sand was glued onto the clay before baking using liquid clay as an adhesive. The small shells are just artfully arranged on the bottom. The seahorses are supported by #14 wires which are brought out the back behind them. The wires are painted flat black to hide them. Additionally, the object beams are blocked in the areas of the wires to further hide them.

H1 is on BB-640. H2 is BB-640 with 3% TEA presoak for color control. Processing is JD-4 developer and Fe EDTA bleach. 35 mW HeNe laser.

Also, I found a source for nice matte-black finished frames in the 4x5" size - A.C.Moore. ('Burnes of Boston' is brand name).
