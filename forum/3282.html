Hello everyone. My name's Tom and I'm in San Diego.

Way back when (late 80s, early 90s), I was making holograms with a nice 15mw HeNe and good old Agfa 8E75. Then college and work intruded, and here we are, 15 years later. Laser prices have dropped dramatically, the technology I was using is hopelessly obsolete, I have more disposable income to spend on frivilous hobbies, and I have absolutely no idea of the state of the industry.

I'm spending my copious free time reading your threads, and I'm glad to see that the laser and holography community is still going strong.

I'll be spending plenty of time here and will contribute where I can, but for a while, hopefully you can bring me up to speed.

Back in The Day, Meridith Instruments and MWK were the best places to get lasers and optics cheaply. Now with Ebay, they seem to have gone the way of the dodo (I see them around, but they're nowhere near as popular as they used to be, nor do they have the wide range of stock) - what are the best places for great deals on surplus optics? Film? Chemicals? (etc)

Also, from reading a number of posts here, it seems that most people are still using <50mw red sources. Any pointers on using green sources? I just picked up an ILT 750mw (well, maybe - I have yet to measure it with a calibrated meter) argon, and am about to pick up a 3W Spectra Physics DPSS unit (and a 50W CO2 unit - but not for holography!), and am unsure as to whether I should attempt to use these for holography, or just enjoy the light show and pick up a ~50mw HeNe head.

In any case, I'm glad to be here, and expect to learn plenty. I also make beer. If there's anyone in the San Diego area who'd like to hitch up for a beverage-of-choice on me and chat about your setup, let me know.

Regards,

Tom
