Thank you. It's funny how some subjects get more response than others. I think Star Wars has completely invaded the world consciousness.

This was an H2. The master was made with two object beams that were fully diffused using a frosted halogen light cover in each beam. There was slightly greater illumination from the left side. The light saber center was approx 4" from the master plate and when making the H2 the rear of the black exit-cap was intersecting the H2 plane.

The H2 film was pre-treated with 5% TEA, developed with JD2 and bleached with EDTA and post treated with 1% ascorbic acid.

The master was processed the exact same way except that the TEA was washed out before exposure.
