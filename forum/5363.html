dave battin Sat Feb 04, 2006 5:39 pm #1
while attempting to align three laser beams thru a 25 micron hole i thought you might be interested and see how ill do it .......

im really setting up to do a few test shots of quasicolor one beam holograms.

all my lasers are set on a side table including folding miror,two flip mirrors

an aperture stop and spatial filter' my isolated table holds object/filmholder and collmating mirror.

the flip mirror pictured works quite well, by using a magnet as a stop, it really locks in to is original position,

once aligned properly i can easly flip back and forth and switch my spatially filtered laser light to red or blue (and soon green)...........

![Image](http://members.aol.com/holoart/flip4.jpg)

the filp mirror started out as a 4 inch strap hinge!

ill be making two more with some slight modifications (ill post pict. when done)

![Image](http://members.aol.com/holoart/flip2.jpg)

sorry Holodisc if im being a hack again! LOL

its much easer when "zeroing in" on final alignment to only tune the spatial filter partially to you see the "airy discs" this allows you a larger margin of error....

![Image](http://members.aol.com/holoart/disk1.jpg)
