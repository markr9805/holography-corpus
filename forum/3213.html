Only a year and a half late, here's my entry to the Porcelain Cat Group:

![Image](http://home.comcast.net/~stampydoo/japanese_cat_2.jpg)

The subject is a genuine porcelain cat, purchased from Japan (in Epcot!). It came complete with polarized gold coating!

This is a Denisyuk hologram on BB-640 (2.5 x 2.5 plate). 11 mw Hene, 6 second exposure, JD-4 processing with no post-treatment. Illumination is from hydrogen fission heated helium incandescent source at a distance of 93,000,000 miles. (give or take).

This is why I like BB!

Tony Sun Mar 13, 2005 12:05 pm #9
The original reference angle was less than Brewster. I started at Brewster and then cheated in a bit to get the object more centered in the plate, so I'd guess it was around 45 degrees. Woodgrain was not a problem.

The illumination angle on replay was not measured, but it was certainly greater than the 45 degrees used for exposure.

The BB-640 when processed for no shrinkage will replay anywhere from the original deep red at the nominal angle to a yellow-green at very high illumination angles (near tangent to the plate).
