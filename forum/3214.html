Here's my first attempt at an H2 on BB:

![Image](http://home.comcast.net/~stampydoo/bunny_2.jpg)

This is an H2 copy on BB-640. It was made from an H1 original also made on BB-640. 2.5 inch square plates. 11 mw Laser, 3 minute exposure for the H2. No TEA, JD-4 processing, no post treatment. Illuminated by sunlight. Very bright and low noise (photo doesn't do it justice).
