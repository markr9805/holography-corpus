Have been looking at a few old posts and found an interesting topic on an Air Knife Squeegee.

From what I've read, I gather it's kind of like a compressed air blast but coming out from a slit. Thus blasting off water from plates during the post TEA bath squeegee or final squeegee procedure.

Having easy access to compressed air source I thought I might give this a try. Has anyone tried using compressed air to squeegee a plate or film with good results? Any help would be most appricated.
Ed Wesly Thu Feb 03, 2005 1:23 pm #2
I had heard, but had never seen the device, that Edwina Orr of Richmond Holographics used something like what you describe, an "air knife". They had it mounted above a drawer so that the plate could be moved underneath it and the solution blown off. They were doing 50 by 60 cm duo color plates in the '90's, and they were very clean.

Alternatively, I have seen, and it was reported in the now defunct L.A.S.E.R. News, that Greg Cherry and Nancy Gorglione were spin drying their TEA'ed plates. It used a monster 208 V 3 phase motor and the plate was held in a chuck directly mounted to the motor. Again, they exhibited very clean, streak-free results.

I hate squeegees, too!
