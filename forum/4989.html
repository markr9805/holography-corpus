[quote:e92612a1e3="Bill"]whats the next best thing? [/quote:e92612a1e3]

Depends on the application.

[quote:e92612a1e3="Bill"] I would imagine one of the first criteria to look at in a recording medium for this would be one where the refraction index of the emulsion closely matches that of its substrate and to just do liquid matching for the rest.[/quote:e92612a1e3]

Again, depends on the application. The first criteria for a medium is usually it's efficency and it's sensitivity. For display, usually you want high sensitivity and relatively low sensitivity since you need pretty broad bandwidths which, in turn, lowers the de. The eye can pretty much make out less than 30% under reasonable lighting conditions. For technical work, the bandwidth determines the de necessary. A narrow band notch filter, for instance, needs a high de and low bandwidth whereas an optic to launch into a WDM or DWDM fiber may need a broader bandwidth but can accomodate a lower de.
