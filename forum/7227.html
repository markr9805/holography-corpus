Danny Bee Thu Mar 20, 2008 2:42 pm #2
Hi Tony

Yes there is a point that the hologram gets brighter a bit but also as it gets thicker the bandwidth narrow and gets dim because of its narrowness (unless the purpose is a master it will look dim but very bright in replay laser light).

It also depends what light source you use. If 532 is use the green light will go through the yellow ok or lower the amount of chrome a little. If blue us used there is a point where you will have to use a split beam setup because a lack of light going through the plate.

Think of it like a stack of mirrors, as the thicknes incresses their is more mirrors refecting the light back... but as the stack incresses the filtering of light gets stronger and bandwidth gets narrow
