I just spent a week in New York City, and was able to visit many of the galleries in Chelsea. One, Jim Kempner Fine Art, had a series of four holograms by Chuck Close. I was only familiar with his "photorealistic" portraiture & didn't know he was working in holography. According to the price sheet, each group of four was going for about US$100,000 (in a series of 25 groups).

Anyway, here's the gallery's website. [http://www.jimkempnerfineart.com/](http://www.jimkempnerfineart.com/) There were many pieces to see there, including a very realistic sculupture of a giant swimmer in the entryway (and no lack of pretentious-sounding staff...).
