JohnFP Tue Dec 07, 2004 5:06 pm #2
Even though you have twice as much developer (A + B), the bleach should still outlast your developer. When I was using Potassium Dichromate bleach I would reuse it over and over again. It usually outlasted the developer. Make sure your rinse between devoper and bleach is running water and of addequate time. When I was doing 4 x 5 plates I would make up the bleach and divide it into 2 - 500ml bottles. I only needed 500 ml in the tray at a time. Then when one bottle was exausted, many holograms later, I would use the second 500ml bottle.

Are you discarding the bleach with the developer?

Sorry if this doesn't answer your question directly.

JohnFP Wed Dec 08, 2004 11:57 am #4
From what I understand the mixed A and B have a shelf life of 8 hours but every time you develop a hologram in it, it changes it's characteristics and you will have to alter your developing times and procedures. So in my opinion, anytime you have a two part developer, mix just enough A and B to develop a single hologram then discard. If you use a tray that is just slightly larger then the hologram (hard plastic sandwich containers are good) then minimal liquid is needed. I think I used to used 50ml A and 50ml B for a 4 x 5 glass holographic plate which yielded enough for 20 plates I could develop.

For the bleach, as I stated I used 500ml and would use it until it stopped bleaching in under 2 minutes. When done with the bleach for a single session I would simply but it back in a 500ml container and use it again the next time. Most of the time the bleach would last a good 40 plates. I would NOT recommend trying to use 100ml of the bleach and then put it back into the other 400 or 900ml. I would use 500ml or 1 liter all at once.
