Joe Farina Fri Jul 13, 2007 11:10 am #6
> vamsi_talla: At least I do have someone listening to me on this forum.

I would suggest that you take a look at some books which cover the subject of color holography, such as Lasers and Holography (an excellent book by two Indian authors, Mehta and Rampal), or even better, Practical Holography, second edition, by Saxby. (There is a newer third edition of the Saxby book, but I like the second edition better.)

If you are using PFG-03C there is information on the Slavich website regarding processing. I would also take a look at some of the more recent papers by Bjelkhagen (who has a lot of experience with PFG-03C).
