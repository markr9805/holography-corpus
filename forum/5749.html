Is it just me or have we lost the ability to make really cool, holograms?

I remember over ten years ago going into a store and seeing for the first time, a hologram. It was a huge, 2 foot by 3 foot hologram of a pitchfork coming out of the hologram right into your face.

I also remember seeing one of a window that looked out over a boat dock. There was also a telescope looking out the window and the eyepiece came out of the hologram. If you looked through the eyepiece of the telescope you could see a woman sunbathing on one of the boats.

Another was of an old doorknob and keyhole. If you looked closely through the keyhole you could see, as I remember it, a woman in a bathtub.

The telescope one seemed to be done in daylight. You could see the ocean, boats, cars, people. The keyhole one also seemed to be done in daylight.

Now don't get me wrong, but the pics of holograms that I have seen on the web made by people like you and me don't even come close. And this was over ten years ago. How were they doing these kind of holos that we can't do ten years later? How did they seem to capture real life, as it happened, in the daytime, through a telescope, of a boat on water, and still have a very clear image?

Like I said , Is it just me, or have we lost the ability to make really cool holograms?
