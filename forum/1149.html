Dinesh Thu Jun 17, 2004 6:08 pm #4
There are two seperate problems here. The first is the film sensitivity and the second is the laser stability.

Silver Halide film is has no sensitivity to any color but deep blue. In order to make it 'see' any other color, dyes have to be added. The dye 'sees' the appropriate color and passes it on to the Silver Halide which then reacts. The sensitivity of the material, the amount of light needed to create an image, now depends on the sensitivity of the dye (among other things). To make a particular film sensitive to a particular color, a specific dye is added. In the case of 03M a 'red' dye is added - a dye that abrorbs red and transmits everything else. If you hit this with green or blue neither the dye nor the Silver Halide will see it. The exact shade of red that the dye 'sees' is the dependant on the dye chosen. In the case of 03M, the dye chosen will 'see' 633nm and 650nm.

To make film sensitive to all colors, you add dyes for all three primaries - red, green and blue. Now the film 'sees' all colours because any color is a combination of red, green or blue. Yellow, for instance, will be 'seen' by the green and blue dyes. Such a film is known as 'panchromatic' or just 'pan'. PFG-03C is pan and so it'll see all colors. In particular, it'll see green.

Tommy Fri Jun 18, 2004 3:45 pm #8
> MarkCavin: Tommy wrote:while its a bit less picky than the argon, its nowhere near as forgiving as the hene.What do you mean?

The goal is to have as little fringe motion as possible, with the rule of thumb being 1/10 of a wavelength or so at most. A hene laser is 634 nm, so motion of 63 nm is "allowed". The Coherent 315M is 532nm, so now the max acceptable motion is 53 nm, 10nm less than the hene. The argon laser is blue, I don't know the exact line but its around 480 nm, another 5 nm less permitted motion.

So as the wavelength shortens, the vibration isolation becomes harder. A setup which was stable enough for hene (+- 63nm) may not be good enough for blue (+-48nm), but you might be able to get away with green (+-53 nm)
