Colin Kaminski Fri Mar 07, 2008 4:03 pm #2
The laser has to have some very specific qualities. Some laser pointers have them and some don't. My experience from about 5 years ago when I did some testing in different laser pointers was that if you expanded the beam with a lens and it looked uniform without black rings or patterns then it would work.

It is only the specially designed green laser that can form a hologram.

This link will give you some more information:

[http://www.holowiki.com/index.php/Laser](http://www.holowiki.com/index.php/Laser)
