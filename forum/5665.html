alackofcolor Fri Jun 30, 2006 5:46 am #1
I made this today, it's by far the brightest hologram I've ever made, the picture doesn't do it justice. Instead of mounting the plate vertically like usual with the object behind it, I clamped it flat to the table and just laid the plate on top of the object, supporting it a little with screws in the table at the four corners. For some reason this gave much brighter and deeper results than with the previous setup even though the angle of incidence, power, exposure time, development time was all the same.

![Image](http://img.photobucket.com/albums/v144/ktulu_js/DSCF1428.jpg)

This was made with a 10 mW HeNe laser, PFG-03C plate, and developed with a formaldehyde harderner (6 minutes), JD-2 developer (3 minutes), Amidol Bleach (5 minutes), and Photoflo. Exposure time was 166 seconds and settle time was about 15 minutes.
