I don't remember ever using a density meter. We monitored carefully the temperature, ph, regulated DC voltage, kept the bath moving and insured the sack of nickle plugs did not get depleted. You want a hard durable shim for the mother and a more flexible shim for the child shims. Controlling and modifying the voltage achieved this.
