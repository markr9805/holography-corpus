floh Thu May 17, 2007 1:21 pm #8
For a beginner it will be the best to get a small HeNe. Plug-in and be happy.

Power around 5-10mW is ok, TEM00 is required. Linear polarization is good but not really neccessary. I have done my first steps without it and it works.

Some holographers use the small HeNe from the philips video disc player that was popular in the 1980's.
