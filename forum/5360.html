Uli Wed Feb 01, 2006 2:30 pm #2
> holoandy: 50 and 30 magnification. I intend to use this with my coherent 532 DPSS 50 mW. Can someonetell me the necessary pinhole to begin with and maybe where to get some cheep pinholes with 15 mm holder.

For the 50x you can use a 5 or 10 micron, and probably the 15 one will also work (it does for the 40x). For the 30x I would suggest a 15 or a 20 micron. I have looked in the Edmund catalogue for mounts, but apparently they only do 9.5 mm mounted pinholes. You probably can adapt the bigger mount into the small housing (the opposite would be the difficult thing to do), just by gluing both mounts (bluetack is very useful for this).
