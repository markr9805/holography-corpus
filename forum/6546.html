Tom B. Fri Nov 16, 2007 4:29 am #2
I have usually seen "modulation" used in reference to the degree of variation of refractive index induced by bright and dark fringes recorded in a holographic medium. The greater the refractive index modulation, the higher the diffraction efficiency.
