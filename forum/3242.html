I have to say I have been worried about trying to tune the reflection to be used as a master to the recording wavelength and I am sorry I didn't try them earlier. I will post photos but found out tonight that even a reflection that is broadband or replays primarily in a differnt color is fine for image planing an H2 reflection from and H1 reflection. Sorry, but I am really excited. I don't see any burn in problems and I am getting really bright copies from gold, white, green almost any color masters. I even made on e of the "lime green" catd and used 488nm to shoot. This is very promising and I think they are more forgiveness then led to believe.

I actually have reflection holograms from reflection masters that are in front of the plate. I love it. Don't be dicouraged, give it a tryl you will love it.

I also tried a split beam, used for additional lighting to test the table and the secondary lighting came out fine with a 2 minute exposure. I think the table is very good.
