Tom B. Sat Mar 29, 2008 4:34 am #4
Interesting! My TEA has always come from Photographer's Formulary and I noticed that my milky haze was cured by carefully damp-wiping and cleaning the non-emulsion side of the plate before exposure. Apparently the dried TEA solution left a kind of oily looking residue on the glass side of the plate that was not at all obvious until it became part of a hologram. The scum is most easily viewed by reflection from a distant safelight. It could be impurities, or more likely the TEA itself is responsible for this - I mean when you remove water from a syrupy liquid film it is unlikely to dry optically clear. If you have found a variety of TEA that dries cleanly without wiping and polishing, that would be great!
