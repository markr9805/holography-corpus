Hans Mon Jan 29, 2007 8:21 am #2
With mold coating I use about 4 ml for a 4x5 inch plate as some liquid always spills off the side of the plate. So, that's about 25 plates out of 100ml. Curtain coating uses even more.

So, that comes to 4 USD per plate, excluding the cost of the glass. Plus shipping and handling. Sorry, but for me that is too expensive. But I guess for people who are not able to order the chemicals separately...
Hans Mon Jan 29, 2007 8:42 am #3
I just noticed that I made a mistake on the quantity that I use in my Wiki article. I stated 2 ml for a 10x15cm plate. That is a bit on the low side for mold coating, but doable with a meyer bar coating machine. Theoretically one needs: 10x15x0.006ml=0.9ml for a mold coating on a 10x15 plate with a tape thickness of 60um. But because of spillage you will need several times that amount. I will adjust the wiki when I come home from work.
Hans Mon Jan 29, 2007 8:48 am #4
Daniel made a small mistake on his web site with his calculations. He calculated the amount of stock MBDCG needed for a 5um wet layer of MBDCG. In that case you get 100/(10x13x0.0005)=1550 plates for a 4x5 inch plate size at 5um. But stock MBDG is loaded with water and thus more than 10 times the volume of dry MBDCG. Thus in order to coat that many plates with a 5um dry layer, you will need at least 1000ml of stock MBDCG and not spill any during coating.

Hans Wed Jan 31, 2007 6:09 pm #19
Actually, if you make the MBDCG with Boric Acid instead of with Acetic Acid and sell the whole mixture pre-mixed, the PH indicator will not be so critical anymore. The PH stays much more stable in this solution. The advantage of selling pre-mixed is that the user won't need a fume hood because of much lower concentrations of TMG and the absence of handling of Acetic Acid.
