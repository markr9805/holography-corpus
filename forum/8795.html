jrburns47 Fri Apr 07, 2017 3:47 pm #2
Whatever it was, it was pretty short, 85 pages, done after 1985. They look like they could be an instructors class notes and pretty basic although gives leads to following up on more sophisticated work. Doesn't match anything in my library. You could probably do as well or better by picking up a used copy of any of Graham Saxby's books published from 1988 onward, variously called "Practical Holography" or "Manual of Practical Holography". Edition four which came out last year is excellent.

I suspect, therefore, Professor Chapman taught holography at Simon Fraser School of Engineering as a special topic, and these are his notes.
