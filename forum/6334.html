floh Sat Jul 07, 2007 3:51 pm #2
without measuring or experiments we cannot say how long your shot should be. From a friend who use the red sensitive BB plates I know you will get a result even if the exposure is bad. So your first shot should be a hologram.

What you can do is wasting just one plate that lies on a black paper and another one is on top giving only a small slit of the plate. then shot one second move the top paper shot one second more and so on. After developping you should be able to see which one is the best. Keep in mind the object light which is nearly absent in this experiment would also do exposure so take the second or third best time from experiment.

Colin Kaminski Tue Jul 10, 2007 1:26 pm #5
I like to put the spatial filter farther away and pass the beam through a iris to help block stray light. This can be quite a problem in laser diodes where the reflection can cause a mode hop when the shutter opens.
