I have made holograms of paper money by laminating the money to a sheet of 2mm thick glass with ordinary glue and a laminator. Just be sure to put your glass+money between to sheets of paper before inserting it into the laminator or you will end up with a lot of glue inside the laminator.
Joe Farina Mon Jul 07, 2008 6:42 pm #3
Welcome back, Tony.

Long exposures will likely cause a problem when trying to make a hologram of paper. My guess is that ambient humidity is moving in and out of the paper, causing minute amounts of warpage here and there. Perhaps temperature variations also play a role.

I would suggest placing the paper between two sheets of glass, and making sure the edges are well-sealed with adhesive, so that moisture can't get in or out. (Basically make an O-ring seal like some people do for DCG holograms.) Epoxy might work well for this purpose, and it doesn't need to touch the paper. I would try for an O-ring of about one-half to one inch in final "depth" along the outer edges (between the glass sheets). Then I would let the whole thing stabilize for a few days.

Not sure if that would work, but that's how I would try.

I've done this before! I used a spray adhesive on the back, and mounted it to a piece of glass. Doing it that way avoids any glossy effects.

Jeffrey Weil Wed Jul 09, 2008 5:49 pm #7
Hello Everyone,

I don't want to get you worried or poop on your party, I"m not saying this will lead to problems its just an interesting fact. Even though this is probably going to be on glass and most likely will have only one side of the bill recorded........technically this is a counterfeit.

The law says that any same size reproduction of a bill in current circulation is a counterfeit. To be legal it has to be smaller than 50 percent of the original size or more than 150 percent.

Long ago there was a DCG art piece called 1 million dollars. Its was an array of a bunch of individual holograms of 10,000 dollar notes, all adding up to one million. There were also lots of the single holos of the 10,000 bill around, not the big array just a single.

The Secret Service went around the country and collected as many as they could find under the federal counterfeiting laws. I don't think anyone was arrested or anything like that but the holograms were taken away.

Like I said, your not going to get arrested or in any real trouble but don't do something like show your cool hologram off to your bank manager or anything like that. Some people are, by law, required to notify the Secret Service of stuff like that, even if it is silly as its a piece of glass and no one is going to accept it. And of course if your recording anything other than a 10,000 dollar note the hologram is worth more than the bill anyway. Its still a counterfeit.
