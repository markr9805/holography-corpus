Dinesh Sun Feb 13, 2005 6:20 pm #3
The specs are very similar to PFG-01. The sensitivity quoted (80-100 microJ/sq cm) is what Slavich quotes, but I've found in practice that it's more like 150 - 200 microJ/sq cm. I'm not sure where the 40% efficency comes from. I assume these were the results of tests. I know I've achieved closer to 70% using PFG-01 for an H1 and roughly 50 - 60% for an image-planed H2.

They say that the same processing as Agfa will work for this film. If it is PFG-01, I've also found that processing Slavich is very different from processing Agfa. The pyro process, which gives great results for Holotest, gave so-so to not-so-good results with Slavich. Also, alcohol drying seems detrimental to Slavich, I've had images disappear on me when drying Slavich in alcohol. Mostly my experience is with VRP. Having found that the pyro processing, or any reversal bleaching, of Slavich doesn't work well with VRP, I wasn't going to try it with PFG-01. Maybe PFG-01 is closer to Agfa in terms of processing.
