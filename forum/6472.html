This piece of film is 3,5x2,5cm, so it's rather small window

YAGna puts out 8-15mJ of 532nm, but SSY1 I'm driving SSY1 at very low power (6,7J; starts lasing at 4,8J).

At full power (15J) i would expect something like 50-55mJ output.

Lamp can be overdriven to 30J, but the original q-switch won't survive such a punishment.
