Hello everybody,

My last batch of S.H. home made plates was not very good.

I made 2 or 3 denisyuk holograms.

I use JD-4 as developer (2 parts).

Unfortunatly, I found that plates was not very sensitive, I got no density, even after 5 min. in developer.

I switch the light on and observed that the plates turns brown in developer.

I guessed it was colloïdal silver.

Some words with Martin (thanks Martin) gives confirmation about my guess.

It's certainly the gelatin who causes the colloidal silver formation.

Martin pointed a method from the Glafkides's book to check the gelatin.

Here is the method :

- Gelatin solution (10%) at 40°C : 5ml

- Ammonia D:880, with 3 parts of water : 0,5 ml

- Silver Nitrate (17g/L) : 1 ml

- Methylene Blue (1/5000) : 1ml

Expose for 5 to 10 minutes in the sun and observe :

- No decoloration : inert gelatin and contains thiosulfate

- decoloration : inert gelatin

- decoloration and formation of brown colloïdal silver : active gelatin

I made the test with culinar gelatin and photographic grade gelatin.

(I don't remember which one I used for my last batch)

Because there was no sun yesterday in Belgium, I exposed the solutions to halogen light for 3 hours.

Guess what !

The culinar gelatin seems beter than the photographic one.

With the culinar, I get a light decoloration (blue to light green) and just a very light colloïdal silver formation (even after a day in daylight).

But for the other one...all the solution turns brown (after a day in daylight)

I think I will retry one more time because I'm very surprised of the results.

Maybe someone knows an other method that the one exposed here ?
