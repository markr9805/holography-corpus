I think I have mentioned that a good source of front surface mirrors is overhead projectors. Well I wanted to get another one so I went to a government suplus warehouse yesterday and they had an overhead projector. It looked to be a pretty good one but I was real surprised that the mirror in it did not look to be front surface. As best I could tell from looking at the edge of the mirror it was rear coated. But they also has some microfilm viewers. This definetly had a nice size front surface mirror in it so I picked it up for $5.

So the tip is, whenever buying surplus supplies make sure you look closely at what it is you are buying.
