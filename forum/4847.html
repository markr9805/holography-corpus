I know this is not going to be a one liner!

If I have a dcg hologram that comes out with the nasty greenies, I reprocess starting with water wash and then a soak in warm/hot soapy water, then three ISO alch baths. It seems to help in making it more even in color (most times when I reprocess it shifts towards green).
