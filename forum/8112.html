This past Friday, the MIT museum opened a Winter holography exibit of about 6 or 8 holograms that are viewable from the sidewalk outside the museum. Included in the display is "hand in Jewel" which, as far as I know, has not been on display for a long time. I went to the opening ceremony Friday evening and all of us (about 30 people) trooped outside in the cold to see the "curtain" go up on the display. If you are in the Boston/Cambridge area, it's well worth a visit.
