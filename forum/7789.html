Light Magic

holograms and laser works

Rudie Berkhout

April 18, 2009 through May 30, 2009

open Saturdays 12:00PM - 5:00PM

and by appointment

:dance: Opening: April 18, 2009 from 4:00PM - 7:00PM

This retrospective exhibition showcases Rudie Berkhout's holograms and laser works to celebrate his life in art.

"My hope is to reach the subtler levels of perception, holding up mirrors for thoughts, that reflect the magic that surrounds us."

- Rudie Berkhout (1946 - 2008)

Center for the Holographic Arts

45-10 Court Square, Long Island City, NY 11101
