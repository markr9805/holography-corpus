Hi, I need to buy some film/plates and developer, so far I have had some success with both transmission and reflection using PFG-03M and JD-4. I see on Integraf’s site that for almost the same price, I’d get ten 4*5 inch films of PFG01.

- My questions are is the film much more difficult to work with than plates, how flimsy is it, will it tear?

- I think I’m quite practiced with the PFG-03M/JD-4, if I switch to something new, will I have to re-learn exposure times, developer times, drying etc…

- can he film be easily cut with scissors ?

Thanks, Rick
Colin Kaminski Sun Nov 21, 2004 6:22 pm #2
I will address a couple of the questions. Film can be cut with sissors but does not tear. PFG-03 makes better reflection holograms but is more difficult to work with. You will need to relearn all exposure and processing stuff.

Mounting film can be a pain and I will let those with more experience talk about it. I did 50 or so exposures with 2x2.5" squares and found them easy but messy to mount. I don't have very much experience with large sizes.
Dinesh Sun Nov 21, 2004 6:42 pm #3
I sandwich film between two 4x5 glass pieces and clamp them into my plate holder. This has two screws on either side which holds the glass tightly clamped across both edges with the film in between (I believe Colin posted a picture of this plateholder some time ago). My usual exposure is from 3 seconds to 15 seconds and I haven't noticed any motion problems. I don't use index matching fluid because I think it's messy and does not help that much, but mostly others do use index matching. This is really a personal preference. One thing I have noticed is that unless the film is exactly vertically/horizontally mounted there tends to be increased woodgrain. In my case this is not important because I use film just to test before going to glass as the final hologram.
