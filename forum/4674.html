Has anyone used a good Fresnel lens for a collimator for a simple single beam hologram? I have one from an overhead projector. It is very rigid and was used as an imaging lens in the projector. Pros and cons welcome.
Ed Wesly Thu Mar 31, 2005 12:05 pm #2
Run a spatially filtered beam through it and see what comes out. It won't look too pretty.

Because of the stepped rings there is no smooth transformation of the wavefront and the beam won't be completely collimated across the profile. See for instance "Seeing the Light" by Falk, Brill, and Stork, p.93, Figure 3.29.

Fresnel lenses are typically used as field lenses like in the overhead or lighthouses (their original application) to manage light sources and are rarely if ever used to form images.
