I thought I would comment about about a statement that was made in this article, concerning the maximum sensitivity of the human eye to the color green.

The article stated that the maximum sensitivity to green is at 540 nm.

On an interesting note, dye lasers also can create green at 540 nm.

Pulsed holograms, made with YAG lasers, output at 532 nm, which is a little off.

So dye lasers would make holograms that are more pleasing to the human eye, than YAG lasers.

Now if somebody could just do something about the coherence problem!
