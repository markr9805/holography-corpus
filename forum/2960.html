andy75180 Thu Dec 16, 2004 2:40 pm #1
Completed a reflection hologram last night on PFG-01 Plate. 5% TEA pre-soak and finished image turned out a nice green colour.

When emulsion had dried & hardened. I notice a few small brown spots near the centre of the plate.

Any idea what caused this? Are these spots what is called 'Print Out'?? I'm gave the plate a good wash between Developer & Bleach and also after Bleaching.
Dinesh Thu Dec 16, 2004 4:39 pm #2
It seems too localised to be printout. Printout occurs when unexoposed silver grains in the emulsion start to darken in white light. Unless there was a very localised set of grains that survived when all the other unexposed grains got washed out, printout doesn't occur as spots. I've noticed the brown spots when I clean out the processing area while the hologram is drying nearby. The developer tray splashes developer onto the plate. I solved this by drying the plate in another room. If this is what it is, try re-washing the plate. It could also be contamination in the processing trays. A dust mote or some such object(s) that settled on the plate while in the tray. Another possibility: These spots are where the plate got a lot of exposure and darkened much more than the rest of the plate. You may not have bleached it long enough to get rid of these spots and they may have been too small to notice in the darkroom.
Colin Kaminski Thu Dec 16, 2004 4:42 pm #3
I have made the mistakes Dinesh talks about. The other one I have made it to bleach the plate emulsion down so the brown spots did not come into contact with the Bleach. My trays have a little bump in the center.

You should be able to re-bleach with no problems.
