Hi BP,

Make one! Take a look at this plan:

[http://www.holographyforum.org/HoloWiki ... ilter_Plan](http://www.holographyforum.org/HoloWiki/index.php/John_Klayer%27s_Spatial_Filter_Plan)

Now remember, it does not have to be this nice, remember what you need, a lens and a small hole. You can make a pinhole by taking a stack of foil and pushing a pin part way in. Peal back the pieces of foil and look at each hole, They will all be different sizes. You can try them until you find the largest one that cleans the beam.

A simple spatial filter can be made for $10.
