Colin Kaminski Fri Nov 19, 2004 2:10 am #6
Wow, was this kit interesting. The workmanship is very good. The diode mount was a little finiky but was usable. The model was not very good. The film was a sandwich of glass-goop-plastic sheet. I guess the goop was likely a photopolymer. The surface texture was very irregular.

In 10 tries we were unable to get any interesting holograms. We did get 3 shots of about 3% efficiency when we switched to gratings. Paul at liti has measured the film at 90% efficiency so I am sure there is something wrong with our batch of film. John has some more and will be making further tests.
