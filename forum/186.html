I've gotten some good results with PFG-3c, and its definitely a pain.

For drying, hurrying it up works poorly... I've used a small fan (like a computer fan) blowing on the plate leaning nearly vertical on something. Just air-drying it takes an hour or two, with the fan maybe 1/2 hour.

For exposure, the datasheet says 3.5 milijoules/cm^2, and I've gotten good results there (with 532nm). I don't entirely trust my meter's calibration though. The film seems to come from the factory pre-fogged. I've gotten my best results using Jeff's anti-fog bath. Which means drying the plates twice... So my routine is usually anti-fog the plates one day, let them dry overnight, and then holograms the next day (or next weekend). At 8 millijoules/cm^2, the results were brighter, but my setup wasn't stable enough, so it was just a band of brightness in one corner of the plate.

For the rest of the chemistry, I used JD-4 developer (30 seconds), ferric EDTA bleach (till-clear and then 30 seconds more, typically 1:45 or so), then 15 seconds in a 2.5 percent solution of potassium dichromate to kill the dyes, and finally a rinse in photo-flo (but not enough to wash out all the dichromate in hopes of avoiding print-out).
