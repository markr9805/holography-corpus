in single beam hologram the (refrence Beam) is the light going through the plate to the object. if you use higher DiAm concentrations in 458nm the amout of light would be attinuate. if you use the light spilling around the plate as your main light and refect that back to object and use the light going through the plate as your fill light yes you could do that in 458nm.

in green the DiAm concentrations do not attinuate that light going thro the plate as much so higher DiAm concentrations would work to your advantage.

now in a 2 beam setup that rule doesnot aply (refections or tranmission) and you can have higher concentrations of DiAm in 458nm
