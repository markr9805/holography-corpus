inaki Wed Aug 09, 2006 6:14 am #5
Many thanks everyone for your really positive comments. I use plates made by Colour Holographics, the earlier stuff in the gallery on my website (upto about the year 2000, eg lalique glass, Beano) was made on material by HRT Gmbh, who then sold onto Colour.

Thanks Kaveh, Melles Griot sent me a small 532 DPSS laser to try out sometime back. I only had it for a few weeks and couldn’t resist trying out some (almost) true colour with red and the green. Results were good, but there were some very small fringes generated by the green component, not noticeable in the photo but there. It was so consistent I think it must have been something in the electronic circuitry. Again, I was using Colour Holographics plates for these (early Panchromatic plates). Great contrast.
