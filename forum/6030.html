I attended the show in San Jose last week, specifically looking for info. on SLM lasers. (There was a lot of stuff there, I didn't have much time). I spoke to 2 cheap chinese vendors - CNI Laser and Beijing Viasho Laser. Both offered 1 SLM 532nm product, both claiming 200mW.

CNI:

The person at the CNI booth was very happy to speak with me for some time. He put me in touch with Ms. Ding - the USA sales rep in China (cniding at vip.163.com). Lead time is 2-3weeks. They guarantee single mode operation and no mode hops over several hours. They claim >50m coherence length. They offer a 1 year warrantee.

I told them that my alternative was a second hand Coherent 315M from Mr Gizmos. They offered me a unit that produced 150mW, and very competitive pricing. I was asked not to disclose the price publicly, but holographers from this forum may contact me personally for the details.

Viasho:

The sales person here was much less happy to talk with me. He told me the 200mW was a peak, and 100mW average was to be expected. His English was very poor though, and comprehension was difficult. He quoted $2000 for the unit, and 4 weeks lead time.

Frank from Viasho did follow up with a generic sales email (wzc at viasho.com).

other stuff:

I spoke to Coherent - the 315M is about $10-12k new.

There were many vendors offering DPSS crystal kits, and TECs. I bought a pair of 532nm goggles for $60. (They look identical to the ones Wicked Lasers sells for $49). I found a Taiwanese vendor who sold 30mW 532nm modules for $50 I think. (perhaps good for lighting holos?).
