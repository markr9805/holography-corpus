lucaslab Wed Mar 02, 2005 9:12 pm #5
I have bought this kit and I'm very impressed.

It contains:

2 lenses for the laser beam,

2 mirrors,

1 splitter,

1 curved mirror for making cylindrical holograms,

films,

a very good manual,

several other items for helping making holograms.

Also the prize is very affordable for what it includes.

I purchased it because it was the only kit that you could make cylindrical holograms , but the other items proved very useful even for holograms in films or plates as we know them.

Especially the manual has all the technices that are available for reflection or transmission holograms.

I believe that your buy of the kit worths every cent that you spend.
BobH Wed Mar 09, 2005 1:42 am #6
I received the kit today, and I'm glad to see that the box is in great condition. The manual is based on T. Jeong's "A Study Guide on Holography" (a draft of which is also in my collection) and appears to be very complete. The transmission hologram included with the kit is a very noisy image of a human skull (what else ), and the beamsplitter is missing. The jar for making cylindrical holograms is plastic (wasn't it originally glass?). Despite these things, I'm very happy to have the kit and think it would have been easily worth the money for someone starting out as a hobbiest. Especially if they had some experience developing photographs before they tried holography.

If anyone has an original beamsplitter that came with the kit, I'd be happy to trade a hologram or some other optic for it. I'd like to have the kit complete for my collection, and promise you'll be happy with the trade!
