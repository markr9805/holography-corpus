Joe Farina Mon Oct 02, 2006 10:51 am #1
When I was making comparisons between diode and HeNe lasers, it seemed that diodes (when stable) produced interferometer fringes which drifted less than HeNe. It also seemed that the holograms I made with the diode were brighter. Has anyone else noticed this?
Tony Mon Oct 02, 2006 12:14 pm #2
My experience has been quite the opposite - I switched from diodes to gas a long time ago. One thing that did make a huge difference with HeNes is to get the heat of the tube away from the table. I had a 10mW HeNe inside the air protected table, a 30" cube, and found the heat to be a problem. First I thermally insulated the laser (gave it it's own 'room' so to speak); then when I went to a 35mW laser I moved it off the table completely.

If you are careful to thermally isolate the laser from the experiment, and see these results (HeNe drift > Diodes) then maybe it's a different effect from my experience.
