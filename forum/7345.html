We are glad to announce that we have finally decided to use term Synfography for all what is related with our digital holographic printing.

Our holographic products name went through a long evolution process - before Geola's holographic prints were known under different names such like 3D Printing, 4D Printing, Synthetic 4D printing, Digital Holographic Prints. The term Synfography was proposed in 2007 by Dr. Stanislovas Zacharovas. We have realized that this term reflects properties and nature of this Geola product in a most accurate way and was enjoyed by our customers.

Term is combined from words Synergy, Form and Graphy:

Synergy (from the Greek syn-ergo, συνεργός meaning working together, circa 1660) pp refers to the phenomenon in which two or more discrete influences or agents acting together create an effect greater than that predicted by knowing only the separate effects of the individual agents.

Form (Lat. forma Eng. mould), refers to the external three-dimensional outline, appearance or configuration of some thing - in contrast to the matter or content or substance of which it is composed

Graphy - suffix -graphy means either "writing" or a "field of study", and is an anglicization of the French -graphie inherited from the Latin -graphia, which is a transliterated direct borrowing from Greek - γραφία, the -ία abstract of the verb γράφειν "to write", via Latin graphia. It often functions literally as a reference to writing (e.g. calligraphy, cartography, biography). However, it also has evolved to denote a field of study, particularly studies that involve some degree of collecting, cataloging and/or surveying (e.g. geography,

demography, oceanography).

Gram - suffix from Ancient Greek suffix -γραμμα (-gramma) < γράμμα (gramma), "'written character, letter, that which is drawn'") < γράφω (graphō), "'to scratch, to scrape, to graze'").

We hope you also will like this term.
