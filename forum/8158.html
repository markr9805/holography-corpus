Tom B. Sat Jan 23, 2010 11:02 am #2
First, try to contact them by phone. If no success, assuming you used a credit card order, find out if you have been billed yet.

If, after a few tries, you cannot contact them by phone or email and you have been billed and they do not deliver, you have some hope of disputing the transaction with your credit card company. It COULD be that they are just badly organized/on vacation/whatever. Or, as sometime

happens, things do get lost in the mail. Disputing transactions is pretty drastic and is a last resort, but if the company can't even provide

minimal customer support they should not be in business .

If you have not been billed, and you cannot contact them, it is possible that they have walked away from the business and are defunct. Just to

make sure, send an order cancellation with all order details and file it for future reference in case a charge should later appear on your card account.

Keeping records of your attempts to contact the company are a good idea. This link seems to provide reasonable advice:

[http://www.ehow.com/how_4616028_cancel- ... order.html](http://www.ehow.com/how_4616028_cancel-credit-card-order.html)

I once ran into a situation where I was sure I was being scammed but it was all innocent, a combination of their site being hacked thus cutting off email, unfortunately timed vacations, and general incompetence. I did get my stuff and bear them no ill will but won't buy from them again.
