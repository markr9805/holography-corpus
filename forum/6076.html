JohnFP Mon Feb 26, 2007 8:21 pm #3
If you want a true 3d image and not just color gratings on you business card then you will want the embossed hologram. Although these holograms can cost pennies if 100's of thousands are made they can be quite expensive for 500 or 1000 due to all the preliminary steps needed (artwork, master hologram, copy hologram, silverizing, nickel plating and finally the embossing process).

But for a really cool holographic business card that you can do yourself do the following;

Get some self adhesive holographic sheets (a google search for self adhesive holographic sheet will yield a large selection, I tried it with google images and found quite a bit).

Attach a piece of this sheet to some card stock the size a laser printer will handle.

Trim to card stock size.

Work out your lettering for business card on computer.

Print out on holographic side with laser printer.

Cut to business card size.

I have done this and a laser printer will print on the hologrphic sheet holographic side. You will have a holographic business card with lettering on the holographic side for a very inexpensive amount. Very eye catching.

Hi Lurkin

A business I am involved in specialises in Hot Foil Printing. Amongst the many different types of foil available are some VERY eyecatching holographic foils. If used in the correct manner, that is to create an emphasis on certain aspects of the card, they can be very effective. As a designer i'm sure you will know exactly what I mean.

May I suggest that you find a local Hot Foil Printer and ask to see some samples of their work. You may be able to acheive the effect you require without going to huge expense. Hot Foiling is very underrated and is quite often overlooked by graphic designers, it can however acheive stunning results.
