Colin Kaminski Sun Jan 13, 2008 5:47 am #2
Congradulations!

I can't imagine the developer will like being mixed. If it turns black throw it away. My guess is it will be black in the morning.

The only way to clean dust off is to either blow it off or to soak the hologram in your last rinse. Photoflo or dish soap or whatever you use. Then redry it. To keep dust out it is great to have a new hair drier to use. The one in my bathroom blows dust on the holograms every time. Now if you are patient, I am not, then you can just set the holograms on edge and check them out the next day and skip the hair drier.

Yeah, I'm with Colin on this one. It's ruined. Fortunately it's not horribly expensive.

> spazzguy35: 2) Is there any way to clean dust off of the plate after it has dried? I tried and it scratched the emulsion side.-How do you keep your plates clean when drying?

If you need to get dust off that's dried into the emulsion, the easiest way is to immerse the hologram in distilled water with a few drops of dish washing liquid added. After it's soaked in that for 10-15 seconds very gently rub the emulsion with your fingers while it's under water.

After removing the plate from the soak squeegee it off and gently blow-dry the glass side of the plate so that the dryer won't blow dust on the emulsion and the water is forced out from the "bottom" out.

If you're using PFG-03 you'll have to be especially careful rubbing the emulsion as it's very delicate.
