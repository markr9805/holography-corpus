> http://www.optigrate.com/Publications/pub-239.pdf

In this article is a description of how they coherently coupled two standard laser diodes to work together in single mode. They use thick Bragg gratings in Photothermorefractive (PTR) glass. An interesting note in the article is that they also used this PTR glass with a single diode to reduce the line width from several nm to less than 30pm!!!

I wonder if a reflection hologram of a mirror would be a sufficient replacement of the PTR glass to reproduce this experiment.
