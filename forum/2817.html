Hello. I'm back. Friends of mine have said they'd like me to get back on the forum since a lot of discussions have ...well....they say my input would be useful. Ok.

I'm not going to argue the various merits of possible and impossible. In the end, I should have realised that such questions are basically on the level of flying saucers and the existance of Nessie. Those who believe will never be disuaded and those who don't will never accept. In my experience, there is a ontology underlying the conventional teaching and acceptance of "standard" physics, as it is taught in accredited universities. There are some (many!) who dispute this and are convcinced that they can come up with better or alternative "thoeries". Fine! To my mind, the "proof of the pudding" must lie in either experimental verification as published in a peer reviewed journal or in some mathematical justification. You cannot argue the laws of Physics as if they were the product of legislation in a court of law. Some feel you can. Some feel that the (self defined) "cleverness" of the argument justifies the alteration of a law. Some even feel that they can "understand" complex and arcane mathematical formalism by recourse to intuitive concepts of the macro world of classical Physics. There are even some who feel the the justification of the alternative laws are (indeed, must be!) justified simply because they are alternative laws. To me, these arguments are mainly heuristic and epistemological. Usually, there is a great deal of tautology mixed in. The tautology is sometimes subtle, but it's always there. I find that seekers of these alternative laws are chasing a pot of gold at the end of a colorless rainbow. But, I make no comment. I'll simply answer where I can as regards the practice of holography. Holography is a process. A complicated process, to be sure, but still a process. As such, I''m going to stick only to the process and leave aside any speculation on the more complex issues facing Modern Theoretical Physics.

I'd like to suggest that we seperate the more speculative ideas that some here see fit to post in a seperate section.

I hear that a lot of people have suggested helping with Triple Take. For that, thanks. Danny is doing a great job with the new site and hopefully it'll be up soon.
