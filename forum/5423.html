Kaveh Thu Mar 09, 2006 9:39 am #6
> Carolyn: ok, I have to ask....what exactly is it? Is it a lighting stimulator?

It's a general 3D rendering program, with the important distinction that you don't model the scene interactively using a mouse, but write the code in the povray language.

Most people these days expect everything to be easy and interactive and intuitive, but the price they pay is the power that programming code has.

You can have a look at

[http://www.povray.org/](http://www.povray.org/)

for details. I think povray is one of those gems that has largely been ignored, but one day it will have its day. I plan to put together a video tutorial to show how easy and powerful it is. I happen to think that for non-programmers, it's the best way to start programming.
