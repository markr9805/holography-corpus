I have discovered since vforge's original posting that Safranine "O" works exactly as a drop in replacement for MB , it has good water solubility and

is a cationic dye like MB ie it is a chloride salt whereas erythrosin is an anionic dye ie you buy it as the sodium salt.

So the significance of this is that you can get panchromatic sensitivity

because Safranine 'O' (let's call it SO)gives photosensitivity to the 532nm region and you can mix it with MB OK. Whereas MB mixed with erythrosin B can cause problems with their opposite charges.
