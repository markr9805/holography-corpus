Wasn't sure where to put this and Colin or Michael if you feel this post should be moved, feel free.

I moved some of lab equiptment, not all yet to the new location. It is in the garage that has been used as an apartment for family or cousin or whomever needed a place to stay. It has a bathroom with shower, a bedroom (the laser-table room now), a kitchen, with fridge and sink (film making and processing now) and living room (computer shutter and video viewing, electronics, office and general engineering). Not all is set up yet. Anyway it is in the garage which has a sub floor of 2x6's or whatever. I bought a $50 solid wood, yes wood, indoor door and layed it on 3 inner tubes right on the wooden floor, carpeted. I know I have to cut holes and go below the floor to the concrete because depending where you stand it throws the beam out so far it moves across the plate. The laser was another obstacle in itself. I had to run the 3/4 inch hose up to the laser which is on the floor on a piece of hardwood (part of the hose runs through our den, wifey loves that). Then for the excess water, I am using the shower in the garage until weather permits discarding it to the woods (clean water). I reran the 3 phase electric myself from the cellar to the garage with a nice safety breaker panel.

I set up a single beam reflection and by goodness got a hologram on the first try. I am so excited I cannot contain myself. I still need to get the laser and the table off the floor but a hologram is possible. NEWPORT, that is my dream but wifey says not yet.

I still have a lot of moving to do, all the coating stuff, clean flow bench (won't call it laminar anymore unil I can prove it is laminar), stock optics etc.. I didn't want to move all unil I poved I could get a hologram, even if the simplest. The best part will be environment control year round especially with plate fabrication. Can you imagine being able to control temperature and humidity when fabricating plates...what a concept. As soon as I get set up I will take some photos and post them. Sorry I am rambling but I am excited. Did this come with a price, just e-mail the wifey and ask, major price but I believe worth it. Yadda, yadda, yadda..........

Peace!
