Colin Kaminski Sun Jun 20, 2004 3:22 am #1
Michael sent me a reflection copy of the hologram we made in silver halide on Michael's new table. Andres and I did most of the work setting up the table, Dinesh chose the beam ratios, Joy carded off the stray light then Michael made and processed the transmission H1.

After we processed the master I realized I made the master with a side reference! Micheal, on his own time, made some reflection copies rotating the reference to overhead.

The processing is perfectly clean and the copy I have is very nice. I do think I should of diffused the main object lighting a little to soften some of the hot spots reflecting off the object, but overall it is a very nice keepsake of the event.

Thank you so much, Michael, for sending me a copy and allowing me to play on your table.
