Ed Wesly Mon Jul 30, 2007 4:26 pm #5
R. W. Wood's classic text, Physical Optics, from the early 20th century, has an enlarged zone plate pattern in it for just that purpose.

I recorded some low spatial frquency gratings on the late lamented Kodak Technical Pan film, now out of production. They were not as efficient as the same gratings exposed on Kodak Holographic Film, S0-173, even though both were processed in the Pyrochrome system. (Both films were exposed using a 35 mm camera body without a lens as the film holder.) I'd be curious to trying that Gigabit film mentioned in the link above.

The big stumbling block to doing this would be more like the lens used rather than the film!
