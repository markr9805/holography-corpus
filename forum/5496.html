LP Mon Apr 17, 2006 7:12 pm #6
> Colin Kaminski: I am not familiar with the KP-4 Bleach, can you share how it was made?

The KP4-bleach exists of 3 grams of potassium permanganate, 10 mL concentrated sulfuric acid and 1 liter of dest-water. I use the chemical for half a year. Before, many years I had worked with the Dichromat-bleache. KP4 is a similarly good bleach. The biggest advantage consists in the fact that it is less dangerous and has an effect very fast. It is disadvantageous that one can dirty himself. I would like to renounce no more the bleach. After bleaching arise a stain (colouring), how with some developers.

One can remove that by short influence by developer (with Vitamin C) . Afterwards please wash up well .
