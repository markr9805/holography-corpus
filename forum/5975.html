Joe Farina Fri Jan 12, 2007 7:11 pm #3
I built a nitrogen laser, but never tried to use it for holography. Perhaps the above post meant the coherence length might be 1 to 2 millimeters (mm instead of sm)?

I heard it from good authority that a nitrogen laser was used to make holograms at the University of Michigan, but I presume it was for flat objects. I also have (or had) a paper on interferometry using a nitrogen laser.

Nitrogen lasers produce superradiant emission, and hence do not require a cavity. They are the simplest lasers to build at home. I have always wondered if there was a way to increase the coherence length of nitogen lasers by using a short cavity between two mirrors. But I've never heard of such a thing.

There are dangers with homemade nitrogen lasers. I used a 15KV transformer to charge the parallel-plate capacitor (with exposed surfaces) so please exercise the utmost care if you build one. The spark gap is extremely noisy and almost sounds like a small firecracker when the gap conducts. The gap must be shielded, because the spark is rich in UV emission which can harm the eyes. The output from the laser itself consists of invisible and powerful UV pulses.

Sogokon'A Mon Jan 15, 2007 5:42 pm #6
> Perhaps the above post meant the coherence length might be 1 to 2 millimeters (mm instead of sm)?

It is not a mistake. I used in experiments N2 laser ЛГИ-21. It is the sealed-in capillary tube in length nearly 40sm with two electrodes on the ends. On electrodes the pulse pressure 25 KV with frequency 100 Hz moves. The first hologram has been written down under Denisyuk’s scheme on DCG. Object – a coin, inclined to a plate under a corner approximately 30 degrees. The top edge of a coin concerned a plate. The beam extended by means of a spherical mirror and slightly exceeded the sizes of a coin. Time of an exposition in these conditions could be varied from 40 seconds to 5 minutes. As in the restored image the bottom edge of a coin had noticeably smaller brightness, it is possible to draw a conclusion, that the coherence length is in the limits of 1-2sm (10-20mm).

This work has been executed at the Kharkov University in 1981, and published in 1982:

Kaminskaya В.А, Nikishina L.E., Sogokon A.B. «About restoration in white light of the holograms generated by ultra-violet radiation». The Collection of works 4 All-Union conferences on holography, V.2, P.601-604, Yerevan, 1982г.

This work was an incitement to research of features Lippmann's photos on DCG and to use of effects of color coding.
