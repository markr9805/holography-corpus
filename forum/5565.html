I too wonder about the Ultimate chemistry. I did ask Yves if the bleach was Fe EDTA and he said no, but he didn't say what it was. I have both and they are different. I suspect Ultimate bleach may actually be Brilland bleach but I have no data to support that assumption.

Note: I have found Fe EDTA not to work well on old Agfa 8E75. However, the Ultimate bleach works very well on that.

As for the Ultimate developer, I suspect it is some sort of ascorbic acid developer, and I suspect the concentrate he provides is actually the active ingredients in a propylene glycol base to prevent oxidation.
