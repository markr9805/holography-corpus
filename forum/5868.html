Hello everybody,

I am new on this forum so let me introduce myself.

My name's Pierre, and holography is my business (Ph.D.). As my avatar could let suppose, I like to not be too serious. That is seven years I am making holograms form DCG for scientific applications (no artwork, sorry) and my largest one is 500mm wide. I wrote several papers (SPIE, JOSA, Optical Engineering, ...) on topics like cryogenic temperature diffraction and wavefront aberration, RCWA diffraction efficiency and made several scientific applications (HUD, HOE, ...).

I just change my position and get involved in photopolymers. My question is: there are still some manufacturers that sell photopolymer sheets at affordable price. Dupon Omnidex seems no more produced nor Polaroid DMP-128 the only one I found was Memplex from Laser photonics technology Inc. but it is over expensive (about $250 for 38x38 mm² sample!).

I would like to test my setup before going to our one designed samples. The best recording media should be green (532nm) sensitive, work well on pulsed or CW, have a dry and simple post process and works great with reflection hologram.

Please let me know to which product I should turn.

Sincerely,
