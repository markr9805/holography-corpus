Hans Thu Jan 25, 2007 3:52 pm #1
I have finally done it! :):):):):):) x 1000

After a long conversation with glajciorz on Skype I got the courage to try it one last time. The problems I had with this process before was severe fogging during the ascorbate sensitizing step, and no sensitivity after that. And before that I had problems with gelatin detaching from the glass because of bad silanating. (The IPA+Silane formula works better for me).

It turns out that the fogging in the ascorbate sensitizer was due to contaminated silver nitrate. I made a very small quantity of AgNO3 this time in a small plastic container that was never used before. And after the diffusion with the LiBr, I gave the plate an extra long wash under running water. That seemed to do the trick.

The hologram is not bright enough to photograph, but it is a first result at least. YAHOOO!!!!
