Holfocus Wed Mar 10, 2021 9:35 pm #9
I have a few standard questions on this material since no performance specifications are shown on their website.

Did you measure the growth in diffraction efficiency during recording to assess the rate of development of refractive index modulation ?

Did you measure the shift in the reconstruction angle to assess the degree of shrinkage during recording ?

What was the mW/cm2 value at the recording plane during recording ?

Did you measure the final achieved diffraction efficiency ?

Did you try to multiplex additional images ?

What is the thickness of the recording material ?

Did you have to post expose the recorded hologram to stabilize the image, such as with a strobe flash lamp ?
