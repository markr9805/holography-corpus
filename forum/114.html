For what it's worth, there was some discussion a few years back about the connection between Holography and 3D. There was a vote on whether the holographers should go with SPIE or with IS&T. I believe that if the holographers voted to stay with SPIE which meant that we could no longer go to the 3D conference because they were holding their meeting at a different site at a different time. I remember at the time having a longish discussion with Joel Kollin and Pierre St. Hilaire. Joel (as I remember) said that if there was a split, he'd migrate to the IS&T 3D conference. As is well known, the holographers did split off from the 3D, which, for me anyway, meant that I don't see some of the usual faces I used to see in San Jose. Of course, all of this is dragged from memory so I may be hazy on some of it.
