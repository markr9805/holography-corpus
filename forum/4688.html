JohnFP Sat Apr 02, 2005 4:17 pm #3
Dave I do have yellow lights but they are the frosted kind. Anyway I set up the HeNe with a spatial filter and checked some freshly made plates. Boy oh Boy does that technique work. On one of the plate what looks by eye to be a very small speck of dust or something, there is a big area around it which shows up with your technique. Now to take a photo of the plate with this technique, mark the plate on a corner make a hologram and see what happens at that point.

Also, one of the plates was coated with the veil and instead of spinning I let lean as you do. Where there was a speck in that I could see a varying thickness of "run" of the emulsion around the spec. This technique seems to work very, very well, thanks!
JohnFP Thu Apr 07, 2005 9:45 am #4
Well, I can honestly say that this technique proves to be very beneficial. The area around the "spec" which by eye seemed very very tiney had an emulsion deformity around it when using this viewing method. Then in making the hologram the deformed area (larger then the spec) is what affected the hologram.

So now, make 20 plates and view with light. Put them in order in the box from best to worst. Use worst for testing and best for finals. I LIKE IT!!!
