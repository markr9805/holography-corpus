manalokos Wed Mar 03, 2010 4:29 am #1
Hello all!

I've just received a 215 30mw from Dave, and tried on some plates,

I still don't know very well how to process, the plates I had were left several days

in a very humid box that developed fungus and the gelatine was probably inhabited by millions of bacteria

and got bumpy, I tried some dip-n-shoot, so I soaked the plates in KDi 2 days before

and left them in that humid box,

excess drops of KDi crystalized and ruined most

of the surface of the plates, I made 4 tries and this was the best one (don't laugh!),

I tried some coins and I think I can see one of the coins! That is nice for a first try,

I processed quite wrongly without heating the last IPA bath and drying cold, so I reprocessed the

plates following Dave's advice and they got much better...

It is incredible how sensitive to moisture these plates are, you need only to breath

onto the plate and yo can see the hologram disapear!

It is good that you can reprocess and make it appear again... :dance:

I'll see if I can do some more tomorrow, I mold coated 4 more plates

and veil coated one big one that I will cut in tiny pieces to make more tests

with dip-n-shoot! This time: thicker emulsion and soapy dip!

Best regards

Filipe Alves

Also, lessening the concentration of your KDi sensitizing bath will do away with the crystalization.
