This is my first split beam transmission hologram. This is the Virtual ual image. While I have not finished constructing my full size table, I thought I would give it a try. The picture does not do it justice.

[](https://holographyforum.org/forum/download/file.php?id=1928&mode=view)

The image was made with beam split once after Diverging lense. No collimating mirror. Shot on VRP and developed with JD2. exposure time was approx 6 sec.

When I turn the plate 180 degrees the image is magnified greatly and only seen on the plate (only a small portion of the is viewable at any time).

I next tried a different object, a pin. The picture attached is even worse than the above (take with I-Phone). When this plate is turned 180 degrees I can see the object, primarly on the plate, but "in the air" - but only when viewed at just the right angle.

![BS Pin.jpg](https://holographyforum.org/forum/download/file.php?id=1927)

The results were enought to spurr my interest in improving my setup, So now I will work on adding the collimating mirror, expanding beam to accomodate 5x7 plates (but still shooting 2.5 x 2.5) to determine approx exposure times. I now see that having variable beam splitters would be very convenient, may want to buy one...
