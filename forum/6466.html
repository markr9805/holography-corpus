I found this seller on internet, Knight Optical, that sells 1/2 wave fresnel rhombs for £120 before VAT. I believe this is quite cheap. Though I do not know if they are antireflection coated or mounted.

[http://www.knightoptical.co.uk/acatalog ... arders.htm](http://www.knightoptical.co.uk/acatalog/RetardersAchromaticretarders.htm)

Half wave fresnel rhombs, as well as half-wave plates, change the direction of a laser beams polarisation and can therefore together with a polarising cube beamsplitter be used as a variable beamsplitter. The special thing with fresnel rhombs is that they are broadband and therefore very useful in colour holography. Wave plates are usually wavelength dependent.
