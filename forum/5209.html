Dear friends,

Our new holographic project is almost ready for release. This is compact set for amateur holography (even smaller than famous "shoebox" scheme). We've developed and tested all the components of this project. Difficulties and lacks of the technology are finally eliminated. Now we are preparing manual for it. Estimated time of release - February, 2006.

This set consists of:

1) our patented ultra-compact holographic setup,

![Image](http://www.holography.ru/images/forums/hset1.jpg)

2) laser pointer (3-5 mW) with a set of special batteries (original ones are removed),

![Image](http://www.holography.ru/images/forums/hset2.jpg)

3) holographic developer (our improved OD-1 with some additions),

![Image](http://www.holography.ru/images/forums/hset3.jpg)

4) tube with 30 PFG-03m photoplates (size - 6x6 cm),

![Image](http://www.holography.ru/images/forums/hset4.jpg)

5) tools for chemical treatment,

6) manual,

7) package

The setup allows to record qualitative reflection holograms 6x6 cm (potentially up to 9x12 cm). It was designed for amateur holographers, especially for schools and small labs. This is absolutely safe, including chemical components. Moreover, it can work in almost any conditions, even on your cook-table!

We would like to find out: what price of this product do our customers expect? Please leave your message here, in the forum. If you are interested in this set, please publish your opinion and you'll have a discount.

Tom B. Sat Nov 26, 2005 3:57 am #7
Some questions:

- What material is the large angle bracket made from?

- It's hard to tell from the pic, but does the bracket put the beam at 57 degree (Brewster) angle to the horizontal plate to minimize woodgrain reflections? (when the laser polarization is correct) This would be a good reason to include a bracket. Also including some black tape strips (or a roll) to cover the edge of the plate would be nice for better quality holos - or at least the manual could suggest this.

- It would be nice if the bracket and laser mount could be rotated 90 degrees to allow the use of vertical plates and objects. The other angle would be 33 degrees, which would be 57 degrees as seen by a vertical plate. The user could improvise plate support.

- I assume the laser has the collimator removed?

- What is special about the batteries? What type are they and where do I get replacements?

- What general chemistry is used for the developer/bleach? Metol/ascorbic acid with ferric sulfate bleach or something else?

- Typical exposure time for a 6 cm (2.5") square PFG-03M plate?

- How are the laser and the exposure controlled? I see no shutter or external on-off switch for the laser. Normally one would want to turn on the laser and let it warm up several minutes before exposing...

Anyway, looks interesting. I like that a reasonable number of plates are supplied. My suggested pricing would be $50 more than the cost of the plates and chemicals purchased separately.

This is wood (lightweight).

(when the laser polarization is correct) This would be a good reason to include a bracket. Exactly Brewster's angle is adjusted. Thank you for the note about black tape, it's already included into the kit.

> - It would be nice if the bracket and laser mount could be rotated 90 degrees to allow the use of vertical plates and objects. The other angle would be 33 degrees, which would be 57 degrees as seen by a vertical plate. The user could improvise plate support.

Thank you for valuable advice. The bracket is mobile. However, it would be correct to put appropriate alidade marks on the bracket.

> - I assume the laser has the collimator removed?

Yes.

> - What is special about the batteries? What type are they and where do I get replacements?

This is just a set of batteries of certain famous brands. They are "special" because we test each power supply for our kits to provide stable characteristics. Cheap power supply from laser pointer manufacturers is absolutely no good for holography.

> - What general chemistry is used for the developer/bleach? Metol/ascorbic acid with ferric sulfate bleach or something else?

Bleaching isn't necessary for our process. Formula of OD-1 was given earlier in this forum.

> - Typical exposure time for a 6 cm (2.5") square PFG-03M plate?

Average exposure time for our diodes is 20-30 sec.

> - How are the laser and the exposure controlled? I see no shutter or external on-off switch for the laser. Normally one would want to turn on the laser and let it warm up several minutes before exposing...

The light is blocked by black cardboard (part of the set).

Thank you very much for all your comments. We appreciate this.
