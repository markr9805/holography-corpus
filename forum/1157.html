> It looks as if the plane is whizzing out through the clouds because the image plane is on the wings. Was this intentional? Anyway, great effect!

Thanks. Yeah, that was the intention. I didn't do anything to the model to make it look like the prop was moving so I thought I'd bring the plane farther forward and make use of the natural blurring of the parts that aren't in the film plane. This helps to make it look like it's in motion.
