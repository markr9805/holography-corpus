Colin Kaminski Wed Dec 12, 2007 3:41 am #10
315 is a Coherent C315 532nm 100mw laser that is a take out from lithography equipment. I am told they all were replaced by blue lasers to upgrade the resolution of the printing machine. Mr. Gizmo seems to have bought quite a lot of them. Now they are harder to find. They go for around $1500 if you can find one. Many people here got them. I can think of 6 off the top of my head (including me). Before they became available it was thought that you needed blue to expose DCG. However, that has shown to be incorrect. While blue is faster than green, green works surprisingly well.

The preferred sealing method I see is to scrape a small ledge all the way around the gelatin and glue on a glass cover plate with either cyanoacrylate or UV cured optical cement.

Colin Kaminski Fri Jan 11, 2008 4:04 pm #13
> bernardb: Thanks for the explanation.I bought myself some Ammonium Dichromate with the intention of joining the DCG crowd someday.If I have the choice of using a Melles Griot 532-GSYS-A01 (532R-MA-A01) Argon or a 532nm DPSS, which is preferred for exposing DCG?Thanks for the help!Bernard

A laser for holography has to have long coherence length. Look up coherence length in the wiki. I have not had an air cooled Ar+ laser on my bench but I understand that they have very short coherence lengths. In fact even water cooled Ar+ lasers are fitted with an etelon in order to increase coherence length.

Most DPSS lasers have a very short coherence length. The best ones for holography are ring lasers. If you can still find one look for a C315 or if money is no object a Coherent Compass is very nice. I believe the Coherent Verdi is also single longitudinal Mode (SLM).
