Hi all,

a few days ago I bought a Coherent Compass 315M-100 system on [http://www.ebay.co.uk](http://www.ebay.co.uk) auction #280347133909. A similar item form the same seller is available here [http://www.ebay.co.uk](http://www.ebay.co.uk) auction #280358169295

System came working and in excellent cosmetic conditions (See picture 1), although the 24V/100W Power Supply was inadequate due to the C-315M high peak power-on current.

The problem was also due to the widget provided by the seller and used as autostart which wasn't compliant with the Coherent power-on timings (See picture#2 - note that the resistor trimmer has already been cannibalized!)

Nowadays the system assembled on a generous fan-free heatsink with a proper 99cents minimal control panel (thanks Sam) works stable and free of mode-hops (see picture #3)

During the auction, seller declared a (808nm) Laser pump current of 2.05A@100mW measured with the Coherent Digital Controller

Shunting the pump laser with a 0.1 Ohm 1% power resistor I measured 2.33A@100mW with a drop voltage of 4.45V across Anode-Catode pump laser itself. Please note that I measured the current of the pump laser (808nm) not the whole system current.

In according with Sam's C315M pump Laser characterization web pages, I'm concerned about system lifetime.

Does anyone have any experience of C315M-100 life expenctancy for a so working Laser?

Joe Farina Tue Jun 23, 2009 1:33 pm #2
Hopefully someone with more knowledge/experience will chime in regarding this question, but I will throw out a few comments.

Those El Cheapo startup adapters are useless, that's what I got when I first received my 315. Output power of the laser was excessive (>120mW as I recall) and quite unstable with reagrds to output power. Sam sent me one of his $50 autostart adapters, and the thing is fantastic. It has two green LEDs, and when the second LED comes on, the output is absolutely rock-steady at 100 to 110mW. After the second LED comes on, I can repreatedly check the output power, and even if I take multiple readings with the Lasercheck, it usually shows exactly the same figure, say for example 101mW. Sam really did an incredible job with that $50 adapter, and I highly recommend it for anyone with a 315.

If the drive current appears excessive for the laser diode, I wouldn't rule out just reducing it, if lifetime of the head is a major concern. 100mW is quite a bit of power. If you are using a green-sensitive dye with Jeff's TMG-DCG system, then I would think you could easily get by with less power (especially after seeing what you can do with your HeNe on MBDCG!)
