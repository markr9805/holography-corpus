Tony Fri Feb 17, 2006 11:04 am #5
I'm sure it's the dichromate bleach causing most of the shrinkage. Try Fe EDTA or CuSO4 bleach. I've never noticed any effect from moderate blow drying in silver. Also, pyro developer (JD2) causes some tanning and may be responsible for some shrinkage.

You could also try a post-swell with TEA or sugar.

Or, preswell with TEA and you've got your UV hologram!
