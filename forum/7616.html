Kaveh Sun Nov 30, 2008 9:51 am #3
Well done on your first transmission hologram. I remember the excitement when I made my first one!

It is interesting to find out the origin of the fringes on all the objects. I thought this might be due to two wavelengths, which would give you a kind of depth contour, but I think it is all the objects moving. But I am not sure. What you need to do is to make sure the objects are really settled and still before you shoot. The object just to the left of the coin seems to be paper, which is crumpled and moves irregularly during exposure.

Colin Kaminski Tue Dec 02, 2008 4:19 am #8
That is interesting. I have not seen any of my 5mm diodes mode hop. Make sure to leave it on for about 15 minutes befor you shoot. I did see some holograms about ten years ago that had banding and the holographer was using a computer to turn on the diode as a shutter. This left the diode too instable to record a hologram. Your hologram really looks like the laser mode hopped during the exposure. This happens as the diodes warms up of if a blast of warm or cold air hits the diode.

Let me know if you see this again.
