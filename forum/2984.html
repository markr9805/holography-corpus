Martin Tue Dec 28, 2004 12:42 pm #2
> Or perhaps there is a way to make a waterproof hologram I can stick to my board? Can DCG be shot on film? Is Slavich the thinest film in silver halide? Perhaps I could shoot a rainbow in silver halide and back it with mylar...

You could use:

the traditional optical adhesives etc. to laminate a film between two transparent plastic sheets;

there might be office laminators around that allow for optical quality sealing;

you might also get along with some of the materials Michael used for film mounting (index matching).

Regarding film materials, yes, Slavich is the only option at this moment.

And yes, you can make DCG on those – fixed – silver halide films (though probably not at the brightness levels you'll see on Dinesh's DCG plates).
