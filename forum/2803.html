A rainbow hologram is a white light viewable transmission hologram. It is made from a slit master which causes the white light to be separated into its different colors. You can find many detailed description with a search on the internet. Here is one.

If you are asking if another holographic image can be embossed onto the existing holographic diffration grating shown in your link, then I cannot answer from experience but I would have to say it may prove to be very difficult if possible. The reason would be, when you emboss an image you are taking a nickel shim which has the holographic fringes and stamping those fringes into a material with heat and pressure, this heat and pressure may be enough to "flatten" out the existing fringes of the diffraction grating, even if they were on the opposite side of the material.

One method to obtain your desired results would be to incorporate the image with the diffraction design into your photo resist hologram from which your nickel shim is made.

Another method may be to create the image you want and stamp it into clear "plastic" material by itself. Then merge (sandwich) the image and the existing diffraction material together. Maybe a laminating process. This is just a thought.
