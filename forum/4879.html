Following further from John W thread of May 10 where he said

> John W: Hi all,I recently tested some oldish plates from Slavich, PFG-03M (expired early 2005). I dropped one straight into a JD-4 developer (no exposure) and it blackened almost immediately, is there any hope for these plates? The half empty box had been sitting around at room temp for about a year. I though somebody had mentioned you could salvage expired plates using reversal bleach, but I could not find the post.

I now find if I do the same as John mine behave the same .

Ted Park has just posted that he is finding variable results too with them.

I find the fogging which is no doubt chemical is quite severe even so you can still get a modest Denisyuk hologram out of them and it just shows they would have been good but for this serious problem.

This is not an acceptable situation and just because they have a "best before date" that had expired last September (I think I bought them about then) we should not put up with this and I suggest that all you Slavich plate users should do a fog test in developer first with no exposure.

I will take the matter up further and will post results here.
