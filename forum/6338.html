> kiwasabi: Ok, another question for you guys: I'm a 3D Artist; Is there any good way to bring computer 3d images into holograms? Everything you guys are doing seems to be with lasers and physical objects, but what about digital objects?

Well, Liti Holographics main line of business is making integral holograms from a series of images. what you wuold do is take your 3-D modeling software and output a series of images with the camera moving a few inches in between each one.

Look Here:

[http://www.liti3d.com/cg_hologram.htm](http://www.liti3d.com/cg_hologram.htm)

I bought one of the high end samples and the work was very nice. Kaveh was not has happy with his but he is a holography perfectionist. He can point out defects that I don't notice right away. I would expect there is a big quality difference for the $150 and the $600 versions.
