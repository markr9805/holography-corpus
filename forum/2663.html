Dinesh Tue Aug 10, 2004 1:42 pm #3
Unfortunately it's going to take an hour or so to really get into the patent. However, on briefly reading it through, it seems to be a variation of the Polaroid method, but with different materials and a different mechanism. From a first glance (probably wrong, it's pretty obfuscating!) it seems that his Silver Halide contains other materials which promote the reduction of Silver, I think that's what the metals and gold sensitization is for. The "processing material" he talks of is then attached to the Silver halide and this combined system is heated. Whatever is in the processing layer then processes the Silver halide. The whole point being that it's no longer wet processed in trays. You simply zap the shot, stick this "processing material" to the Silver and heat. The various solutions etc in the material go into the Silver and you almost get self-developing. As I said, it's reminescent of the old Polaroid cameras - if I'm right.
