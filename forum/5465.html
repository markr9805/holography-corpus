Colin Kaminski Mon Apr 03, 2006 2:18 am #5
To check for bifringence place the lens in between two crossed polarizers. If you see colors it is changing the polarization of you laser.

Remember it does not just have to rotate the polarization it can also "retard" it making the beam elliptically polarized.
