If you are using [magnetic base mounts](http://www.harborfreight.com/cpi/ctaf/Displayitem.taf?itemnumber=5646) from places such as Harbor Feight, you've probably found that they often wobble slightly when turned on. I expect that true flatness isn't needed when they're used for the original intended purpose. For our purposes you should get rid of that wobble.

Two ways that I know work are:

1) Attach a piece of fine-grit sandpaper to a large piece of marble or float glass and sand the base flat. This is time consuming but works well and also is an excellent method for [sharpening chisels](http://www.shavings.net/SCARY.HTM).

2) Start again with a piece of marble or float glass and put down a piece of wax paper. Get some high-strength, somewhat viscious, epoxy and spread some along the skids of the mount. Press the mount hard onto the wax paper and let cure. Once the epoxy is hard you'll have a very flat base with the added benefit that some of the wax will adhere to the epoxy, providing just enough slickness to be useful when moving the mount on the table. Some additional detals can be [found here](http://www.dragonseye.com/holography/2004_10.html#oct15).
