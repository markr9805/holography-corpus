Stating the obvious, the majority of the posts here deal with display holography. I am curious if any of the readers ("lurkers") are working on or are interested in non-display applications of holography, such as HOEs in unusual optical systems (optical computation, neural networks); holographic storage; or other exotic applications. For example, although our firm has done a lot of work in 3-D display, we have a [patent](http://www.google.com/patents?vid=USPAT6570681) on a diffraction-based beam steering device that was inspired by our work in quasi-holographic video display.

Sergio Tue Apr 10, 2007 12:46 am #2
I'm working with photopolymers, specially liquid type, that now is almost solid, viscous to improve the quality of transmission holography, because silver halide does not fulfill some niches, people from University and Research Centres are using and testing photopolymers because you can precisely (now recently with this viscous-gel polymer) control the thickness and difraction efficiency in transmission HOE's, holographic storage tests and evanescent wave holography.

Liquid photopolymers were until now some experimental but now is very promising to serve as main recording system for Hoe's, filters and a serious candidate for Holographic Versatile Disks.

Some non-display holography I have been involved with are designing and fabricating HOE's to be used in medical instruments as wavelength dedicated spectrophotometers; ditto for HOE's to be used as tail lights or Center High Mounted Stop Lights; and an HOE for 3-D display of video information.

I don't know if bubble chamber (small particle) holography counts as non-display or not. But the results usually weren't very pretty!

Sergio Wed Apr 11, 2007 6:47 pm #8
Dupont released a liquid photopolymer in the 80's that was a really liquid to be exposed in a horizontal plate..

Polygrama now make a viscous semi-liquid emulsion (but pourable) that hardens recording the holographic fringes to form a flexible chemically resistant plastic. I'm working now with a photopolymer film (also red sensitive) with tons of tests for release as security photopolymer mainly, although one can use it as display holo device. Display holography requires superb plus ultra diffraction efficiency materials! So we concentrate with special applications first.
