It may not be that difficult for broadband. It's possible to use dispersion compensation, but only at a specific angle. Off-axis viewing will cause dispersion, especially from a distant star. However, this may not be a problem since the angle of view of a star is extrememly narrow anyway. As the article says, it's been talked off before (Denisyuk, 1971 etc). In fact, I'm told that it was considered as a method to correct Hubble. The fly in the iontment seems to be the reconstruction beam. Any mismatch down to fractions of lambda could easily cause more aberrations than you're trying to correct!
