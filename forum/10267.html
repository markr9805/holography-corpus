The Home + Studio of E. Wesly & Sons invites you to the nth Annual OPEN HOUSE and HANSFEST! Celebrating over 30 years of working and partying together!

Every year on his return trip from Photonics West, Hans Bjelkhagen stops off for some nice cold Chicago weather, (must be pining for the frigid fjords) for the annual Open House! Check out the only functioning Holography Labseum in the City of Chicago! (Labseum = Laboratory + Museum.) See how the dark art of holography is practiced! Feast your peepers on some real holograms, recent and ancient! Meet other like-minded individuals infected by the curious 3-D affliction! Bring some of your own holos to show off!

Sunday Afternoon, February 10th, 2:00 PM

At the Home + Studio of E. Wesly & Sons Chicago, USA

For more details: [http://edweslystudio.com/HnEPortfolio/Hansfest2019.html](http://edweslystudio.com/HnEPortfolio/Hansfest2019.html)
