Thank you! Nice survey.

Skeptics about the technological challenges to be overcome should reflect on the development of color TV which required several major advances in video system design and manufacturing technology, comparable to the effort and expense required for the Apollo moon landings. The question being whether 3D is addictive enough to compel a similar effort. I think it unlikely at present with current small displays, the effect of which seems to amount to a blurry puppet show. We need a wall-to-wall Ray Bradbury Veldt experience [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Veldt](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Veldt) . It's a nice story - quite Ballardian.
