Although I still have a lot of research I want to do, there is one hologram I want to make next but I am not sure it will work. I want to make a single beam transmission DCG hologram that scatters the light like a crystal ball and it close to the plane of the plate. Basically, I want to bust up a piece of glass and then glue all the teeny tiny shards as close together on another piece of glass, occupying as much surface area as possible, maybe even two or three layers. Then put this piece of covered glass over top a DCG plate and expose through the covered glass. I an hoping enough light passes through and enough light is diffracted from the shards that I get a very interesting display of diffracted light in transmission format. One thing that concerns me is that there may not be enough overlapping light to fill the plate and there may be many voids. As it has been unseasonably warm this week and is expected to be so for the next week. I should be able to try this this weekend. NOW I AM EXCITED AGAIN!!!
