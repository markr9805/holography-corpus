Yes, dichromates are toxic and a hazard to the environment, especially to aquatic organisms. You can use Sodium Metabisulfite to convert CrVI to CrIII, but maybe you have to get the dichromate into a solution first.

JohnFP Tue Feb 05, 2008 12:34 pm #3
Another technique, if you are using fixer, is to pour all unused, scraped and excess emulsion into one of your empty distilled water jugs. Then as you use up your fixer (it is blue) pour the used fixer in the gallon jug with the AmDi. You should instantly see the orange turn to blue. When it is blue it is in the CrIII state. I have always had enough fixer to nuetralize all my excess AmDi.
