Hi guys.

A video of test transmission holo made using acrylamide-based photopolymer

I took the recipe from "Photopolymer holographic recording material" by J. R. Lawrence, F. T. O’Neill, J. T. Sheridan

7g PVA/70ml DI water

8ml TEA

2,4 g acrylamide

0,8g bisacrylamide

1ml 0,4% methylene blue

up to 100ml with DI water

I apply 3ml of this stuff on 5x5cm leveled glass plate.

It dries 2 days, requires high (60% or higher humidity) and 50mJ/cm^2. Besides its cost is much closer to $15/l than per ml
