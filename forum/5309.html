I am afaid I lack knowledge to answer your questions, but perhaps some of mine will help.

1] John, I think you make your holograms broadband by pre-soaking them in water after illumination yes? It seems to me that the more narrow-band your hologram is, the more channels will fit.

2] Do you mean by multiple channels, the ability to store more images at different reference beam angles? If that is the case would it be possible to take an existing single channel (narrow-band) hologram and checking at what replay beam angle the image disappears? Would the next channel be available at double that angle? In that case the amount of channels would be approximately 180 degrees divided by 2 divided by the viewable angle. Then take three holograms with different thicknesses and calculate the possible amount of angles for each one and curve fit the results in a graph.

3] Does the amount of channels depend on the amount of information per channel? Is that the reason why an reflection H2 is more bright than a proper beam-ratio'ed Denisyuk?
