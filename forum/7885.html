Colin Kaminski Mon Jul 06, 2009 2:13 am #4
The exact how to for digital holography has not been written. If you study general holography then read up on what is published about Zebra and Liti imaging you could replicate the process but at a very high cost. For figuring all of the analog setups a great practical introduction is Graham Saxby's Practical Holography.
MfA Tue Jul 07, 2009 1:05 am #5
I think Zebra/Liti/Ultimate Holography all use variations on the method described in [this thesis](http://arxiv.org/pdf/0902.0551) (Direct-Write Digital Holography by John Peter Tapsell). The founder of Zebra Imaging originally researched pure fringe based methods, but I doubt that is what they are actually using now.

Anyway, digital holography is about research and thousand dollar holograms ... consumerization of small run digitally created display holograms is a way off, let alone any type of realtime display for stage shows.
