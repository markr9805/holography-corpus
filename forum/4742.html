Ed Wesly Tue Apr 12, 2005 1:41 am #2
Might have to have heap good stock options to cash in on!

Buying a Ruby laser would be a serious holographic commitment. They are huge pieces of machinery, with power supplies the size of refrigerators!

It is imperative that any ruby laser for holography has either an etalon or 2, or a leaky optic known as a resonant reflector, which is like an output coupler with etalon in one piece. Lasers that were used for machining or medical purposes might not have one.

The Rolls-Royce of Ruby would be JK cum Lumonics cum I don't know the name of their current incarnation. Their one Joule model, which consists of an oscillator and 3/8" by 8" amplifier, more than enough to do full body figure studies retailed around $60k in the mid-80's, so it would have to be deeply discounted for me to afford one.

Other brands that may pop up in the surplus listings would be Korad, Apollo, and Holobeam. They did make holographic quality lasers, but these units would be 20 to 30 years old!

The first thing that would go bad on these would be flashlamps, which have a finite number of pulses in them. A helical lamp would run $500 on up!

Then who knows what would be going on in the power supply. One would have to have huge cajones to want to play with the enormous capacitors in them.

Then there is the problem of whether or not any of the contemporary recording materials can see that 6943 Angstrom emission.

And then there's always that waxy looking flesh problem with that almost IR wavelength that makes subjects look like they've been embalmed.

But if you've got the time and the dough what the heck!

BTW, there is a pulsed Ruby laser lab operating in the Chicago area. It is the descendent of Holicon, now owned by a gentleman named Rick Bruck. If anyone has any projects they would like to tackle, please feel free to contact him.

Also, if anyone would like a copy of an article I wrote for holosphere, the late great advocate of holographic technology, science and art, about the JK/Lumonics Ruby laser line up, e-mail me and I will reply it back to you.
