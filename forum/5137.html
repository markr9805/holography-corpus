Yes, it was me, and I think with a bit of persuasion they will open it up. Here is the reply I got in April 2003. If anyone wants to follow up, we can cite this as a prior communication.

===========

We regret to inform you that Massachusetts Institute of Technology will not grant permission to Focal Image to scan and make available electronic copies of holosphere on the Focal Image web site. If you have questions on this, please feel free to call me in the MIT Office of Intellectual Property Counsel ({phone number}). MIT may, subject to the following, consider granting Focal Image permission in the form of a nonexclusive, nontransferable license to post the attached index prepared by the MIT Museum listing the publications of holosphere which may be available in the MIT Museum's archives, on Focal Imaging's web site for the purpose of informing the public on a no-cost basis of the publications of holosphere which may be available in the MIT Museum's archives, so you can direct interested persons directly to the MIT Museum at: {contact Kaveh for this}@mit.edu to request a copy of a specific issue of holosphere from MIT. If you are interested in making this index available on your web site, you will need to first send me a copy of your proposed use of this MIT Museum index on your web site, so we can review it and provide you a written determination of whether it is acceptable to MIT.

============

Damn copyright and damn intellectual property.
