The Holography Group

of the Royal Photographic Society

presents an international one day conference

Holography, Art and Design

2005

The Royal College of Art, London

United Kingdom

Saturday 30 April 2005

Following the very successful Holography, Art and Design conference held in

2002, the Holography Group is pleased to announce the presentation of

Holography, Art and Design 2005, a one day conference to discuss holography,

its role as an art medium and its application in art and design. Subjects

to include latest commissions and projects, digital influences, developments

in technology and materials. A post-conference reception and exhibition will

be held at Gallery 286 hosted by Jonathan Ross, featuring work from the

splendid and rarely seen Karas Studios collection of Spanish holography

curated by Ramon Benito.

We would like to hear from anyone who would like to contribute to or attend

the conference.

Please contact Kevin C Brown:

holo at clara.co.uk
