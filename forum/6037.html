doug694 Sat May 19, 2007 7:02 pm #7
Hah,

Some of the terms I have used for laser optics shipments to/from various countries.

Items with these descriptions travel quickly and without fuss...

- lasers = 'redundant optical source', 'optical equipment', 'pulsed light source'

- ruby rods = 'transparent optical media', 'craft materials', 'optical rods'

- laser mirrors = 'second-hand optics'

- flashlamps = 'replacement bulbs', 'linear electronic bulbs', 'spare lamps'
