MichaelH Sun Mar 27, 2005 11:06 am #3
I know there isn't supposed to be a discussion regarding these tips, but this does leave some questions un-answered.

I can see that it would show variations in emulsion thickness which is what caustics are (in a sense). They're variations in density that cause light to be bent in different directions by different parts of the emulsion.

[here's a link to a caustic generator](http://www.lysator.liu.se/~kand/caustics/) if you want to play with some.

This technique would also more easily reveal dust in the emulsion since it's often easier to see the shadow of something rather than trying to focus on a mostly clear plate when looking through the plate at your safelight.
