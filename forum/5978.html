I received some sample BB530 plates some time ago and have resumed testing them. I am doing 8x10 masters of an object that I will copy into DCG. Even though I was informed that the plates are on thin glass, I am not seeing any adverse effects. I am only running the laser at 200mW and am getting about 15uW for the reference and 1.5uW for the object beams. Doing exposures of 15 seconds gives the plate about 240uJ. I am presensitizing as per their documentation but in water alone with some photo flo for 2 1/2 minutes. I am processing in straight pyro developer they recommend for reflections (I remembers using this years ago for tranmisisons and it not only worked well but I had some pyro setting around). I am bleaching with JD2 bleach. The holograms are coming out beautifully bright with no noise, very clean.

Unfoartunately I lost my battery charger for my camera and have to order another one. As soon as I get the charger I will post some photos.

I am very pleased with the plates.

Uli Thu Jan 18, 2007 2:19 pm #2
Thanks for your feedback, John. I never processed a transmission hologram with pyro, but, if this developer helps reducing the noise with reflection holograms, why shouldn't it do the same in transmission. The thing is we normally develop these plates for our transmission holograms using an ascorbic acid - methol or an ascorbic acid - phenidone developer (the latest is on our webpage). The result is also very clean and the plates are clearer, therefore with a brighter image for making the H2.

By the way, the green plates are called BB520.
