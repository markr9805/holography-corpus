Your observation is interesting Michael and suggests to me that on your side of the pond you have particularly adept bacteria or moulds at breaking down this amine and removing I guess the OH group which has been making the chemical not very smelly. So that means it is probably forming the much more smelly triethylamine . As HoloM suggests in this thread, this pongy TEA is no big deal on the holography side and works just as well as the fresh stuff apparently.

Fact is we have actually evolved from our ape or possibly pre-ape past millions of years ago to not like the smell of volatile amines because they were given off by rotting meat, ( same for hydrogen sulfide gas too.) So any ancestral forms of pre-humans who found the smell unpleasant were selected for, leaving behind those protohumans who ignored it to die early from botulism, and general food-poison nasties.

Meanwhile of course blow-flies had long earlier evolved the mechanism to luxuriate in rotting meat as do kommodo dragons and vultures.

Anyway back to the holography lab. My bottles of TEA/water solutions have kept for years and have not gone off pong wise, according to my young colleagues whose noses have not undergone 50 years worth of various chemical assaults like mine has.

So perhaps the organisms dont thrive in the British climate so well.
