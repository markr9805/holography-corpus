> Danny: you can use it ...but the contrast in dcg will be much higher. thats why they dont use dcg for h1's.. and what is your laser? and what is the power?

Danny, can you please explain? From what you post is sounds like DCG is not used for H1's because it is too contrasty. What do you mean high contrast. How is this negative for DCG transmissions? If you mean noise, that can be controlled. If you mean brighness, that is a positive attribute.

I always thought

1. transmission DCG H1's were more difficult to process to get replay to desired replay wavelength compared to silver.

2. DCG is hard to obtain commercially, especially larger formats needed for large window transfers (8 x 10's or larger)

3. DCG masters, whether transmission or reflection need to be sealed for longevity, thus UV epoxy or some other index matching sealing method needs to be used for masters without undesirable effects.

Dave, I am not sure if I understand what you are looking for. Slavich VRP-M is available in smaller quantities of precut film and plates. Check out this site. [http://www.laserreflections.com/film.html](http://www.laserreflections.com/film.html). They have VRP-M in film format and plates at much less the price of $500 for the sizes I belive you are looking for. 5 - 12x16 for 125.

Also, if you are making a Master for a rainbow copy, remember you only need to record a "slit" master. Thus a Master for a nice big windowed 4x5 can easily be created on a 12 x 4 inch plate thus providing 4 masters for each 12x16 and a total of 20 - 12 x 4's for $125. Even if you want a wider window you could cut the 12 x 16 into 3 - 4x16's yielding 15 masters for $125. I used to get two "slit" channels on a 16 x 4 plate so you may even cut it narrower.
