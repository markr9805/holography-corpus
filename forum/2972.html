What is the best matching fluid for pfg-03M?

What is the best way to clean the matching fluid before developing?
Colin Kaminski Wed Dec 22, 2004 8:26 am #2
The ideal index matching fluid is in between the index of refraction of the two materials. In practice it is not that important and I have tried many. My favorite is the refined oil used for indoor lamps. It's name is different in different countries. Lamp oil is one. If you get the smokeless it is better. (more refined).

Before developing, rinse it in distilled water with gentle agitation for 2 minutes. Then develop it as normal.
