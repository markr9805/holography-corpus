I made my very first DCG dim hologram with TEA instead of TMG using 5mW 650nm lasermodule. Exposition time was around 45min. It was great feeling

I used this composition:

Gelatine………….….…..12g

Water…………………....80ml

TEA acetate………………10ml

K2Cr2O7 5%¤…..…….1ml

MB 0,4%¤.……..6ml

Finaly adjuted pH na 9,0 – 9,5

with 25% solution TEA

Processing was: immersing in potassium thiosulphate, distilled water 25°C, 50% isopropropanol, 100% hot isopropanol

Yes, the speed was very slow. Unexposed plates were milky, exposed areas were clear. The plate was 5x5cm and fit the processor. I used the high speed spincoating made of the big like PC fan. I didn't make any power meterig. Iteresting is, that there was no shift during exposition, because I live in 8th floor in noisy concrete pannel house. The plates were little bit tacky and my hologram after three reprocessings shift to UV region. I didnt control the temperature also.
