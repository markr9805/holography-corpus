I periodically buy odd optics from eBay and while going through my piles today, working to get a better idea of what I had and what needs to go in the trash I ran across this guy.

![Image](http://www.dragonseye.com/gallery/d/9106-4/telescope.jpg)

It's AR coated (the purplish gleam on the lenses gave that away) and I thought, "that looks just like what I need for my green laser, to collimate the beam". Turn out that except for being dirty it works great and even at 100mW output, there's almost no back reflection.

I mentioned on the skypecast today that I still needed to build a telescope for my 315 but that's one task down and one step closer to using the laser.
