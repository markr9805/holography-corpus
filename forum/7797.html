Hans Mon Apr 06, 2009 1:57 pm #10
The 215M is nearly the same as the 315M, but there is less temperature control. The 315M has very accurate temperature control to keep cavity length constant and a separate temperature control for the KTP crystal.

The temperature control on the KTP is very important in the 315M because it is that part, combined with a Brewster window that guarantees single mode behaviour.

I once phoned with a technical representative of Coherent because I could get one cheaply. He said that the 215M lacks this accurate temperature control and is therefore absolutely not guaranteed to be single mode. Now I am not sure which TEC is missing in the 215M. I don't know if it is the TEC that stabilizes the cavity length or the one that stabilizes the KTP temperature. But I decided it was not worth the risk to buy one.
