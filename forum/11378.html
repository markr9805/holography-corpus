I now prepare the materials are: deionized water, potassium dichromate powder, gelatin. What is the most appropriate ratio between them?
Joe Farina Mon Oct 14, 2024 12:21 pm #2
You can try 1.2 grams potassium dichromate, 6 grams gelatin, and 70ml water as a starting point, and see what happens. Ageing your plates for a long time at this concentration will greatly improve your results. One holographer prefers using a lot of dichromate when exposing at 532nm, so you could also try doubling the dichromate. DCG involves a lot of trial and error . Dave B. ('holomaker" on this forum) has done a lot of DCG using 532nm, I suggest using this forum to search his posts for more information on DCG at 532nm. I think he used a high amount of dichromate.

For example, see:

[https://www.holographyforum.org/forum/v ... DCG#p71555](https://www.holographyforum.org/forum/viewtopic.php?p=71555&hilit=DCG#p71555)

I've been told that potassium dichromate is more sensitive to 532nm compared to ammonium dichromate, so I think that's a good choice.
