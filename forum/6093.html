8) For the website I am building [http://www.holovision.tv](http://www.holovision.tv) I need to accurately display holograms on the web.

Today I experimented by taking a digital camera mounted on a tripod. I added an extra arm where I can mount a hologram and it could rotate freely around up to 310 degrees. By focusing on the hologram and moving it from side to side in front of a stationary camera I was able to get a (mostly) jiggle-free video of a hologram. I then processed the video through a program called CompositeLab Pro so I could tilt the video backward roughly 30 degrees toward the horizontal axis. The first attempt looks roughly as if you were holding the hologram in your hand. Using the video file I can create an animated .gif.
