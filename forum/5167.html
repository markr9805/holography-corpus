This is an H1 to H2 copy from silver to DCG with an old left over plate. I believe my set up will work. I cannot wait to try some good plates.

![Image](http://www.holograms3d.com/PhotoGal/MichHolo/Elephant.JPG)

The set up. I tried a set up that least disturbed my existing set up. Blue lines are reference beam paths , reds object beam path. I can adjust the ratio by moving the intensity of from the spatial filter up or down. I can also, because of the Blox, adjust the flat mirror and collimator at differn angles. The collimating lense is for the illimination of the H1. Two flat mirrors redirect beam for reference in copy set up. Once again the Blox come into play. A simply engineer as you go design allows me to set up a single beam from a spatial filter with two differently directed beams, reference off of flat mirror at top and object off of collimator (just added) at the bottom. The colorful top between the H1 and H2 is just a blocker and it acutally ties the H1 and H2 together statically. It just sets on the plateholder of the H1 and directly on the plate of the H2. Looking at the holo in real person, the second flat mirror used for the reference beam needs to be raised to get a better replay angle.
