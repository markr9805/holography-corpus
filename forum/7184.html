The Feb. 1, 2008 issue of Applied Optics finally arrived in my mailbox, which I had been looking forward to because half of it is dedicated to articles on holography. They call the special feature "Manipulating Light Waves."

If you don't already subscribe, you might enjoy browsing through it in your library. There are articles by Bjelkhagen, Caulfield, Goodman, George, and others. You can see the table of contents on the OSA website for this issue, here: [http://ao.osa.org/issue.cfm?volume=47&issue=4](http://ao.osa.org/issue.cfm?volume=47&issue=4).
