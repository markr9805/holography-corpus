With visual use, the 2465B will work, but for photographic use it's phosphor is a bit harder to capture.

Here is an example of an enhanced model.

[http://cgi.ebay.com.au/Tektronix-2467B- ... dZViewItem](http://cgi.ebay.com.au/Tektronix-2467B-Oscilloscope-Four-Channel-400-MHz_W0QQitemZ7611951821QQcategoryZ105806QQcmdZViewItem)

Of course if you get a DSO with a sample rate of at least 2GS/s and at least 32k buffer or better, then that work well at capturing single pulse events fairly accurately. Digital data is limited by the Nyquist limit of 2x. So a 2GS/s would resolve 1 nsec event on a very limited basis of just 2 data points.
