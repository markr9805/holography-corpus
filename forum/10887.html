jrburns47 Wed Jun 10, 2020 4:09 pm #2
Bob Hess should be able to tell you what kind of bulb for the voxbox. For a 120 multiplex, a clear incandescent bulb with a vertically oriented linear filament is best - 60 watt should be fine. They can be tough to find though. 100w runs a little hot. These will work but the eBay supplier below often sends horizontally oriented filaments instead of the vertical filament in the photo. That would mean redesigning how the bulb is usually mounted in the display to be useful.
