Dinesh Tue Jun 29, 2004 1:18 pm #2
To make it look wet, I'd suggest a thin coating of plastic. I remember there used to be a two part plastic used as a child's game. the idea was to immerse something into a receptacle and mix the two liquids together. After a while the stuff polymerised or something and the object was encased in plastic. If this is still avaliable, apply both liquids to the starfish in a thin coating. The sheen coming off the plastic will make it look wet.

The foggy water is a bit more tricky. Try putting a standard diffuser between the beaded glass and the scene. By varying the seperation between beaded glass and diffuser glass, you may be able to vary the "fog" density.
