Dinesh Sun Jun 05, 2005 1:49 pm #5
The first one has a description stating it was made using a glass sculpture. So it was done just by shooting light through the back.

"This hologram was made at a lab at The Ohio State University in Winter 2004. In order to view it one must have a decent bright light (or the sun) shining down at a 45 degree angle, then move it around and notice all the colors of light. The photograph taken was in a dark room with a bright light shining from the back. A glass sculpture was used in order to achieve the shape in the hologram, but don't get this confused with the other holograms for sale, those are reflection holograms, and are very common. ..."

The various internal reflections inside the glass model will give the colours by internal scattering and arriving at the plate at different angles. The fact that the it needs light at 45 degrees implies that there may have been a reference, or that the expanded beam was simply launched into the glass model. It would depend on how transparent/translucent the glass sculpture was. In effect it's a mish-mash of gratings at different spatial frequencies.
