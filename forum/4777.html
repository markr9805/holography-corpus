I have been thinking much of late on how to make a more realistic image with a hologram. I have been confused by Dinesh's use of the words scotopic and photopic and how they relate to this problem so I looked them up.

Scotopic - The situation when the eye is dark adapted and the eye is using the rods. (Pupil diameter 7mm in the young, decreasing to 5mm in the old.)

Photopic - The situation when the eye is using the cones and the image is very bright. (Pupil diameter about 2.5mm.)

I have found with astronomy that scotopic vision is a dull green color. If you start to get into photopic vision then you can see true color.

The main problem with holograms is two fold.

1. The hologram is not true color. I have seen true color holograms and they can for the most part correct this problem.

2. The hologram is not "solid". I don't know the proper term for this effect, but when I look at a hologram I can see through it.

A very interesting secondary problem is that the hologram appears to "emit" light rather than "reflecting" light. This is the same with a movie or a TV.

What methods can correct for the problems of solidity and make the image appear to reflect light?
