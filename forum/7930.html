Ed Wesly Tue Aug 04, 2009 8:25 pm #3
Yag is pretty high gain so there may be many modes even in a short cavity. For one last experiment, I would suggest running it just over threshold, the lowest power that it is stable at. That way the strongest mode is the only one lasing (hopefully).

Seeing the set up as Dave has suggested would be helpful. What kind of plates are you using? If the plates are capable of making single beam reflection holograms at 532, probably the only one that I can think of is the Colour Holographics green plates, you could try making a single beam reflection with a coin touching the plate itself. Another configuration that could give some results with short coherence is called single beam transmission deep scene, you might need to look in like Unterseher's book to see how to do it, or I could send you a pdf (and anyone else who would like a copy) of my Seven Single Beam Reflection Projects that gives an idea of how to do it, if you PM me c/o this forum with an e-mail address.
