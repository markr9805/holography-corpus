Colin Kaminski Sun Nov 14, 2004 2:09 am #1
We all met last night and talked, eat Indian food and drank beer till the wee hours. This morning we listened to 5 lectures and then went to lunch at a local pub. We are now making holograms in two labs. In one lab John Klayer, Dinesh and I are working on making the LITI kit work. We have repeatedly failed at making a hologram with an object and have switched to making gratings to learn more about the film. We are narrowing in on the exposure energy with a 1 to 1 beam ratio. Once we have decided on the proper exposure we are going to switch back to the object.

In the other lab we are making a group hologram. We have made a very large collection of objects we are jamming into one plate and each object represents one of us. As I am writing this we are setting up the table to make a master. In the morning we will make some DCG copies. Lets of information had been exchanged and we all will post more soon.

We wish everyone was here...
