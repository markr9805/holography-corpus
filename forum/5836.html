There are many ways to enhance the signal (laser) to noise (sun) ratio, but this depends on the specific application. Eg sending information over tens of miles using a low-power laser in plain sunlight is a different task than building a light barrier switch across a door...

Generally using a filter is the simplest possibility. Dielectric filters are best and can have a width of a few nm, which should cut out most of the ambient light. Color glass filters are much cheaper but have a higher attentuation for the signal and less for the noise. Collimation optics such as lenses or parabolic mirrors on both transmitting and receiving ends can help a lot too. Depending on the application, one may also modulate the laser light say with 1khz, and then look for an AC component of 1khz in the photodiode signal; such a setup is very insensitive to ambient light and works well for switching applications.
