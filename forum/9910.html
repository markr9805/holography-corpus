pluto Mon Sep 17, 2018 11:43 am #2
The holograms shown in your examples video are brilliant! Very concrete looking, truly like a window into a box with the real object!

What type of laser and formulation are you currently using? Are these all recorded based on transmission masters?
thycore Mon Sep 17, 2018 3:06 pm #3
These are simple reflection deniyusuk holograms

No transmission / master here

I used :

Plates from Yves Gentet’s Ultimate U08M with R

Sensitivity and 70nm preshift ( makes the developed hologram yellow-green instead of red)

Laser is a Hene uniphase 1125 which I believe to be something like 5-6 mW and not polarized.

Exposure time is approx 10 secs on a very stable set-up

Developer chemistry is the one provided with plates. Developing time 4 minutes

Bleach also comes from Ultimate-holography. Bleaching time 3 minutes

I made a remote control laser shutter with an arduino and a step motor which rotates a cardboard.
