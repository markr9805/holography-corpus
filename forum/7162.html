I was doing some thought experiments in my hotel room and came up with the following idea:

Has anyone ever tried to expose a mold coated DCG hologram BEFORE separating the mold plate? I calculated that before separating the plates and exposing at Brewsters, fringe planes would be at a distance of about 352nm apart. If the thickness of the mold is 60um and after separating the plates and drying, the thickness becomes 12um, the new fringes will be about 153nm apart and slanted at about 24 degrees.

- At Bragg angle, this hologram would reflect light at about 306nm.

- BUT: Light that reflects off every other fringe would intefere back at 612nm.

So, I guess that my question is: Can a Bragg condition be fulfilled with light bouncing off every other fringe instead of every subsequent fringe?

It would be very beneficial if this would work with MBDCG because of higher sensitivity in very wet emulsions and because of much greated environmental stability while still inside the mold.
