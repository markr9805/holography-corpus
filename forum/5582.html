In SPIE 5636 (2005) there is a paper called "True-color reflection holograms recorded in a single-layer panchromatic dichromated gelatin material" by Jianhua Zhu and others.

As far as I know, these are the best published sensitivity values given for a panchromatic DCG material thus far:

For 80% diffraction efficiency,

35 mJ @ 633nm

25 mJ @ 488nm

25 mJ @ 442nm

15 mJ @ 514nm

The sensitivity at 532nm was not stated, but based on the spectral absorbance graph on page 247, it looks considerably better than the sensitivity at 514nm.

This system is partly based on the work of Jeff Blyth, but they also added the dye Rhodamine 6G.

Hans, I would use a yellow safelight. In that paper I mentioned, there is a graph of the spectral absorbance, showing a drop in absorption around 575nm. The eye is most sensitive to yellow, so I think that would be a good choice.

Here are the details given in the paper:

1. Soak 2g of French inert gelatin in 40ml deionized water at 25C for 12 hours.

2. Heat the suspension in a thermostatic water bath to about 45C, then keep the temperature and stir the mixed solution slowly for 15 minutes.

3. Add 2.5ml of 0.5% potassium chromate solution while stirring at 45C for 2 minutes.

4. Add 0.6ml of 25% TMG solution while stirring at 45C for 2 minutes.

5. Adjust the pH value to 9.18 with TMG or acetic acid solution at 45C.

6. Add 0.3ml of 0.4% MB solution to the suspension while stirring at 45C for 5 minutes, then add 0.3ml of 0.2% R6G solution to the suspension while stirring at 45C for 5 minutes.

7. Pipette out 8ml of the mixed solution and spread it over 8X24cm2 leveled optical glass, keep it in dark and dry in horizontal position at 25C for 24 hours.

Processing:

a) Soak in F5 solution at 25C for 1 minute

b) Wash holographic plate in running water at 25C for 30 seconds

c) Swell holographic plate in warm water at 31C for 1.5 minutes

d) Dehydrate in 60% isopropyl alcohol bath at 25C for 1 minute

e) Dehydrate in 90% isopropyl alcohol bath at 25C for 1 minute

f) Dehydrate in 100% isopropyl alcohol bath at 25C for 2 minutes

g) Dry hologram rapidly with flowing hot air

My note: I assume the "F5 solution" is Kodak F-5 Hardening Fixer, which according to one of my books, contains sodium thiosulfate 240g, sodium sulfite 15g, acetic acid 28% 48ml, boric acid 7.5g, potassium alum, dodecahydrate 15g, water to make 1 liter.
