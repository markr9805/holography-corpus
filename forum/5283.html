Is this the first try or the brightest example? I guess my question is how much twiddling have you done with the process?
MarkCavin Fri Dec 23, 2005 4:49 am #3
It was the first shot of the day but I have been using 03C for a long time now. It represents the typical brightness I get on average; I never really get much brighter Denisyuk than this without assisted lighting. I flipped it and made a copy that came out brighter, but because of the distortions in the emulsion of the master, there is a bumpy dark texturing out in front of the image. I have not done anything except use the standard JD-4 kit; might try the whole formaldehyde thing someday, but trying to avoid it. I wish they would adapt a version of the 03 material sensitized for 532nm only.

Here is the H2 made a short time later, you can see the bumpy junk that is out of plane in front of the image. Yuck, it ruined what would have been a very nice hologram!!
