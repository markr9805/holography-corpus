Top of the morning to you all,

The label states the laser's class and maximum power until the next class of lasers. In other words this is a class IIIb laser. So it cannot exceed 500mW or it will be in the next class of class IV.

[http://www.epanorama.net/documents/ligh ... class.html](http://www.epanorama.net/documents/lights/laserclass.html)

I have been working with my laser mounted off the table for years now and never have any serious problems. The trick it to have a transfer mirror off the table (mine is just in front of the laser). That way if the beam is off a little the first mirror (off the table) can be used to tweak the beam back in without even touching the spatial filters. I do it all the time. I also have had to change wavelenths in between exposures. This surely moves the beam and disallignes the spatial filter(s). With the first transfer mirror I can get the beam back in allignement through the spatial filter(s) quite easily.
