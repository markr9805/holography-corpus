MarkCavin Fri May 06, 2005 1:41 pm #10
John, I don’t know for sure what my temperatures are at this point. I only have one thermometer for my last bath; for the others I use the Mark 1 Mod 0 finger. It reports the baths to be right at about body temperature. The reprocessed plate only went slightly red, but in the past when I was working with Triple Takes plates I had several that where processed straight from the soak bath to hot 100 ISO and they came out a deep redish.

Here is one of them.

![Image](http://home.wi.rr.com/cavin/owlDCG.jpg)

Of course I am still struggling with many tuning variables from beginning to finish.

I don’t have running water in the Lab, Okay okay, it’s a garage, so I after fixer I rinse in a container of water then soak in another container of warm water for no more than 6 minutes; to complete my process I only use two or three baths, either 10/10-50/50-100/100 or 50/50-75/25-100/100 or 50/50-100/100. I notice that if my last bath is as hot as I can get it, I get the least number of green blotches on average.

The thing I get to witness is how everything changes everything. How I mix, how I coat, how I expose, how long I fix, how long I soak. I suspect that if a sparrow farted five miles south of Cape Horn; this would affect my final outcome. Its Awesome!!
