Yes i purchased my laminator on the big"E", it was an older one with a hand crank (the motor wont burn out), well theoretically that is. i then built a small platform on each side to lay the plate on, then just crank it thru. Reversing the feed is easy to do too!

it will only hold 1/8" glass with film. I don’t mount my film until after processing and trimming is done, as its easy enough to screw it up in the dark!
