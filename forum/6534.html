Ed Wesly Thu Nov 08, 2007 11:01 pm #4
A trick to make the center of a shim thicker is to make an aperture or dam between the anode and the cathode in the tank. The width should be around half the dimension of the shim as a starting point. This way plating will occur faster in the center where there is better flow straight through the aperture, and less in the corners where flow is restricted.

Hope this helps.
