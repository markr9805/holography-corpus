I used EDTA bleach. Very bright transmission hologram. Brighter than my tests with Ultimate 08 transmissions. And the Fuji film is so much faster with Ascorbic TJ1 developer than the Ultimate.

TIP: Kris Meerloo gave me this idea. You can use Philips Amber Deco Led for safe lights. They are equipped with replacement fittings for normal halogen spot lights. The wavelength of these lights is between 585nm and 595nm I have three of them and bathe my lab in yellow light, pointed at the walls of course. I notice no fogging on the Fuji film.

Another pleasant aspect of the Fuji Film is that it can be used for all your lasers. So no experimenting required when you switch lasers.

The film is coated with gelatin on both sides. It index matches easily on 2mm glass with lamp oil. It kinda sucks it-self onto the glass plate.
