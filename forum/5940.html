I did some testing of two Edmund optics, green and red dichroic filters, one of which I plan to use for combining 532 and 633. The Edmund part numbers are 47268 and 47266. I wanted to know what filter would produce the highest power for the 633 component of the combined beam.

A typical result for the green filter, with the HeNe beam going through it (the transmitted beam was the beam of interest) was (in milliwatts):

Input beam: 37.7

Transmitted: 36.1

Reflected (off two interfaces, combined): 0.8

A typical result for the red filter, with the HeNe reflected off it at 45 degrees (the reflected beam was the beam of interest) was:

Input beam: 37.9

Reflected: 37.3

Transmitted: 0.1

So, an arrangement using the the red filter will result in slightly better performace as far as the HeNe is concerned. I was impressed by these dichoics, and the price of $45 each wasn't bad (they were 25mm in diameter).

The laser was a Spectra-Physics 127, and the meter was a Coherent Lasercheck.
