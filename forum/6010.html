JohnFP Tue Jan 23, 2007 11:32 am #2
Well, the biggest difference is, you can make an image planed hologram (one in which the image is in front of the plate or bisects the plate at some plane) but you do not have to make 2 holograms, a master and a copy. It is all done in a single step.

The difficutly with this technique is that if your final hologram plate is 4"x5" and your focusing lens is only 5" in diameter and the distance from the lens to the plate is 5 or so inches, then the viewable window will be small. The image will disappear as you move up or down or left to right past the virtual lens your are looking through. Think of the lens as being your master H1 hologram that is only 5" in diameter. Now if you made a 2"x2" hologram with a 5" lens about 5" away it would be much better. So you need a lens that is quite large in relation to the hologram size.

Of couse then there is the distortion caused by the lens. Look at an objec through a lens and move your head back and forth. You will see the image distort as you get near the edge of the lens. So this again suggest needing even a bigger lens.
