First, I'd like to say that I'm thrilled that this forum exists. It's good to be able to talk with others who have more than a vague idea of what holography is. I'm looking forward to posing some really stupid questions in the future.

So to start off, maybe not such a dumb question. What's the story on green DPSS lasers and usefulness in holography? My assumption has been that coherence length is relatively short. I've noticed quite a few people mention using them. Admittedly, I've been of the old school view that it ain't a laser unless it's a big tube in a box and sucks power like a

vacuum cleaner. (Not that there's anything wrong with that.)

I'm just getting back into this after many years, and then I made only crude holograms with a little 2mw HeNe from Metrologic. I'd like to improve on that.
Colin Kaminski Thu Nov 18, 2004 6:43 am #2
Most DPSS lasers are unsuitable for holography. The coherent C315 and all of the ring cavity DPSS lasers have very long coherence lengths. On the order of 50 meters or so.

The C315s are available for about $1000 used at 100 mw. The coherent compass lasers are available from 50 mw to 5 or so watts and are a ring design.
