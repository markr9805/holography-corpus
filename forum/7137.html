Hello Holographers,

Not a lot of embossed stuff here so I thought I'd post this. Most of my stuff is security and I'm not so sure about posting it on the net, even the older jobs. But this defiantly does not fall into that category.

This is a 2d-3d/3d hybrid hologram. It has 2d-3d and 3d elements. Let me explain this a bit, its crazy.

This company had bought an old missile silo somewhere in the desert. They put an argon in the bottom, aimed it up, and were planing on selling time modulating the laser to send messages into space.

I know, don't say it.

That company were idiots but it did result in a fun hologram. They used it somehow in their marketing stuff.

Your looking down into the missile silo and that's the beam coming out of the doors and going off into space to talk to the aliens. I included the video so you can see the beam better. If you look at my hands and the stuff in the background you can get an idea of how bright it is.

This image is about 4 inches square. The surface is mostly 2d surface stuff. In real life the doors are a very nice steel gray at the right angle. The ground is sand yellow and the rings are nicely colored. It didn't record great in the photo. The beam is the same green....at that same angle. Behind that about ¼ inch is another 2d layer, the concentric octagon. Behind that another ¼ inch is the base of the green beam coming out through the center of the other layers and going about 3 inches into real space.

Not nearly close enough to reach the aliens.

Jeffrey Weil

WOW, I forgot what kind of forum this is! Your right, I should have posted the details with the image.

Its shot with a He/Cd. The foreground, surface, is from 3 color separated contact glass masks used to make 3 one step surface gratings. The 2d-3d concentric octagon is from flat artwork again. This time an h1 was made and transfered. Then the 3d beam of light is a model H1 and transfered. You can't see it here but theres also a laser viewable encrypted image with the companies initials. Thats a one step image.

So lets add that up.

red surface

green surface

blue surface

octagon h1

octagon transfer

light beam h1

light beam transfer

encrypted image

So its the end result of 8 exposures. The final hologram itself is on polyether with pressure sensitive adhesive. Standard stuff.
