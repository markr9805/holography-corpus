The tube mount looks OK to me. I mean the ballast tank mount. The plasma-tube is the small diameter tube that runs the whole length of the resonator, and its mounts are not shown in the pics.

Tony Wed Jul 26, 2006 5:01 pm #6
Michael-

If you gently apply pressure with your fingers to the mirror mounts on the end while monitoring output power, you can determine if your laser needs alignment. You won't actually see any movement - it's on the order of fractions of a wavelength. If gently pushing, pulling, or twisting the mirror mounts results in an increase in output, then you could use an alignment. On the other hand, if no change is noted, your low output power is probably gas related.

I have a 107 that has heaters mounted to it. I suspect it is of slightly different construction from others, as it doesn't get up to full power until it has run for a few hours and is thoroughly warmed up. The diference between cold and hot is 20 mw vs 35mw. When it's cold, I can get more power by moving the mirrors with the above technique. When it's hot, the power only drops as I move the mirrors.
