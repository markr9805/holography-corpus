Recently Polygrama developed a dry photopolymer film (name CROMA) intended for a versatile high efficient photopolymer. Exposed with a good beam ratio for a reflection hologram, after UV curing it can be used with the same wavelength reply as a master, but after removing the special film cover the hologram is air developed to a level (depending on beam ratio quality) of a "DCG" appearance with a marked bandwidth increase.

The result polymer is stable to environment and scratch resistant.

Green (532nm) and Red (635nm) sensitive.

Small samples are available and the possible use substituting the embossed hologram marketing is real with lower cost.

Regards,

Sergio Oliveira.

Hans Tue Jul 22, 2008 10:51 pm #7
You made a small mistake Jem.

The power of the expanded beam is 197uW/cm^2. That means that the energy per square cm hitting the plate is 197.33uJ per second. (Assuming no Gaussian profile). The material needs 12 mili Joule per cm^2. So the exposure time would be around 12000/197 = 61 seconds.
