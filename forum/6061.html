> holography4fun: If then. i think the only need for a photo resist plate is when we need the hologram for a mass production?

No. A silver halide emulsion can be processed to form a strong surface relief image. A great many aspects of this have been exploited throughout the course of history of photography/lithography.

So yes, silver halide emulsions can be used for the making of embossed holograms principally (though in practice it might turn out to be a difficult task to achieve enhanced relief depth on such emulsions).

I think the procedure consists in forming a hard polymer layer on the gelatin relief. Over several steps that cured polymer is being silvered. At the end of the process nickel is electroplated on top of the layer.
