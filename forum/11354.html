I am still alive and kicking and making holograms. I relocated from Australia to France in 2018 to the Lot Valley in the south west of France.

A one man show is scheduled for mid 2025 which will highlight new holograms and include oldies such as the 9 metre 'I Dreamt I Had Something To Say' seen at the 2009 Shenzhen ISDH and perhaps the 1 kilometre 'Hololujah' about which I gave a presentation at the 2012 MIT ISDH. There is a YouTube of this presentation. My exhibition will also include paintings and photography.
