![Image](http://www.martymouse.net/tiger.JPG)

this is shot with 457nm 45sec exposer

dcg mix 1/13/100

film 2 days old (normaly 1 day it would play in yellow)

water rince 1 min temp cold

(blot with paper towl)

iso 35% 80 degrees 15 sec

iso 70% 80 degrees 15 sec

iso 91% 80 degrees 15 sec

hot air dry both sides 2 mins

micro wave 1 min on high (this eliminate streaks and evens out the color)

then seal hologram
