> Technodreamer: EDIT: "Computer Generated Hologram (CGH) program" Perfect!

Are you referring to this?

[http://www.medcosm.com/MedCosm_CGHMaker.html](http://www.medcosm.com/MedCosm_CGHMaker.html)

I played with it last weekend and couldn't get it to work for me. (Perhaps my printer is doing some mangling of the image or some other issue). In any case, it's going to be extremely low quality. You're going from holographic film that can resolve 127,000 lines/inch to 300 in a decent laser printer. (And before someone corrects me, that assumes 600dpi == 300 lpi, which is unlikely. ;')

But, you might actually be looking at doing something like how DeFreitas does his Holo-Portraits from multiple stills:

[http://www.holoworld.com/holoportraits/index.html](http://www.holoworld.com/holoportraits/index.html)

Essentially, you take several (more the better) pictures/renderings from a series of side-panning perspectives and make a hologram exposure in slices of each.

As you pan and do the different exposures, you can also animate. For example, as you view from, say, left to right the animated gears turn.

He does projection onto a ground-glass surface, but I was thinking of trying simple black-and-white laser prints a few inches from the plate, to start with.

-Phil
