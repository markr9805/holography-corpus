What strategies do you use for preventing light from entering the edge of the plate? Ive tried various ones, such as using a felt pen to blacken the edge of the plate, placing tape on the edge of the plate, and having a blackened popsicle stick positioned so that it covers the edge of the plate.

All these strategies work to some extent. The felt pen tends to come off in the developer, the tape sometimes comes off (I've got some holograms that are fine, except that there's a spot where the tape came off and blocked the reference beam). The popsicle stick method works mostly, except that it required modification of the jig.

Thanks,

--Ted
Dinesh Thu Aug 19, 2004 1:58 pm #2
I generally use tape but I wrap it around the edge of the plate to secure it. Another method is to baffle it off so that the edge of the baffle j-u-u-st touches the edge of the plate. In this method, you need to be careful of Fresnel fringeing.
tjohnson Thu Aug 19, 2004 7:01 pm #3
I always used a piece of mattboard or other stiff material about the same width and thickness as the plate, with a piece of black electrical tape just overlapping one edge by a mm or so. I insert this into the plateholder and the tape neatly shadows the edge. You can remove the adhesive from the overlap with naptha or something if it tries to stick to the plate.

> tpark: What strategies do you use for preventing light from entering the edge of the plate? Ive tried various ones, such as using a felt pen to blacken the edge of the plate, placing tape on the edge of the plate, and having a blackened popsicle stick positioned so that it covers the edge of the plate.<snipped>

For single beam Denisyuks, I want to absolutely minimize the edge shadow, so I use a black marker and clean off the edges with an isopropanol dampened rag before developing. I find the Staedtler Lumocolor 357 Permanent works well on glass, producing a good black edge with one or two quick swipes. I usually just do the two opposite edges - the first to prevent light from entering, and the second to reduce light reflected back into the plate.

I would like to find a marker that resists JD developers (it would also be useful for marking test strips), but have not found one yet.
