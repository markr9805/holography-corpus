I don't know if people have seen a 3D drawing tool called [Sketchup](http://www.sketchup.com). I have been using it for a long time and find it the best and quickest way of visualizing a 3D project. Best of all, the company was recently bought by Google, and the basic program can be downloaded free. It is available on Mac and Windows, and I thoroughly recommend it.

If we have Sketchup installed on our machines, then we can upload files which other users can rotate and look at from different angles.
