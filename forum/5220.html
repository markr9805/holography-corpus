Hello Everyone, Let me properly introduce myself first. My Name is Andy Lesniak, I am a visual effects supervisor in los angeles, working on commercial and feature film special effects, my primary job focuses on photo realistic rendering in 3d as well as color control for output to film.

I have been interested in holography since I was 8, I remember the first sandbox holography set I had, and trying to convice my parents to build me a sand table in the garage...

Since getting into the 3D animation field I have always wanted to have a holographic hardcopy of my work, but never did I have the time or money to start such an endevor. But now 20 years later, here goes. Thanks mostly to the discovery of this forum, I have my first stab at doing stereograms. Just want to let you all know how great of a resource this forum is especially for a medium such as holography, where it's a small but growing community. I hope now (after lurking the forums for a few months absorbing information) I can contribute to the community.

few stereograms (wmv9) format.

andyl Tue Nov 29, 2005 4:18 am #6
LaryD:

Thanks!, it's been fun... the table is actually just my dining room table, with a 2x3' piece of particle board with a thin piece of sheet

metal glued to it (from an old sears workbench), it's seperated from the table by a thin piece of 2x3' insulating foam.

I mostly did that to keep the table clean, and stick magnetic bases too.

Eventually plan to get a newport table from ebay or something

once I move to a new house that's not 10 feet from a busy street.

Surprising though, it's rock solid on an interferometer in about 3 sec after a car drives by.

The code was just slapped together from other bits of code I have written, I am working on cleaning it up as I test the system, it's mostly code from a stepper controller I built for my laser scanner.

I don't really have any marketing plans now, for the next year or so I plan on R&D to get to a full color/full parallax system. soo much to do soo little time...

Sergio:

Thanks a lot. yes, the 3d laser scanner is interesting it's a very forgiving system, I designed it to work on movie/commercial sets where you have 5 min on set before production kick you off, then expects you to fix everything afterwards. That scan was actually done with a office chair, home depo laser line level, and a dv camera.... my software does the rest..

I plan on posting all the code (client,server, microcontroller) soon can't say it will be tomorrow but sometime this month when I get a chance to do some code cleaning. (circuit schematics too.)

andyl Fri Dec 02, 2005 7:51 pm #11
lcd driver is just a shuttle pc, I wrote a program that will control the stepper and index the images to the lcd, as well as handle the exposure.

the lcd panel is just a regular pc lcd monitor 17", removed the back light and had to re-arrange the electronics a bit, in the end I get about a 10" x 8.5" display at 1024x768. only problem is I loose lots of light because of the polarizers on the lcd, and they can't be easily removed since they are laminated together...

I just recieved my shipment of VRP..

and finished rendering out some new stereograms, as well as some

stereograms from live subjects with the camera rig that I built, it basically

let's you do bullet-time (matrix effect) the camera will fire in sequence

from left to right, right to left, or both sides in, and you can set the delay in milliseconds. can't wait to see how it turns out... going to have to wait till

monday or tuesday since family is in town..

stay tuned...
