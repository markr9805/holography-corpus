Do you get better results with hene lasers as opposed to semiconductor ones? I've made a substantial number of holograms with diode lasers, and some with a surplus, randomly polarized laser hene. The image quality seemed a bit better with the diode lasers, but I suspect if I had a decent hene (i.e. properly polarized) that I could get good images with that. The wavelength is somewhat shorter on the hene lasers, so I suspect the film I'm using (pfg03m) might require less exposure than at 654nm. Also, the color of the final reflection hologram might be more yellow. I read the earlier messages about using sugar to swell the emulsion - has anyone used TEA with PFG-03M?
