OK, here's something I've been wondering about. What is the current state-of-the-art in red sensitive DCG materials? I've got a 639nm laser diode now and want to start shooting something soon. There was an old trick of adding methylene blue (I think) to a standard DCG and getting decent quality holograms. I've only got about 25mw but still, if I work with small plates, I should be able to get good single beam reflections, no?
Tom B. Sat Sep 17, 2005 5:50 am #2
State of the art as far as I know is Jeff Blyth's Ye Olde Red-Sensitive DCG (1989)

[http://cabd0.tripod.com/holograms/id4.html](http://cabd0.tripod.com/holograms/id4.html)

If TMG is unobtainable for you, you can still get some interesting results at 25 mW with plain old Methylene Blue sensitized DCG - it's just much more difficult with the lower sensitivity.
