Bird of Prey Fri Feb 04, 2005 5:59 am #1
I spend most of my day driving down the road, so I have lots of time on my hands to think about things.

It occurrs to me that the average truck or car might be a ready made vibration isolation platform, like the kind used in holographic experiments. It has a large mass, which dampens the low frequencies; and it sits on tires - which in turn sit on inner tubes - which should dampen the high frequencies.

Although I would not want to try this on my first hologram, just as an interesting experiment, eventually I want to see if it's possible to shoot holograms on the hood of a car. It might even be possible to make a hologram of the car itself; if the plate is mounted on a separate vibration isolation platform, some distance away. This assumes of course, that you have the coherence length to make this possible; and that the experiment is done on a dark, moonless night.

Other things might have little or no vibration as well; such as a statue of something in a park.

On the subject of making your own holographic plates . . . .

It seems to me that the main advantage of this, is that they could be scaled up to any size. And I see no reason why a holographic plate couldn't be made of plexiglass; which would reduce the weight for very large holograms.

You could have life size, full scale holograms of things.

And if the vibration isolation platform were scaled up as well . . . .

Then it might be possible to make a full scale hologram of something like a motorcycle; every bit as large as the original, and hang it on your wall. For something this large of course, if a reflection hologram were made of it, you would need something a little brighter than a small lightbulb or LED.

And it might be necessary to paint over any shiny reflective surfaces, since these do not appear to make good holograms.

An old Civil War cannon might make a good full size hologram.

And there might be other large objects as well.

This would require a more powerful laser of course, than the kind you would find at the 5 and dime store.

Just a few random thoughts.

And imagining the possibilities . . . . 8)
