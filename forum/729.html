That's a good point, it could be better. You can push that really far too with some components depending on their shape.

I used to buy 670 wave plates. I could get them surplus for about 5 bucks each. Since they were 12mm I could tilt them in the beam so far they worked fine with 632.8! I would use a polarizer turned to absorb the beam. I would then turn and tilt the wave plate looking for the best extinction. I used those for years without problem.
