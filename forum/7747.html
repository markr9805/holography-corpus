jeff-blyth Thu Feb 19, 2009 10:52 am #1
Colin, In my humble opinion your commendable effort to bring order and tidyness to the Forum by subcatagorizing is at odds with the problems that we all have with needing to do quick scans of things in our busy lives. If you let ALL the questions go into a General Holography dump then we could all just quickly scan the lot and click onto anything that catches our eye. As it is now, segregating off Silver Chemistry, DCG , beginners questions , photopolymers , laser questions, pulsed Holography is just irksome. We are just going to miss more and more postings and lose interest in helping and learning from others.

We are all interested to some extent in most of these catagories. So I think that segregation is a mistake. Let us hear what others think.

Jeff
