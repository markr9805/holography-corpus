Just wanted to add to what Martin said . Ammonia is definitely BAD to presoak our ultra fine grain plates unless you want to turn them into coarse grain plates. Here's the explanation of why you should'nt use it instead of TEA.

Ammonia........NH3

TEA................N(ethanol)3

TEA you can see has the 3 Hydrogens on NH3 fully substituted.

Hydrogen-nitrogen bonds have a devastating effect on AgBr.

You can easily use concentrated ammonia instead of hypo to fix plates as was done over 100 years ago, (I still do it if I want to avoid all traces of sulfur complexing on my holos for special reasons.)

So if you were to use more dilute solutions of AgBr solvents (ie complexing agents) such as hypo, thiocyanates, ammonia or halide solutions , you get the finest grains dissolving up and depositing themselves on the larger grains (energetically more favourable because more surface area on small grains gets substituted for less surface area

but more lattice energy on the chunkier crystals. --Dinesh can explain it better I expect.)

Jeff

P.S.and even strong TEA soln has some complexing power (reason why you cannot market it pre-TEA'd or after a few days you get poor coarse grained material ).

Dinesh wrote:

"Why? And what's the effect? Is it some form of redox, or does it create some form of complex? I've never understood why TEA sensitizes AgBr and swells it. Is the sensitizing action part of the swelling action or are they independant? "

According to the Bible (Theory of Phot. Process 4th Ed TH James )

There are several reasons why some tertiary amines may act like this.

The charge barrier effect due to excess Br- ions around AgBr grains causing developer negative ions such as ascorbate or hydroquinonate to be prevented from approaching the Ag+ ions. TEA apparently also breaks the AgBr-amino-gelatin bonds, it also has a mild reducing action.

The swelling is due to the alkalinity breaking up the internal charged arrangement of amino acids in the gelatin (zwitterionic bonding) and is common to all proteinaceous stuff hence taking the alkalinity to extreme we can clear our drains and ovens with caustic soda.
