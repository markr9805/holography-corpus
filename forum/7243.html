BobH Sun Mar 30, 2008 3:14 pm #6
I made a four inch diameter on-axis reflection hologram for a sculpture titled "Aladdin's Lamp". The image shows the two hands of the figures in the sculpture in "holographic space" with two planets way deep inside the scene. The hands are right up under the plate with their arms extending down. The planets are about 18" below the plate. The illumination source is a microscope illumination lamp buried in the sculpture about 18" above the plate. The plate itself is the top of a lamp that the two figures are rubbing with their other hands.

The set up was very complex, with many beams needed to illuminate the sculptures of the hands, which were shot as painted wax objects. The planets are about 8" in diameter. There is lots of parallax between the hands which are up close to the plate and the planets that are deep down beneath. No projection, but lots of movement as the viewer changes their position. One copy of the sculpture is now on my front porch. One went to the person who commissioned it to be done by the artist Tyrone Chollar, who also has the third.

I also realized last night that the first holographic product was also an on-axis reflection hologram. The King Vitaman secret compartment ring had an image-planed on-axis reflection hologram of the king, done on unbleached silver halide film. They mounted it with the emulsion on the outside of the product, exposed to the elements! Amazing some survived almost 40 years now.
