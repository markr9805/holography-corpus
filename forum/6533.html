JohnFP Thu Nov 08, 2007 11:38 am #3
You question is still not pefectly clear to me but;

Depending on the vibration frequency and the design of the table, I would imagine you could get some vibration amplification from the table. In that case, having the isolation on the floor would elliminate your problem. But do you really want to work on the floor? For many years we all have been working on isolation tables at standing height. So if you have a problem, I would think fixing it on a the table would be better.

The key to a good homemade isolation table is Rigidity, Mass and Isolation. You want the table top to be very rigid so that if a vibration does move the table, the entire table top moves together and there is no flexing or bending or torquing. Thus the optic stay in perfect RELATIVE position to one another. Mass for the table top is good to dampen out the higher frequencies. And of course the correct isolation medium is needed.

If you can expain what you are seeing on your inteferometer, you are studying that and not just guessing right?, and describe or show with pics what type of ilsolation table you have then we may be able help you find the faulty link.
