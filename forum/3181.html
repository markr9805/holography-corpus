The lamp linked below is being sold on Ebay. I bought one a while back because I thought it might be a sample of the Japanese lamps of the type made in the '90s (can't remember the name of the company), and to have a sample of this type of thing in my collection.

This is far and away the worst Multiplex type hologram I've ever seen, and I've seen some really bad Multiplex type holograms! The gaps between each exposure are as big as the exposures. The image is noisy, distorted and overexposed. The lamp itself is also a cheap P.O.S., not worth $20. Nice packaging though. With garbage like this on the market, there's no wonder pictorial holography is in the tank!
