Thought I would chime in on the intro board and make my presence known. I am a business owner and student. I've been doing various technology jobs for the past 20 years including military service. I currently market commercial laser systems. I am also a Ph.D. student in physics at Michigan State University.

I've had a lifelong interest in lasers having built my first laser at 14. I've always been aware of holography but frankly never thought I'd be able to do it since it would require an investment to acquire the mirrors, beam splitters, table, chemicals, etc. As such, I have generally spent my time building or acquiring various types of lasers just to have them.

At the moment, I have four working lasers. I have two hene, one DPSS CW 532, and a pulsed Nd:YAG homebuilt system. I also have a bunch of laser diodes, and other laser components in various states of assembly.

Every now and again you will find a part or two listed on eBay from me. These are usually YAG components. I don't offer any of our commercial systems on eBay because when you are an honest company you simply cannot compete with the scammers. I cannot tell someone that a system is 1 watt when it only peaks at 1W on startup but settles down to 500mw, for example, when it stabilizes. I can tell scammers from a mile away because I can get wholesale information on quite a few systems. What really chaps me is companies that state their lasers do not fall under Title 21 CFR1040.10/11 for what is clearly a complete and working unit. I am very suspicious of any company that makes that statement for anything other than parts. I guess somehow they think they can avoid liability. More often, however, they have altered the laser (usually upping the output power at the expense of the pump diode) and thus the laser cannot meet the original FDA registration. This doesn't mean they are exempt from Title 21, it means they have to re-register the laser system but, of course, they don't and sell it anyway.

Well, that's me in a nutshell. I suppose my next step is to build a table. Long exposures for me are probably out of the question since I am about 60 meters from a major road with heavy truck traffic. I'll peruse the posts, however, and see what others have done.
