Hi,

I am posting a question which, I am sure, has already been asked somewhere in forum.

I have been using a diode laser to make holograms for about 3 months now. I am noticing that the red, elliptical spot of laser has two rather prominent, nasty looking black spots with fringes around them (see attached picture). One of these seems to have developed in last few weeks (besides some faint ones). So far I am able to make holograms using the unaffected portion of the beam. But I wondering what is cause of these black spots. Dirt on laser diode? Or imperfections in the diode itself?

The diode is being operated on 3Volts using D-size batteries as recommended by the supplier.

Thanks,

Sanjay

Colin Kaminski Sun Dec 07, 2008 4:48 pm #2
Each bullseye pattern is a single spec of dust. As long it is not on the diode window its self then it is easy to clean. To clean the window look for instructions in the wiki. It will take repeated tries to get the diode window clean unless you are lucky the first time.
JohnFP Mon Dec 08, 2008 3:55 pm #3
Try to first blow off with canned air.
BobH Tue Dec 09, 2008 1:27 am #4
Those spots are way off to the side. I wouldn't do anything. Only clean the laser when one of those spots is interfering with the beam. Never clean a laser unless you have to.
Ed Wesly Tue Dec 09, 2008 7:03 pm #5
That's the basic philosophy of cleaning optics: DON'T! (Only if you have to.)
