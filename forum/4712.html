That is a hologram I'm doing for a co-worker of his Minas Tirith model from LOTR. It's a great model for holography as it's got tons of detail.

Vital Statistics:

un-treated PFG-01 film exposed HeNe for 30 seconds.

Developed 50 seconds in JD2, bleached with EDTA

Lit with a 20W halogen
