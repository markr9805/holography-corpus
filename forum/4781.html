The Michelson Interferomter is one of the most useful tools a holographer can use to determine a variety of parameters prior to setting up his holographic recordings.

It can determine;

Table stability

Mounts stability

Laser mode stability

Laser coherence length

Destructive air currents

Destructive temperature changes

I highly suggest if it is possible and one has the optics required, set one up and play with it, study it, learn it.... be the Michelson.
MichaelH Mon Apr 25, 2005 10:29 am #2
In what way does the michelson help you determine or evaluate polarization?
JohnFP Mon Apr 25, 2005 10:50 am #3
I took it out as it requires more then just the interferometer and a polarizer would be easier. But it is beneficial to see. I have taken a 1/2 wave plate and put it in one leg of and interferometer and rotated it to look at the change in contrast of the fringes. If the fringes go from high contract to low or no contrast then you have a polarized laser. The blacker the constructive areas go to black the more polarized your laser. If you take the 1/2 wave plate and put it in one leg of the Michelson an rotate it and the fringe contrast does not change the the laser is probably not or very slightly polarized.
