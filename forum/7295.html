I was thinking to day about holography and the common person ... and seen from time some people who visit the site not knowing what a hologram is or what it can do....Many of us have gone on ebay and seen how loosely the name "hologram" is tied to anything (laser) or 3d. And some of the great misconception out there pertaining to holography. You know like holograms at Disneyland Haunted Mansion....

in this thread "Introduction to holography" our members can post some answers to common misconception on holography or its process

To begin I remember hearing about Holograms at DisneyLand in the Haunted Mansion.

and going there so excited to see holograms ...I went away disappointed seeing no holograms ... Most of the illusions at the haunted masion are done with mirrors and large sheets of glass like the dancing ghost... The crysta ball at the end is just a outline of a face in side the ball with a image projected on it giving it the look as if its a hologram... But sorry no holograms at DisneyLand
Justin W Fri Apr 18, 2008 5:06 pm #2
Great thread, Danny!

Even as a beginning holographer, I've apparently developed something of a proprietary sense about the whole business - I've invested enough blood, sweat and tears slaving over a hot laser to be almost a little offended at the way the word hologram is thrown around.

Bless you, George Lucas for the original three movies, but...

I'm anxious to see what others will add to this list - I'll cite the two (in my uneducated opinion) leading offenders of holographic imposterhood - I see, time and again, in conjunction with a wave of disappointment, these two being referred to as "holograms":

Pepper's Ghost - a reflection on plate glass mounted at an angle - smoke and mirrors at best... a parlor trick.

Spectral imagery - amazing volumetric displays to be certain, but about as related to holography as I am to a turnip.

I hope this thread stays alive for a while and makes at least a little progress toward dispelling some of the common myths that run rampant out there!
