Colin Kaminski Sun Jan 30, 2005 10:31 pm #5
Wow, That went cheap. Just for the record there is no problems using spherical mirrors off axis. Parabolic mirrors have better correction on axis but at some critical angle they perform worse off axis. If you get a book on optics (Like the one by Hecht) it will give you the equations to evaluate a mirrors performance.
