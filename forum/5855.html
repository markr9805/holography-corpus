Colour tuning a DCG plate.

Thus far I have never bothered to increase the temperature of my alcohol baths because room tenperature gave nice broad-band reflections.

LOW PRE-SWELL TEMPERATURE: Very sharp and narrow band blue-green hologram.

HIGH PRE-SWELL TEMPERATURE: Broad-band holograms. Sharp, but with all the characteristics of a broad band hologram (not much depth).

I am now beginning to get interested in making my MBDCG's more narrow-band. But at the same time I would like to have some control over the reflection wavelength.

Is it possible to tune a hologram with the temperature of the final bath? Or should all alcohol baths be at the higher temperature? Can I use a alcohol bath that has a higher temperature than the pre-swell bath?

Is it possible to colour tune a DCG hologram in such a way that it reflects in nearly the same wavelength as the original laser light?
JohnFP Fri Oct 27, 2006 11:46 am #2
> Is it possible to tune a hologram with the temperature of the final bath?

I have not seen this.

> Can I use a alcohol bath that has a higher temperature than the pre-swell bath?

Yes

Temperature and time in the acohols mofifies the drying rate. I usually use higher temperatures and very quick soaks in the alcohol to achive broadband white holograms. Lower to medium temperatures and onger baths for narrow band.

Color tuning can be achieved by varying the amount of AmDi or KDi in the emulsion and/or by altering the tuning bath concentration of alcohol and and/or exposure time. You should be able to get narrow band reds to blues.

But I will have to say I have always been able to reprocess and color tune toward the blue and never toward the red. That is, to get a red narrow band hologram from green or blue it has to always happen on the first processing pass.
