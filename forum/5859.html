Go to Michael's craft store and buy a 'FloatFrame' picture frame - its 2 pieces of glass (or plastic?) held together at the edges with plastic channel that clamps the glass firmly.

Being designed to hold 2 pieces of glass together, it does an excellent job of holding 2 pieces of glass together (with a piece of holographic film in between, of course).

You can cut it to the desired length with a razor knife or electrical wire cutters.
