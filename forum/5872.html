I have found an article that shows a recipe for a PVA+Acrylamide+MethBisAcrylamide+Acrylic Acid+TEA+MB solution that has a sensitivity of 50mJ/cm and a resolution of more than 3000 lines per mm.

The article calls for a PVA with a very low molecular weight of 9000 and states that PVA with higher molecular weights don't work with resolutions higher than 2000 lines per mm.

The PVA with the lowest Molecular Weight I have found thus far is 3-98. That has a MW of 16000 and is still much too high.

Does anyone know where I cound find PVA with MW 9000?

(p.s. The article claims a real-time diffraction efficiency of 85% in a 20um film, exposed in red HeNe light!!!)
