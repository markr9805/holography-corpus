> John Sonley: I am planning to make a 10 inch plano convex lens with a focal length of about 12 inches using Bonda casting resin - polymerised styrene.Has anyone attempted this before and can you give me practical guidance as to a suitable mold and cooling to prevent thermal cracking during the polymerisation. I found styrene based resins very messy (to say the least) to work with: cooling thick layers turned out to be awkward to control. In addition cracks may form due to local heating. Regarding the health risks the polymerized stuff may keep releasing styrene for several months.

You might consider a liquid lens. Vidar Hegdal, who used to post here, seems to have made good experiences with liquid lenses. See: [http://www.techsoft.no/holography/lenses.htm](http://www.techsoft.no/holography/lenses.htm)
