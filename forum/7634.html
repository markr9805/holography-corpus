OK, from the other thread it seems as there might be some interest in a group project. I have a few ideas that have run across the table over the last few years that I think we could do as a group. Here is what I am thinking, we produce a set of masters for a desk display that we share among us. Everyone can borrow a master and sell the result through a forum website. Each person receives the entire proceeds from the sale of their work and is required to sign the work. If someone can not produce an order then the customer is notified and is offered the work of another holographer.

The sale price would be about $50 and could be made in silver or DCG.

Thoughts?

How 'bout lamp shades. They would need 4 transmission holo's that are narrower at top then bottom to give it that "skirt" look and would allow the bulb light to hit the holos on an angle. The image could be abstract taking into account the image would be color smeared. All the end user would have to do is us an unfrosted bulb.

Or

How 'bout mailbox numbers. You know, the ones you see on your mail box posts. Once headlight hit them, they could be dazzling. I have seen them before during a search on the internet (after I proposed the idea) but never could find a patent.

Or

If we had a good glass cutter/blower/worker available, how 'bout designing a dual wall glass for drinking (wine, etc) in which we could put a cylindrical hologram in between the walls and thus it would look like the image is floating in the middle of the glass. Sets would be off differnt hologram so persons could identify their glass, sort of like those charms for wine glasses.
