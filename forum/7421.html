Hans Wed Jul 09, 2008 3:46 pm #3
> 1. Is the best chemistry the Ultimate chemistry?

The Ultimate developer is not expensive and by far the easiest to use. All you need to do is take a few ml from the stock and add water. You can develop several plates in one dilution and once diluted it stays usable for the whole day. You can also get very good results with Pyrogallol + NaCO3. One effect that you get with the Ultimate safe developer is that over exposed plates will be milky after bleaching. Pyrogallol developed plates don't have this effect when over exposed. You will find that with Pyrogallol the plates are much more forgiving with relation to over and under exposure.

The Ultimate bleach is simply fantastic!! I think it is the same as Ferric Brillant bleach.

> 2. Is it very critical to keep the material cold for storage?

Yes. They last longer when stored cold. Old plates tend to be fogged. You can revive an old plate by immersing it into the bleach solution for a little and then re-sensitizing it in a Ascorbic acid bath adjusted to PH5 with NaCO3.

> 3. Is it best to Dev hot or normal?

I am not sure what is best. I think that as with any developer, as long as you always use the same temperature the result will be more consistent.

> 4. How about consistency, has anyone noticed differences among batches of plates?

Kris is the true expert on Ultimate material. I think he mentioned a few times to me that as plates age they become a little more sensitive. New plates from different batches behave similar. Kris....?

> 6. HAS ANYONE SEEN PROBLEMS AFTER HOLOGRAMS ON ULTIMATE HAVE BEEN SITTING AROUND FOR A WHILE? THIS IS VERY CRITICAL. Like bad print-out, emulsion deterioration or any signs of color shifting, strange odor, anything "CHEMICAL" happening to the

The holograms I have here are still fine after two years.

> 7. What is the story with this material, the history...where can I find this info?

No idea. Yves Gentet is very secretive about many aspects of this material. He does sell the developer and bleach separately, but does not divulge about the contents. He does make Panchromatic plates bu does not sell them.

At first there used to only be Ultimate 15 plates. They were already on par with the best materials in the market and give about the same brightness as Filmotec HF53. Now he added the Ultimate 08 plates. They are by far the best plates I have ever seen. He advertises that "the first hologram you make on them is the best hologram you have ever made". I agree wholeheartedly. Unfortunately they are far too expensive for regular experiment work. This is one of the reasons I keep trying to make my own emulsion.

> 8. I have made a lot of holograms, reflection holograms and transmission holograms on Agfa plates and film. Some of my holograms are from 1988. They have been stored in really bad conditions...and they look really good for the most part. How do people feel out there about the Ultimate...(on glass), does it seem as hearty as the Agfa?

I have only seen Denisyuk type reflection holograms on Ultimate. They were all made by Kris Meerlo. Before he used to make holograms on Agfa. All the Ultimates are much much brighter than any of the Agfa ones. Even the Ultimate Denisyuk's that could be considered to be a failure are still ever so much brighter than any Agfa. Ultimate Denisyuks are brighter than Agfa H2's.
