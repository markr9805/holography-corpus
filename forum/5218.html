HoloTalk Update -- 11-25-05

Enjoy this latest broadcast of HoloTalk with guest Matthias Söllner of Germany (and also HoloM of the online holography forum at [http://www.holographyforum.org](http://www.holographyforum.org)).

Matthias talks about his lab and his work, and discusses his recent experiments with making home-made stereograms.

A replacement for the ground glass is also covered -- material called "Translum". This is a material suggested by Steve Michael -- who is also working with his own stereograms in his home lab.

To listen to this latest show, please go to:
