Colin Kaminski Mon May 30, 2005 8:04 pm #2
You will do better with the mirrored one for H1 to H2 because of the ratios needed. You might even be able to get away with a piece of glass as a 90/10 beam splitter. You will be surprised how much light you need to send to the H1 to get your beam ratios correct.
