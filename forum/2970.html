I need to generate a 'phase' object. Something with thickness or refractive index variations in it. I will a 'transmission' hologram a carefully designed phase object.

Colin Kaminski Tue Dec 21, 2004 7:25 pm #2
A standard process would be develop, rinse, bleach, rinse, photoflo, squeegee and dry.

PFG-01 and JD-2 are easy to use.

You might try one of the Lab chemical suppliers:

[http://www.labpakchemicals.com/contacts.asp](http://www.labpakchemicals.com/contacts.asp)
