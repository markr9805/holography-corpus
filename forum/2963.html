Dinesh Sat Dec 18, 2004 8:41 pm #3
If you're just drying it, just place it in photo flo and let it drain. I dunk the plate for about 30 secs. in roughly 2% photo flo and let it sit over a paper towel vertically or sometimes on a corner. A 4x5 dries in about an hour. If you're in a hurry, you can wash the plate in running water for about 1 min and use a hair dryer and then re-wash the plate for about 5 minutes and dry with photo-flo. With this method, don't hold the dryer over any one spot for more than a few seconds. With agfa you used to dry in alcohol for about 1 min, but Slavich doesn't like alcohol drying. Since BB plates are supposed to be very similar to agfa, alcohol drying may work for BB.
