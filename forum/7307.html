Silver-halide sensitized gelation (shdcg)

1 Higher reolution can be attained with special prepared gelation layer,{Marakova, Pancheva, Kandilarov & Sharlandjiev, 1982a}

For this, glass plate coated with a 20um thick layer of gelatin containing 1 per cent of (CH3COO)3CR as hardener are soaked in a solution of AgNO3 and bathed in an alkali halide solution so that very fine grain of silver halide are formed in a gelatin layer.

2 The excess alkali halide is then washed away and the layer is sesitized by treating it with a solution of dichromate and a optical sensitizer.

3 process as DCG

the first part looks like Jeffs DYI process.......

It looks like in this process the silver halide is passing the energy to the chrome and causing it to brake down and crosslink the gel... it claims 10x higher sensitivity and efficiency of 50 percent
