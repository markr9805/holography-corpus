Made this as a gift for some extended family that Susan and I are going to visit this weekend (annual family reunion).

[](http://www.dragonseye.com/gallery/v/family/MichaelH/Holograms/4thwFireworks.jpg.html)

The flag, pie, shake and hot dog were shot first

[](http://www.dragonseye.com/gallery/v/family/MichaelH/holography/flag_pie.jpg.html)

and then replaced with a piece of glass that had fireworks painted on with glitter added and a second exposure was made.

[](http://www.dragonseye.com/gallery/v/family/MichaelH/holography/fireworks.jpg.html)

The fireworks appear to be coming from within the flag and bursting above it. The lighting on the curve of the flag works out such that it almost looks like the light is coming from the fireworks.

The matte was home-made by printing out a flag at 45% transparency and glueing the matte and printout together. The matte was then cut specifically for this hologram.
