Hey Tony, your holograms are looking great, I mean fantastic.

First I would say gold is not what Rallison was getting. He was getting white broadband holograms (and usually a little milky in which he descibes as scatter and precipitation). Usually the byproduct of using film that was very young, in his case 5 minutes. Probably something like this. [http://www.holograms3d.com/PhotoGal/Gen ... Pseudo.JPG](http://www.holograms3d.com/PhotoGal/General/LightHousePseudo.JPG) Second, in the post you posted he stated droping the broadband hologram in 75% then 100%. He does not mention putting it in water first. I would give it a good couple of minutes in the 75% till the surface is evenly saturation with the water that is in the 75%. You will actually see this.

A small tid bit. How do we know if a gold hologram is truely broadband or a narrow band centered around yellow? If you will, take a green and red laser (or lcd) and illuminate the gold hologram with each separately at the recording angle and realize how bright the image is under each of those wavelength.

> Hi john I would say tony are a bit broad band, just like dinesh gold dcg where broad band with a gold cast,

Hey Danny, good to hear from you. I will have to disagree a little and here is my reasoning.

If you look at our now "famous" color triangle you will see our Yellow/Gold to be right at about 572nm. Now does a "broadband" hologram that replays between 552 and 592 have the same color tone as a narrow band at 572? Also if you had a broad band that plays from right at 572 with a little replay towards the blue (460) that will whiten your yellow a bit and make it more gold. So I disagree that you can tell that a gold hologam is broadband at all really, it may replay exactly at yellow and a little at blue giving gold, or it can replay only green (530) and only red (610) and give a gold yellow.

This has always boggled me, So unless you actully test for wavelength replay, if you don't see any white, you cannot be sure a gold (yellow) hologram is broadband at all. It may be slightly broadband (which most DCG's are unless you really control your variables) but I don't think you can automatically call a yellow/gold hologram broadband no matter what some may say. A truely broadband hologram in my understanding is white or pastel looking. If you get pastel then you are at least reflecting a little of all primaries as in the other side of the light house below. The pink pastel show the hologram is reflecting red, green and blue (the only way to move that way on the color triangle). On this side the hologram is reflecting more red then green and blue, on the other side (white) it is reflecting all colors in ratios such that we arrive at the white center of the color triangle.. JohnFP Tue Jan 13, 2009 5:03 pm #14
Hey Tony, you up for some experimentation?

Take one of those gold hologram and put it in some cool water with about 2% AmDi for a few minutes. Then reprocess the hologram in Fixer, water and alcohols. Have the fixer, water and all alcohols except the 100% cool (no higher then room temp). The 100% can be hot. Use 4 alcohol baths 35, 70, 91, 100%. Leave in each alcohol bath for at least 2 minutes, longer in last if needed. You should get a narrower band and a blue shift. I even sumize the cooler the AmDi, fixer, water and first three alcohol baths are the narrower and more blue shift you will get.

I have not failed. I've just found 10,000 ways that won't work.
