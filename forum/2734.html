Dinesh Thu Sep 23, 2004 2:59 pm #2
Briefly, the originator is the person or organisation that produces the actual hologram, ie the resist plate. Some of the larger companies such as ABNH ( [http://www.abnh.com/](http://www.abnh.com/) ), Crown or Applied Holographics ( [http://www.aotgroup.com/](http://www.aotgroup.com/) ) have dedicated holography labs that employ full time holographers. These companies are also manufacturers. Smaller companies or individuals make the hologram and then send it off for electroforming and printing. Hologram converters are the people that take the resist plate and electroform it. This is the process where the actual hologram is placed into a Nickel solution and the surface profile is transferred to a Nickel plate. This plate is then once more electroformd and the resulting, final electroformed plate is used to mass produce by embossing.

There's a pretty good explanation here: [http://www.jrpusa.com/](http://www.jrpusa.com/) (look under "products" in the side bar). The IHMA is an organisation for mass production holography companies: [http://www.ihma.org/IHMA/index.html](http://www.ihma.org/IHMA/index.html)
