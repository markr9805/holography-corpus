Not that particular one but another, probably similar one: Sharlandjiev/Markova, On fabrication of large format optoelectronic elements. It's here: [http://joam.infim.ro/JOAM/pdf5_1/Sharlandjiev.pdf](http://joam.infim.ro/JOAM/pdf5_1/Sharlandjiev.pdf)

That Bulgarian group used triphenylmethane and thiasine dyes for red-sensitized DCG. Their system only consists of dichromate and one of these dyes – so this is nothing like Jeff's TMG system.

I assume speed is inferior to Jeff's system but has the advantage of being fully compatible with “normal” DCG.

The commercially available special diazonium compounds are stable for long periods at room temperature. Onlywhen exposed to short-wave light (maximum absorption between 300 and 450 nm) does photolytic cleavage of the NNarrangement take place in the molecule, followed by the substitution of hydroxyl groups. As a result, thedecomposition products manufactured in this way are able to form water-insoluble complexes with PVOH.
