Hi guys I'm fairly new to Holography and I recently purchased a Coherent Compass 315M-100 DPSS 532nm laser. What are your guys' opinions on this laser and do you have any tips for using it in holography? Also does this laser require any special techniques or equipment?
Ed Wesly Wed Feb 08, 2012 11:38 am #2
I am very happy with mine, wished they had been around ten fifteen years ago...

The only thing special is to use recording materials designed for the green wavelengths, like Harman's Green sensitive plates or Sphere-S GEO-3 orPolygrama Photopolymer. Otherwise the optical equipment is the same as for red or blue laser holography. Might need wavelength specific retardation plates (half-wave plates) if you use a polarizing beamsplitting cube.

Have fun!

Ed Wesly Wed Feb 08, 2012 5:04 pm #4
What a coincidence, I just shot some Ultimate over the weekend! Sorry I forgot to mention them in my reply above since they are such a recent addition to my repertoire. The U08 green is similar to the GEO-3 from Geola, and it’s hard to tell the difference in side-by-side comparisons. The tricky part is acquiring either of those from the manufacturer.

Since you’ve got power to burn and assuming you are going to do Single Beam Reflection holograms, I’d got with the U08 for the ultimate in signal to noise ratio.

The 70 nm shift means that the emulsion is prepared so that the replay color of a reflection hologram has a wavelength 70 nm shorter than what you began with. So 532 – 70 = 462, which is a nice navy blue, but it might be not as easy to see as a hologram replaying at 532, which our eyes see a lot easier. Helium-Neon holographers like that shift to go to 563 nm replay color, another green, which is easier to see than the original red at 633 nm. But I prefer viewing the red color over the green.
