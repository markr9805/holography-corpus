> dave battin: This week we spoke about various schemes on how to run/cool our 315 coherent lasers.Here’s a picture from an old postCan anyone else post a picture of what his or her 315 looks like?

Here's mine...

![Image](http://www.bobdbob.com/~tjohnson/misc/315mhead.jpg)

The laser head and power supply are both on the same heatsink. I havn't noticed the heat sink getting warm enough to warrent a fan. The laser head is mostly obscured by the shutter mechanism, but you can see the end of the ribbon cable on the top edge.

The shutter is a volt meter from radioshack with a piece of foil from an anti-shop-lifting tag glued to its needle (its very reflective and springy foil). The end of the meter can be seen on the left, with the green and white wire leading to it. The meter is angled relative to the laser, to reflect the beam into a piece of welder's glass, which is the black part on the front left side. The rest of the shutter enclosure is cardboard and lots of electrical tape. (Yes I should have used better materials, but I got tired and wanted to /use/ it... (-: )

![Image](http://www.bobdbob.com/~tjohnson/misc/315Mcontrol.jpg)

The two cables (D15 on the bottom edge, and 3 pin power line on the top right) go to a box containing a 15VDC power supply and error indicator LEDs. The black and yellow tape is really there to cover 3 holes in the recycled box I used. (Building this is what got me tired enough to skimp on the shutter...)

Tommy Wed Oct 18, 2006 1:48 am #9
> dave battin: nice set up tommy, so you are able to vary to output? my dc power supply is running with an on and an off switch ,i would sure like to be able to vary it's output.my laser also has a small "dogle" that hangs off the controllerwhen its warmed up both LEDs come on (about 5min.)and the laser stops mode hopping.

Yes, the power can be adjusted with a 10K pot as a voltage divider, to feed 0 to 5V to pin 13 on the 15 pin connector. Just set the voltage and ground pin 8. 5V is available on pin 11, with the caution to draw only 200ma.

I usually just leave it at 100% output though, to get the shortest exposure possible. The only time I've turned it down was when it started oscillating. The controller got into a weird state and the output oscillated at about 1 hertz.

I got all the information about how to hook up the laser from the laser faq. [http://www.laserfaq.org/](http://www.laserfaq.org/)...
