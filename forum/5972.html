MarkCavin Wed Jan 10, 2007 12:54 am #1
After seeing what Hans and others have done so far with mold coating, i decided to give it a try. I think it holds huge potential; the coating is as smooth and even as i have seen in any of my previous coatings. Unfortunately some of the emulsion sticks to the Rain-X plate, but i suspect it is because i am using a recycled commercial plate treated with Rain-X and that could be a big blunder on my part.

Exposure 4 minutes

fixed 15 seconds

3 mins water bath room temp

2 mins 70% IPA room temp

2 mins 99% hot IPA

Not a very good hologram, but after a whole series of failures, its good to be getting images again.
