Hi folks,

I have been testing with VRP-M (Geola) plates for transmission holograms using HeCd Laser - 441.6 nm @ 150mW O/P.

I have some difficulty in getting PBU - Amidol here for bleaching. I tested with Ferric nitrate bleach although its written its for Pulsed lasers. The result was not good. The plate has black color after developing(CWC2) and bleaching. I put it in Bleach soln for about 5minutes, still the black color is not disappearing.

Is it bcoz of the anti-halation backing as suggested by John Klayer in his previous thread? Or is it coz the bleach is insuffient ?

Anybody have tested with this with bleach other than Amidol?

Anybody can tell me wats the diff it make in using diff bleach for continuos laser and pulsed laser? One thing i note is that the developer suggested is same for two lasers.

Please reply.
John Klayer Mon Dec 22, 2008 11:34 am #2
The VRP-M does not have the anti-halation backing. The VRP does. I've been using JD-2 with the dichromate bleach. Michael Harrison has had good results with ferricyanide bleach.
Ed Wesly Mon Dec 22, 2008 3:15 pm #3
I have been successful with using a Fe-EDTA bleach. The one time I used this stuff I was nicely surprised that it was significantly brighter than Agfa Millimask in the identical situation, which was masters for holographic stereograms shot at 458 nm.

In a similar vein in the Beginning Holography thread I can believe that there could be a null result using VRP whatever flavor for SBR. I could never get one on Agfa 8E56HD; the grains are kinda chunky and there is a lot of scatter. But split beam reflection worked OK. (Notice I said worked OK,not great.)
