Tom B. Sat Jan 30, 2010 9:07 am #2
Gelatin is a really hairy organic mishmosh and Bloom is only an approximate measurement. That being said, the least surprising result would be

that the mixture would be somewhere in between 200 and 300 and, since there is likely no drastic structural difference for the two strengths,

the transition from 200 to 300 would be smooth.
