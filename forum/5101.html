I finally got to try putting a freshly coated plate on a piece of sheet magnet and letting is set up that way. Not really knowing what I was trying to do or look for, I just wanted to try it. I guess I wanted to see if the gelatin particles, water, AmDi or intermediate form of either or all combined exhibited any reaction to magnetism. I only made one plate that way, and due to the unpredictability of DCG fabrication, the observation below is by no means conclusive.

I will say that I did use another plate as a base line. That is, I took a plate and veil coated it. I then laid it flat a non metallic and non magnetic surface to cure. I then coated another plate with the same veil method and laid it flat on a sheet magnet to cure. I exposed and processed both plates the same.

On the plate that was cured on the magnetic sheet I observed arrow head deformations in the gelatin all facing the same direction. They were about 2 or 3 mm and looked almost exactly like this character ">". There were lots of them that covered about 70 % of the plate. I did not see this in the control plate. The deformalities were not real evident until the plate was viewed with an extreme lighting or viewing angle, then it was evident in not only the emulsion but the image.

I would like to take some iron filings and a sheet of paper and cover the sheet magnet and see what the magnetic pattern looks like. Then on my next plate, place the plate on a known pattern and see if there is any correlation to the two.

I am not sure how the magnetic sheet poles are oriented but I may also try to use a bar magnet or other known magnet with defined poles on just a small portion of the plate.
