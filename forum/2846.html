This is a very simple yet very stable setup for single beam holograms. Since the weight of the object holds it to the plate there is very little chance for movement.

![Image](http://www.designerinlight.com/holo/ccac2b.jpg)

![Image](http://www.designerinlight.com/holo/ccac4b.jpg)

Notice the wrist weights on the stand. The object table is simply a 8x10 picture frame that can slide in and out of the stand. The cart has a metal top so things can be attached with magnets. I am certain this whole stand can be rebuilt for less than $200 including the cart/cabinet to hold everything. The two mirrors are placed with magnents. A white card is placed in the location of the plate and the concave mirror is adjusted until you find a dust free place on the mirror and even illumination.

Smooth reference beams are very important in this process as the redevelopment stage is very sensitive to any variation in reference intensity. We have a large colection of glass plates of different thicknesses in order to space objects away from the plate. Some of the students have used them to make very interesting 2d-3d holograms from graphics arts.

redevelopment stage? How are you processing these holograms?
Colin Kaminski Fri Oct 29, 2004 2:02 am #3
Presoak in TEA or distilled water

dry

expose 8 (if TEA) or 14 seconds if water

Rinse in distilled if TEA 90 seconds

D19 for 90 seconds

Distilled for 90 seconds

Rinse tap water for 3 minutes

Bleach in Phillips "no Patchy Haze" 90 seconds or until clear

Rinse in tap water for 3 minutes

Put in Ascorbic Acid redeveloper (100mg/L) for 30 seconds

Turn halogen light on until tomato colored (still in redeveloper)

Rinse in tap water for 3 minutes

Photoflo

Dry

I have been able to make holograms as bright as my brightest example (Tom's award winning PCG hologram) on PFG-01!
