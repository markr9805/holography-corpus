Use dark bottles for storing your light-sensitive chemicals (glass or plastic doesn't matter unless you're using a chemical that eats away at plastic). This will make your chemicals last much longer.

Some of these chemicals include:

D-19.

All the A portion of the JD developers.

Ascorbic acid (likely any developer with an ascorbic acid component).

Others more chemically inclined that I can add to this list.

You can combine using dark bottles with [this tip](http://holographyforum.org/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=17184#17184) to gain even more life from your solutions.
