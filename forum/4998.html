HoloM Fri Aug 26, 2005 2:20 pm #1
Last week I started a small journey through Germany and the Netherlands to visit people involved with holography to talk to them and to see some holograms in exhibitions.

First I drove to Aachen and visited Lutz Pietrusky, a hobby holographer (forum member LP) , we made some denisyuk holograms together and talked a lot about holography.

![Image](http://4c-s.org/v-web/gallery/albums/album18/lutz.jpg)

The next three days Lutz and I continued the journey together.

The next day we visited a museum of photography in Monschau, and in the afternoon a small holography exhibition in Pulheim near Cologne.

![Image](http://4c-s.org/v-web/gallery/albums/album18/pulheim.jpg) ![Image](http://4c-s.org/v-web/gallery/albums/album18/pulheim2.jpg)

There are about 20 holograms from the former Lauk Collection on exhibit in the basement of an old monastery. There are a few interesting ones for example from John Kaufman. In the evening we did a few denisyuks again.

The next day we drove in the Netherlands to visit Kris Meerlo (also a member of the forum). There we had a nice talk, made a denisyuk hologram together and had a nice dinnner with him.

![Image](http://4c-s.org/v-web/gallery/albums/album18/kris1.jpg)

We stayed over night in the Netherlands and on our way home to Aachen, we made a stop at the Dutch Holographic Laboratory. On my way home from Aachen I drove to Jena in Germany. There is a optical Museum with a small holography exhibition.

![Image](http://4c-s.org/v-web/gallery/albums/album18/jena.jpg)

There are also about 20 holograms to see. Really interesting ones like "Lucy with a tin hat" from Patrick Boyd, "Chrystal Beginning" from Stephen Benton, or "Digital" from Advanced Holographics for example.

The whole tour was a very nice experience. Thanks a lot to all people I have met!
