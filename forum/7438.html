The Polygrama liquid emulsion SM-532TR were recently characterized by a Czech team of Technical University of Liberec and Czech Technical University in Prague by Ing. Vít Lédl and Ing. Milan Květoň.

It deals with exposure sensitivity, refraction efficiency and examples of practical interferometry is applied. Will be published in the SPIE proceeding PHOTONICS PRAGUE 2008. As soon as I got the final author paper and authorization I would send a copy for those interested for private use by a message onto this forum.
