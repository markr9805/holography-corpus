For those of you wishing you could get together with other holographers and talk shop, there's another PCG meeting coming up in May.

[http://www.pcgholography.com/](http://www.pcgholography.com/)

You don't have to do anything to be a full member of the PCG other than participate. There's also no cost to attend the meeting, all you have to do is get yourself to the meeting place although if you can give a talk (doesn't have to be anything elaborate, just share information with us) or demonstration that's greatly appreciated.

To register contact John Klayer or myself through here or the PCG forums.

Hope you can make the gathering!
