Martin Mon Dec 17, 2007 8:41 am #11
What would be interesting in my opinion would be to weakly pre-expose a DCG layer to short wavelength light - possibly to some small UV lamp or even a photographic flash. Then have a look at the absorption spectrum.

The idea behind would be to verify the assumption of that French research group (Bolte et al.) that upon 380nm - blue exposure the Cr(VI) is reduced to Cr(V) which was said to be relatively stable and to show an absorption peak around 520nm.

Perhaps that kind of pre-exposing DCG to UV-blue might actually speed up green sensitivity of the system also.
