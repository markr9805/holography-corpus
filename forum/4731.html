You may be able to minimise the glare by a gentle application of a mild sandpaper on the cockpit. I sometimes found that spraying shiny surfaces with hairspray kills the fresnel reflection a little. The idea is that the large particles in the aerosol scatter light making a more diffuse illumination. This doesn't always work becuase the aerosol particles aren't large enough. Also, the aerosol sometimes evaporates during the shot and so you apparently get a motion spot.
