Ed Wesly Wed Jan 17, 2007 5:23 pm #3
Using a pair of lenses in the typical telescope configuration will yield a fixed size ratio.

In the incredible book, "History of the Photographic Lens" by Rudolph Kingslake there is a prescription for something called the Donder's Zoom Telescope. (I googled it but no luck.) If a negative lens is put inbetween the two positive elements of a telescope, different sizes of output beams can be obtained by varying the center lens's position. This way you can match size and divergence by tweaking, instead of ordering lenses and hoping for the best.
