Hello Everyone,

For this kind of thing the best person you can talk to is Spatial Imaging in England. They've been producing dot matrix machines probably longer than anyone else in the western world. Ask for Rob Munday. He's a great holographer and has all the knowledge and experience to make the device your looking for. Hardware/software, etc....

He's worked with polymers and silver so don't think Spatial is just an embossed company that can only do dot matrix. He can make your printer better than anyone else I know. You also won't be working with someone who "thinks" they can make a proper machine. Rob has the experience making different types of origination equipment that proves he can do it well.

But, if your going to put down this kind of money, and it's going to cost a bunch no matter who makes it, don't trust any one source, even me. Ask around about Spatial, I'm sure you'll only hear good things. Rob has one of the best reputations in the biz.

Jeffrey Weil Wed Dec 01, 2010 10:03 am #20
> Sergio: Jeff do you know about someone that is making a photopolymer contact copy machine for mass production (or pilot small machine) of photopolymer holograms?

Hello Sergio,

Spatial could develop one for sure, but there might be polymer units out there already. Many people have tried to make a biz using those types of machines for security and other things.

Probably the best person in the world to talk to about that is Han Bjelkhagen. He's been involved in a few different projects that used mass repro machines for polymer. And your best path to Hans is our own Ed W. Hans also has a page on Ning that you could use to reach him.

Another person that's been involved a bunch with this kind of thing is Günther Dausmann. He's an excellent researcher in Germany. His company is HSM. [http://www.hsm-holography.de/](http://www.hsm-holography.de/) Which is now owned by [http://www.hologram-industries.com/](http://www.hologram-industries.com/) and Hugues Souparis.

Gunther might be tied up in contractual things that would prevent him from working for outside people. I'm not sure, you'd have to check with him on that yourself.

You would do better starting with Hans. He's much more approachable that Hugues. Talking to Gunther is always worth your time even if he can't make your machine. He's a very smart guy. I always learn something new when he's around.

Jeff Weil
