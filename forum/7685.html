But I actually did this as the coherence length of my laser is so long.

[http://www.holograms3d.com/Illustrations/H2SetUp.jpg](http://www.holograms3d.com/Illustrations/H2SetUp.jpg)

Here is how I did the elephant. Not exactly what you're asking but I wanted to illlustrate how I used non standard configurations to keep stability in check. By ever so slightly tweaking the single spatial filter I could adjust the beam ratios of the H1 and H2 beams. Move the beam just a little up and more light goes to H2 and less to H1. I did this in the two previous set ups also. The simple way, and you can observe this, is to slightly move the mirror directly before the spatial filter. This allowed me to move the light while keeping the spatial filter in allignment.
