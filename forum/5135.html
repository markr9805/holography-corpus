Tom B. Sun Oct 23, 2005 2:32 am #2
Nice article! I've done this but was too lazy to write it up. My shutter flag is pretty large to accomodate an uncollimated beam, about 2x3" made of balsa wood with sticky-backed velvet glued to it to minimize back-reflections. The flag is stuck to the pivot arm with double-sided foam tape.

Some drives are more suitable than others. Often a lot of the bearing support mechanism is integrated with the case and more work would be required to use it. I think older drives are likely to be easier to use. It is amazing how little power is needed to swing the arm. I use a 1.5V AA cell wired to a 3 position double-pole toggle switch to provide bidirectional drive. A 470 ohm resistor limits the current to the minimum needed to swing the arm. I put some rubber bits over the limit stops to minimize vibration, but this part still needs work.
