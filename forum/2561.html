I'm not sure if this reply is most appropriate for the general RudiB/amazing HOE thread or the Lighting thread. Ahh, here goes anyway.

Over the weekend I found a somewhat serious flaw in my latest P-51 hologram. When I tilted the top of the hologram towards me a number of interesting serpentine shapes leapt out of the surface of the film. I figured I'd missed a mount in my carding off and set about finding it. What I found instead was that the far edge of my master glass was reflecting light directly onto the copy plate. Because that edge had a somewhat uneven break it was creating interesting shapes.

Something to think about when creating a non-traditional display hologram (IE, one that tries to break out of the copy-of-an-object box) is to work in all different kinds of reflection and refraction to create unique/interesting shapes in the final hologram.
