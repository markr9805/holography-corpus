What they're referring to is that any really useful passive suspension is by necessity underdamped. At the corner frequency the thing will actually resonate. This is also true, only to a lesser extent, for systems that rely only on feedback. The best method is to combine feedback with a forward term using a sensor on the floor. The amount of cancellation afforded by a forward correction is not determined by Bode's inequality but merely by sensor performance and circuit precision.
