Nice job in getting the PDC off eBay. Is the "info" you mention above the paper by Shankoff? The way I take it, that -10C figure is only given in relation to synthesizing the PDC itself, so I don't think it's relevant if you already have the PDC. I would just mix it like regular dichromate (I guess), and expose to 532nm or shorter. But note the following comments in the 1968 Applied Optics paper by Shankoff (you don't have it??):

"....Unhardened gelatin films were made from aqueous solutions of gelatin and 38 wt % PDC using a Fisher dipcoating apparatus."

Please don't ask what he means exactly by 38 wt % because I don't know!!

Also, more importantly, "....significant dark reaction occurs in PDC sensitized films in several hours."

Other than that, good luck!
