Go with the 10mW red initially. The power will come in very handy in reducing your exposure times.

If you haven't already, look at the Integraf holokit (see the discount link above). It's a great kit and has everything you need to get started.
Tony Fri Aug 19, 2005 6:27 pm #3
I agree with Michael's advice. Also, you didn't say if the red was Hene or diode. I would recommend a Hene to start with, as it is much more stable and you won't have issues with mode hopping. There are more varieties of film/plates available for red. The 5mw green laser pointers are usually too unstable to make holograms with.
