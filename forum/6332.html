Hello All,

I have a Melles-Griot spatial filter that I would like to integrate into my holography setup. It is my understanding that the spatial filter should be the last optic in the beam path before the plate or film. However, my optics table is so short that I would need a couple of "folding mirrors" to allow the beam to expand to the size of my plate.

Do you think it would be worthwhile to set up the spatial filter and contaminate the beam quality with a few moderately clean mirrors, or would they end up negating the effects of spatial filtering?

Thanks,

-Andy C.
MichaelH Fri Jul 06, 2007 2:09 am #2
In the end, use what works.

I too have to fold the beam and I haven't had any problems. Just keep your work area clean and use compressed air to blow any dust off before shooting.
floh Fri Jul 06, 2007 8:43 pm #3
folding is no problem as long as the mirror is clean. Even small pieces of dust are not a big problem. Just try it.
