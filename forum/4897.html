> HoloM: yes tom you are right, the picture is taken in sunlight, and i had problems to get a good photo because the cam always took the surrounding light as reference, could try to make a better photo

After reviewing this thread I would like to make a suggestion to HoloM.

I have the same problem with my digital camera. What I have found to be very beneficial is to get a piece of white paper and lay it next to the hologram at an angle where the sun or other light source diffusely reflects off of it, about the same angle as the hologram. Put it at the same distace away from the camera as the hologram. Then hold the camera button half way down while focusing on the white piece of paper. Keeping the button half down, which locks in focus and light meter reading, move the camera from the white paper to the center of the hologram and finish pushing down the button. This also allows two fine adjustments if not satisfied with the shot. One, you can focus and lock with only half the white card in the viewfinder or a portion thereof. This allows different recording light levels. For a better focus you can move the white card nearer or farther from the camera by a couple of mm thus locking in a better focus.

Sorry I didn't reply with these suggestions earlier, I missed your post.

Hope this helps.
