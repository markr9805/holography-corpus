Colin Kaminski Wed Jan 17, 2007 5:03 am #3
I tried shaking my developer in a circular motion for a while. I was doing coherence length tests by making 2.5" x 2.5" exposures on film. I would then change the temperature of the diode and try again. I did not have wler's cool tools! I was trying to make similar map but one hologram at a time.

I found when I swirled the tray, it was round, I would get density on the edges before the middle. This is backwards of the exposure energy so I abandoned it and started rocking the developer in a back and forth motion. Waves.
