I thought I posted this but I will try again. I am posting here because the soaking process did not red shift but broadbanded thus the other thread would be misleading from title.

On the left

488nm

30 second exposure

Fixer, water, alcohol

On the right

488nm

30 second exposure

Water 25 seconds

Fixer, water alcohol

Both exactly the same except the water prior to fixer soak.

If you look on the right near the head in the white hologram you will see a type of whiteness and it starts to blur into the angels head on the white hologram. It seems to be where the emulsion is thin. The emulsion actually seemed to crystalized. It's not that is it cloudy but it reflects the light off the emulsion like a white haze. The more more of an angle the replay light the more diffusely reflecting the haze is and the further into the hologram it moves (all the way over to about half way across the head where it is blurry).

Also what I noticed is the hologram on the left, when dried with the hot air just dried and got brighter and shifted colors. The one on the right exibited that white crystalization (not cloudy) that then cleared up and went away to yield the hologram.
