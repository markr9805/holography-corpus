BobH Thu Dec 16, 2010 8:50 pm #3
How difficult is it to get at the output coupler face? The beam is very small coming through the OC, and centered. Make a tiny little pad of paper (like 1/2 the diameter of the OC), hold it with the tiniest long skinny tweezers you can get or make, and give the OC a few wipes with acetone. Use the smallest possible drop of acetone. Use the paper pad for only one wipe. Be reeeeaaallllll careful you don't hit the surface with the tip of the tweezers!!!!!!
