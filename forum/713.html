> holomark: I am eliminating the development bath as the culprit as I used the same container for developer bath and two rinse baths (befor and after bleach). The second hologram didn't start the development process until the first completely finished. The first plate developed has the larger less dense stain than the second (pictured above) For this reason I have eliminated foriegn substance in developer bath tub.

It may be that you're not wetting the plate fast enough when it first goes into the developer, or not agitating the plate well enough in the developer. Using the same container for washing that was used for the developer isn't a good idea. It results in uneven developing. My guess is that you may be using too little developer in the tray or going into it too slow. It's very important to get the plate fully into the developer as fast as possible and be certain the fluid covers the entire surface as the plate is put in. One second of a bubble leaving part of the plate dry for that second and you could easily see a problem.
