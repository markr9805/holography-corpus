I have been in contact with Filmotec. They make red (HF 65) and green (HF 53-slow speed, HF 55-medium speed) holographic film. At the moment you can only order green film but they will make red film available later. The interesting thing is that they are working on producing a panchromatic film (HF 456). They say that this film, and the red film, will be available at the earliest in the end of this year. In the e-mail they state that you can buy samples of all the emulsions.

Uli Tue Nov 13, 2007 3:06 pm #4
For my current setup, with a He-Ne, a 532nm and a He-Cd (442 nm) and using a 40x microscope objective, I have a 15 micron pinhole and is working perfectly ok, clean round and uniform beams. I can make 8"x10" Denysiuk holograms. But it is all down to the power, beam diameter and beam divergence of your lasers. You will need to try. I hope this helps.
