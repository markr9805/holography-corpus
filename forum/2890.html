a thread concentrating on the building/construction of components would be helpful to fellow holographers....

![Image](http://www.geocities.com/greenpagoda/holobasetop.jpg)

the bases use on my table are made from 2" PVC pipe with plaster of paris and lead shot embedded and a 3/8" dia.steel rod

![Image](http://www.geocities.com/greenpagoda/holobase.jpg)

i used a router on the bottom to trim away a portion to makea three point

mount.

i made about 15 of these bases and they are easly adaptable to hold any optics
