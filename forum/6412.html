Kiffdino Thu Aug 23, 2007 10:10 am #1
Yesterday night I made all in all two holograms.

1 with the russian PFG-01.

and 1 with the german filmotec orwo HF 65.

One could think i might be prejudiced, favouring the german film,

but it has been so much easier working with the HF 65.

Setup: Simple Denisyuk. I build an exact copy of the frame Saxby described in his book.

Fitst, the index matching to glass. Due the thinner film of the HF65 it is very easy to laminate it onto the glass.

The PFG-01 on the other hand gave my quiete hard time, and after all the index matching did not work. So I had to put it between to pieces of glass.

Both films have been TEA pretreated ( 10%) to get a colour shift towards green.

Both films got a 5 sec exposure with a 20mW Hene and a processing in pyrogallol and bleaching with EDTA.

The outcome:

HF - 65 bright green holgram with very nice contrast.

PFG - 01 feeble image. slightly orange.

Anyone else on the forum, who tried the HF - 65 yet?

You should give it a try.
