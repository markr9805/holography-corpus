Hi Dave -

That is a good paper. I recall reading it around 2000-01 when my startup was engaged in reverse-engineering the DMD control electronics for our volumetric 3-D display. (It was prior to the DMD being a "catalog part.") We needed to pump 6,000 XGA-resolution frames each second out of them, at 100% duty cycle if needed.

Nowadays, several companies sell DMD controller boards, like Vialux in Germany, with their "ALP" boards. There is also a domestic group whose name I forgot.

I started writing this note and then forgot your question. What's up?

Oh - we don't make displays anymore but we are featured in this week's Economist here: [http://www.economist.com/sciencetechnol ... d=14299526](http://www.economist.com/sciencetechnology/tq/displaystory.cfm?story_id=14299526) -- the 3-D display at top uses 3 DMDs at 6,000 frames/sec at XGA resolution each, for a total of a 100 million voxel display.
