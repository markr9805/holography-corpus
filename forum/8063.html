Bernadette and I are offering our new studio in Poulsbo, Washington on a for-hire basis. From November 30 to January 17 (excluding xmas to new year) we will be offering our staffed studio Laser Reflections) for $500 a day plus the cost of materials. We will limit access to (2) days per artist and we will produce only H1 transmission masters on silver halide. As I have often voiced - we feel that there are many extremely capable stable-tables out there on which to produce H2's - here's your chance to gain access to our pulsed Nd:YLF/glass H1 mastering system - we'll supply the light and the darkroom processing and you'll leave with ownership of the H1's. Our only stipulation on subject matter (assuming technically feasibility) is no "straight" portraits (we've done more than 200 to date). If need be, we can supply you with Agfa plates up to 16.5" x 12.5" and we're hoping to offer 24" x 16" filmplates by mid-December but no guarantees that the larger formats will be ready in time for this introductory offer. If you're interested, contact us to schedule a slot. This (our sixth) studio just about an hour West of Seattle is promising to be our very best and we're hoping to bring in new artists along with established holographers - anyone with an imagination and a desire to produce high-quality "aesthetic" holography.
