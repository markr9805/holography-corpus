glajciorz Sat Oct 11, 2008 2:17 pm #1
Today I tried to improve SSY1's beam quality.

SSY1's q-switch was kept in place, no rod/q-switch in the cavity during rough alignment.

Here's how the typical beam shape for SSY1 with its planar and fixed HR and OC mirrors looks like :

[](http://www.glowfoto.com/user_imageredirect.php?iid=2279841)

Photographic paper was placed 30 cm from the laser. Mark diameter is 5mm.

Both mirrors were placed on indexing pins, so no alignment is possible.

My first experiment was to mount both planar mirrors in kinematic mounts, cavity lenght changed from 10cm to c.a. 15cm.

After trying to align plano-plano resonator I got something like this (again 30cm from the laser):

[](http://www.glowfoto.com/user_imageredirect.php?iid=2279843)

As you see no improvement.

And here's what I got after replacing planar HR with 2m spherical one and lenghtening cavity to 40cm (paper 30 cm from the laser):

[](http://www.glowfoto.com/user_imageredirect.php?iid=2279857)

Mark diameter is c.a 1mm.

Paper 1 m from the laser:

[](http://www.glowfoto.com/user_imageredirect.php?iid=2279819)

c.a. 1,5mm mark diameter

And 2 m from the laser (in the center):

[](http://www.glowfoto.com/user_imageredirect.php?iid=2279820)

c.a. 2,5mm mark diameter

No intracavity iris was used and the laser was fired just above the treshold (4,5J).

As you see guys - so far so good

Adam
