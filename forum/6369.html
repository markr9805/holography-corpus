Colin Kaminski Thu Aug 02, 2007 2:06 pm #5
No, the depth is limited by your images. However you can eliminate the lenses. What you would do is use a laser to project the image onto ground glass. Then you would tape off the holographic plate so a vertical slit was showing that was one 1/4 of the width. Repeat this process moving the slit until all four images are recorded.

This is often caled a holographic stereogram or multiplexing. If you carefully match the camera movememnt with the slit size you can make quite real looking 3-D images.
