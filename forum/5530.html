3po.3po Fri Aug 11, 2006 7:38 am #8
> Tony: How do they magnify the fringes?

In fact it is possible, not to magnify the fringes, but to magnify the object as in conventional microscopy. The curved object wave (curvature induced by the microscope objective) interfers with the reference wave in off-axis geometry on the CCD camera. If the reference wave is a plane wave, the interference fringes are curved. During the reconstruction process, it is possible to compensate numerically for this curvature (see for example [http://ao.osa.org/abstract.cfm?id=87835](http://ao.osa.org/abstract.cfm?id=87835)).

It also possible to introduce the same MO in the reference wave as in Linnick configuration, to have the reference and object wave with same curvature (see for example [http://www.opticsexpress.org/abstract.cfm?id=86034](http://www.opticsexpress.org/abstract.cfm?id=86034)), but this solution is difficult to achieve perfectly because the MOs should be very precisely adjusted in the same configuration.

Finally, instead of a MO in the reference wave, a lens can be introduce in the reference arm to produce a reference wave curvature on the CCD camera very similar to the object wave, and the residual curvature can be compensated numerically.

On the other hand, digital holographic microscopy has the opportunity to perform digital processing such as:

- Numerical filtering to suppress parasitic interference and unwanted image order (to keep only virtual image for example) ([http://ao.osa.org/abstract.cfm?id=62321](http://ao.osa.org/abstract.cfm?id=62321))

Compensation for aberrations [http://www.opticsexpress.org/abstract.cfm?id=89909](http://www.opticsexpress.org/abstract.cfm?id=89909)

You can also find more information about the digital holographic microscopy on the research group Microvision and Microdiagnostics of LOA in the EPFL:[http://apl.epfl.ch/page12232.html](http://apl.epfl.ch/page12232.html)

or on the website of the start-up company Lyncée Tec (Lausanne, Switzerland), which commercializes Digital Holographic Microscopes:[http://www.lynceetec.com](http://www.lynceetec.com)
