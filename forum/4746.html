> DannyBruza: has any one used Dektol rather than D19 for film or plates?

Probably. I didn't see the exact formula. On [http://www.darkroomsource.net/chemistry.shtml](http://www.darkroomsource.net/chemistry.shtml) I found this:

> Kodak's D72 Formula DEKTOL750 ml Water3 g Metol-Elon45 g Sodium Sulfite Anhydrous12 g Hydroquinone80 g Sodium Carbonate 1-Hydrate2 g Potassium Bromide AnhydrousWater to make 1 literUse diluted 1:2 at 68°F for 45 seconds to 3 minutes (or longer).

Maybe around 1984 we started testing tons of commercial photographic developers (my friend Ralph working in a photo supply shop then). Surprisingly, a great many of them provided equally good results on 8E75. Due to its moderate price and ease of handling, we chose Agfa's paper developer Neutol. By the way, its 10x concentrate is formed by:

potassium sulfite........312g

potassium hydroxide..15g

potassium carbonate.90g

hydroquinone................45g

phenidone.....................1.5g

EDTA..................................8g

potassium bromide.....10g

water....................................1L

Later, we run additional tests with special holographic developers like CW-C2 etc. but Neutol remained our first choice. At least on those "crude" 8E75 emulsions (PFG-01, VRP may perform similarly), our tests showed much more important differences from one (film) batch to another.
Ed Wesly Tue Apr 12, 2005 10:54 am #4
Here's D-19:

Water at 50°C (125°F): 500 ml

Elon (Kodak's name for Metol1): 2.0g

Sodium sulfite, anhydrous: 90.0g

Hydroquinone: 8.0g

Sodium carbonate, monohydrated: 52.5g

Potassium bromide, anhydrous: 5.0g

Compare to D-72 (close enough to Dektol)

750 ml Water

3 g Metol-Elon

45 g Sodium Sulfite Anhydrous

12 g Hydroquinone

80 g Sodium Carbonate 1-Hydrate

2 g Potassium Bromide Anhydrous

Water to make 1 liter

There's 50% more of both developing agents in the D-72. I have used Dektol for holography, except dilute it two part developer to one part water to get the similar concentration of Metol and Hydroquinone like in D-19. pH is about the same. Works like D-19.

Another surprise is that Kodalith Developer works well on Agfa and PFG-01
