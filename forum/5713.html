Hi all

I have to confess to being one of the 'lurkers'. I am based in the UK and I am VERY new to holography. Six months ago I basically didn't know holography even existed as an amateur hobby.

It all started when I was browsing Ebay one evening with a glass of Southern Comfort in hand. I came across someone selling a HeNe laser - The hour was late, the alcohol had eased my pain and I ended up placing a bid for that laser. I can't even begin to think why I even placed a bid, but I ended up winning the auction.

Suffice to say that's the last time I browse Ebay with the SC anywhere near !! Anyway, the laser arrived, I plugged it in and marvelled at the tiny red dot (sad I know!). This fascination lasted about 10 minutes before I had started to hunt for something else to do with my 'new toy'.

I turned to Google and started to search... And Holography came up! I thought to myself 'now there's an interesting subject and so I started to look up as many holography related sites as I could find including this one. I joined as a member and introduced myself.

Thanks to this forum I have learned loads of information in quite a short space of time. I am now well and truly hooked on what I see as wonderful new hobby. So far i've only produced some simple Denisyuk holograms, but the feeling of pride seeing that first faint image with black parallel lines running through it will never leave me.

Since then i've been getting more and more successful with each attempt, my holograms are getting brighter, the parallel lines are gone, my squegeeing is improving and i've learned how to use TEA.

I have to confess to having had the help and advice (by email) of a professional holographer here in the UK. But and it's a big BUT, without this forum and the wiki I wouldn't have been able to learn anywhere near as much as I have in the short time i've been involved.

I read the forum most days and marvel at the knowledge that some of you people have, I read and learn in the hope that one day I may be able to make a useful contribution to the group without making a fool of myself. I try not to ask too many questions until I have searched for the answers myself, that way i'm not seen as someone who is always taking and never giving anything back.

I can honestly say that were it not for this forum my interest in holography would not have been stimulated in the way it has. SO, a BIG thank you to you all for all your help and advice, even if you didn't know you were giving it.

Cheers

Jem
