Personally, I have trouble keeping optics planted in sand from drifting...

Or should I say - hadtrouble until I took to affixing said optics not to a stake of some kind but instead a spray paint can lid. Small mirrors and various optics, hot glued to spray can lids and twisted into the sand stay where you left them!

BobH Mon Nov 05, 2007 3:01 pm #3
Some have used old coffee cans filled with sand, and planted at strategic positions in the sand box. You then put your optic mounting tube (or post or lid as suggested above) into the sand in the coffee can. This allows you to dig and shift the sand near the optic without affecting its position, and stabilizes the optic in the sand.
