Contact copies are pretty easy to do unless it is a security hologram. They are very difficult to produce.

If you posted a picture of your hologram it would help us decide.

Do you want it in DCG or SIlver Halide?

Do you want it in film or plate?

Is it a rainbow, or full color, or monochromatic?

Do you want the final color to be the same?
