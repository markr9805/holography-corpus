but just got a sample of some ultimate, here is the first shot

using a C315 @ running @ 30mw (turned it down)

spread to just over 4x5, 1 sec exposure... I'm thinking it might be a little over exposed.

other info. develop 6min ultimate developer, bleach 2min.

gotta say if it wasn't for the haze/fog it's as bright as a dcg.

another note... tried developer/bleach on vrp-m, results are close.

vrp-m is a dark red which blocks majority of the beam, resulting in dim hologram. Split beam with a close 1:1 ratio is almost as bright as the ultimate.

config. Denisyuk, plate resting on object.[/img]
