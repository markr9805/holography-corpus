I am reposting here as I would like to try to keep discussions to a minimum in the Tips and Tricks section as asked.

I am sorry for not being totally complete. If anyone has ever had a laser pointer and is using any type of laser to make a hologram I assumed the obvious would not need to be stated. Yes the laser beam should be expanded with almost any type of lens and then can be shone on the wall near the area needed to lit.

I had two. One I used as described above and another as a "Safe Flashlight". I could carry around. I used a plastic lens (quality does not matter) that I taped right on the pointer. I kept the pointer with its batteries and this allowed me carry the pointer around and shine the light wherever additional lighting was need without any worry of fogging plates.

> Dinesh: I just use standard 25W bulbs with solid, as opposed to transparent, glass

I personally stayed away from using any type of "standard" light bulbs with VRP emulsion. I actually did not know a standard light bulb chould be used with VRP without a filter.
