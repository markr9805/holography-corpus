Din Thu Jan 16, 2025 12:51 pm #2
It looks like object motion. Temperature fluctuations are unlikely, considering the cat seems to be made of a non-metallic substance. Non-metallic substances have a low thermal expansion coefficient. From the look of the stripes structure, it looks like the back end of the cat moved, the interference lines look like they're radiating outwards circularly from the hind leg.Is the cat simply placed on a platform, or is it glued down?

Other possibilities include touching, placing, the cat and allowing too little, or no, wait time. Your fingers transfer heat to the object, and you have to wait a little before exposure to allow the heat from your fingers to dissipate.

How long was the exposure? The longer the exposure, the more stable the object must be.

A useful trick is to gently tap the object with your fingernail. If the resulting sound sounds tinny, you'll probably get motion lines.
