DJ Mathson Sun Jun 17, 2007 11:30 am #2
In the original configuration when the 315M came from some kind of graphics machine, the laser head was mounted on a small heatsink with a small fan. Together with the controller they were both directly screwed on top of the power supply. The laser head and especially the controller got quite hot from this arrangement. Also, the small fan sounded hideously loud.

![Image](http://www.eyedream.se/bilder/holografi/315m/315m_original.jpg)

This weekend I mounted the laser head and the controller on two separate large heatsinks with thermal grease in between. If I place the heatsinks on the side to facilitate air convection, the laser head and controller just get a little bit warm after several hours of operation. I have also tested with a silent computer fan when the heatsinks are placed in a normal way, and this works very well. I have not yet decided if I will place the laser on the side or not. You can always turn off the fan during exposures.

![Image](http://www.eyedream.se/bilder/holografi/315m/315m_with_heatsink.jpg)
