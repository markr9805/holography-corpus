Nice table. I think you are one of the few people working in sand here. Can you post some more detailed photos of your optic mounts and table construction? I would like to see more people working in sand.
eric343 Sun Nov 07, 2004 11:01 pm #3
Everything is pretty crude. The table is the tension table described by Unterseher, and the mirrors and optics are hot-glued onto sections of cardboard mailing tube. (The big mirror is glued to two tubes side-by-side for added stability.) The 'periscope' consists of a mirror hot-glued to a piece of wood that was cut at a 45-degree angle reflecting the laser up onto a mirror hot-glued to a golf-club head. The golf club is stuck in a bucket of sand to keep it stable...

Most of the mirrors came out of a Metrologic in-counter barcode scanner; the Philips color-combining prism and the periscope mirrors from an LCD projector my school was throwing away.
