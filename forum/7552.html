We are all familliar with those scratch holograms. In fact, when I first heard of it I had to try it. I tried it on a plastic CD case using a two pinned compass and was amazed at the dimension of the final product.

So here is my thought. Everyone is striving for that computer generated hologram. Why not design a program that creates the arcs based on an image. Then have the plate move on an x-y table based on the arcs. The table moves under a laser that has a lens that focuses the beam just below the plate creating a v shaped burn in the material? I believe the beam could be focused to a pretty narrow spot and would allow pretty nice resolution.

It would also be intersting to try this diretly in resist where you could actually remove the exposed material.
