Type B Bone 250 bloom. Single beam reflection. 5x7. Veil Coating method. Color tuned each for different replay wavelengths. 30 second exposures. Fixer and water warm, alcohols hot. Plates about 24 hours old.

I made a image planed copy and it turned out great except I hit the emulsion with the dryer and it cause a large chuck of the emulsion to come off. I will do another image planed copy from one of these again.

The center one replays excellent in 514. Photos taken outside and glare is visible in the photos. I tried to take a photo with laser light but photos came out extremely grainy.

Regarding the grainy laser-lit photos, can you change the aperture size on your camera? If so, increase it and you should be able to decrease the grainy look. You won't be able to remove it altogether but it will help.

Also, make sure you use a tripod.
