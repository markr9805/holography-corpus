One of my earliest remote shutters was a servo and transmitter from a radio controll plane. It was crude but worked for what I needed at the time. I actually took the servo and mounted it with a rod extending from the servo to a manual camera shutter. (cannot remember which camera I used but it was a cheap old one from a yard sale). Of course this was not a great design because I would have to hold the transmitter lever in the open position for the length of the exposure, which I timed with a second hand on a clock, then I released the lever and the shutter would close. I like your design better.
