Has anyone encountered this before, or know what is likely to have caused it?

I have been helping clean up and organize TJ's hologram collection and lab, and the amount of hologram decay is depressing. but seriously, it does look like machine or squeegy marks. It could have been that the streaks are actually slightly damaged areas that are not fully visible when the hologram is completely dry. It may be that the black paint on the back has finally failed every so slightly as to allow moisture to get in. I wonder if you put the hologram into a desiccator to pull out all moisture if the streaks will go away. If they do, then you can repaint or seal the back and edges again after drying the hologram fully out. A desiccator should not harm a hologram. Other techniques of drying may cause irreversable damage. But please do not hold me responsible if the desiccator damages the hologram for some unknown reason. Perform remedial steps only if you cannot live with the hologram as is. At least if you monitor the hologram in the desiccator and it seems to get better you know it's a moisture thingy. Did you environment change in the last two weeks toward a higher humidity? Good luck and peace!
