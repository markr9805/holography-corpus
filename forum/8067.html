rzeheb Sun Nov 08, 2009 4:06 pm #1
I have been getting a lot of help from Dave (thank you!) and he suggested that I might want to post some photos of my latest efforts. It's all still a work-in-progress but over the past two weekends I successfully made my first rainbow hologram and I significantly improved on my H2 image planes. The subject matter was a bunch of jacks, a pingpong ball and some plastic stars which were all mounted at different heights above a black background. The rainbow is a bit grainy (and you can see a reflection of me taking the photo :doh: ) but all in all I feel pretty good about them.
