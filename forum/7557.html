Yellow has always captured my attention. Like how do you know if a yellow flower is reflecting yellow or green and red or some combination of yellow, green and red???

The problem you will have with using a yellow laser is it reflects it's peak at yellow. Now some green paints will look black to the pure yellow wavelength and some red paints will look black to the yellow paint. Furthermore, if the red paint reflected 20% yellow and the green paint reflected 20% yellow then both colors would look the same in the hologram.

I will have to say sorry, but in my opinion it will not work as proposed. You may be able to control the yellow laser light to look green in the hologram with green paint and some shrinkage and the yellow laser to look yellow in the hologram with yellow paint but then again this is two processes disrouted and I doubt you will be able to extract red without further disjoining the processes. This would be even more complicated in finding the correct compliments of paints then the process I describe in doing my color DCG hologram.

But I like the thought, we need more thinking like this outside the box.
