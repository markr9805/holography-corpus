Yes, last year I cut one of those Fresnel lens Kleenex boxes up (the silver ones were best) and left it soaking in an acetone/strong hydrochloric acid

solution. The plastic was removed off the cardboard and the aluminized backing dissolved into the HCl. The little Fresnel lenses were amazingly good. What a waste to put them on disposable boxes! However they were

reducing lenses. Then my colleague Colin Davidson poured PDMS monomer solution over them , (gelatin would have worked too) and pealed it off once it had set . They made perfect imprints but can you guess the major change that had occurred? ........................

They became magnifiers!
