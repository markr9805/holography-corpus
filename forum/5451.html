Tom B. Tue Mar 28, 2006 3:51 am #6
> JohnFP: May I ask, what did you use as the supporting backgound, flat back painted steel?

I just laid down a square of black velvet (oriented for minimum reflections) on my steel plate table and plonked the pearls, silver cake decorations and cat on the velvet and let it settle for 10-15 minutes. It's amazing what you can get away with if you have a stable environment, in my case a concrete unheated basement floor in a quiet neighborhood. The plate holder rested on the table, holding the plate just over the cat.

It's hard to find good black velvet nowadays ... I have some scraps of good stuff, but when I searched for more in fabric stores all they had was synthetic stuff that was too shiny. Something I have not tried that I think would work even better as a black background is a rough dark metal texture well blackened with candle soot. It would be messy though for any objects touching it...
