BobH Sun Sep 26, 2004 9:20 pm #2
A 40x objective will overfill the mirror and a 20x will underfill it. I recommend getting one each of 40x, 20x and 10x (they're cheap enough). Use as big a pinhole as you can to get a clean beam. Forget about the calculations. If you only have one pinhole, make it 25 microns. Anything smaller is a hassle to keep aligned, anything bigger might not clean the beam up enough. 25 microns works well with most beam diameters, and all three objectives.
