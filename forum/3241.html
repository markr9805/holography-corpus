JohnFP Sat Mar 19, 2005 9:33 pm #5
Oh, it does. Almost every hologram I have done so far in DCG is done by that method. The Vodka bottles were done with that method and I think they turned out good. But there are a couple of draw backs. One, you have to have more emulsion then needed. Two, controlling the thickness of the emlsion is limited. Three, there is wasted area around the glass. When I mount a finished 4 x 5 hologram I have to mask the outside 1/2 inch on all sides, somtimes more as the gell gets thick at the that edge when on the spinner. And how long is my 1970 phonograph (spinner)going to last? And finally, I would not mind trying large DCG plates and feel a better method should be learned for that.

I think the vail method has been very good to me so far but I just want to take the next step.
