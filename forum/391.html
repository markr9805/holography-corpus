Lamp1 goes on when I turn the power on.

Lamp2 comes on, when the laser has reached the output power I have set on the dongle and REMAINS stable.

Today I realized, my anger at him was probably uncalled for.

Johnfp Tue Nov 08, 2011 1:21 pm #2
I am not sure of your set up, but most industrial places, at least here in the US, have 3 phase electricity. The compass 315 runs on a single leg and ground. It is very possible that the spike in usage could be from just one or two of those legs from the 3 phase. If you can test all three legs coming into your building, it may be you can piggy back off of of one of the legs that does not get the drop in voltage.

Just a thought.

What about running it off of a UPS (uninterruptable power supply)? We use those in my lab to support vital equipment in the event of an unexpected power loss.
