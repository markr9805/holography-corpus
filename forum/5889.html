JohnFP Fri Nov 17, 2006 2:34 pm #2
Wouldn't a Kr-Ar mixed gas Ion laser with intra-cavity etalon do the trick.

You could use broadband mirrors in the laser, then feed the "white" beam through a prism and separate the lines. Then use the lines wanted. Or simply use all lines exiting the white-light laser at the same time.

I used the broadband mirrors in my Argon laser with the intracavity etalon and have very good sucess in making holograms with all lines produced at the same time (no intra-cavity prism). I had to play with power and etalon to get a Tem00 mode but it was not that difficult.
JohnFP Fri Nov 17, 2006 2:43 pm #3
Here is a photo of the fringes made via an interferometer of the Argon Ion laser using the broadband mirrors (all lines) and internal etalon.

Interferometer leg difference was about 1 meter.

Multiple line fringes are easily seen. Just add Krypton gas (red) and you have a single white light laser for holography.
