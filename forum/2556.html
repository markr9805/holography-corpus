twilightwindow Thu Jun 24, 2004 6:32 pm #6
The holographers behind the Queen's portrait are Rob Munday and Jeff Robb of Spatial Imaging Ltd in Richmond, UK.

As in previous projects with Spatial Imaging, Chris Levine served as agent/finder rather than holographer, although somehow, and sadly, managed to obtain credit for the creation of the portrait.

Munday used a digital camera moved along a steel rail to record a series of digital images, which were subsequently combined holographically to make an integral or 'multiplex' hologram.

The master was then copied (I think) by John Perry of Holographics North in Vermont.
