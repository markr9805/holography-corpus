Hello Holographers,

I am in the process of building up an inexpensive 532nm pulsed holography setup. It is based on old parts from the flashlamp pumped medical lasers we make at work. 2600 microfarads at up to 1000 VDC to the flash lamp, gated by a solid state IGBT handling 1200V at 600A max. Flash the lamp for about 160 microseconds. Electronics automatically trigger and simmer the flashlamp so it is easy to dump the charge to the lamp at the appropriate time. The rod is Nd:YAG, 5mm diameter. Need to select a (passive Cr4+:YAG ?) q-switch crystal to handle ~500MW/cm2, 10-50ns pulses, with proper initial transmittance, which I can't figure out. Also need to know HR and OC transmittance / reflectance, and coatings. Then frequency double the 5mm diameter 1064nm beam to 532nm (KDP or KTP ?).

Anyone know how to calculate these components? I am weaker on optics than the electronics. Have optical / electronic parts to swap so you can do tests too. Need a source of the crystals for testing. Any interest would be appreciated.

Sincerely,

Bill Jensen

Energy = power x the time over which the power operates

so Joules = watts x secs

so as a matter of interest a 1 joule pulsed laser can be a dangerous

beast because in a 10 nanosec pulse (10-8sec)

Power = watts = 1/10-8 = 100million watts!

No wonder it blows holes in your aluminized mirrors and ionizes the air

if you put it through even a mild convex lens.
