Please for those of you starting out with dcg please be careful in handling glass, the edges can be very sharp and cut without you being aware, I have cut my self several times, a couple of times really bad, I attended to them very quickly and it didn’t leave much of a scar, Now what I do when I get my cut glass, I dull the edges with sandpaper 180-220 grit. Holography is a lot of fun, but not when pain is involved. be safe
Hans Sun Jul 06, 2008 2:35 am #2
I once cut myself really bad on a glass plate while handling green sensitive Ultimate plates in a red safe light. I hardly felt the cut and in the red safe light I saw no blood. So I went ahead and installed the plate in the holder. When I opened the shutter of the green laser I noticed strange a dark liquid on my hand. I switched on all light and there was a bloody trail from the place where I cut the glass all the way to the exposure table. My hands were covered in blood.
