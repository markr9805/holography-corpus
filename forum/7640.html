holo_cyware Tue Dec 16, 2008 10:46 am #2
Well, from the specs we learn that this camera has a 1/2 inch CCD.

Now, the CCD's tend to be the same in the same technology "limits"; we can find at least the size of the pixel for a "certain" 1/2 inch CCD 1200x1600, like here: [http://viewbits.com/2-megapixel-CMOS-camera.html](http://viewbits.com/2-megapixel-CMOS-camera.html)

I'd say that the physical size of a pixel on the senzor must be in the 4x4 microns range (the pixels are square; this particular camera that I have found has 4.2x4.2 microns)
