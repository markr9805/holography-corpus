soda Tue Jan 07, 2020 12:05 pm #9
The best way how to handle the holofilm250 I found use few drops of LOCA uv glue (cheap on ebay for cell phone repair) to sandwich it to glass and press the sandwich firmly, then irradiate it by the UV lamp (or direct sun). Then the hologram can be used as master from the other side, or simply paint the back glass to black. An acrylate based black spray also can be applied directly to the emulsion side.
thycore Tue Jan 07, 2020 3:33 pm #10
What exactly does UV on this film ? Do you use it on freshly developed / bleached film ? Is it acting like a « fixer » ?
soda Wed Jan 08, 2020 4:26 am #11
The UV cures the LOCA glue. Complete sequence is: Pre-swell the film to get blue shift and replay in green. I use alcohol with small amount of water. The amount of the water control the blue shift. Simply drop few drops on glass and lay the film on it, add another drops and add another glass. Wait few minutes and the sandwich is ready to be exposed by pulsed ruby. For CW is better to use TEA. You can skip this step if you use chrome bleach or you want to preserve original color. After exposition I develop it roughly 2min in SM6 (for CW work CW-C2 should be better) and bleached it in Brilland bleach. After slow and complete drying I drop small amount of LOCA to clean glass, lay on it the hologram and press it by another glass. Then expose it to UV to cure the LOCA glue - resulting with permanently attached hologram to glass. The LOCA glue have simillar index of ref. as glass, the interface between disappears.

See the result - H2 copy of this way processed pulsed ruby master, dandelion in my dirty fingers
