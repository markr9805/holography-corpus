Colin Kaminski Sat Feb 25, 2006 9:00 pm #2
A Fourier Hologram means that the complex amplitudes of the interference recorded are the Fourier Transforms (FT) of the complex amplitudes of the object and reference beams.

The traditional way to record one is to have an object and a reference beam coming from the same direction to wards the recording medium. In order for this to work the object needs to be a transparency. Note, there is a large 0 order emission during reconstruction and two images are formed during reconstruction corresponding to the +1 and -1 solutions of the FT.

This is not a method used for art holography although it makes neat lenses for glasses.
