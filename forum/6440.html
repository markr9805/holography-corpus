Colin Kaminski Wed Sep 12, 2007 6:10 pm #2
Ah, yes. The lines above the teeth are a great example.

I once did a hologram with an 8 year old and we where using rubber Sponge Bob Square Pants and Patrick figures and we could not keep them still. We have the coolest banding on the objects but as you moved the plate it was obvious it had come with movement as the rest of the hologram was band free. I only mention it so students remember to check for object stability before they look at the laser.
