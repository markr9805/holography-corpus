Newest DCG Hologram.

8" by 10"

I know, a lot to be desired in the coating and the rushed glue job. I made the dreaded mistake of mixing the fast cure epoxy before I scraped the edge and would you know it, I didn't have enough epoxy to mix up another batch so I tried using is sticky, stringy and half set.

but... Nice and bright, large format with no greenies (or very small and minimally - under chin) and I tried to make the light much brighter at his face and chest region which seemed to turn out as expected. My fist 8x10. No matter how bad, I will treasure it.
