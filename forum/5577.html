I just read throught the latest update. Nice work! Instead of replacing the YAG rod for the resonator have you considered my idea of placing a telescope in the cavity? Since the aperture has to be small for the difraction losses a 4 to 1 telescope would improve the energy extraction from the rod.
Ron Michael Fri May 26, 2006 3:57 pm #4
no, the 4 inch yag rod is fine, I'm manipulating the saturable absorber q switch parameters in the oscillator. If you use the telescope idea, remember the rod initially goes negative lensing during q switch time on low rep rate. So you need to tune for that. The pumping spectral tests show I should get enough energy in the limited diffraction volume of a conventional design, plus I'm trying to avoid the larger radius depolarization issues by keeping the beam small in the center.
