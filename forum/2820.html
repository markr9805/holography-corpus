So I picked up a ruled diffraction grating from Edmund Scientific for about $8... For the heck of it, I cut out a square and taped it to my desk grating side up, then clogged the grating with some vaseline (get some vaseline on a kleenex and rub it over the grating side). I then tensioned a bit of clear mailing tape between two Bic pens (one tensioned by hand, the other taped to the table) with the adhesive side over the grating. Using a ballpoint pen, I found I could "draw" on the grating: where I press down on the tape, it picks up vaseline out of the grating. The result is cool "holographic" text (in the plane of the grating) surrounded by what seems to be clear plastic.

Best of all, you can start over again by wiping the vaseline out of the grating (wipe parallel to the grating direction with a clean kleenex to get it out).

Fun thing for a boring afternoon
