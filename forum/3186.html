Tom B. Sun Mar 06, 2005 7:59 am #1
You can either breathe on them (no condensation occurs on the emulsion side because the gelatin absorbs the moisture), or just look at the edge of the glass with a safelight - the cleanest (non-ragged) edge is the emulsion side. With a bit of practice you can detect the difference in the dark by rubbing your thumbnail along the edge..
Colin Kaminski Sun Mar 06, 2005 9:56 pm #2
You can also put the plate between your lips and pull. The side that sticks is the emulsion.
edelbrp Mon Mar 07, 2005 9:06 pm #3
If all the plates are oriented the same way, you can label the box 'emulsion this side ->'

If you don't know (or forgot), I use the two finger method: spit on your thumb and index finger and pinch them together a few times. Now do the same motion with the plate between them, and it should be easy to feel which side is the sticky emulsion side. (I'm a little wary of chemical coated glass plates on my lips. ;')

-Phil
