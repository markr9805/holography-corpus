john congrats! i purchased one from sal a month ago and i cant say how great this laser is ! bruce a former member gave me the idea of mounting it

on the tripod.............

![Image](http://members.aol.com/holoart/315laser1.jpg)

the laser is hot glued to the platform and a small project box with a portion of the side cutaway(also hot glued in place), to make box draw air through the fins and out the mini box fan.

with the small fan running the laser stays very cool so the hot glue is not likely melt !

![Image](http://members.aol.com/holoart/315laser2.jpg)

the shutter (as well as the mini fan) is a take out from the original box of parts the laser came with.

its a ac 12v plus shutter, i plugged it into a small wall/plug type transformer, and attached a new ,from Stanley ,a remote switch made for turning on/off x-mass lights ,so now my shutter is wireless!
