short question

ive got several bottels with different concentrations of TEA.

If I soak a film in the solution, is it better to use it only once and waste the TEA afterwards or can i put it back in the bottle.

Thats what i do with the EDTA bleach.
MichaelH Wed Aug 22, 2007 1:13 am #2
If you're using TEA for sensitization you can re-use it for along time unchanged.

For pre or post swelling you can also re-use it but you'll find that you need to add more TEA periodically. Trial and error will lead you in the right direction.

I found that after 4 pieces of film I usually had to add another 3-4ml of TEA to the existing solution.
Tom B. Thu Aug 23, 2007 3:01 am #3
Yes, I often re-use my TEA mix. I found that after a while it was turning murky (bacteria?) and also developing a chemical smell which I suspect was due to preservatives and fog inhibitors soaked out of the emulsion so I now give the plate a brief rinse in a small amount of distilled water to remove these before putting it in the TEA. I also keep the TEA mix in the refrigerator to slow down bacterial growth.
