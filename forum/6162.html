When I get to the cave, I want to do a larger plate such that the finished transmission hologram will be like looking thru a window into a room of the cave. John Klayer Wed Jun 06, 2007 1:06 am #19
The set up is a 1.5 inch thick honeycomb breadboard with another .75 inch thick honeycomb breadboard for the film platten ridgidly attached to it at 90 degrees. I have an aluminum beam attached to both holding a reference mirror. The C315, a shutter, a wedge beamsplitter, a couple of mirrors and a space filter are all mounted on the thicker base breadboard. A diffuser provides the object illumination. I managed to fit this all in an empty cat litter pail with some padding to survive the rough trip into the cave. I ran the laser with two motorcycle type batteries.

This set up worked in the lab and on my fireplace mantle. Both my digital cameras died, otherwise I'd post some pictures.
