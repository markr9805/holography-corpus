Ed Wesly Tue Sep 27, 2005 10:31 am #6
An elliptical pinhole for spatially filtering an oval beam is not necessary. The focus will, hard to believe, be a circle, but after that it will diverge with the oval shape.

Hardly anyone shoots square plates, but almost everyone shoots off axis. An elliptical beam oriented properly, the widest part to the non-tilt axis, should give efficient coverage of the flux.

Using a cylinder lens to circularize the beam will add astigmatism, which could end up haunting you later downbeam.
Colin Kaminski Tue Sep 27, 2005 10:26 pm #7
