Harry, do you really think you are going to get a lot of help in reproducing a security hologram? That's kind of like posting on a printing forum, "As a gag for my friend I want to reproduce a $100 bill but I want it to look real"....LOL

Security holograms are very unique in that they are very, very difficult to reproduce. Surely not a task to be taken on as a first project and in a rush.... That's why they work for security.

You are correct that the silver looking security hologram that is on CD cases are embossed rainbow holograms. So here is what you need to get started.

A table, optics and laser to produce a trasmisison master hologram. For the security type, additional novel techniques will need to be incorportated.

A table, optics and laser to produce a rainbow transmisison hologram in photo resist from the master hologram.

Processing stations to develop the master, usually in silver halide and to delveop the resist.

A silverizing station to make the resist electrically conductive.

A nickel plating tank/station to create the shims.

A hot foil stamping press to stamp the image into plastic.

All in all, I bet you can get in for 1/4 mil and would only need a year or so to master if you have no knowledge to start with.

How about getting started with a simple diode laser and a small kit and make your friend a hologram?
