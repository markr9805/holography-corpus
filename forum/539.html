ron olson Tue Aug 06, 2013 9:45 pm #2
It's early August and I try to avoid spending much time indoors but this week I had occasion to shoot a number (3 actually) of filmplates (film laminated onto glass using 2-sided adhesive films). The film is VRP-M (green sensitive) from GEOLA - we routinely purchase 14" wide by 10 or 20 meter long rolls. I have probably shot on Agfa plates as much as anyone and I have been a big advocate of Millimask for fifteen years; before that Ilford; and somewhere along the line Fuji. I am now willing to say that I have never used film that compares favorably to the current crop of VRP-M. The overall look (contrast and brightness) is really excellent and the decreased sensitivity seems to result in considerably reduced tendency for burns and overexposure - especially in the H2 process. Bernadette continues to be a distributor for GEOLA but our primary reason in reselling material is to keep abreast with anything new that comes down the pike. VRP-M is today the material of choice for Laser Reflections and I would urge anyone interested in serious holographic imaging to try the combination of Q-switched Nd:YLF or Nd:YAG lasers and this wonderful silver halide material. I shot (3) 18" x 14" H2's using 3 different large format H1 transmission masters. We always use Ed's/Han's SM-6 developer and PBU-Amidol bleach with Blythe colour-tuning or Sorbitol swelling to red-shift from green. This last batch of reflection H2's was among the best we've produced in 23 years and I felt compelled to let others know of our success - especially when so many out there seem to be forever bemoaning commercially available materials. Thank you Ramunas, Stas and Gidrius.
