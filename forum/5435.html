BobH Mon Mar 20, 2006 2:43 pm #3
> -How much is the maximum allowable fringe movement without affecting the hologram quality?

Depends on the recording material in use, and exposure times. If using silver halide and with a long exposure time (say 2 minutes), a transient vibration of a few seconds which settles back to the original position of the fringes is OK. If the exposure time is 10 seconds, a 5 second long transient vibration will give a very bad result.

Fringe drift is way more damaging to a recording than vibrations caused by busses or trains because if it's there, it's always there. If the fringes start in one place and finish in another, regardless of exposure time, a bad hologram will result.
