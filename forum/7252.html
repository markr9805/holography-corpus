JohnFP Tue Apr 01, 2008 10:12 am #4
Jeff turned me on to LaserCheck, a pocket meter by Coherent. I think it is about $400 brand new. But it is wavelength selectable so you can use it if you decide to change color wavelengths. It also allow you to measure in mW and uW. So you can use it to check raw beam power and power at the plate after expansion. I am very pleased with it.
