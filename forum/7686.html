It sometimes is difficult to treat the glass with 3-amino-propyltriethoxysilane because a monomolecular layer of it must be applied to the glass. If the layer is too thick, the amino endings of this Silane will fog the emulsion. If it is not done just right, there will not be any adhesion or mediocre adhesion to the glass that does not stand up well to the alkaline developer.

Here is a method that will almost guarantee good adhesion, using Dow Corning Z-6040 Silane. This Silane has epoxy like endings that bond well to the gelatin and does not fog an emulsion. So it can be pre-mixed with the gelatin before coating:

1] Clean the glass with a popular glass cleaner and then with household Ammonia.

2] Mix 1ml of Z-6040 with 20ml of methanol.

3] For every gram of Gelatin in your emulsion add 1ml of the mixture made in step [2] to your emulsion.

4] Coat immediately after.
