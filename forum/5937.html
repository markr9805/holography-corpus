JohnFP Fri Dec 22, 2006 2:09 pm #6
Michael, I think that is a good find. If you have a spare hologram you don't mind doing some tests on, try to repeat the proceedure but then dry the hologram with a blow dryer "hot and heavy" and see if it goes back to the green in minutes instead of days.

If you believe in the molecule binding theory of Myerhofer for DCG alcohol drying then it seems the alcohol left in the emulsion, being highly hydroscopic, has water molecules bound to it. Then the alcohol evaporates more quickly then the water and the water is left in the emulsion. This water give the red appearance. Once the water is taken out the hologram will go back to green.

Now, is this even worth doing? Does the "cleared" hologram seem cleaner the the original? Less noisy? Is their any increase in brightness?
