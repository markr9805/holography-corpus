Gall Thu May 19, 2011 2:53 am #2
Theoretically, if there are several silver-halide holograms recorded on a single plate, the brightness is divided by the number of holograms. A colour hologram is considered to be 3 different holograms on a single plate. However, due to 3 colours, it appears almost as bright as a monochrome one.

Many LEDs and halogen bulbs have not enough red in their spectrum. Some fluorescent lights and LEDs have narrow emission lines that do not match laser wavelenghts. If so, the hologram may lose some color or appear dim under such illumination. Try direct sunlight and bright incadescent (photography grade) bulbs first and then change to more appropriate light sources. You may check the spectrum using a $1 CD spectroscope
