MichaelH Fri Apr 15, 2005 7:17 pm #2
Possibly both. It all depends on your environment.

If you're causing movement on your table by:

1) Standing near it, or

2) Breathing, or

3) Moving the shutter, or

4) Any of the above.

You should rig a remote shutter.

Even before all that, you should set up an interferometer to find out how your table performs. I can't urge you too strongly to see how it behaves so that you'll know what you can do near your table and how the overall environment affects it.
