I was perusing [this site](http://www.jackspcs.com) (btw, I don't remember who you are, but thanks to the person that first shared this site on the forum) and I was looking at the processes used by others, thinking about what experiments I might try in the future, when I ran across this bit...

[http://www.jackspcs.com/fx1.htm](http://www.jackspcs.com/fx1.htm)

> The shelf life of Solution A can also be improved by adding 50 ml of isopropyl alcohol just prior to the final dilution to 1000 ml. This addition will also decrease development time and the amount of agitation that should be used.

Has anyone here tried adding alcohol to the active half of solutions like JD2?

Personally I'm using brown glass bottles and sucking the air out and also using the solution fast enough that I don't need greater shelf life right now, so I'm mainly just curious.
Martin Thu Apr 28, 2005 10:52 am #2
> MichaelH: Has anyone here tried adding alcohol to the active half of solutions like JD2?

I used to add alcohol (IPA) to Neutol developer when processing unhardened SH layers etc. In my opinion this rather seemed to shorten shelf life of the developer solution...

> Personally I'm using brown glass bottles and sucking the air out and also using the solution fast enough that I don't need greater shelf life right now, so I'm mainly just curious.

We sometimes used fine glass spheres to "sequeeze out" the air from the (developer) stock solution.
