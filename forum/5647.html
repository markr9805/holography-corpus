when we make grating,DE can be defined as η＝I/I。×100％ where I is the intensity of diffractive beam,I。is the intensity of incident beam.

because there are absorption and scattering of holographic film, i wonder that: can we define DE in another way,namely,η=I/(I+I')×100％ where I' is the intensity of transmission beam?
BobH Sat Jun 24, 2006 4:01 pm #2
Diffraction efficiency is as you first said, diffracted light in the first order divided by incident light. It's OK to subtract glass-to-air reflections from the incident measurement, but anything else is sugar-coating the result.

Extinction of the transmitted beam can be used as a indicator of diffraction efficiency. You want to keep the processing constant, however, so that scatter and absorption are not variables.
