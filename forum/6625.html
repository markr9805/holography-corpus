Colin Kaminski Tue Jan 01, 2008 8:26 pm #5
OK, this is a little strange but it just might work. Take some 1/4 sawn soft wood like spruce. A piece left over from a guitar top would be perfect. Take one piece about 8" long and 1" wide and cut across the grain then glue it to a piece 8" x 1" cut along the grain. Attach one end and let the other end flex. You will have to calibrate it with a sling psycrometer to make it accurate. I find them to be very consistant and not damaged by humidity swings. I used to make them often for guitar making. I found them to be much better than digital or hair driven hydrometers. Second to only sling psycrometers.
Colin Kaminski Tue Jan 01, 2008 9:29 pm #6
