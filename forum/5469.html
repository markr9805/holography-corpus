favalora Tue Apr 04, 2006 1:42 am #3
Hello -

My understanding is that it processes ambient humidity to create a locally thin sheet of "fog." A modifiied 2-D projector illuminates it with imagery. (I believe it's not laser-based; probably your usual "PowerPoint" projector with modified projection and/or relay optics.)

It projects 2-D, not 3-D images. Like any 2-D display, you can use it to project rendered or so-called "2 1/2-D" images that look like monoscopic 3-D.

One group - their overseas competitors at Fogscreen - actually did create a stereoscopic image by projecting (was it field-sequential? probably not polarized...) stereo images onto the fog & viewed with goggles. See paper 6055A-45 of the SPIE Stereoscopic Displays & Apps conference (2006).
