Tom B. Sat Oct 27, 2007 4:48 am #4
I like LEDs too. When the LED is the same color as the holo, the contrast can be very good and the apparent brightness even for dimmish Denisyuks can be quite acceptable. I find Amber to be the least objectionable color for wall display and tune my holos to match. Red is too alarming, Green is too garish, Blue might be nice but clean, high contrast blue holos are not exactly easy to find or make.

Many LED lamps use multiple small LEDs which is bad, producing a blurry image. The preferred type uses a single high-power LED. OptiLeds are of this type and produce a very clean, narrow beam, good for lighting 4X5 holos from as close as 2-3 feet or bigger ones from further away. Some sources:

[http://www.laserreflections.com/LED.htm](http://www.laserreflections.com/LED.htm) (Most of my LEDS came from here)

[http://www.lightworld.com/](http://www.lightworld.com/) Have not tried these guys yet, but they claim to offer OptiLeds too

Most of my gallery pics used OptiLed ambers for lighting - [http://members.shaw.ca/holopix/My_holograms.html](http://members.shaw.ca/holopix/My_holograms.html)
