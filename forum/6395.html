MichaelH Tue Aug 14, 2007 11:03 am #4
How much light does your home made material block? If you put an unprocessed plate in front of your reference beam is there much difference between the pass-through and unobstructed light?

Have you tested your laser in an interferometer to determine what its coherence length is? It's possible, though not too likely, that your laser has a short CL.

Hans Tue Aug 14, 2007 8:20 pm #8
40 seconds seems about right for homemade film. That's about what it takes with the film I made according Jeff Blyth's diffusion method. If the hologram looks foggy after exposure, it is usually an indication of over exposure.

What kind of developer do you use? If you use Ascorbic TJ-1 developer, the film should turn black in about 5 to 10 seconds for a good hologram.

Hans

My standard developer is gp-2 diluted 15ml/200ml d.w. with a development time of 4 min. The film usually darkens in about a minute ,and then I soak in 70% and 99% IPA for 3 min each. After a minute in the 99%, the image begins to appear.
