Here is the procesessing from Vintek:

Introduction to processing the BB plates:

General: The BB plates produced by Colourholographic Ltd England are at the moment using the same formulae and coating technique as well as identical packaging to that used by Dr Richard Birenheide at HRT in Germany. Dr Birenheide trained and successfully transferred the HRT technology and equipment to Colourholographic Ltd where we continue the entire range of HRT plates.

We are intending to improve where possible various area such as sensitivity and spectral range and we will update our customers when these changes occur.

One of the characteristics of the HRT plates is that due to lack of humectants (which we hope to address soon) the plates appear to become insensitive after a few weeks. In fact it is due to a dessication that can easily be addressed by the following methods which renders the plate as sensitive as when it was coated.

The process is called presensitisation and there are 2 methods.

Presensitisation method for interferometry and security holograms:

Prepare a solution of water with a 2 % volume of photographic wetting agent such as Ilfotol or Kodak Fotoflo(doesn't need to be deionised or distilled)

Example:

1 litre of water with 20 ml of wetting agent. Stir enough to homogenize the solution. Soak the plate for 2 to 3 minutes in the appropriate safelight conditions in a tray of the solution with the emulsion facing up. There are various methods for drying the plate. Favourites are squeegeeing and then air drying with a warm fan heater. Blotting with high quality absorbent blotting paper is another option but airdrying is always necessary after that. The presensitisation will last about 2 days.

Presensitisation method for display holograms:

The method is the same except that the solution also contains a 3% solution of TEA (treiethanolamine).

Example:

Solution would be 1 litre of water, 20 ml wetting agent, 30 ml TEA. Make sure mix is stirred adequately. The solution can be reused many times. The TEA confers more sensitivity than the plate would normally have, so if high sensitivity is required, use this method.

Processing the plates for transmission and master holograms:

The standard developer used for BB plates is a vitamin C based formula which is very low in toxicity and is a fast developer. It is also reusable until it slows down development time Keep stoppered to avoid oxidation by air. Most other developers contain sodium sulphite which has a deleterious effect on the small grains and should not be used.

The recipe is, in the following order In 1 litre of deionised or distilled water 25 grams L-ascorbic acid (Vitamin C) 70 grams Sodium Carbonate (anhydrous) 15 grams Sodium Hydroxide 4 grams Metol (or 0.5 grams Phenidone) Different customers prefer different levels of density so we do not prescribe what density is best. However we use between 1.8 to 2.4. Many people prefer less density. After development, wash and put in the bleach

Bleach:

We use an EDTA based bleach because it is reusable and gives good results. However there are many equally good bleaches and we advise you to experiment for example the PBU bleach.

Ferric EDTA bleach recipe: In 1 litre deionised or distilled water 30 grams EDTA (disodium salt) 30 grams Ferric Sulphate (iron lll). Do not use ferrous sulphate 30 grams Potassium bromide 30 grams Sodium hydrogen sulphate (or 8 ml concentrated sulphuric acid ) or equivalent After bleaching (emulsion now clear of black silver) wash and then add wetting agent to the final rinse and either blot or squeegee dry then airdry using a fan heater or hairdryer. Do not at any point use fix or hypo in the processing.

Reflection holograms:

For reflection holograms we recommend the pyrogallol developer and then the EDTA bleach described above. The general recipe for reflection developers is:

Solution A 60 grams Sodium Carbonate (anhydrous) per litre of deionised or distilled water Solution B 10 grams Pyrogallol per litre deionised or distilled water Mix the 2 just before development in the quantities required for size of plate. Some people prefer to use the developer at quite high temperatures (25 to 35 degrees C) While others prefer to use cooler around 18 to 25 degrees C This depends on exposure and density required.
