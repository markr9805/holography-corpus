Dutchelm05 Mon Apr 14, 2008 1:39 pm #3
> Colin Kaminski: For coating plates I am thinking I want an "oven" to preheat plates below a coating station and a drying rack up above. Any ideas?

Hey Colin, sounds exciting.

I use something a bit different for plate coating. This is for mold processing but could work with anything

I have a 5X12" (size of my glass) 1/2 inch piece of aluminum. Under it is a resistor element which heats up the plate. I have a temperature controller ($36 on e-bay) that regulates the plate to about 120F. I place my mold glass on top of that and control the glass temp pretty tight. I really have two of these units one for the mold and one for the film glass that is waiting (although I only temp control the mold plate).

The plate has levelers on it and the temp staies pretty close. It allows me to not have to rush taking glass from an oven to the station since glass cools pretty fast. I've also reduce the excess gel mess to almost zero by placing a paper towel under the glass just before coating. As Bob has told me, make the process a zen experience and things tend to turn out better

Good luck Colin
