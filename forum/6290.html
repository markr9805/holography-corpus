ron olson Fri Jun 08, 2007 3:02 pm #2
Big Q-switched lasers are much happier on land hence I would advise "throughwater" imaging as opposed to "underwater" imaging. With the help of the Steinhart Aquarium in San Francisco, we produced ~ 10 marine images that we sold with our PortHol frames. For these we used a customized (large and thin) 75 gallon tank. Very interesting project determining which fishes, mollusks and invertebrates could deal with red safelight long enough for me to get a decent composition. I intend to revisit marine holography for use with laser back-lighting. Green and blue lasers transmit beautifully through water and holographic back panels make for some very interesting effects.
