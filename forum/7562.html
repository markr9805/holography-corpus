The New England Section of the OSA presents a talk by Steven Smith: "The History of the Optical Print Display Hologram."

The Talk

The advent of the commercial Hologram industry in the early 1980's lead to the development of small to large format optical display holograms as large as 2 meters square, and has progressed to larger format digital holographic imaging technology in full color available today. Where has the market gone? Was this a promise unfulfilled or yet to be realized?

Steven Smith

Steven L Smith started one of the first successful commercial holographic imaging laboratories in the early 1980s, The Lasersmith Inc. of Chicago Il. He saw it progress from small to large format optical display holograms into high yield security holograms that you see today. He developed some of the first full color real time capture systems and fulfilled a long time goal of working on full color digital hologram printers under the research of Dr. Stephen Benton at Spatial Imaging Group in MIT's Media Lab.

Examples of work over this period will be presented.

Where / When

Waltham, MA - Oct. 16.

Steve Smith's talk was very enjoyable. He brought a collection of display holograms of what I thought were very high-quality, some of which used special processing (whose chemistry I didn't understand). Also, he brought quite a few experimental holograms, such as one of the first from the MIT Media Lab's holographic printer that focused light from a DMD onto a rotating film-carrying drum, and his work in full color holographic stereograms.

I didn't know that he made MILLIONS of holograms for Sears, too.
