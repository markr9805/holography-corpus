Hi! I'm new to the forum, and to holograms in general, so bear with me here. Is there any way to remove holograms from a material, and be left with a surface that looks unharmed compared to the original? I ask because I have a sheet of plastic with a hologram on it that when looking at the sheet from specific angles is transparent, and at other angles the hologram is visible. I want to remove the hologram from two specific portions of the sheet, and be left with just the transparent plastic. Thanks!
Ed Wesly Tue Jun 07, 2005 12:44 am #2
Most of the time the hologram is designed not to be removed as an anti-counterfeiting measure.

If the hologram that you are interested in was designed for a security application, it would probably melt if subjected to the usual organic solvents to remove the adhesive.

Another protective measure designed into the fabrication of the hologram is a "void" feature which leaves half on the package and the other half on the label as it is lifted from the surface.

You can try your best shot, but if you are trying to remove the hologram from a secure document there is an almost certain chance of failure.

I am just trying to remove the hologram without damaging the plastic so that they can still look through it. Thanks.
Ed Wesly Tue Jun 07, 2005 7:49 pm #4
