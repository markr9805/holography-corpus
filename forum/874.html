soda Thu Jun 05, 2014 6:01 am #1
Hi everyone,

I'm trying DIY AgX chemistry for deep red pulsed holography (a happy owner of nice small ruby rod) and soon I found impossible to obtain cryptocyanine as other cyanines spectral sensitizers due to lack of some permission to handle with toxic chemicals. I start lookig for ewerything blue and green aroud me and finally I found easily available deep red sensitizer - Taiyo Yuden cyanine CD-R dye! Prelimiary results are promissing, the dye - or micture of dyes - used as raw ethanol extract sensitizes AgX emulsion to deep red region, perfectly fit the ruby line and cheap 660nm diodes, moreover sensitize also a bit to He-Ne surprisingly. It seems to do not cause fog or desensitivation, even in excess concentration. Extraction is fast and easy: scratch a protecting layer near edge and using strong blast of air peel of complete reflecting layer, leaving PC substrate with dye layer. Aplying little of ethanol to the dye layer, it disolves quickly, producing the dye extract.

!!!! The dye is surely also toxic as other cyanines, despite easy availability !!!! It needs to be handled carefuly, avoid any contact with it!

Have a nice bright H's!

soda Fri Jun 06, 2014 3:26 am #7
Here is pic. of small test plate, just few drops of emulsion on 3x3 cm glass, intended for sensitivity testing. Only centre went well due to uneven thickness and processing, but it is evident that it works for holography!

My particular CD-R sample was quite old TDK CD-R74 48x, only little burned. Is seems that the same, or very similar, dye stuff is present in all CD-R produced by Taiyo Yuden, but it needs to be proven.

Wast majority of the dye extract was invested to spectroscopic measurement, but I still have enough dye for sensitivation approx. 1000cm2 - quite cheap source of sensitizer.

Spectral absorption of heavily dye over-doped plate shows that the extract probably contains micture of two similar dyes which perfectly match to all red laser lines.

While looking for new CD-R sample, I found that majority of CD's are AZO or Ptalocyanine, only small part is the desired Cyanine types reselled here in Europe as Verbatim, JVC and TDK. I also found that Taio Yuden produce DVD's - if it contains cyanine, it perhaps match to green lines. It sounds like a joke to produce panchromatic emulsion ot of CD/DVD extracts...
