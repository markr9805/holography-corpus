LarryD

Title: Catique

Type: Single beam Denisyuk

Materials: 4x5 PFG-01 glass plate, dev. w/JD-2 kit.

Laser: 10mW polarized OEM style HeNe.

Exposure: Approx. 1 second.

The object is hand molded from modeling clay which was baked, then painted with a diffuse liquid gold-leaf paint.

I made the cat and the pedestal she is on myself, but the background is an old flower pot courtesy of the FTD florist.

I spent some time finding a nice looking frame to complement the design, and tried to convey and "old-time" antique theme.

![Image](http://4c-s.org/v-web/gallery/albums/album20/catique.sized.jpg)

![Image](http://4c-s.org/v-web/gallery/albums/album20/catiqueclose.sized.jpg)

Tony

"Trischizophrenic cat"

![Image](http://home.comcast.net/~stampydoo/pcg05.jpg)

This is a reflection H2 on PFG-01 film, developed in D-19 and JD-2 (Dichromate) bleach.

This was made from a transmission H1, made on Ultimate S-R-0, developed in Ultimate chemicals. The trick here is that there was only 1 cat - it's actually a triple exposure, and each exposure got about 40% of the nominal exposure. There wasn't enough room on the H2 to show much of the third cat - it's on the right edge...

Hene laser, 25-35mW (depending on warmup).

Ed Wesly

It is entitled "Tuffy II", the cutest little black cat you ever saw.

The file is slightly cropped from the original scan that I got with my roll of 35mm film dropped off at the drug store.

The photograph is pretty representational of the piece itself, except for the kinetic effects of the rainbows coming in through the window whirling around.

Technical notes:

Approximately 5" by 7" image area in an 8" by 10" frame. Backlit. Black and white transparency on Kodalith 4556, developed 1.5' in Dektol 2:1. Sandwiched with a Holographic Optical Embellishment on Agfa 8E75HD, processed in CWC2 Developer and CWPBQ Bleach.

67mm Focal Length lens glued with Norland Optical Adhesive to cover glass.

Piece completed 8/8/05.

![Image](http://www.dragonseye.com/TuffyII_sm.jpg)

A larger version is available at [http://www.dragonseye.com/TuffyII.jpg](http://www.dragonseye.com/TuffyII.jpg)

Dinesh Padiyar

![Image](http://www.dragonseye.com/holography/calvin.png)

It's a 4-colour "pseudo-colour" rainbow with 4 H1's: Calvin, Hobbes, the laser beam and the background. The images are on slightly different levels, but the difference is too small (about 1/4 inch) to make it worthwhile to show as a movie or in stereo. Both H1's and H2 is on Slavich VRP shot at 514.
