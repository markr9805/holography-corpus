I'm going to try my first TEA presoak on BB-640...

Do I need to rinse it out before developing, or can I just develop as usual (and throw away the developer afterwards)?
Colin Kaminski Sat Jul 30, 2005 1:57 am #2
I do a distilled water rinse first.
HoloM Sat Jul 30, 2005 5:17 am #3
I don't wash out the tea. it works fine for me.
jean Sat Jul 30, 2005 6:05 am #4
Depends of what you want.

If you want a color shift, don't rinse it.

If you want only a increase in sensitivity only, rinse it.
HoloM Sat Jul 30, 2005 6:19 am #5
But if you only want to increase sensitivity and not shift color, you must rinse it and wash out the tea BEFORE exposure. if you wash it out after exposure but before developing, there will be a color shift, too.
Tony Sat Jul 30, 2005 11:14 am #6
I didn't see any replies so I just went ahead and did it!

I left the TEA in for exposure and development, which got me approximately 2x in sensitivity and a nice shift (which was desired). I also used Jeff-B's Fe EDTA bleach, which was slow acting but did a great job. Very nice, bright reflection H2 hologram.

Now on to the cat!
