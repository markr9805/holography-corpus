BobH Tue Oct 20, 2009 2:22 pm #2
DMDs can be used to make holograms, but not like you're thinking. Holography is a recording technique, not a projection adtechnique. Generally a DMD is used to display an image, or series of images, in a hologram recording system, insted of an object. It allows an object to be rendered using a camera or a computer.

A DMD can also be used in the reconstruction beam for special effects. Please read a bit more about the fundamental holography technique and I'll be happy to answer more specific questions. Good luck with your project! And welcome here.
