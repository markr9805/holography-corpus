Hello Holographers!

I read that Pyrogallol should work very well ("truly works miraculous") on the PFG-01 plates I've got here, but I had not so satisfying results this night. I thought, before I order luxury plates like BB640 or Ultimate, I wanted to learn and optimize a bit for myself.

This night I used developing times between 30s and 1 min (checked the density), and the recipe

~ 10g Pyrogallol / 500mL

~ 60g Na2CO3 / 500mL

I produced holos with decent depth, but bad brightness, and they look really ivoryish white when viewed from a steep angle. Could you please tell me more about the circumstances of this "broadband" processing, as you call it, I think? I would prefer brighter, but more one-coloured holos.

I couldnt measure the temperature of the developer bath, but it must have been around 25-30 degrees Celsius, because I mixed NaCO3 together just before the processing, so it heated up a bit.

Exposure was 1-2 sec with 6mW HeNe, has always been sufficent, and bleaching with K2Cr2O7 (<-yes I know, but soon I'll try a better and healthier alternative).

Thanks for your help!
