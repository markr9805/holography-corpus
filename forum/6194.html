You can say it may be a continuation of my earlier topic BB640, but i think this deserves a new discussion.

I tried out grating formation with BB640 at various angles.

1) I set the beams at 20 + 20 degrees to the plate.

I got a wonderful grating. The order when we replay with the same laser was 1 !!!!. I mean the groove spacing was maybe around 1200 lines.

I wanted to create a grating with less spacing, so i again tried out with a diff angle.

2) I set the beams at 40 + 40 degrees to the plate.

I did got the grating, to my surprise, the groove spacing was more fine. Means it had more resolution than my previous step. Groove spacing maybe around 1300 lines.

When we observe a monochromatic light (almost) like a green CFL lamp though the grating, we can see the color seporation very finely in case of both the gratings.

When i passed the laser through it, no order was found..!! But we can see the full spectrum like a rainbow wen we look a tubelight through it.

3) I again set the beams at 10 + 10 degrees to the plate.

This time the grating had less resolution, i mean we can see more orders wen we pass the laser light through it.

I think the theory says that the less the degree the more the resolution.

But it happend just reverse for me.

The seutp was built with a polarised HeNe laser 5mW., Plate-BB640, presensitised with 3% TEA, JD4 bleach and ascorbic developer. I exposed the plate wet in all the setups.

Can anyone please help me out in the theory side.
