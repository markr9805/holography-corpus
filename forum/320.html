Hello John,

Ok, this might be total crap, not sure as I've never tried it or know anyone else that has but here goes.

With machining there's lots of 3 phase equipment. Motors that need large starting currents and such. We used to use rotary converters. Big motors with fancy brushes that give you 3 phase coming out with only 220 single going in.

Now that's crap for sure in a holo lab, but nowadays there's solid state equipment that does the same thing, but much cheaper of course. They are called variable frequency vector drives.

220 60hz going in and 220 3 phase coming out. At about any frequency you want. That's for over and under speeding motors. I bet it would destroy a lasers controller if you used that knob. But if you leave it at 60hz it might run your laser.

Now, once again, I have to state, I don't know if this will work, I don't know if its safe for the laser. But these things should be easy to find out. I bet the laser manufacture will know.

You can get a vector drive for under 200 bucks. If it can run your ion laser that's much cheaper than setting up proper three phase wiring.

But back to generating 3 phase, and it does work and I have used it is to get a 3 phase motor as you stated, like a 5 Hp. Then connect the 220 to two of the legs on the motor. Then connect the two of the three lines off the power supply to the same 2 legs and the last line to the third and lonely leg on the motor. Then get a belt pully and attach it to the end of the shaft of the three phase motor. Get another 1/2 hp 120 motor with a pully and mount it so that it can swing toward and away from the 3 phase motor while keeping the two pullies in line (I mounted mine on a hing).

So to get the whole thing working, start with all current off. Swing the 1/2 hp motor such that the belt is loose between the two pullies, the one on the 3 phase and the one on the 1/2 hp. Turn on the 1/2 hp and let it get up to speed. Then slowly swing the 1/2 hp motor and tighten up the belt little by little such that you get the 3 phase motor running up to speed (still not voltage applied to it). Once it is up to speed then close the breaker supplying the two legs to the 3 phase motor, it will run by itself and the little 1/2 hp can be swong out of the way and turned off. The 3 phase is now generating the third leg in a delta 3 phase set up. Works pretty good. Pain in the a**, loud and quite a drain on electricity, but does work. I have actually been considering that again. If anyone wants, I can diagram it up for you.
