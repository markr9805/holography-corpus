Regarding the stability of model-making materials for holography, I was wondering if holographers have experienced any failures, due to movement, for any of the listed materials. It would be especially interesting if the failure resulted in dark bands or patches, instead of the complete disappearance of the model, since that might suggest that the entire object moved, and this might not be directly related to the material from which the model was made. Please elaborate in a separate response as needed, but I didn't want to include things which are obviously unstable, like flowers, fabric, paper, etc. More than one item can be selected. Thanks.
JohnFP Tue Nov 06, 2007 6:46 pm #2
I tried quite a few times to make a hologram of some plastic figurines that came with a childs toy. The toy was a push train and the little people had a hole in the bottom so that they could be seated in the train. The plastic seems hard enough but could be flexed with moderate effort. Every time I tried to make a hologram part of the person would be dark, surely movement. If I find some old photos I will post them. I am having a dickens of a time being able to work with my web site.
