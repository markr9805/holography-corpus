I am glad to inform the forum that Geola now is offering a new technology that separates holographic image capture and holographic image printing. The capture can be be done in the studio, or outdoor.

People and other life creatures can be captured without any pulsed laser and the life scene capture for holographic print can be done at your customer's place. Then, the captured files are sent to our printer, we will print your holographic print and send you. We are calling this technology Syn4D imaging and printing.

The equipment functions are similar to one that was used for Queen capture, just our equipment setup takes 15 minutes at the most.

Here are the photo of the equipment ready for shooting:

![Image](http://www.geola.lt/content/holocam_assembled.jpg)

And here is the same equipment packed to go to the customer place:

![Image](http://www.geola.lt/content/holocam_packed.jpg)

In March 2007 this technology was presented in Brussels to the EU commission ([http://www.fascination-of-light.net/cam ... f_brussels](http://www.fascination-of-light.net/campaign/events/copy_of_brussels)) and it is now commercially available.

Discussion in the photography forum:

[http://www.openphotographyforums.com/fo ... php?t=2825](http://www.openphotographyforums.com/forums/showthread.php?t=2825)

Syn4D holographic print properties:

Digital files used for Syn4D printing - Life movies, movies rendered, with 3D design programs, combined movies of real and virtual scenes.

Maximum size of the life scene captured with a portable apparatus - 120x90cm (47.2"x35.4")

Motion imprinted - Up to 10 seconds (different subjects’ positions will be observed from different angles)

Scaling - Limitless (enlarging and reduction)

Colour - Full colour

Print resolution - ~320 pixels per inch

Size of the print - from 18x24cm to 100x150cm

Lighting - Multiply light source

Viewing angle, degrees - Vertical 27, horizontal 75

Apparatuses are currently available for rent and purchase directly from Geola or from its distributors in USA, Germany and UAE.

More information with samples using the camera apparatus can be found on [http://www.geola.lt](http://www.geola.lt) , [http://www.Syn4D.com](http://www.Syn4D.com) and [http://www.forthdimension.net](http://www.forthdimension.net)

Your opinion about the new technology is highly appreciated.

Stas Mon May 28, 2007 1:51 pm #3
Postcard size Syn4D print is possible, but we are recommending not smaller than 18x24cm (big post card:)). The reason is that we are printing with 0.8mm holopixels. Visually the print resolution is then like 320dpi, but holopixels itself are visible - not good for sizes smaller than 18x24cm.

And yes, since it is a digital capture - we can reduce the captured scene to any size. Some people even have made a human portrait shims from the files made by our capturing device. So it is possible. Anyway all serious embossers are writing their shims digitally and of course they can use our capturing device with easy.

As for the mass replication of our prints - we are working on it by we are not there yet.
BobH Mon May 28, 2007 2:36 pm #4
Does the camera always point forward (perpendicular to the track), or does it pan and zoom?
Stas Tue May 29, 2007 5:14 am #5
Camera always points to the center of the scene. So it has two synchronized movements - rotation and linear. Then our software transforms the images to same that would be taken moving camera perpendicular to the track. And we are not using keystones for transformations - each image pixel is put in phase space and moved there.
