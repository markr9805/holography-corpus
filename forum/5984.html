When the emulsion of a transmission hologram shrinks or expands, the Bragg Angle changes. But could the emulsion still be used as a transmission hologram by changing the reference angle? Or would it now only re-play when using a different wavelength?
MichaelH Wed Jan 17, 2007 6:11 pm #2
It replays using a new angle. A transmission hologram actually reconstructs using multiple wavelengths (the full range really) but you get distortion as you move away from the original wavelength. For most visual work the distortion is negligible unless you're working with people as subjects.

a transmission hologram will act as a lens does ,the replay angle will shift as the wavelenght changes, if the film shrinks ,the bragg planes will lay down causing you to skew your plate further from normal
