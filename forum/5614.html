Dear Holographers,

I am building a 2 stage pulsed holography system based on the SSY-1 units. Pictures of the setup are below

The first stage is a standard SSY-1 with a brewster window installed inside the cavity. It is driven by the standard 15 Joule PFN1 at 850 VDC. It does not have an internal 0.7 mm aperture inside yet, for this test.

The second stage is an SSY-1 unit with the OC mirror, HR mirror, and passive dye q-switch removed. It is colinear with the first stage. It is driven with my 50 Joule PFN2.

Both units are triggered simultaneously by the same trigger board that comes with the standard SSY-1 set, and uses a pushbutton trigger signal.

At 850 VDC, I am able to get more than 0.5 Joules of 1064nm output !

The next step is to double this with a much larger KTP crystal (so I can convert the 5mm diameter beam without clipping and distortion, soon to be ordered, when I get the money). It is anticipated that greater than 0.25 Joules of green 532nm radiation will be attained. Holograms will be taken, to see if they are clear and good. I have given up on the spatial filtering idea for now.

If the holograms are not clear and bright, an internal aperture of 0.7mm diameter will be placed in the oscillator cavity of the SSY-1 to force the TEM00 mode even further. A reduction in the overall 0.25 Joule power will be the result.

I have allowed space in my setup for yet a second amplifier stage if this becomes necessary.

Has anyone else worked along these lines? I know JohnFP has proposed this setup, and I send him my thanks for his, and other's, input.

Sincerely,

Bill Jensen

Dear Holographers,

I needed two diodes to pass the 3.9K Ohm limited positive high voltage to both the PFNs. The way I had it, both flashlamps were sharing the high capacity of my PFN2 (what I am calling the beefed up pulse forming network). That made the system run at 500 mJ.

Now at 900 VDC, the oscillator outputs 180 mJ and the amplifier outputs 470 mJ.Since both flashlamps are sharing the same trigger voltage, sometimes the amplifier will not trigger. It looks like both tubes need their own trigger board, so I will add it later.

This is so much energy, that it is scorching my joulemeter head from the direct beam. Zapit paper gets a tremendous white zap about 0.2" in diameter. Don't look at the amplifier cavity, the white light is blinding.

Not much more I can do now until I raise the money to buy a larger KTP / KDP.

Sincerely,

Bill Jensen

Why not try and see how much single TEM mode you get out of it while you wait. As you have already indicated by wanting to use a spatial filter, spatial quality is important to a clean hologram. There are many techniques you might consider for controlling spatial modes including consideration for propagation techniques (gaussian, flat top, image relaying) which effect you spatial quality as well. For example, controlling diffraction (spatial frequency) sources in the propagation. Don't worry their's plenty to do when trying to achieve holographic quality.
