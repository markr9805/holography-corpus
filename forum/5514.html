Depends on what you need a simple Denisyuk holographic kit or a full pulsed laser laboratory. On the side AFTER exposing the hologram we may use photopolymers as available material, on this after state I would:

-Eliminate the development, only a self light develop.

-Use a low cost glass/plastic emulsion with a good beam ratio exposed hologram. Possibly with a pulsed laser sensitive without RF material.

The holographic kit may use a ECDL laser diode, that indeed can be made with a very high quality at moderate cost as I did pointed in this forum sometimes, 300mW continuous may be used for a 4X5 plate with significantly reduce the exposure time to a level that eliminate some movements.
