I ran across a formula for a rehalogenating bleach based on potassium dichromate and potassium bromide, but instead of using sulfuric acid they are using toluene-4-sulphonic acid. Is there any advantage to using this stuff?
Martin Thu Jun 26, 2008 7:36 am #2
> Ed Wesly: I ran across a formula for a rehalogenating bleach based on potassium dichromate and potassium bromide, but instead of using sulfuric acid they are usingtoluene-4-sulphonic acid. Is there any advantage to using this stuff?

Here's an excerpt from a mail I received from Jeff Blyth in 2004:

> I did investigate the effect of using milder acids in dichromate baths over 20 years ago and like you seem to have found, it can give noticeably better results. So one is restricted to finding alternatives which won't oxidise.One acid I got very nice results from is toluene 4-sulfonic acid. I put this in a paper in the Lake forest symposium of July 1985. Compared to this the hydrogensulfate is second best as it is still too strong ideally.

I guess ascorbic acid cannot be used in this context.

Could we first please just get our terminology sorted or we are going to confuse ourselves and others.

The word Tom should have used was "non-oxidable" acid and not "non-oxidizing".

Dichromate is itself an actual oxidizing acid as explained before here.

In water it breaks down into chromate ion (CrO4)-- and chromic acid (H2CrO4).

I originally (around 1985) did some experiments to see if reducing the acidity of the DICHROMATE reversal

bleach actually improved the final image. The answer was that it did (using Agfa material then.) The improvement was not huge but worth pursuing. I first switched over to using the non nasty sodium hydrogen

sulfate instead of the generally used nasty sulfuric acid. I then tried other acids . Toluene sulfonic acid worked nicely, bleaching took longer though. (but you should'nt Hurry Holography ).

I found I could not wait for ever though by relying on the natural acidity of dichromate alone even ammonium dichromate. Acetic acid which is another non -oxidizable acid in the solution was simply not strong enough .

However please note now that there is no need to use p-toluene sulfonic acid , I have found that cutting down the sodium hydrogen sulfate to about 1% (or even 1/2 %)does the same job. I have put this on the Holowiki.

Jeff
