Two-color 4x6" reflection hologram on PFG-01 film.

The masters were exposed on PFG-01 film laminated to glass with Kapco. The faces are made of clay and the background is rough wood with a black circle where the faces go. Alignment still needs some work but overall came out the way I'd envisioned.

All were developed with JD-2 developer and EDTA bleach.
