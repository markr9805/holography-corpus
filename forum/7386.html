Interested in recent developments in 3D imaging techniques?

Want to find a new market for your holographic skills,

meet fellow holographers and make contacts?

Then you should attend

A one day conference at De Montfort University

on Friday September 19th 2008

in association with

The Royal Photographic Society Holography Group

Experts in the field will talk on holography’s past, present and future applications in museum displays, its role in documentation and analysis of artefacts, and its appeal as a subject for exhibitions of fine art and popular culture.

There will be an exhibition to accompany the conference and and a hands-on workshop for those wishing to gain practical experience of working with the medium

Further details and on-line booking at a discounted price are available at

[http://www.holography.co.uk/RPS/events.html](http://www.holography.co.uk/RPS/events.html)
