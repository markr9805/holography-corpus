Regarding softening the existing emulsion, have you tried the old pre-toning emulsion softener? It was a standard for softening the emulsion of prints that had been fixed with a hardening fixer, so that they could be more easily toned and washed. The content was simple enough, 30 grams of sodium carbonate (monohydrate) and water to make a liter, with the print being immersed for up to ten minutes. I've never used it, so I can't vouch for any amount of softening or what it might do to film stock.
