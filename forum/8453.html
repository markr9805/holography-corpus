Dear all,

In 1979 i set up a hologram studio with a hughes 5mw Hene, In 1984 I bought a coherent (about 12inches), polarised NEC HeNe laser. I am about to build a new studio but am hopelessly out of date. I have gathered that I can now buy a solid state LED Laser with a ridiculously long coherence length of a metre or two and up to 200 mw in the green wavelength or maybe up to 50 mw in the red. Please to save me hours of searching can anyone recommend the most powerful, coherent, polarised, thermally stable non mode hopping LED laser and power supply. Price is not an issue, but I want as good if not better results than I had with my 25mw laser. I tend to make 10x 8 holograms and want to do even larger formats. Thank you in advance. sorry to be such a bore.
