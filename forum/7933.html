I think he uses some sort of web translator.

What I understand is that he may have made a hologram with a red laser, with a "TMG less" formula, where he may have used ammonia hydroxide instead of TMG. Now he wants to make a G308 one with a 50 mW greenie pointer. If so, my answers are:

-a green laser pointer will work, but you'll get a hologram full of "lines" as it was sliced due to the mode hops. It is a good educational tool though.

-there is no such thing as "parrasitic Lipman". For the system to work, a face of the glass should be highly reflective. It looks like you've got some interferences there, so it may be a dim hologram.
