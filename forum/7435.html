Seventh Son Thu Jul 17, 2008 7:43 pm #1
I am proposing something that I believe would help this DCG forum. I think a universal standardized process would be to everyone’s benefit. None of us will ever be speaking the same (DCG) language if we don’t share certain constants. I realize that there is a host of reasons why everyone does their processes differently (gelatin, lasers, development). In my brief time on this forum I have experienced a great deal of frustration with the multitude of comments that bring little more than confusion to the individuals receiving them (mine included).

I believe that the first BIG variable to be tackled is the GELATIN that we use. I have read a lot of cavalier comments about which type to use, and where to get it. But I guarantee that if my viscosity and bloom is different from what you are using, then virtually any advice I dispense will be of limited value.

For example, if we’ve agreed to formulate 10-20-100, what have we really done? My gelatin is from Mars, and yours is from Venus. We think we’ve mixed the same 10grams of gelatin. But have we?

Because of the expense, I can’t imagine how we can unify the Laser portion of this problem. However, if we lock down the gelatin, and then agree upon a formula we will be dramatically closer to speaking the same language. Along with formula, a standardized developing process can be adopted.

How about we buy a communal barrel or two of gelatin?

I’m leaving a lot out, but I wanted to get some reaction before blathering on.

United we stand, divided we fall.-Aesop
