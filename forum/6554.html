> Now, It is my understanding that if I dry a hologram (I have not tried) with submerging it from a water based bath into a heated 100% alcohol bath it will dry the outside layers more than the inner layers and make a broadband hologram. Yes that is for the most part correct with DCG. The temperature change from one bath to another helps accentuate this. Cooler and temperatures closer together from one bath to the next helps with narrow band and warmer with temperatures with big steps from one to the next helps with broadband. Also short baths help broadband and longer baths in each alcohol help narrow band.

I have never taken my alcohol to Dave's extreem. 120F (50c) is the hottest I usually use.
