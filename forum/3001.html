JohnFP Mon Jan 03, 2005 7:57 pm #4
Funny you stated that. The last batch of plates I made I used the finest draw bar I have with Scotch tape around the sides of the glass and was very pleased with the results. Also, I found that when you make your emulsion, and then filter it, you may see a couple of bubbles. You can also take a cotton swab, or I use the corner of a paper towel, to lift these bubbles out of the emulsion prior to coating. I found these to be a detrimental if they ended up on the plate. Keep up the good work and congratulation on your work. It looks great.
