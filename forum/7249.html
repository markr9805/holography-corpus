Stas Fri Apr 11, 2008 11:33 am #14
> Is The Slavitch film and plates the cheapest? I don't want to make 2 and a 1/2" square holograms, I want to make 10" x 8" plus size.

The Slavich plates are the only plates manufactured on industrial equipment. Those plates and film sold through Geola distribution network has same consistent quality. So if you want cheap solution, you can try film and plates manufactured by hobbyists. But if you want certified quality materials, you are welcomed to geola web shop at [http://www.geola.lt/eshop/index.htm](http://www.geola.lt/eshop/index.htm) . As for the general good materials price level, when Agfa stopped film manufacturing ten years ago, Agfa film price was then 190USD, now certified film from Geola cost ~200eur. Having in mind the inflation level of ~10% per year, nowadays film prices actually ARE CHEAPER than it used to be in 1998.

Stas Fri Apr 11, 2008 1:18 pm #16
> The Slavich plates are the only plates manufactured on industrial equipment.1) How do you know this?

Because we are in photomaterials distribution business from 1998 - this is my job to know who is manufacturing what.

> 2) PFG-03M and PFG-03C film seems no longer available – why?

Because of very short shelf life time. To make good coating, we need to coat at least 500 square meters of film. And we are doing that for VRP, PFG-01 and can do that for PFG-03C. So PFG-03C can be available in quantities starting from 200 square meters.

> 3) What happened to that new ultra-fine grain photofilm for colour holography (named, I think, PGV-P) you announced at the 2006 Symposium on Display Holography?

We are using it for our synfograms (digital holograms). If somebody is interested in quantities from 200 square meters and higher, e-mail me. Or if somebody will advise how to prolong cut sheets shelf life, we will be able to offer this film in small quantities as well.
