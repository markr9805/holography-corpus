Two days into using DCG plates for the first time, and I am having a ball. Sure there are problems to overcome, crackly looking streaks in places caused by my processing, some minor burn out in places, evil greenies. But the brightness and the color make those other problems so minor. DCG is awesome, it is better than Silver. Dinesh and Joy are awesome, they are better than Gold.

What do you mean my color control? Color control is not difficult in itself. It's called a tuning bath or AmDi concentration or shooting wavelength and is public knowledge. Tuning bath for color control post exposure, AmDi for color control pre exposure and wavelength for color control during exposure.

I don't use a wetting agent in any of my baths and I don't have streaking or greenie problems at all any more. Is the wetting agent supposed to compensate for some variable left uncontrolled? Greenies and steaking are all due to, not one variable but many, any of which might have gone unchecked.
