MichaelH Thu Jan 20, 2005 11:42 am #4
When I used sugar ( [http://www.dragonseye.com/holography/2003_02.html](http://www.dragonseye.com/holography/2003_02.html) ) to color shift my PFG-01 I used anything from 5-20 ml of granulated sugar and filled the container to 100ml of water. Soak the film for five minutes, squeegee and let dry for an hour or so (depends on the size of film) or hit it with a hair dryer on the non-emulsion side until dry.

Here's some info on how I soak and dry PFG-01 film [http://www.dragonseye.com/blog/archives ... eeing.html](http://www.dragonseye.com/blog/archives/11-Squeegeeing.html)

and a thread here on what can happen if you use a dirty hair dryer and dry the emulsion side. [http://holographyforum.org/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1950](http://holographyforum.org/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?t=1950)
andy75180 Thu Jan 20, 2005 12:14 pm #5
> rja4096: Would my method be ok, pre soak for 2 mins, then dry for 2 hours, then use normally?

Well I highly recommend you squeegee off the emulsion side or else the finished hologram will be all streaky and messy.

The procedure I use at the moment is:

Plate out of box and into 5% TEA for 1 minute.

Squeegee off plate and dry with hairdryer about 2 mins.

Put plate in holder and leave for 30 mins

Take exposure and develop.

This seems to be giving me good results at moment. Takes a bit of practice with squeegee though.
Colin Kaminski Thu Jan 20, 2005 4:08 pm #6
The advantage of sugar is you can use it post exposure for shifting the replay color more red permently. Sorbitol is the preffered sugar. There is some anecdotal evidence that it tends to shift over time. I know of one hologram that is 10 years old and the owner thinks it seems to be shifting more green.

This is going to become more common for VRP-M and DPSS lasers. I still have not bought any VRP-M but I expect I will have to learn these techniques soon.
