This is my first piece made in the kitchen. It is the DCG vith rhodamine 6G.

Water................................100ml

Gelatine for cooking................12g

P11...................................5drops

Adjusted pH to 9.5 with acetic acid

Amonium dichromate...............4g

Rhodamin 6G 0,4%................2ml

The P11 is comercial name for epoxy hardener dietylenetriamine, [2,2´-iminodi(ethane-1-amine)]

The size of the plate is 25x15 cm, depth is 4cm.

Exposed by 532nm diode module (35€), 5mW, 60min

![Image](http://www.digineff.cz/galerie/data/535/10384Zamek-med.jpg)

After this success, my laser burned out.

I was looking for a chemical, that is low cost and can have some similar properties like TMG. Diethylenetiamine contains in molecule secondary amine and also is strongly alcaline. And it cost just 1€ fot 20 ml, so I tried it with succes. But very big munus fot this chemical is, that plates must be exposed within two days maximum because of quick dark reaction. I used plates one day old.

For the coating I used the method with glass treated with atirain. In the corners of the plate were 1,5 cm squares of 0,1 mm thick PVC tape.
