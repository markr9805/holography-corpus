A friend here at work does an outdoor haunted house with various 'rooms' which have some effect or experience for the visitors. I was thinking of ways add to some special lighting or holographic effect that would be both interesting yet mostly automated at the same time.

One idea is to have a white bedsheet and rear-project a crazy mad scientist DannyBruza type character on to it. ;') He could have a table with various lab equipment on it bubbling away wth interesting lighting (not on video, but actually there in front of the projection). He (the scientist) would be toiling away chattering to himself (a DVD loop). The video would need to be shot of a person with a black background, I would suppose, to give the illusion that he's not just an element of a larger static projection, if you know what I mean?

Anyways, since Halloween is around the corner, I'm curious what things you've done for the occation using holography (or more basic use of projections or lasers) that were interesting?

-Phil
