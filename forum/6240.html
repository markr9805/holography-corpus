Joe Farina Tue May 08, 2007 4:25 pm #2
> d paul: I would jump on the s-p unit but: 1) it's an as-is deal ( though he says the tube's okay)

Unless the seller can provide more information, under no circumstances would I buy it. Well, I might give him a couple of hundred. But if the tube were good, why didn't the seller show a picture of the tube lit? The power supply has a huge number of hours on it. If the tube doesn't light, you will just be buying a boat anchor until someone refurbs it.

I gave a lot of thought as to whether I wanted one of the big old 125's or one of the newer, smaller 127's. I chose the latter. It's true that the ultimate in power is achieved by the 125 (or the Russian or Japanese counterparts). But the power needs to be weighed against other factors. First, I would want an etalon for the 125, but I can get by without an etalon for the 127. Etalons for the 125 are very hard to come across, and when they are installed they are finicky (so I hear). So there will be a power loss when an etalon is used.

More importantly to me, the old SP-125's are soft-sealed (epoxy) tubes, and this is not good. If you go the SP-125 route, then it would be essential (for me) to have someone available who could repair or refurbish it. The world authority on SP HeNe refurbishing is Don Gillespie in Ann Arbor, and I would basically need Don himself, and he may be retiring one of these days. To be painfully honest, refurbishing these lasers competently may rest in Don's hands alone. So, to make a long story short, I decided to live with the lower-power 127 (or OEM equivalent), which can be replaced instead of refurbished (at least at this point in time).
