Has anyone tried a reflection hologram using a tranmission LCD screen?

Basically, put a mirror behind the LCD screen and use it in a Sinle Beam Reflection set up? I know it would be 2-D but was just wondering if anyone has tried it?
Ed Wesly Thu Nov 01, 2007 3:52 pm #2
A million years ago (actually about 20) I shot some holograms of an LCD screen in a two beam set up, the LCD screen parallel to the holo plate, back lit with a diffuser, reference from the other side at 45 degrees.

The goal was to layer CAT scan data, similar to the Voxel group idea, but we didn't know about them at the time. The LCD screen was a big one, designed to sit on top of an overhead projector. It was the weak link in the experiment, since its dynamic range was 2:1; the black pixels were half as bright as the bright ones. So with 3 or 4 layers of info, it was like trying to look through a snowstorm. Maybe with contemporary screens more favorable results could be made.

Don't forget to remove the polarizers from the screen!
