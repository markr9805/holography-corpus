dcgman Fri Oct 27, 2006 5:50 pm #7
> Tony: They're around $75 from Edmund optics....

quoting from the Edmund site: These are continuously variableneutral density filters. The density (and film thickness) varies linearly across the filter’s length.

The items you're talking about have metallic coatings, and will absorb a portion of the transmitted beam.

The advantage of all dielectric coatings is that they do not absorb anywhere near as much light as metallic coatings
