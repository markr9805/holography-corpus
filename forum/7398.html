In the Gallery there are a couple of images of the 9 metre hologram now on show at the Canberra Museum and Gallery. There are also a couple of metre square animated stereograms, some Russian reflections and a new pulse laser hologram being exhibited; six in total. The lights are being left on 24 hours a day so it looks very spectacular at night; drawing in the crowds I'm told.
