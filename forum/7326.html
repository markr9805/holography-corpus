what will remove rain-x from glass?

and what is the difference between rain-x's Rain-x and Rain-x Anti-fog ?
Hans Sat May 10, 2008 10:35 am #2
Rain-x makes glass hydrophobic. When treated with it the glass repels water.

Anti-fog does the exact opposite. So instead of forming small droplets when water condensates, it forms a very thin transparent water film on the glass.
