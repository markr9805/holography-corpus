Tom B. Thu Mar 01, 2007 3:22 am #3
I found several pages using the second quote with a citation - "The future cannot be predicted, but futures can be invented." - Dennis Gabor, winner of the 1971 Nobel Prize in Physics, "Inventing the Future," 1964, so at least this version is verifiable (or at least falsifiable).

I think it quite possible that in public speech and in other writings he could have delivered some of the other variations on the phrase that are out there.
