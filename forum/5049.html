Would it be possible to replace the infra-red diode in a dpss laser with a higher powered one? That is, are the parts behind the infra-red diode separable from the diode in cheap green laser pointers or are they glued to the diode. It would be an interesting project to replace the diode with a current stabilized high power diode. I don't own a green pointer. Has anyone ever taken theirs apart? It would be an interesting project since 808nm diodes with the integrated sensing photodiode come very cheap in the internet.
wler Fri Sep 09, 2005 5:19 pm #2
It's possible but very difficult. One problem is the correct adjustment, also the tiny crystals get easily damaged if pumped too strong (there will be bad spots in them). For each crystal there is a limit beyond which the lifetime is dramatically reduced. If you read german (not unlikely given your name;-), check the website laserfreak.de, there are lots of knowledegable people who did this and related things. Perhaps building from scratch is at the end the best way....
