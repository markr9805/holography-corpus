Hans, I wonder if:

You could expose the hologram to laser light for hardening as you do but do it when the hologram is just placed in the water bath such that the fringes are expanded a little while it is hardening.

Or, process the hologram completely as you normally do up to the completion of the water bath. Then soak the hologram in a 3 or 4% concentration of AmDi or KDi in water with a couple of drops of Photo-flo. Then lean the plate against the wall and let it dry for a few minutes (10). JohnFP Tue Jan 09, 2007 5:42 pm #4
I have found that the concentration of the tuning bath gives color shift direction. The tuning bath should be between 30 and 40% alcohol to water. I find it difficult to red shift but easier to blue shift. My envionment changes so much from day to day and month to month that I can not tell you an actual % for shift. Higher concentration is red, lower is blue. To perform reprocessing, just soak in tuning bath then 100% (90% can be used before 100 to save the 100%). The length of time in the tuning bath helps with the amount of shift. A good starting place is between 15 and 45 seconds. The longer you leave it in the tuning bath the further in that direction the shift is. A higher temperature yields broadband characteristics up to a point. I have found that if I leave it in a high temperature bath for a longer period of time I can get color shift with broadband yielding pastels. Too high a temperature will cause milkyness. Lower temperature with long times causes narrower band.

Try to take one of your yellow holograms cut it in half and put half in a bath of 40% alcohol to water for 45 seconds at room temp with constant agitation. Then put in 100% for 1 minute with constant agitation. Air Dry. Then try the exact same with the other half but use 30% alcohol. Hopefully your shifts will be different.

I also found that shifting blue to red or red to blue is very difficult. I have had most of my success shifting 100nm or so (green to orange or green to blue).

The best way to control color is with concentration of AmDi or KDi and exposure time. And the best way to control broadband vs. narrow band is age of film. and exposure time.
