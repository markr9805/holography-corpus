I was in K-Mart and as always I keep my eyes open for anything off the shelf that can be used for holography. Well I found these LED keychain flashlights for less then $4.00 US. I bought one and measures it output. At 1mm I got 10 mw. More then my HeNe lasers put out. I opend it, took out the switch and soldered a wire to the switch end of the LED and the matching end of the switch (very easy to do). I fed the wires through the switch hole which was left after the button part of the switch was removed. Thus connecting the two wires (an exterior switch could be added) I was able to keep the LED on full time.

I did notice through my handy dandy cool diffraction glasses that this is a little yellow and a very miniscule amount of green in the diffration pattern.

Is this normal for Red LED's?

Anyway the LED are from K-Mart and are the brand RAYOVAC.
