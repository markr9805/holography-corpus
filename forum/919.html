Jeffrey Weil Mon Aug 04, 2014 5:16 pm #3
India has many embossed holography companies. Try the Hologram Manufactures Association of India. Find a company near you and go and talk to them. Most people that apply for jobs are just looking for a job. Let them know you're interested in not just a job, but a job in holography.

If you've made any holograms at home take them with you. No matter what they look like. Take them even if they are very poor quality. If you show them that you really want to be there for more then a weekly pay check they will find a place for you.

Good luck. Here's their website and their company list.

Dinesh Mon Aug 04, 2014 6:37 pm #4
If you want to work in holography, that is, get a job in a holography company, then Jeff's suggestion is a good start. Bear in mind that a lot of these mass production houses treat holographers as part of an assembly line. You learn very little holography, you simply follow the routine.

If you want to understand holography and make your own holograms, then, in India, your biggest challenge is probably equipment. You need to get a laser, an unobstructed power supply (I know in India power abruptly simply stops for hours at a time), chemicals and a place to work. In this case, I'd say your best way is to find a college or university near you that has an engineering or physics course, and ask them where to find lasers etc.

Dinesh Sun Aug 17, 2014 1:29 pm #9
It's difficult to give a method on how to make a hologram in this forum. The process is complicated. I know that books in general are not available in India, since I was teaching holography to a company in Kerala and they had no access to books easily available in the west. The question is, are you a hobby holographer (do you have a job that does not involve holography), or are you a holographer in a new holography company? Or are you a student?

The first problem is equipment. Do you have access to a laser?, Film? Chemicals? I would suggest three alternatives:

1. Call HOMAI (Jeff Weil posted a link). If you want to be a professional holographer, then joining HOMAI is a good beginning. They will give you useful information and connect you to other hologram manufacturers in India. If this is a hobby, HOMAI will give you contacts with colleges and universities and places where you can get equipment.

2.Go to Integraf : [http://www.integraf.com/](http://www.integraf.com/) ( I know Integraf supplies some companies in India).

3. Find a local college that has a laser and other equipment and ask the professor there if you can join or start a holography group.
