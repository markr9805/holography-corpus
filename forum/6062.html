MichaelH Tue Feb 20, 2007 11:03 am #3
jungjedi, you can certainly make it work. How much work you have to put into it all depends on what your goals are.

The most critical aspect is your basic stability. Before attempting anything beyond a very basic single-beam hologram (which you can make in a pot of sand on a paver) you need to test your area with an interferometer. Once you know what affects your work area (for instance, can you stand in the same room without your heart beat showing up?) you can work to isolate your setup from those elements. IE, AC coming on, people walking around, doors opening and closing. Knowing what your starting conditions are will determine how much work is needed to create a stable, vibration free area.

You might be interested in the work I've done to create a stable 2nd floor lab area.
