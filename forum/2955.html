Try taking it out in the sun and orientate it the same way.If the grasshopper is there it'll show up much better and there are no other sources nearly as bright (sometimes I've been fooled by reflected sunlight off metal surfaces forming a second reference).

There's a second probability. That is, besides the off reference reconstruction from some other source creating aberrations. It's possible you're looking at a second order. The hologram needs to be pretty bright for second orders to show up, but I have seen them in some resist holograms. I doubt the cat is that bright, though.

A third probablility: Is there a grasshopper (or grasshopper type) object nearby reflecting off the surface of the silver foil?
