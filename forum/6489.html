Hans Wed Oct 17, 2007 7:06 pm #9
I used Bison optical glue for gluing my beam splitter to 0.5cm thick glass. Works fine. I cured the glue under a uv facial tanner lamp. These lamps cost about 20 Euro new. Any spilled or run over glue can be cleaned with acetone.

Word of advice. Do practice on small pieces of glass before you try it on your spiffy new variable beam splitter.

JohnFP Mon Oct 22, 2007 1:47 pm #17
If the glue and glass that was added is the same index of refraction of the glass of the bs then you should not see a third reflection. The most important thing to consider about the glue is it's index of refraction when cured (and make sure it's damage threashold is above the power you are using.

If done correctly you should see only two dots and they should be separated more then they were without the added glass. The object is to have the no reflections at the junction of the two pieced of glass, just a reflection off the front and back which is now thicker and sepated more.
