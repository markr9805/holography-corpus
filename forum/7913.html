Hello everybody.

A few months ago I found here a note from Frank (Beijing Viasho Technology Co.,Ltd) about their single mode lasers for holography.

We had some bad experience with other Chinese lasers, as they were not exactly single mode, and did not work for holography.

After discussing this with Frank, we decided to order and test one unit (green 532-SLM-150mW).

It arrived with some small damage (I think shipping problem) - but Frank took quick care of it, and soon after - we received a fully functional replacement.

Wow! This laser is gooood! Crisp, stable, small, easy to use in different setups - and perfectly single mode, great for holography.

The power is good enough for our commercial system, but Frank now offers even stronger, 200 mW.

If any of you have same thoughts as we did have before - believe me, we are just now ordering more of them - to upgrade our whole mastering system and production. Thanks to Frank and his guys, now we can have fine quality lasers - which couple of years ago would cost tens of thousands of Dollars, each! Thanks Frank.

It sounds like advertising (I know) - but I think good news need to be shared too, not just warnings and complaints. email to Frank is: [wzcdehao@hotmail.com](mailto:wzcdehao@hotmail.com)

Best regards to the group and to Collin.
