Hi Rob.

Mix everything except for KDi and MB and pour it in a botlle wrapped with aluminum foil (to protect the mixture from ambient light). Add KDi and adjust the pH (since it changes a little after adding KDi solution). You may use pH papers. Then add the dye - you can do it in regular light. I use this approach and it seems to work fine.

Have fun
