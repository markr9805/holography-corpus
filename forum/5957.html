JohnFP Wed Jan 03, 2007 11:45 am #6
Kris, that is a great hologram. I love the white. I always liked a white holograms instead of a red or green or yellow holograms because I feel it looks more natural. The public is more used to seeing black and white photos instead of black and green photos.

> the rodcoating machine doesn't work very well the coating is not nice ( air bubbles ) who has a solution?

A couple of months ago I was playing around with "evening" out a homemade emulsion that I could see had some ridges and uneven spots. I took a hair dryer and heated the bottom side of the plate (the glass side) while the emulsion was facing up and the plate was horizontal. I watched as the emulsion liquified and all the uneven ridges disappeared. You could possibly try the same. I am not sure if the bubbes would burst, but it may be worth a try. If it is just a few bubbles, then you could heat from the bottom with the hair dryer and then pop the bubbles with a tooth pic.
