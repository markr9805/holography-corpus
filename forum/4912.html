tpark Wed Jun 22, 2005 6:18 pm #1
Hi, we're having some odd things happening, and I wonder if you have any ideas/comments.

I've made some holograms with the PFG-01 film, and they've turned out pretty good, except that some of the ones

that have a slightly higher exposure show a dark area in the area of greatest exposure. Exposures of 150

microjoules/cm2 seem to be good, but ones of higher exposure show the dark spot. These are all done with

a 10mw HeNe laser, making reflection holograms.

The hologram itself turned out fine though - it's only slightly darker in the darkened area. I saw another message about dark spots, but this one isn't random - it's clearly where the center of the gaussian spot was.
MichaelH Wed Jun 22, 2005 7:16 pm #2
It sounds like burnout. A greatly over-exposed area of the film.

What are you using for development and bleaching?
tpark Wed Jun 22, 2005 7:54 pm #3
I guess I should've mentioned that - I'm using the JD-3 process.

--Ted
MichaelH Wed Jun 22, 2005 8:28 pm #4
Ok. With that bleach you should find that areas that are greatly burned-out are darker. If you'd been using the jd2 bleach you'd find that they're greener.
