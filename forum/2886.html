Blondie is a former fridge-magnet angel with wings surgically removed by me. Her companion reptile is named "Sid", but is not vicious at all. The usual technique - 4X5 BB-640, 660 nm laser, single beam Denisyuk, TEA preswell - I've lost track of the percentage as I'm always tweaking it. About 12 second exposure at ~100 uW/cm^2. Lighting for pic was an Optiled amber LED.

Thanks! But it's not really deep - the sun's only about an inch from the plate and is already a bit fuzzy on replay. Maybe the blurring conveys an illusion of depth? The other objects are almost touching the plate.

Another oddity is that I'm using a diverging beam (8-10 degrees) for recording. This leads to slightly exaggerated perspective - among other things it increases the apparent depth of relatively flat objects like fridge magnets. For some strange reason I like this distortion better than the more faithful results that would be obtained from using a collimated beam.
