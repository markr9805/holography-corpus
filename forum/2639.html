Or have someone found any better option than the Panasonic diode? The Mitsubishi diode seems to be a bit unreliable.

If you're near a hop zone, the LNCQ05PS can be unreliable too, but I've generally had good results with that panasonic device. I have had good results with the ML1016R after getting it away from a hop zone, and driving the device with a constant current driver.

[http://www.thorlabs.com/ProductDetail.c ... t_ID=36213](http://www.thorlabs.com/ProductDetail.cfm?DID=6&ObjectGroup_ID=7&Product_ID=36213)

This diode looks promising, but I haven't purchased or tried it yet. It looks like it can put out 80mw, 658nm of eyeball cooking laser light, but I suspect you can run it at lower power, probably resulting in shorter wavelength and somewhat less coherence length. I think you need an external photodiode if you want to have a constant power setup, however I suspect it might be better to use constant current and go way below the maximum rated power.

On another note, are there recommendations for a 635 nm diode out there? I haven't tried too many of the 635 nm lasers, but I haven't had good results with the ones I have tried.
