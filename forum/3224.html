No response from alt.lasers, however since this is a rare species there is really not much information, except which can be gleaned from older SDL catalogs.

Problem fixed with my new laser! There was an internal spatial filter which wasn't aligned causing much decreased output! I'm now getting output >100mw at 670nm, the most my power meter can measure. Output is probably at least 200-300mw!

Punches a whole through paper almost instantly

I have been told by a researcher who has one of these that linewidth was tested with theirs, and ended up w/ coherence >3meters. With tweaking probably more. Should be useful for holography.

I had no idea about this type of diode laser. Specifically, it is useful for holography because of the type of laser diode, called a tapered amplifier, you get both high power >500mw and single mode, whereas most higher power diodes are mulitimode. Since it uses feedback from a grating it is also rock solid stable. A link to some info. about tapered amplifiers- [http://www.toptica.com/index.php?catego ... d=52&menu=](http://www.toptica.com/index.php?categorie=1&itemid=52&menu=)

Here is a link to a site which has a laser similar to mine, except the one I have is 670nm not 785nm, but is otherwise identical minus the internal spatial filter. [http://drip.colorado.edu/~schlotta/SDL-8530.html](http://drip.colorado.edu/~schlotta/SDL-8530.html)

I guess the only real problem is finding an emulsion sensitive to 670nm. This laser can be tweaked 'a few nm' but that would still be 665nm or so...

I wonder how sensitive the Yves Gentet emulsions are at the upper end of the curve-the ones that are rated 620-660nm?
