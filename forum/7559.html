Colin Kaminski Thu Oct 09, 2008 7:15 am #2
Narrow bandwidth filters can be made from an HOE but the efficiency can not be as god as a DE stack. All modern narrow band filters are a DE stack. I have seen DE stacks going down to 1A! I can't see a HOE doing that well. The idea is a reflection hologram that reflects everything but the passband. It would not be hard to design one and the recording setup. See "Practical Design and Fabrication of Optical Thin Films" for some theory.
