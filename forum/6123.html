Joe Farina Sun Mar 25, 2007 11:33 am #1
Edmund has some new beamsplitters. I'm not familiar with them, but they look interesting. I would have provided a link to Edmund, but their site seems to be down this morning.

They are called "polarizing plate beamsplitters."

"These polarizers consist of a thin layer of aluminum MicroWires adhered to a glass window....When incident light strikes the grid, P-polarized light contacts a dielectric and is transmitted while S-polarized light contacts a mirror and is reflected...."

The wavelength range is 420 to 670nm, the transmission in this range for P-polarized light is between ~85 to 88%, and the reflection of S-polarized light is about the same. The stock number is C48-544, price $95.
