Make a series of strips in your graphics program (eg photoshop ) with each one a fixed amount darker than the previous using greyscale mode. Tape these to the wall of your processing area. As you process, compare the film darkness against these strips. When you find a darkness that works well, not the strip number and always develop to the darkness of that strip.
