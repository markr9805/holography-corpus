Hans Sat Aug 18, 2007 12:08 pm #6
I did try the GF-40 once for DCG. That is a gelatin only film. I got the brightest hologram I ever made. I found that the problem with the GF-40 was that the base was very thin: 125um. The GF-55 comes in a thickness of 190um. That should make it somewhat easier to index match it flat.

For the sensitivity issue. You are right. But the basic specification is about the same as that for Fuji: 250uJ for Filmotec versus 200uJ for Fuji. I found that I could get away with exposures of 70uJ on Fuji for transmission work. Perhaps the same goes for Filmotec. Kris Meerloo will send me a sample package that he still has in his fridge. I will test it next week and let you know how it goes.
