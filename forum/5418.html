I’m still experimenting to find the best Index matching fluid.

I first made a holo using veg oil which I have used fairly successfully in the past – this time on my new bench in the garage so the ambient temperature and temp of my rinsing fluid was about only just above freezing.

My procedure is just after exposure, is to rinse the film in a tub of water to remove the oil, then to develop. When developing I noticed quite a bit of oil still stuck on the film, as a result the development was blotchy in places.

I then did another holo using white spirit for index matching, This washed off ok, but I think the fluid moved in the middle of the holo, as the final holo has dark specks in the middle.

I was then explaining the problem to my wife – who was not really interested, but said – ‘What about using KY Jelly’. I’m not sure what this is called in the US, but it’s a sexual lubricant – which is clear, thick and most importantly water based.

So, hopefully, it should be thick enough to remain still in the sandwich (unlike white spirit) and wash off in water (I’ll try having it warmer too).

I’ll let you know the results when I’ve tried it.

Regards, Rick
