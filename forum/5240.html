JohnFP Thu Dec 08, 2005 12:01 am #11
Ok, I just got my bottle so I can be very specific. There are two working solutions. One for film and plates and one for paper. I think this was mentioned but I did not have the bottle in front of me and only remembered the ratio. I originally used their directions they gave for film/plates (to make one gallon) and since the fixer is a quart size this is 3:1 as I was fixing a "plate".

But this incorrect. You need to make the solution as per their directions for paper which is (to make two gallons) which is 7:1. Straight fixer to water.

I am sorry if again I was confusing.

So your final solution is 3:1 then diluted again 7:1 which is probably too weak. I am not sure what will happen if you just try to increase fix time, I never tried it but if you don't have any more fixer you could try another minute or two in the fixer. Additional exposure will definitly help with the weak fixer/hardener.

So in this case I would first mix a fresh batch of fixer for paper (7:1) 700ml water, 100 ml fixer and 10 ml hardener straight....and start with longest exposure and work up (10 seconds, then 20 seconds, then 40 seconds then 80 seconds).

I think you underexposed and/or didn't harden enough due to diluted fixer/hardener strength.
