Are finer threads really that much better?

John Klayer Sun Jan 21, 2007 12:18 pm #1
I've built a few home made space filters. My first used 4-40 threads. Then I used 0-80. One would think that even finer threads would be better. I took one of my 0-80 models and changed it to 2-56 screws. I don't see a much difference in precision of adjustment.
Tony Sun Jan 21, 2007 1:52 pm #2
I too have been making my own spatial filters. What I find more important than the pitch of the threads is the precision of the fit. For example, I had one design where I tapped 4-40 holes in a piece of aluminum that was 3/16" thick, and the screws wobbled so much side-to-side it was useless. I replaced them with Thorlabs 6-80 adjusters with .375 threaded bushings, and it is now totally solid. Another design had Thorlabs M2 adjusters with .21" long bushings, and even those wobbled too much.

Try to analyze if it's the pitch itself, or the fit of the screw in the threads that is important in your design.
