OK sorry to bug you people with another newbie question and i feel it might be a little bit out of the focus, but I am sure as most people are really holography fans you could definitely give me input and oppinions here.

I know that this forum is heavily focused on making holograms yourself, which is of course the best a holography fan could do with his time and money - i think every hologram you produce is a piece of artwork just as a photograph or painting that defines a bit of art you leave for history.

Still as a holography fan you could probably not resist and look at these cheap mass produced commercial holograms that you see in some novelty stores and so. We probably all know the "blinking eye" or the smiling shakespear that have been produced in thousands and always caught your eye in novelty or souvenir shops. You can buy the smaller ones for like 5 dollars and the "larger" postcard sized ones maybe for like 20 - 50 bucks. I think if you were after them you would just check a few webshops and pick the one that charges 25 instead of 30 bucks.

But besides these "super mass production" holograms there are of course also some in limited quantities from "unique" pieces of course up to small series of 5-50 pieces. I find these hard to get, i found like 3 webshops that have a few of them.

As usual the pieces that interest me most are also the most expensive ones. What i really like are "larger" holograms in either dichromated gelatin ones due to their bright appearance in every light and multicolored holograms. I think they should be at least like 8x9 inch or close to that as the wall i want to decorate with it is rather large. Holograms i liked were all between 600 and 1000 Euro, is that the normal price or is there a way to get a nice one cheaper?

What i lack is some kind of "directory of all hologram webshops" or so. I thought maybe i can start posting a list of my favorite links to sellers and if other people in the forum are interested you could also share your links? Or what would you people do if you look for a nice hologram for your wall. Yeah i know most here would probably just make one yourself, but then maybe offer to sell me a nice one at a good price smiles... I would also want to make my own holograms, but i think I am still lightyears behind that regarding equipment, motives and experience.

BobH Tue Jun 14, 2011 7:38 am #7
As much as I love holographic artwork, there doesn't appear to be much investment value in it. Consider those who bought works done in the late '80s. Probably be lucky to find a single buyer at 1/10th the price paid then. Nothing against the artists personally, and I'd love to have some of that work myself. It was always waaaaaaaay overpriced though for me. Same thing with all the "art" holography I've encountered over the years. One good thing is that all that work has probably spent the last 25 years in dark storage, seen by nobody but safe from the printout and color shifting caused by exposure to light, and other degradation from continual display. I'd keep watching Ebay.
