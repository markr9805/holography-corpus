I built a supply for my 107B for under $100 from info on Sam's web page. It has been running fine for over a year now.

As long as you have the ballast resistor, excessive starting voltage won't hurt the tube, as the tube fires before the excessive voltage is reached.

I also have a spare set of parts (capacitors, diodes, oil burner transformer) that I will sell.
