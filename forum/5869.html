Ed Wesly Sun Nov 05, 2006 2:19 pm #6
I have in my hands two sales brochures from the Multiplex Company. On the "Multiplex Company Studio Copies" page of one, and on the "Twelve Bestselling Multiplex Holograms" page of the other, there is an image called DISCO, available in 120 and 360 degree formats.

The hologram is credited to Peter Clauidus with Gary and Gloria Poole, whom I am guessing are the couple in the hologram.

He is wearing saddle shoes, Khaki pants (maybe), and a tee shirt that perhaps says SIT ON IT. (Hard to tell because of the angle of view in the catalog, the font of the letters, and the not so great print job.)

She is wearing a long skirt, has some embroidery on it including a heart and some other appliques that are hard to identify, a tight top and a scarf. They are side to side, holding each other an arm's lenght apart. They both have dark hair which fades into the black background.

On one of the brochures there is pictured another couple dancing, called TANGO, Peter Claudius once again. It doesn't seem to be the same couple, as the guy is light-haired. She has on a dress, light in tone, with a bow in her hair. He's got on light pants and shirt.

There are no copyright dates on either one, but on the brochure that has the "Twelve Bestselling Multiplex Holograms" page there is a cancelled 15 cent stamp with the postmark of Oct 19 1979, putting the images in the time frame mentioned above.

Coincidentally the stamp is of Oliver Wendell Holmes, who invented the most common stereoscope of the 19th Century, the Holmes Stereoscope!

If you would like I could turn one of the brochures into a pdf file and e-mail it to interested parties. PM me courtesy of this forum.
