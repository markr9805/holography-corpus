Not necesarily, but usually. Depends on density. For water, or substances of density close to water( 1gm/cc), it doesn't matter. For alcohol, it's usually 6mls alcohol/100mls water. For a solid in water, it's 6gms/100 mls water only because 100mls of water weighs 100 gms, so you're still 6gms substance /100 gms water. However, if you're making a solution of a substance in alcohol, then it's best to check what's meant by "6%" - I'd take it to mean 6gms substance to 100 gms alcohol (note: not a 100 mls alcohol) for a solid and 6mls substance to 100mls alcohol for a liquid.
