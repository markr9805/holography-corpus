I use the sheets that disco's use in front of their lamps. I mounted them in front of normal spot lights that people use to couzy their houses. The sheets are designed to withstand the high power of disco lamps, so temperature is not a problem.

You can buy them here: [http://www.leefilters.com/LPFD.asp?PageID=320](http://www.leefilters.com/LPFD.asp?PageID=320)

If you stack two filters, you will have absolutely no light other than red. I have two of these setups, one for red and one for green. With them I can bathe my garage in single colour safe light.

Tom B. Sun Jun 18, 2006 4:38 am #15
> Colin Kaminski: [Are you able to use the Amber Optileds with he Fuji film?

I finally did a safelight test and found that there was still some sensitivity to the Optiled amber LEDs with Fuji film. They are useable, but to avoid any fogging it's best to use the minimum possible light levels and exposure times and, for an area safelight, to shield the setup from the safelight if it's sitting there for a prolonged settling time.

Another yellow LED I tried (extracted from a solar garden light) was much worse - it was more yellow than the amber LED and the film was considerably more sensitive to it.
