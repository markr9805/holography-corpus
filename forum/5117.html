Dear all,

a few months ago, I started playing with Jeff Blyth's MBDCG/silver halide recipes.

The first obstacle to solve was bubbles in the gelatine when stirring.

I noticed that allowing gelatine to cool into the baker until it coagulates, helps to dissolve almost all bubbles dissolved in.

When gelatine is bubbles free, I'm heating baker again and proceed with coating.

No filtering step is necessary for my purpose.
