Kris Meerlo Fri Oct 06, 2006 6:47 pm #1
Some pictures from my new home made coating machine:

Some specifications:

-Temp control ( roomtemp to 70 C )

-Variable speed control.

-cooling with fans.

For the temp control I use the amazing sauna belt from amazing discoveries incl a digital temp controller from Conrad electronics.

For quick cooling off the aluminium heating plate I use harddisc drive cooling fans.

I tape the heat tracing from the Sauna belt on the Alu plate with heat resistance alu tape.

The movement of the meyerbar is variable.

( I "stole" some ideas for the coating machine from the internet ).

And I have some ideas from Hans.

One problem I have found with my machine is the following:

If I use normal glass that has only been cleaned with Glassex window cleaner, the machine produces a nice and flat coating.

But if I pre-treat the glass with silane, the glass becomes hydrophobic. This has a negative effect on the flatness of the coating because the gelatin wants to run of the glass in any direction it can while it is still liquid.

JohnFP Mon Oct 16, 2006 9:45 am #8
I am not sure what the difference between MBDCG and DCG is such that there is emulsion lift problems. I have kept my emulsion in water for 10 minutes or more at times and I have never once in my life seen emulsion lift from the glass. I soak my glass, either fresh or with an old DCG emulsion on it in a 5% solution of hydrochloric acid (Muratic Acid) over night or longer. Then wash with water twice and before it is completely dry, finich drying with a paper towel.
