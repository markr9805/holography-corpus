Tommy Wed Jun 11, 2008 2:22 am #1
And it almost managed to make a hologram... but all I got was an unphotographicly dim green blur. So now I'm looking for advice on how best to improve things.

The laser: See [http://www.bobdbob.com/~tjohnson/quantel/optics/](http://www.bobdbob.com/~tjohnson/quantel/optics/) (though thats an old image)

Its a MOPA with 2 4 inch YAG rods pumped with 2 lamps each. The resonator is about 800 mm long, and contains: HR (concave),

aperture (about 1mm), negative lens, passive q-switch, brewster plate, 1/4 wave plate, rod, 1/4 wave plate, OC. After the OC, there are two steering mirrors, the amplifier rod, then frequency doubler and steering mirrors to pick off the green and IR.

Here's my current list of things to try. Which would be the most effective? Are there any other things I have missed?

1. Shorten the resonator.

Reducing it to 530mm long seems straight forward, and would reduce the number of modes from 64 to 42. But that reduces the aperture for TEM00 from 0.9mm to 0.7mm.

2. Replace resonator optics with known components.

I figure replace the curved HR with a flat one, and get rid of the lens. What is a good reflectivity for the OC? Use an etalon for the OC?

3. Replace passive q-switch with electronic q-switch.

The laser came with a pockel cell q-switch, but the existing q-switch circuitry has Issues. I need something which uses the pump light as trigger.

4. Add a second amplifier stage.

I have a YAG rod assembly and capacitor bank (from another laser someone on ebay parted out), adding a second amplifier stage is nearly just dropping the parts in. The question is if the charging power supply can handle the additional load, and the doubling crystalthe additional light. The extra capacitor bank /does/ have a simmer supply. Use it on the resonator, and use the two non-simmer capacitor banks for the amplifiers?

5. Replace the doubling crystal.

The frequency doubling crystal is ancient (the laser was built in 1986). Would a new SHG be more efficient enough to warrant the cost?

6. Replace the lamps.

This probably won't help coherence or output, but I have no idea how old my current lamps are. Where does one get known good lamps? ebay?

Thankyou for any advice!
JohnFP Wed Jun 11, 2008 10:29 am #2
Hey Tommy. You are aware that you will not get a blurry image from object movement or short coherence etc. when making a hologram, even if pulsed. What leads you to believe something is wrong with the laser? You're getting green in I assume silver halide (black and white film) so your are getting some type of refracting fringes.

I would not go changing the laser properties right away. I would play around with your film and processing. These are a little different then CW holography. Have you tried latensification? Have you tried to use a smaller beam diameter to increase energy to the plate? Have you tried different processing and techniques?

Just my humble thoughts.
Colin Kaminski Wed Jun 11, 2008 1:05 pm #3
What is the laser configuration? If the output mirror is flat you can replace it with a etalon. If not you will have to add an etalon. You will need to be very close to single mode.

How is the temporal mode? You may need to install an aperture to make the beam narrower. Look for weights.xls from the old Rotorwave site. It will let you calculate all of the parameters.
