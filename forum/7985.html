Funny, how when you write something you think about it in a different way. I guess it would be pretty impossible to illuminate the fluoresent film with just the light off the hologram and not the raw illuminating light unless....

We polarize the light and the hologram changes the polarization that then goes though an opposite polarizer that then illuminates the fluoresent screen. (yes I just watched how LCD tv's work last night). Any light not altered by the hologram does not make it through the second cross polarizer.
