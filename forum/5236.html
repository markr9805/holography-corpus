Here is a bell that was made with two reference beams. One from above at brewsters and one from below at brewsters. Too long an exposure in first and burn in very evident from below reference beam. Second better but still to long an exposure. I would have liked to do another one with even lower exposure but I ran out of plates. It seems with this technique less then half the exposure is needed then with a single reference alone.
