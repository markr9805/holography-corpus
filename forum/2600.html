Peter, for what its worth I got my laser from car-boot sales and Sunday market stalls. There are 3 great things then

1) they are only about £3 per pack

2) They are inclined to me around 640nm rather than 670

3) Most importantly they are inclined to be well above the local government namby pamby regulations of 1mw max power.

When I last bought 10 of them only 2 were down to around the miserable 1 mw level and most were close to 4 mw(as measured with a special lab laser power meter).

Runnining with a 4.5V cycle lamp battery for 15 mins before shooting hologram gave me amazing coherence length without mode-hoping problems.

I suspect that the suppliers to the Maplin stores make an effort to comply with the power rules for the sake of biz, whereas with market stalls.......

tpark Sun Jul 04, 2004 5:44 pm #6
The laser modules that Intergraf ([http://www.holokits.com](http://www.holokits.com)) sells work very well. I've had good results from some cheapo pointer diodes that I got from the dollar store - it simply says "HI-OUTPUT KEY CHAIN LASER" and "5 IN ONE" on the box. I took the diode assembly out of there,

soldered it to a silver heat sink, and got some good shots with that.

I bought the beamsplitters that I use with my interferometer setup at surplusshed - [http://www.surplusshed.com/](http://www.surplusshed.com/) that's for the cube type beamsplitters. For plate ones, I got those at anchoroptical [http://www.anchoroptical.com/](http://www.anchoroptical.com/) - they sell other cool stuff, and I've had good success with materials from there. I bought mirrors and such from there, as well as some lenses and beamsplitters. I don't know if they still have any, but they had some "sandbox holography" kits, which are good if you have a sandbox setup, which I don't.

My experience has been that you have to be very careful to avoid drafts around your laser if you're using a diode. Also, stable power is very critical. I've purchased higher powered laser modules, and had "sliced bread" holograms with those. The Panasonic LNCQ05PS seems to be a good device (at least the ones that I have). I've generated holograms using the ML1016R diode, but they turned out sliced bread holograms.

[http://www.optima-optics.com](http://www.optima-optics.com) is good if you want to get some diodes and their holder (LDM 1100 kit) - you don't need the optics kit for holography, as the beam from the diode seems to be good enough, although you can get black tape to smoke with the focused beam if you have optics

As I mentioned before in the old forum, the laser pointers claimed 1mw, but I measured significantly higher power output than that. The diode itself wasn't encapsulated though, so there could be lifetime issues with such devices.

If the object moves, the bands will appear to be fixed on the object, where if your diode has mode issues, the bands will "slide" on the object as you move your viewpoint around. Not sure if this is always the case, but it's what I've observed.
