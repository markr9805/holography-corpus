The hologram “Forever Friends” is an Denisyuk DCG H2 shadow gram, it size is 3”x2 3/4” I found the color variation seen was due to the center of the hologram being a little to hot, for most holograms I would normally enlarge the diameter of the beam to even things out, with this variation it gives this image a primitive feeling . The film used to make both H1 andH2 was made by me and is a “green hologram” as the glass I used to seal and make the film was recycled (no not from old holograms), but from the salvation army where they are very happy to give me glass from there broken/chipped pictures for nothing. Just prior to the exposure I lightly touched the two friends to make sure they would appear as a black shadowy type images. Primarily meant for desk or table top viewing and includes a mahogany stand.

![Image](http://members.aol.com/holoart/contest1.jpg)

The hologram “Peace Within Reach” is a Denisyuk DCG H1 hologram; its size is 5” square and its reference was shot diagonally across the plate, requiring this hologram to be hung diagonally and was made using a total of three exposures. The two hand converging on each other are actually a double exposure of one hand shot and flipping the plate between exposures, originally I thought they would look like they were about to shake, but viewing the holoimage they looked more like one hand was reaching for the other, the central image where these hand converge to is a symbol of peace, easily recognized message by all. Again this hologram is a “green hologram as well. The images used in this hologram were sculpted by me as well as the matting used to mount both of these contest holograms
