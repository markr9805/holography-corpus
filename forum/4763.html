Could also be that the current is set too low. If this is the case, then the laser is dropping out and refiring quickly in succession. Does the supply have a current adjust? If so, let the unit warm up (until it buzzes), then tweak up the current a bit. You should find that this stops the buzzing. You may even get a bit more output
