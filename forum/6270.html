Sergio Wed May 23, 2007 5:16 pm #2
Dear Colin,

The Polygrama is still active and developing experimental photopolymer films based on polyester substrate, aiming panchromatic response, the focus now are for a low cost material to be used in the holographic security printing industry; however, with investment it can be returned to the Display (Arts) use, that indeed require more film efficiency than necessary for mass produced copies.

Eventually available to the holographer, there are interests from some companies to use photopolymers as educational purposes due the easy of use.

Regards,

Sergio.
