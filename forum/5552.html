MichaelH Thu Jun 01, 2006 7:31 pm #3
This is a tricky subject that depends a lot on how much power you want out of the laser and how much money you want to pour into it.

It appears that anything over 5mW in red will require precise temperature control to get consistent results which requires increasing amounts of money. The jury seems to be out as far as the reliability of the temperature controlled red units currently available. It seems that they still sometimes mode hop at time and can't be positively relied upon.

You could attempt to find a Coherent green laser on the surplus market but that will run you between $1000.00 - $1,500.00 depending on your luck. The biggest benefit to shelling out that money is that they've proven to be very reliable for holography. That counts for a lot in the long run.

About ECDL:

I have found with my D&S laser that between mode hops, the laser has excellent coherence length. I can make deep denisyuks with it, no problem. The annoying thing is that one never knows when it starts to mode-hop. It usually starts mode hopping when I least expect it. Then I have to adjust the power to a new mode hop free region. This is what I hope to prevent with my TEC+Hitachi diode. I will include the constant current source in the same temperature controlled chamber with the laser Diode. That way the current should stay stable as well. I know that my D&S has a coherence length of more than one meter when it does not mode hop. So what added benifit would an ECDL setup give me?

I would like to revise my statement. I believe now that in-between mode hops the diodes have excellent coherence length BUT (and this is a very big but) only when the current and temperature are extremely constant. I think I now understand why some of my MBDCG have come out quite bright (usually at shorter exposure times) and some less bright, even though I lean the plate against the object. When I look at the diffraction pattern reflected off a cd, I can make the fringes move in-between mode hops by adjusting the power level of my D&S laser.

I believe now that when looking over a short time, the coherence length of a diode is excellent, but looking at longer time spans the wavelength shifts slowly, even when it does not mode hop.

I will now start to make the ECDL setup and hold off the MBDCG until that is finished.
