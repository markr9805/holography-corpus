Steve M Fri Nov 24, 2006 1:50 pm #6
Rick:

The exposure rating for PFG-03 (2000 microwatts/sec) is 25 times what it is for PFG-01 (80 microwatts/sec) at 633nm. You may be unexposing the hologram.

Your darkening of the edges may be what Colin suggests, fogging on the edges. My first processing of this emulsion caused me to get much greater density on the edges than in the middle also. When Tech Pan came out in the mid 80's, it was also a very fine grain emulsion and a special agitation technique was developed to solve the same problem. I applied a modified technique to 03 and it worked well. Place your plate or film in the tray, emulsion up of course, and agitate with a circular motion for 5 seconds. Let the plate/film rest without agitation for 30 seconds, then repeat the circular motion for 5 seconds. Clockwise or counterclockwise, doesn't matter. Continue to do this for 5 minutes (I always use 5 minutes in a developer to enhance contrast in the image instead of the 2 minutes a lot of people use.) If you're doing a 4x5, then you'll need to use an 8x10 tray to let you create the circular motion using your hand to hold the plate/film. This method gives me complete uniformity in the density across the plate/film. Hope this helps.

Steve
