tpark Tue Feb 22, 2005 8:16 pm #5
If you've purchased a module, I suggest looking for cracked solder connections on the board. It's quite possible you've broken your diode, however its still worth checking to see if something else is broken. Fortunately low power 650nm laser devices are pretty cheap - Intergraf sells modules, if you want one you know will work, or you can get a cheapo pointer off of ebay. One of the problems with the pointers is they usually don't have a spring loaded lens. You can still get 'em apart if you have a couple of pairs of vice grip pliers. I bought a 635nm pointer from Atlasnova, and it worked for making holograms - the 650nm pointers seem good (I put it on the interferometer) but I haven't made any holograms with that laser.
