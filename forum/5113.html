I had bought some precut matting for my holograms. Well, if you buy a 5 x 7 matt, the hole is 3 1/4 x 4 1/4. This took a lot away from the 5 x 7 hologram. I tried to cut the inside square hole bigger but it looked like it was hand cut.

Then I realized, buy the 5 x 7 matt and cut the ouside down to 4 x 5 which gets hidden by the frame. The hole is 3 1/4 x 4 1/4 which is perfect for a 4 x 5.

I have not bought or looked at the 8 x 10 matts to see what their hole size is, but if it is slightly smaller then 5 x 7 then great. I will let you know if I find out.
