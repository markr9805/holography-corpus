Does anyone have any wisdom aligning a JK Lasers / Lumonics system 2000 ruby laser? I'll be optimistic and ask anyway... (-:

I've been rebuilding one for the last entirely-too-many months and I'm finally getting down to the final little details. It has a 4 inch oscillator and 8 inch amplifier, for a total of 1J of output.

The resonant reflector output coupler and etalon are in temperature stabilized holders. They have only heating elements, so the set point must be above room temperature. What should it be? My current guess is a couple of degrees above room temperature, 80 or 85 degrees F (summer room temperature).

What should the capacitor voltage be? All the warning stickers say 2500V, and the safety interlock is around 3000V. Threshold is about 2100V, so thats were I've been testing.

The spatial filter is just not happy. Without it the beam through the amp is very clean, but when I put the spatial filter in (or even just the lens), the beam is dirty and (from the sound of the zapit paper) significantly weaker. I'm not confident that the lens and pinhole are in the right position. How far should the lens be from the amp, and how far should the pinhole be from the lens?

Any wisdom for tuning the etalons for single longitudinal mode? I don't have an analyzing etalon right now, the manual specifies a 1mm 65% reflective one.

I've been writing everything up as I go, but haven't gotten it up on the web yet. When its done, I'll put it all up, including schematics and firmware.
