OK, so it snowed like crazy here in Mass today, which provided a good excuse to stay home from work and finish up a few holographic projects!

Anyhow, I have to confess that I've had a relapse. I ran dry of truly original ideas for subjects to holograph, so I resorted back to making holograms of Disney stuff. These subjects were all purchased in Florida a few years back.

They are all BB-640, 35mW Hene, JD-4 developer, Fe EDTA bleach, H2 reflection copies of original transmission masters. H2's have 3% TEA pretreatment.

Enough talk - show the pictures!
