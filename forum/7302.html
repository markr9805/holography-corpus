Joe Farina Mon Apr 21, 2008 10:10 am #2
Congratulations, Cristiano. I don't remember anyone posting a picture of a SHG hologram on this forum.

To me, SHG (or SHSG) has been the most tantalizing holographic material. It might enable DCG-like brightness, at silver speeds (and no printout like silver), and full-color potential (and without the bandwidth-broadening corcerns that DCG has, which would help the color rendition, and also reduce blurring for deep images). I say "might" because I've never actually seen one that can rival the brightness of DCG, although there have been claims in the literature. I have a Russian SHSG hologram that is bright, but not comparable to DCG.

Also, Congratulations regarding the homemade silver material. Can you share a few details about the type of emulsion and processing you used? I've looked into SHSG pretty throroughly, and did a few on PFG-03, and would be happy to share any information that I have, if it would help.
