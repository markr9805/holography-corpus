Dinesh Sat Sep 04, 2004 2:25 pm #6
Thanks for the info. There seems to be some inconsistency between the two links. In the first link, it is stated that for a given bore, the power is proportional to the square of the current. hence log(P) = 2klog(i) and, indeed, figs 5 and 6 in the tutorial bear this out up to a vlaue of about 80A. I had to notice that both axes are logarithmic because I initially did not see a parabola. However, in the tutorial, the crossover from 488 being higher than 514 to being lower occurs at about 30A, while in the second link (drawn linearly so you see the parabolic relationship), the crossover occurs at 18A, or roughly half the value. I assume that this difference has to do with the bore diameter, since the power is proportional to the square of the current density and hence inversely proportional to the fourth power of the bore radius.
