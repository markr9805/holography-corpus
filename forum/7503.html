D.C. Flanders etal. teaches :

"the optimum polarizer structure requires periodicity << than the wavelength, thickness ~= wavelenth and precise control of the line-to-period ratio and thickness"

and describes experimental results of a (30 nm linewidth + 96 nm aluminum shadow) "400 nm high SiO2 structure. The period of the grating is 320 nm.... to give a line-to-period ratio of 0.3. The measured transmission and reflection for perpendicularly polarized light at 632.nm wavelenth was 57% and 6%, and, for parallel polarization, 1.2% was transmitted and 21% reflected."

From Appl. Phys. Lett. 42, 492, 1983 and other references.

My question to Holo Forum is :

Does this polarizer function over the range of visible light, much like an ordinary Polaroid type polarizer? Would a hologram stamped polarizer function with less effeciency than a low grade polarizer for applications like low cost lcd watches?

Does anyone sell a grating with a similar grating shape?

Can you suggest scientific articles and authors who have written similar articles about H.O.E. linear polarizers?

The polarizer you choose depends on the efficiency you need and the money you have. If you need the highest efficiency then look towards the Glan-Taylor polarizers. If cost is your concern the Polaroid are your choice. There are many options in between. What is you application?
