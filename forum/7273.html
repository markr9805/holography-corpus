Hi Everybody

I recently bought some photo gelatin(250 bloom) from CJ Chemical, and when I made up plates with it, they just didn't work! I used a 10% gelatin mix with 1.2g glycerin and 3ml 2% anline red solution. This is mold coated w/one ply clear tape. after drying I soak what I want to use in 4% potassium dichromate , rinse and dry again.

What happened was that I would either have an image out of the last ipa bath, but it would dissappear when it was dryed out and baked, or there would be no image at all,but a blank spot would appear in the middle of the plate, like it was too thin there.

I made two sets of plates totalling 20 or so plates,changing the tape thickness, but no soap

When I went back to plain old Knox from the corner store, everything went back to normal.

Any ideas?
Joe Farina Fri Apr 11, 2008 10:09 am #2
Gelatin can be tricky. Tony can confirm this. Generally, each batch of gelatin needs to be tested, regardless of what the manufacturer says regarding bloom strength or anything else. I also tried a photographic gelatin made by Baker (I think it's low bloom) but got better results with Knox gelatin, which is easily available at the grocery store. So far, I've bought several different packages of Knox, and each one had similar behavior, and I'm pretty impressed with it.

Anytime a new type of gelatin is received, I would assume it could be "anything" and wouldn't rely on the manufacturer's specifications. I think Tony initially tried a gelatin which was said to be of relatively high bloom, but which resulted in a bad case of the "milkies" which suggests low bloom. When he switched to Knox, the problem went away.
