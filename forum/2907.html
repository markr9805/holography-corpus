Hi Pascal!

> Pascal: I would like to buy Pyrogallol. However I am living in Europe (Belgium) and Photographers Formulary doesn't want to ship this chemical overseas for safety reasons.

That's rather curious because I just ordered developers from a

large US photo supply house. But air mail shipping and even surface

mail shipping by US postal service might be a problem because of new US Postal service rules for hazmat. Nearly everything except for

some OMD-R material (e.g. strong magnets) is forbidden in

the international mail since 4-5 years.

You could try a local pharmacy. In Belgium this should be no

problem.

> Do some of you know where I can buy this chemical?

Alternatively you could try it here:

[http://www.fotobrenner.de/](http://www.fotobrenner.de/)

But they have a 25 Euro minimum order. If you order only

pyrogallol you might have to pay an additional fee (as far as I know

Euro 5-8 ).

> In which purity should you buy this chemical for the pyro process. I' ve been seeing chemical websites for laboratories where they offer it in "99 % purity" or in "ACS reagent". Does somebody knows what ACS means and what and where I can order this?

You could try a local dealer for laboratory chemicals. ACS reagent grade

is rather pure pyrogallol used for analytical purposes and of course excellent for such a developer.

Very pure pyrogallol (e.g. purissimum p.a., ACS reagent) stored under nitrogen is white while common grades in pharmacies (e.g. "Caelo", Caesar&Loretz, Hilden) or the pyrogallol sold at [http://www.fotobrenner.de](http://www.fotobrenner.de) look like a slightly gray powder. In my opinion the common photographic

or pharmacy grades should be sufficient.
