Boy did a make a big boo boo tonight. I wanted to add some extra hardener to my fixer and found a bottle of Kodak rapid fixer part 2 that was sulfuric acid. Well without thinking I poured a little in my Kodak fixer with hardener already added (not the two part type) fixer. It turned milky white and I had to evacuate the premise and air out the place. It was very bad. Then I looked on the fixer with "hardener already in it" bottle, it did not use sulfuric acid for hardener. Very bad!
