Gary Deal Mon Feb 26, 2007 7:10 am #4
I took a piece of 1/4" (~6mm) clear plastic and engraved reversed lettering into it, leaving it a little rough so it would reflect. I made a Denisyuk of it from the back, with the film reversed. After processing and viewed from the "wrong" side, the lettering appeared right-reading and 1/4" in front of the film. I used a black background, so the lettering stood alone.

Roughly scratched artwork and possibly multiple layers of clear plastic w/ such scratches could make for interesting work.
