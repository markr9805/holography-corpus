Din Mon Sep 09, 2024 1:04 pm #2
Try Norland 91 ( [https://www.norlandprod.com/adhesives/uvs%2091.html](https://www.norlandprod.com/adhesives/uvs%2091.html) ). It's a fairly good UV absorber in the 350 - 400nm range, known as UV-A by the NIH. This is the range of most of the solar spectrum in the UV.

When I worked at Physical Optics (POC) back in the mid-80's we used Norland 91 to seal DCG holograms for technical holograms, so it's got good optical qualities. It isn't completely absorptive, because, when I was at Triple Take Holographics, we tried sealing silver halide holograms with Norland and it didn't work; the silver emulsion went black (print out). However, as I mentioned, it didn't affect DCG holograms. One reason was probably that the threshold sensitivity of DCG is much higher than that of silver halide. Since the threshold sensitivity of Bayer polymer is of the same order of DCG, it may protect your polymer.
