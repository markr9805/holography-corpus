While I don't know if any researchers are doing it now, I think it's only a matter of time and computer resources before it happens.

Several researchers in the computer graphics field have developed methods to generate full 3D computer models based off multiple shots of a city and from that kind of data and algorithms we aren't far from creating a hologram. The computer power needed to create the hologram is pretty significant but is well within the possible.

Tom B. Wed Apr 25, 2007 5:17 am #8
Maybe you are thinking of a holographic autostereogram? Essentially you can make a very simple multiplex hologram that combines left and right images of a stereogram so that it can be viewed without special glasses and with left and right eyes seeing the proper views - but only when viewed directly facing the hologram. Saxby in "Practical Holography" 3rd ed. has a short description of the method.
