fkienast Sun Aug 07, 2005 4:26 pm #1
I tried making a transmission hologram of some white dice, thinking they would be a good demonstration of a 3-dimensional object. I used a simple single-beam setup, where a mirror reflects the center of the spread beam onto the dice and the plate facing the dice is struck by the side of the beam.

After I developed and dried the hologram, I used a green laser pointer (with spreading lens) to look for the dice. Instead of dice, I was surprised to see several green dots. As I looked more closely I realized I was seeing the black dots on the dice! I took a closer look at the dice and discovered that although they are white, the "white" is actually a bright gray. The dots are painted with black paint, but this paint is reflective despite being black. Thus more light was reflected from the black dots than from the "white" surface, making the dots the only part of the dice visible in the hologram.
