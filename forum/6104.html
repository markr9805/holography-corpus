Did the emulsion peel off in shreds or did it just dissolve in the water? I had PFG03 emulsion dissolve once due to accidentally rinsing in excessively warm water (I decided to rinse with cold water afterwards), but your bath temp should be OK.

One thing to question is the use of the JD-4 bleach with CWC2 (catechol?) developer. Usually that bleach only works well with the metol-ascorbic acid developer supplied with JD-4 and a dichromate bleach is recommended for CWC2..

Martin Thu Mar 08, 2007 10:54 am #4
> holography4fun: Anybody tried PBQ1 or PBQ2 bleaches? Which Develper should be used with it?

I think the “PBQ1” or “PBQ2” bleaches ought to be replaced by the much safer PBU bleaches. If you use the “PBU-quinol” type, you'll actually end up with exactly the same bleaching agent, quinone (=PBQ) – without the risk of handling PBQ. Furthermore, the keeping properties of the traditional PBQ bleaches was limited to a few minutes only, whereas the PBU-quinol has a shelf live of several months.

> Tom B: One thing to question is the use of the JD-4 bleach with CWC2 (catechol?) developer. Usually that bleach only works well with the metol-ascorbic acid developer supplied with JD-4 and a dichromate bleach is recommended for CWC2..

I think the issue is using a reversal bleach with PFG-03 emulsions. Put aside special cases like SHSG processing, we were never able to achieve good results with reversal bleaches on PFG-03. It may be that the reversal bleach actually dissolves a large part of the undeveloped grains. Moreover silver concentration in PFG-03 is far weaker than say, in PFG-01.
