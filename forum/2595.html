As for the difference between reflection and transmission, it's basically the way you view it. A reflection is viewed is reflected light. The light replaying the hologram hits the front of the hologram, as you see it, usually from above and at an angle of about 60 degrees. This kind of hologram is made with the object on one side of the plate and the reference beam coming in from the other side. A transmission hologram is seen by transmitted light. The light for the hologram comes from behind the hologram as you see it, usually from above and again about 60 degrees. To make this kind of hologram you have to have the object and reference both hit the plate from the same side. Reflections usually replay in a single color. Transmissions are either of the 'rainbow' variety, in which case they replay in bands of color that vary as you move your eyes up and down the plate, or 'achromat', in which case the image is all white.
