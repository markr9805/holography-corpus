All,

We are a small manufacturing company that builds educational lab equipment and kits that help teach principles of laser and fiber optic technology. In some of our laser related educational kits we include a small transmission hologram. Nothing fancy or of artisan quality, just an example of what a hologram is for them to view and gain an appreciation of holography. Lots of "WOW factor".

We are looking for someone who can supply us with these holograms. Maybe there is a hobbyist out there who would like to help us out and make a little money. We are looking for 200 pieces of a hologram that is;

Transmission type

Shot on PFG-01 film (the plastic film, not glass plate)

Shot with red 633 nm wavelength laser

Approximately 65 mm square in size (+- 5 mm)

The holograms do not need to be identical in terms of point of view. This means you do not need to create a master, and you can shoot large panels and cut them into 65 mm squares to save time. Whatever works best for you, as long as all of the images are bright, clear, and reasonably centered in each hologram.

Please contact me if you are interested in quoting this job. You can reach me at [mhackett@i-fiberoptics.com](mailto:mhackett@i-fiberoptics.com)

Best regards,

Mike Hackett

Technician

Industrial Fiber Optics

We just brought their Sandbox Holography Kit back to life, (our new and improved version) and I got to write the manual for it. I didn't know anything about holography going in, and this forum was one of my favorite research sources for the manual. Thanks a million guys!! (and gals!)
