I hope everyone's having a great and creative new year.

Since I'm usually busy trying to reconstruct 3-D images out of bits and pixels instead of chemicals and film, I don't have much to add here. However, I thought you might be interested in the following two conferences which are this week and next:

Symposium: Photonics West 2007 (San Jose, CA)

Conference: Optoelectronics 2007 / Practical Holography XXI: Materials and Applications

[See conference program here.](http://spie.org/Conferences/Programs/07/pw/opto/index.cfm?fuseaction=6488)

Symposium: Electronic Imaging (SPIE/IS&T) (San Jose, CA)

Conference: Stereoscopic Displays and Applications XVIII

[See program here.](http://www.stereoscopic.org/2007/program.html)

I'll be at the Stereo Displays one, as usual, and hope to meet some of you there.
