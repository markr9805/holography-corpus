JohnFP Wed Jul 05, 2006 3:25 pm #9
Dave, I am by no means a great photographer of holograms but I do use the technique Michael described with very good results. I take a piece of white card, sometimes a flat white card sometimes a gloss coated card and place it next to the hologram to be photographed. I then focus the camera on the card and while holding the shutter button half down to lock in the exposure time and distance, move the camera to point at the hologram then continue to push the shutter button down to take the photograph. I found with this method I can aim fully at the white card or some percentage (half way on the white card and half way off the white card etc) to alter the image intesity.
