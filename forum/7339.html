BobH Sun May 18, 2008 1:49 am #2
Excellent progress, Tony! For a squeege, forget about photographic versions as they are waaay too hard. Go with the holographer's choice ... Anco windshield wiper blades. I recommend the largest type, cut to a convenient length and glued to a metal bar to keep it straight and get a handle on it.
