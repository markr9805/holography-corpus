The "standard model" of holography is Kogelnik's Coupled Wave theory ( [http://adsabs.harvard.edu/cgi-bin/nph-b ... ..48.2909K](http://adsabs.harvard.edu/cgi-bin/nph-bib_query?1969BSTJ...48.2909K) ). He derives a pair of coupled second order partial differential equations and then looks at the solutions to derive the holographic parameters for all the types of holograms. The theory is restricyted to uniform gratings in two dimensions.

Hariharan presents a pretty good mathematical treatment ( [http://www.allbookstores.com/browse/Aut ... haran,%20P](http://www.allbookstores.com/browse/Author/Hariharan,%20P). ).

There's also Benton's course notes which are a pretty good mathematical description on holography ( [http://courses.media.mit.edu/2002spring ... index.html](http://courses.media.mit.edu/2002spring/mas450/reading/chaptersPDF/index.html) )
