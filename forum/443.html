Dinesh Sun Jan 08, 2012 3:43 pm #2
Agree! I tried to get involved with a conversation concerning the misuse of the word "holography" by the public. I soon discovered that most of the people in that group didn't know a lot of holography and simply understood "holography" from a vague, public/tech perspective (tech magazines, textbooks etc) without actually being aware of all the different forms of holography.These different forms, such as Light-in-Flight or shearography, aren't usually mentioned in brief articles and short passages in tech manuals. I doubt very much that most of them had actually made a hologram themselves. Also, the tone got nasty over a simple difference of opinion because they were so fixated on their specific meaning of holography, they wouldn't look beyond the end of their own noses! It pretends to be about professional holographers, so I assumed that they were professional holographers, but I doubt that 99% of them have actually worked in, or even seen, the inside of a holography lab.
