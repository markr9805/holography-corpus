"The gelatin swells by absorbing water, and the water carries dissolved dichromate into the gelatin. The water to dichromate ratio is nearly equal inside and outside the gelatin, so the amount of dichromate within the gelatin layer is dependent both on the concentration of the solution and the amount of swelling of the gelatin. When the sensitized layer is dried, the dichromate remains in the gelatin while the gelatin shrinks. The dichromate concentration in the dried layer can get quite high: up to 25% by weight. The volume of the incorporated dichromate adds significantly to the thickness of the gelatin layer and consequently strongly affects the ultimate color of the hologram."

this is one step that is totally different than the way we now coat .It could make the difference...............
