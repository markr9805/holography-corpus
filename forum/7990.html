Hi,

i made a transmission master. The problem is, that I hadn't recognized the wrong polarisation. So the holo is way good, but I had some Backreflections from the Glassplate which generated a woodgrain in the master. If I copy this to a whit light reflection hologram, would I see the woodgrain ?
BobH Sun Sep 20, 2009 3:16 pm #2
Yes. It will be most visible when the distance between the master and transfer is small. Whether it is objectionable or not depends on how fine the pattern is, and your own judgement. You can minimize it by using white light to replay the transfer (instead of laser light or LEDs).
