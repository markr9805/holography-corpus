This bears on a subject that Andres and I talk about somewhat regularly.

What environments do the rest of the forum work in when processing their holograms?

I've pretty much got a room (my office) dedicated to the shooting and partial processing of my holograms but have to take them out to an outside bathroom for final sqeegeeing, drying and backing. I keep a hepa fan running most of the time in my office and keep the bathroom as clean (although not "neat" as wifey will surely attest) as possible.

One nice thing about using the JD2 formula is that if you splash any on the counter when mixing or pouring the stock solution, you'll have black dots on your counter the next day. A sure sign that you need to be more careful. (I've got one of those staring at me now)
