Colin Kaminski Fri Dec 23, 2005 3:54 am #3
Andy,

If you want, try and make a hinge by using electrical tape on the botttom edge of the hologram and a glass cover piece. Place the epoxy in a line on the hologram at the hinge and slowly fold the cover plate closed. If the line of glue has no air bubbles left in from the mixing then you will get the cover plate glued on with no air bubbles.

A better method is to scrape 1/8" of gelatin off each first.

Also chosing a lower viscosity epoxy might help as well. Hysol has one I like but I can't find the number. It is not a 1 to 1 mix. It is more like 10 to one. The hardner is red and I have some in unmarked containers that I have been using for 10 or so years. But, lowering the viscosity makes it much easier.

I found that the two part epoxy I use always gets tiny bubbles in it when mixing it. Lots and lots of tiny bubbles.
Colin Kaminski Fri Dec 23, 2005 3:52 pm #5
You can avoid this. Also, if you use a slow set epoxy you can heat it and lower the viscosity and the bubbles will rise to the surface where you can use a pin to pop them. Don't try this with 5 minute epoxy as it will kick off and harden.
