Michael's thread on liquid cooling reminded me of this tip:

Foam core is a great material for building air flow shielding boxes, and as an added benefit it is thermally insulating too. You can even get it in black! Michael's (the craft store) has it in 24x36" sheets, 1/4 and 1/2 inch thick.
JohnFP Thu Jan 04, 2007 2:42 pm #2
Yes Tony, I use it all the time for blockers. Just attack a base to it, as depicted and it can be moved to where it is needed very easily. It also cuts very easily with a razor blade.

BobH Thu Jan 04, 2007 5:10 pm #3
I find foamcore good for many applications, but for ballfing a table I recommend triple thickness black matte board from framing supply stores instead. Both materials take on a curl, but with matte board can be curled the opposite direction to straighten it out. Foamcore can not.

Also, I use black photographer's tape exclusively on the table. It's very durable for making hinges with the baffle boards, removes completely when necessary, and is great for small masking.
