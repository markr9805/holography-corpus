April 23, 2006 -- HoloTalk Update

HOLOSCAPES: Laser & Holography Videocasting

If you enjoy the HoloTalk radio show, the new HoloScapes videocast show will raise the bar once again with Internet laser and holography programming.

HoloScapes will premiere on Saturday, July 1st, 2006.

HoloScapes will not replace the HoloTalk radio show, but will supplement it with additional programming options.

If you would like to view the 1-minute video trailer, please go to:

[http://www.holoworld.com/holotalk](http://www.holoworld.com/holotalk)

You will need the free RealPlayer to view the videocast. If you do not have the player, a link is provided.

HoloScapes programming will include in-studio tips & tricks, holography demonstrations, do-it-yourself segments, educational programming, and videos provided from others. Stay tuned to future HoloTalk shows for more details.

Feedback, ideas and suggestions for additional programming is welcomed. If you would like your programming broadcast via HoloScapes, reaching the entire world-wide HoloTalk email list, please contact me at director at holoworld dot com for technical specifications.

Regards,

Frank DeFreitas
