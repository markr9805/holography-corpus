Hello,

Yes it is essential to use long coherence length lasers for holography. Without special design considerations diode pumped solid state lasers can have very short coherence lengths....millimeters. For green try Coherent 215M or 315M. For blue some Coherent Sapphire OPSL are single frequench (SLM). The newer SLM versions are expensive and never yet seen used so thank $12,000 or more depending on power.

The CNI SLM lasers may be OK but I am skeptical they will continue to be SLM and hold power for a long term. Even their regular light show lasers don't last that long really so I can't recommend them. If anyone has had good results with one for more than a year please post.
