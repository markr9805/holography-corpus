Tom B. Sun Jul 18, 2010 10:17 am #6
In layman's terms, it's using primitive equipment and ironic temperament to make art that I like, sort of a punk do-it-yourself aesthetic. Some examples in my gallery: [http://members.shaw.ca/holopix/index.html](http://members.shaw.ca/holopix/index.html)

The "leftfield downtempo" addition was likely due to recent relistening to Pimp Daddy Nash's acid jazz magnum opus:

[http://www.amazon.com/Private-Leftfield ... B000S9ATSG](http://www.amazon.com/Private-Leftfield-Downtempo-Fuzz/dp/B000S9ATSG)

"Private Leftfield Downtempo Fuzz is a moody, noir-inspired collection of downtempo dance tracks in the tradition of DJ Shadow and Howie B.; although Pimp Daddy Nash is not an innovator of the same caliber, his debut is nevertheless a fresh and inspired work, a smooth assimilation of trip-hop, acid jazz and classic lounge sounds. ~ Jason Ankeny, All Music Guide "

In the cultural field, I think innovation is vastly overrated - execution is everything. Except in the case of Lady Gaga who combines brilliant execution with insipid songwriting.
