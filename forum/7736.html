I encourage any holographer looking to experiment w/ DLP, or who is looking for large FS mirrors, to get one of these tv's.

geso- If the TV has a color wheel, then there will be some form of feedback to sync up with the output buffers. I did this with a Proxima DLP projector. The unit wouldn't even try to put out an image (flashed error codes on the LED front panel display) without knowing the position of the color wheel. This is needed to output the correct R/G/B frame. Actually my color wheel was R/G/R/B. To get around this sync issue I probed what I could tell was the optical feedback sensor with a scope and noted the waveform, which happened to just be a short duty-cycle square wave. Then I used a 555 timer circuit to reproduce that waveform (close enough.) Again, since this signal is syncing the output buffers you don't need super tight accuracy. I used a pot to set the 555's freq. and just cranked it up until the error codes went away.

One more problem was that the HV supply for the lamp, which also had feedback to the controller. This feedback was via an opto-isolator which I cut out and just strapped the input to the controller to +5 and to GND to see what level meant "HV OK" I'd suspect there may also be other interlock/feedback schemes used. Try to get a schematic for.
