Diode Holographer Tue Sep 02, 2008 12:09 am #3
Actually, I'm afraid that at least some of the items you mention and claim to be "NOT-Hologram" indeed ARE holograms.

Not all holograms are 3 dimensional pictures. Many times you see an item that is "prismatic" it is an embossed hologram of a HOE (Holographic Optical Element) of a Diffraction Grating. For example, the prismatic security stickers you find on CD and DVD cases are in fact holograms.

I can understand the reason for your quest. It can be quite hard to find exactly what you're looking for on places like ebay. However, don't think you really have a legitamate complaint with many of these items.
