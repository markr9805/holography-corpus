Hans Wed Feb 27, 2008 3:32 pm #7
The distance between the fringe planes in holograms (both reflection and transmission) is: d=lcos(f/2)/(sin(f)=0.5l/sin(f/2) where l=wavelength and f=angle between object and reference beam.

The distance between the fringes on the surface of the hologram (that is where the fringe planes intersect the surface of the emulsion) is what you see in the holowiki.

The bragg condition for reconstruction is: l/2=dsin(f/2)

The angle of the fringe planes intersects the angles of the object and reference beam. So, one could say: fPlane = (fReference+fObject)/2.

All angles measured with respect to the normal of the emulsion.

In these formulas there is no account taken for index of refraction of the emulsion.
