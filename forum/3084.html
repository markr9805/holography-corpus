There are many, many SLMs out there; the simplest might just be an off-the-shelf digital projector. One challenge in using it, though, is that ordinary projectors use PWM to create shades of color, and that will add a time-domain aspect to your experiment you'll need to be aware of.

If you want to go to the component level, look at [http://www.dlp.com](http://www.dlp.com) for TI's parts, or places like Displaytech or various LCoS manufacturers.

My guess though is that determining the right holographic storage material will be a hearty challenge. It's lithium niobate or something, right?
