Joe Farina Sun Sep 02, 2007 10:56 am #8
Let me add a further note of caution. Wait until you hear from Integraf, or someone you know, who has used this system for holography.

I think it's very unlikely that it has adequate coherence. (A forum member recently sent me a photo of a DCG hologram he made with a 473nm solid-state laser, which was banded.)

Back in the late 90's, Laser Power introduced that dual-beam blue dpss laser with 5-meter coherence (which later became the Melles 58-BLD-605) and we know the incredible lengths they had to go to, to get that kind of coherence (separating the forward and backward propagating beams, for instance). When I inquired about the price in 1999, they said it was $21,000 for the short coherence-length version, but that the long-coherence length version was an extra $6,000, or $27,000. "Blue noise" is even more notorious than green noise.

Has anyone on this forum used a blue dpss laser (other than the one above) with more than an inch of coherence?
