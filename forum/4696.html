Here's my technique for painting the emulsion side of H2 reflection copies:

I tape the plate emulsion side up to a piece of white typing paper. The goal is to mask the plate so no overspray gets on the front side. I run a piece of masking tape along each edge, with 1/32" overlap. Then I run my fingernail along the edge of the plate, forcing the masking tape to pull back from the plate but adhere to the edge of the glass. This way I can get total coverage of the emulsion side.

I use flat black paint. I know Saxby recommends glossy, and MichaelH likes to use Oracal. But that's another thread...
