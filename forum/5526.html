John Klayer Thu May 04, 2006 3:40 am #1
I've been running my 315 with just the included heat sink fins - no fan or water. So far I've been shutting it off when the case temp gets to 90 F as measured with a non-contact thermometer. What is the safe temp limit?
DJ Mathson Thu May 04, 2006 10:36 am #2
In the specifications table in the 315M manual it says:

Laser head baseplate maximum temperature for conductive cooling: 55 deg C (130 deg F).
Joe Farina Thu May 04, 2006 10:39 am #3
I took a quick look at the operator's manual, and didn't see any reference to a safe temperature limit, though I could have missed it.

My own rule of thumb would be that if either the head or controller get "rather hot" to the touch, then I would improve the heat sinking.

Probably the best person to ask is Sam on alt.lasers. He has very extensive experience with the C315M.
Joe Farina Thu May 04, 2006 10:46 am #4
Yep, DJ Mathson found it. It's in section 2, page 5 of the manual.
JohnFP Thu May 04, 2006 10:49 am #5
I remember from some theads long ago on this subjec that you may get mode hoping and other undesireables at the recommended max temperature and for holography additional air or water cooling through the fins makes the laser much more stable. I think it was Danny that provided good information on this.
