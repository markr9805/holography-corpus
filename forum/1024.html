lobaz Tue Feb 24, 2015 5:22 pm #10
I had some free time to make experiments with LitiHolo film.

I set a simple single beam reflection hologram setup. I used a 50 mW 532 nm DPSS laser (thanks, Phil!), a spatial filter with 10x objective and 25 um pinhole, the plate is about 80 cm from the pinhole. I measured about 0.8 mW/cm2 (but my meter is not perfectly calibrated).

I got nice holograms of a white coral (scene depth about 3 cm) for exposures 3 minutes, 1 minute and even 20 seconds. I got no hologram for 5 seconds exposure, but maybe there was some problem with this try.

So now it seems to me that proper exposure for 532 nm should be about 16000/X seconds, where X is the measured power in uW per cm2.

I hope it helps.

Petr
