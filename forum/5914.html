Colin Kaminski Mon Dec 11, 2006 4:26 pm #2
Here is what I do, I calculate the exposure based on the film manufacture's exposure recomendations. I then expose a plate to 1/2 the energy, the proper energy, double the energy and then 4 times the energy. You can do this all on one plate if you wish. Then I drop it in the developer for 1 minute and process. The brightest one become the standard that I then make finer adjustments to.

It should be noted that lately I have been over exposing and only using 30 seconds in the developer based on some posts be Jeff. I have found this to work well also.
