I was reading the Russian Holography section, well actually not reading it but like a child skimming over it for links and pictures and I found a link, the most recent, to the well known scratch holography.

I can't help it but everytime I see those I ponder how much cleaner they would look if they were done with a laser. That is, make some DCG, put it on an X-Y table and use a program that moves the table in the arc fashion typically used in making the scratch hologram. Then process as a standard DCG hologram to start with, but modify to get ideal removal of the unexposed area until the desired effect is achieved.

There would be no reference beam and no interference pattern, Just a bunch of arc's burned into the DCG. I assume for greatest resolution the beam should be fed through a microscope objective to narrow it's diameter, which would also increase the write speed and allow the lines to be closer together. Put the lines close enough and you may even see some color.
