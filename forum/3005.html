I've made some transmission masters from PFG-03M, but I'm wondering if it's worth getting a box of PFG-01 and the appropriate chemicals to make transmission masters. Is there any advantage to PFG-01? I would like to try some larger sizes, and it seems that film would be fine to try initially for experimental purposes.

This isn't so much of a holography question, but do you have some favorite material for making models? Dinesh Tue Jan 04, 2005 10:24 pm #2
I've never used 03M, but I have used 03C. My main problem with 03C was the fact that it goes instantly dark when dropped into the developer. You don't get that exposure/density feedback to judge exposures. I've used 01 wiith a HeNe and found good results with approx 150 mJ/sq cm.

For modelling we sometimes use Sculpey ( [http://www.sculpey.com/](http://www.sculpey.com/) )
Colin Kaminski Wed Jan 05, 2005 3:11 am #3
The gelatin on pfg-01 is harder and therefore easier to work with.
Martin Wed Jan 05, 2005 9:33 am #4
> I've made some transmission masters from PFG-03M, but I'm wondering if it's worth getting a box of PFG-01 and the appropriate chemicals to make transmission masters. Is there any advantage to PFG-01?

Speed, as already mentioned. If you don't bleach the master, you won't see a difference in quality compared to PFG-03. In addition you might even use standard paper developers. In my opinion it's not worth to hunt for special developers based on pyrogallol or pyrocatechin (CW-C2 alias JD-2 etc.) in this case.
Martin Wed Jan 05, 2005 9:35 am #5
> I've never used 03M, but I have used 03C. My main problem with 03C was the fact that it goes instantly dark when dropped into the developer.

I believe the instant-blackening-effect is due to high pH of the developer. You might try to adapt your developer by say, adding 5g/L boric acid. At least that's what we did when using Agfa's standard paper developer, Neutol, on PFG-03M.
