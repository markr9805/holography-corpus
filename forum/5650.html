favalora Sun Jun 25, 2006 4:11 pm #2
Colin -

I'm a member of the OSA and the SPIE.

I subscribe to the OSA's Optics Lettersand Applied Optics. These cover the range of what I'm interested in. Optics Letters is supposed to cover "science" (fundamental stuff) and Applied Optics is supposed to cover "applied science" (engineering). In reality, you can find applied science in both, which is fortunate for me. You should go online and look at the last few months' table of contents. As I said, you'll see that Applied Optics covers a very broad array of optical instruments, diffractive systems, etc., but similar topics - if of hopefully "extraordinary" merit - find their way into Optics Letters.

I very much enjoy the ability to have full-text access to historic articles of Optics Letters online.

Remember the SPIE, too. I susbscribe to Optical Engineering, edited by O'Shea. [Link to OE](http://www.spie.org/app/Publications/index.cfm?fuseaction=journals&type=oe)

In addition, with respect to holography, see their annual conference called "Practical Holography." It is full of hands-on applicable research:

[link to 2006 conference program](http://spie.org/Conferences/Programs/06/pw/opto/index.cfm?fuseaction=6136)

I recommend you purchase the annual proceedings.

Also, the SPIE has a holography society, which publishes a newsletter.
