On the Ralcon website, I read about their troubles with the local zoning board. Apparently this stopped most of the DCG activity in Paradise, Utah.

[http://www.xmission.com/~ralcon/aboutus.html](http://www.xmission.com/~ralcon/aboutus.html)

I once took a tutorial on DCG given by Richard Rallison in Lake Forest. Although it only lasted for a day, I learned a lot about DCG. So much in fact that I would like to spend several more days doing the same. He seems to enjoy teaching, and accepts private students.

Maybe if others are interested, this could be done as a group to reduce costs. There's no doubt in my mind that this is the most effective way to learn about DCG. (I think DCG will be given a boost in the near future due to Jeff and John's recent work, so maybe now would be a good time to think about it.)
