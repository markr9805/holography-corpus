Ed Wesly Mon Jan 09, 2006 5:11 pm #2
Polyester (Kodak trade name Estar) is the one to stay away from. Who knows if anyone is still making this stuff on acetate.

I'm guessing that OHP stands for OverHead Projector? I encountered a problem using some of this type of stock when sandwiching the sheet on top of the holographic film during exposure. The coatings they put on it diffused the reference beam so the holograms were dim and fuzzy. Look through the stuff at a spatial filter to see if blurs the spot.
