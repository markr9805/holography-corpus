MichaelH Sun Jun 04, 2006 3:47 pm #2
Have you looked at it under laser light and through a polarizer?

The red and orange areas will reflect almost as much light as the whiter areas.

To get the proper shading differences between the veins and areas of the muscle and fat you may need to paint the model in varying shades of white and gray.

I'd highly recommend doing some test shots with film to make sure the final result will be what you're after before you shoot the more expensive glass.
