Hi,

doing holographic data storage we (two mates and myself) are considering angular multiplexing. in the literature it says there is quite a bit of cross talk going on and it's always mentioned in context with three-dimensional storage mediums.

Thus my question: is it possible, no doable to use angular multiplexing in holography? Our idea was to use films and put them at different angles to the film, and thus (theoretically) create a virtual three-dimensional object, which we would then be able to read out with a camera or something at the different angles.

What do you reckon?

Cheers,

Dom
Stas Thu Jul 05, 2007 8:51 am #2
Actually it is exactly what we are doing with our 4D imaging ([http://www.geola.lt](http://www.geola.lt)) for example once I've took from the internet tomography scans of lungs and imprinted them on our digital holographic print. Viewed from different angles, the different picture of the lung slice are seen.
