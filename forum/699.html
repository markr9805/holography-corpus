Hi, Joe.

If it is 'woods grain' effect, I think it should show variations in direction as emulsion thickness change. Or your emulsion is perfect, or something else is in question.

If it is recorded at the Brewster's angle, then there should be no such grain. Note that such angle is slightly different for red and green laser. Do you record your hologram at Brewster angle at all?

To inspect whether bad polarization is an issue, use blank glass plate, and put in your plate holder. If there is any reflected light, then it is not set at Brewster's angle, or polarization is not correct (P-polarization). For two wavelengths (red and green), you may chose slight change in angle so that both lasers has the least reflection from both surfaces.

If multimode or mode hopping is present in green laser, then there should be difference on the object; red one without banding, green one banded. I am not sure in difference between multimode and mode hopping, because my blue laser has both, so I really don't know what to blame for that if I get banded hologram. But, as you said, I saw banding only on the object, not on the emulsion.
