Tom B. Tue May 31, 2005 3:53 am #5
> dave battin: Tony ,do you have to remove it befor it goes into the developer ?

Almost certainly yes - use Kleenex dampened with isopropanol to wipe it off, otherwise little black flakes will float off in the developer and deposit on your plate. I have not yet found a marker that resists alkaline developer - even Kodak negative grease pencils wash off!

Another good black marker available at art stores is Sakura's "Permapaque" opaque pigment marker. It's water based, but somewhat water resistant when dry. Very black on glass!

The best of the non-pigment markers (my previous favorite) is Staedtler's Lumocolor 357 permanent. Not quite so black, but it coats glass a little better and is less likely to rub off with handling.
JohnFP Tue May 31, 2005 2:41 pm #6
I take a piece of black electrical tape and tape it down on a piece of glass. I then take a plate the same size I am shooting and put the edge of it on the tape. I then cut the tape with a razor blade such that it is the same size at the edge of the plate. Then peel it off and put it on the plate to be shot. I found I can use the same piece of tape a few times before the stickyness wears out.
