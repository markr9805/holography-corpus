meekan01 Thu May 26, 2005 12:24 am #15
4 liters for $25, lab grade absolute ethanol (<0.5% water) from vwr.com. Contains 5% methanol.

In case anyone is entertained by knowing such things, the postdoc in my lab told me how ethanol is made more than 95% pure. Basically, when you're trying to distill normally, the ethanol binds weakly to water in that 19-1 ratio and carries it along the distallation tube. In order to get it more pure, they used to put in Benzene, which attracts the water more, and then pull off the benzene with the remaining water. It's pretty cool.

I'm pretty sure that they don't do it with Benzene anymore, but I'm pretty sure they still use the same basic kind of idea- you mix in another chemical that has a greater tendancy to stick to the water =)
