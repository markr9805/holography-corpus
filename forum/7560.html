DJ Mathson Fri Oct 10, 2008 10:27 pm #11
I have one of those lasers. I bought it a few years ago because PowerTechnology first said it would work for holography albeit not optimal. Later they said instead that it isn´t suitable after all. The TEC is supposed to control the temp to 0.1 degree. They said that I shouldn´t expect a coherence length longer than 3 mm, and that the IQ1C model is a better choise since it has a better TEC.

I believe MichaelH tested the PPM several times and never got a hologram from it. Therefore I have never tested this laser. Now I need to try out mine also. I believe that if you have a stable environment and find a mode hop free region you can get a long coherence length. But according to the above, I wouldn´t trust this laser for continuous work.

hi guys, the laser Danny is talking about , is a coherent radius laser, its putting out 25mw 0f 635nm. I’ve done a Michelson interferometer test and come up with a total coherent length of @12 inches. im seeing nice solid interference bands (even with laser mounted on table w/tec fan on)..

while the spatial frequency is Tem00, its not as strong as a hene laser. i will try to shoot a grating this weekend and i'll give you an update then.
