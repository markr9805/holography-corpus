In an attempt to reduce the streaks & fog, I tried to rinse the plate in running water just after bleaching. To my horror, as I did this I could see the pale blue gel being washed down the sink. – Damm.

I then decided to use my last plate on the same scene (which I had taken some time to create, see below). By this time I was a little frustrated and did not wait long enough for the distilled water to cool properly, in fact it was quite hot. I think I knew something was wrong when the plate was in the water, just after developer stage. The water was changing to a black colour – something I had not seen before. By the time I was doing the final rinse (not in running water this time) I was pretty sure the gel had washed off. – Looking at both dried plates this morning – no image. Looks like I’ll have to buy some more stuff.

Intended scene for Transmission Holo:

![Image](http://www.geocities.com/rja4096/Winter.jpg)

- Think I need to not use running water, and only at room temp. – Any thoughts would be appreciated,
