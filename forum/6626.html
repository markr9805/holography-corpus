> MichaelH: There have been a number of discussions related toMBDCGand the PH of the final emulsion which leads me to wonder if the classic or G307 formulations are potentially sensitive to PH of the solutions. Anyone know?

The following applies to aqueous dichromate solutions only – no colloids involved (!):

“During irradiation, the pH value of the solution changes towards alkalinity; the rate of change becomes lower with higher pH value of the solution. Hence, it can be said that the photochemical reaction depends on the pH value of the solution. From this standpoint, the photoreduction rates were measured at various pHs.

(...) At pH 3.5 the rate of photoreduction of Cr(VI) is remarkably higher than those at other pH values. The rate at pH 6.3 is smaller than that at pH 3.5. No reduction was observed at pH 8.3 (...)”.

(Sasaki et al. Studies on photosensitive dichromated materials, Tokyo 1978)
