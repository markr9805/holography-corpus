Ron Michael Wed Aug 09, 2006 11:40 am #1
I thought I would open this topic for those who would like to comment on their thoughts or experience on the subject. I agree with others in this forum that one wavelength is okay but two or more would be better!

Also Inaki's introduction and work over on the general forum inspired me to open this up for the issues related also to pulse work.

Years ago I remember reading this article on a RGB system.[http://www.spie.org/web/techgroups/holo ... hy1-11.pdf](http://www.spie.org/web/techgroups/holography/pdfs/Holography1-11.pdf) But of course, it would be expensive to build and I wonder of the requirement for each color to be coherent to each other, since their has been work done on RG and RGB combined lasers setups.

With Pan film like Fuji, it would be fun to try.

For me I thought about building a timing circuit to fire a q-switched ruby and q-switched Nd:yag and beam combine them. The circuit would time both pulses as close as I can but since both are passive q switches, it couldn't get that close, I'd have to add additional active q switches to time time them closer if it was needed.

Besides laser issues, I wonder how cross-talk problems were handled?

My current understanding is that in RG there could be up to four primary images and 4 conjugate images produced. An H1/H2 technique would also require a RG transfer which could be handled by the same laser source. I'm assuming that instead of H1/H2 that shooting a reflection hologram with it's smaller bandwidth for each of the colors might help to reduce the problem. Any thoughts on this also?
