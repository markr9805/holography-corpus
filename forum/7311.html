My 9 metre hologram, I Dreamt We Had Soemthing To Say, along with a couple of metre square stereograms made at Holographics North and some pulse reflections made in Russia at holography.ru will go on display at the Canberra Museum and Gallery, Australia for four month from July to October as part of the inaugural National Photography Festival in Canberra. When they're up and running I'll post images on my website.
