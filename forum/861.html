Dinesh Mon May 19, 2014 10:27 am #2
You're welcome Bob. Thank you all who showed up. I think we all enjoyed the weekend. In addition to shooting holos, there was a lot of holo-talk arising spontaneously all over the lab, which for me was really the best part of it. The conversations that were going on were really quite full of information and I think we were all a little more educated as a result. Meeting face-to-face also created a more intimate atmosphere, where we could put faces to names, and bring up topics of conversations on the fly. There was a lot of back-and-forth about all kinds of different subjects.

I concur with Bob that there should be more of these.
