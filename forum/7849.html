I think I'll call this one "The Squatters".

That birdbath almost definitely isn't zoned residential, but the family of birds has moved right in...

![Image](http://www.geocities.com/nowyermessingwithasonofabitch/Firstrainbowresize.jpg)

Seriously though, it's an adorable little figurine. We have fun.

The image is my first rainbow transmission ever and I'm absolutely delighted at how bright rainbows are :dance:

What you see is an image-planed H2 white light transmission

in the form of 1/4 of a 4x5 sheet of VRP-M

transferred from 4x5 VRP-M transmission master

H2 exposed to ~50µjoules/cm²

Processed in JD-2 and potassium dichromate bleach

Marks from Scotch tape visible around edges

I think rainbows are great! The photo of course doesn't do it justice; this image is stunningly realistic (even in rainbow colors)
