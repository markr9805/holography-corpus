I just finished putting this contraption together to make cave holograms. I'm giving it a try with a space filter instead of the fiber optic for the reference beam.

It is all mounted on a large aluminum L. On the right is a C315 then a shutter and 1/2 wave plate sending the beam to a PBS. The object beam goes up to a relay mirror to the diffuser on the right of the black plate holder. The reference beam goes thru a hole in the upright aluminum block thru the Melles Griot space filter to the transfer mirror on the left side up to the overhead mirror set at Brewster's angle. It will be a couple of weeks before I get the time to take it underground.
