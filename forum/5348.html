Well, I have had this Sharp QA-1200 LCD tranmission screen for a year now and it never worked right. It always rolled horizontally. I Always thought it was the unit. Well the recent discussions fired me up to try it again. I noticed the adapter conneting the cable to a standard VGS input had apple on it. So I did some research and found the pin outs for the unit and a VGA monitor cable. I bought the connector for about $2 and soldered a cable I cut from an old monitor to the connectors one by one (a task considering I don't have experience soldering small stuff like that). Anyway the unit works great. I am very excited. Here I thought I was taken on this e-bay purchase a year ago. So.......

If anyone needs the pinouts for a QA-1200 to make a cable drop me an e-mail and I can send them to you.
