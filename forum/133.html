Joe Farina Tue Jan 18, 2011 12:01 pm #2
About $1,500 (US) should be just enough to get a Coherent 315M with ~100mW (532nm). Just check with Phil who often posts in the "for sale" section of this forum, or maybe someone else has one to sell (or check eBay). The 315 is not a ring laser, but has plenty of coherence. The Coherent "532 Series" are true ring lasers I believe, and less common. They should have even more coherence than the 315M, but I don't think this is of any practical significance for our purposes, because the coherence of the 315 is more than adequate for our purposes. The price should be about the same for a Coherent 532 @ 100mW, maybe a bit more (maybe $2,000), but they are much less common than the 315.
