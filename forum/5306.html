Many years ago I built a concrete block base for my table using felt and butylene to couple and damp between the layers. For years the stuff was just as advertised... soft, and gooey. Then about 4 years came up and I was moving to a new house... so I started to disassemble the table. OH oh! The stuff had turned into hard gum... glue! It took me two days using a claw hammer to break it down enough to fill box after box with rubble and cart it to the dump... two days of hitting that thing with a hammer!

My hands were blistered, even through gloves and bleeding for about a week after that... but now I think back and both wince and smile... sheesh!.... yup! I've suffered plenty for my art! lol Now my construction techniques are based on welded steel l-frames and precision modular fit molded composite bricks for the lower intertia bulk. The intermediary platforms are stressed aluminum honeycomb between molded composities and the tops are all either Newport stock (also one specially constructed Newport clean room superdamped table top) or bolted, welded and damped structures (aluminum, steel, invar and inconel), built to spec for particular functions. The cryogenic 'brassboard' is constructed of invar and beryllium with ceramic segments ...NO MORE MONSTER CONCRETE TABLES! lol... ouch.
