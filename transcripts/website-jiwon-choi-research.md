# Jiwon Choi — Research

## Bio

Jiwon is a Ph.D. student in Optical Sciences at The University of Arizona, under the supervision of Dr. Weimin Zhou in Computational Imaging and Visual Intelligence Laboratory (CIVIL).

In the first few years of her doctoral studies, she worked with Dr. Florian Willomitzer in Computational 3D Imaging and Measurement Lab (3DIM), focusing on integrating machine learning in various optical 3D metrology and 3D imaging techniques.

Prior to joining her PhD program, she was a data engineer at QuickMD, a HIPAA compliant telehealth service.

Jiwon earned a Master of Science degree in Computer Science at Northwestern University in December 2021. She was affiliated with the Computational Photography Lab (CPL), working on holography image rendering with neural radiance fields under the advisement of Dr. Oliver Cossairt, Dr. Jack Tumblin, Dr. Florian Willomitzer from 3DIM, and Dr. Marc Walton from Center for Scientific Studies in the Arts (NU-ACCESS).

Jiwon received her Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science from University at Buffalo, The State University of New York in May 2020.

## 2024

### Accurate Eye-Tracking from Deflectometric Information using Deep Learning

We introduce an accurate eye-tracking method that exploits deflectometric information and uses deep learning to reconstruct the gaze direction. We demonstrate real world experiments with evaluated gaze errors below 1°.

## 2023

### Analog Hologram Preservation With NeRF (DGaO'23)

Proposed a novel method for the digital preservation of analog film holograms. We also evaluate the performance of NeRF hologram preservation method with both qualitative and quantitative experiments.

## 2022

### Quantitative Analysis of Holography Interpolation With NeRF

Proposed a way of preserving holography images through rendering the holography images with NeRF and NeRF--, a neural radiance field representation method. Also measures the feasibility of interpolating photographs of holograms by using the neural radiance field to synthesize novel views. Through quantitative analysis, it demonstrates that the NeRF model is able to interpolate photographs of holograms.

The presented work provides a way of preserving holography images through rendering the holography images with NeRF and NeRF--, a neural radiance field representation method. This paper explores the feasibility of interpolating photographs of holograms by using the neural radiance field to synthesize novel views. To analyze the performance of the view synthesis method, quantitative comparisons between captured hologram images and interpolated views are done with the following metrics: MSE, PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS. The calculation was done by comparing the ground truth and the interpolated images at a particular view point. The quantitative analysis demonstrates that the NeRF model is able to interpolate photographs of holograms.

## 2021

### Compression Fracture & Scoliosis Classification

Proposed a machine learning based approach to classify compression fracture and scoliosis spinal images, and proposed novel DICOM image enhancing windowing algorithm.

- Nov 2021: The proposed classification model is in an installation process to multiple affiliated hospitals nationwide (South Korea), including Seoul National University Hospital (SNUH).
- Dec 2022: The proposed DICOM file windowing algorithm and the classification model are adapted to the deep-learning-based algorithm that measures the compression ratio (CR) for compression fracture (CF) diagnosis in lateral X-rays. This work was presented at Radiological Society of North America (RSNA 2022).
